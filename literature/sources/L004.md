# L004 — Monodefluorinative Halogenation of Perfluoroalkyl Ketones via Organophosphorus-Mediated Selective C–F Activation

**Online reference 124; Tier 1.**

Ha Eun Kim; Jun-Ho Choi; Won-jin Chung. Monodefluorinative Halogenation of Perfluoroalkyl Ketones via Organophosphorus-Mediated Selective C–F Activation. JACS Au 5(2), 1007-1015 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.4c01242

[doi:10.1021/jacsau.4c01242](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.4c01242)

## Chemical contribution

Replacing the designed phosphorus reagent with P(NMe2)3 gave a complex mixture and only 6% of the targeted O–P enol intermediate. P(OEt)3 gave only trace dealkylated enol phosphate, indicating poor efficiency of the targeted activation with this reagent. Alkyl ketone 2q underwent defluorination but no detectable halogenation; partial protonation occurred instead, and deliberate addition of water afforded protonated product 6q in 71% isolated yield.
