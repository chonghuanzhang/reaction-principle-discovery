# L331 — Elucidating the reaction mechanism of a palladium-palladium dual catalytic process through kinetic studies of proposed elementary steps

**Online reference 395; Tier 4.**

Anže Ivančič; Janez Košmrlj; Martin Gazvoda. Elucidating the reaction mechanism of a palladium-palladium dual catalytic process through kinetic studies of proposed elementary steps. Communications Chemistry 6(1), 51 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-023-00849-x

[doi:10.1038/s42004-023-00849-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-023-00849-x)

## Chemical contribution

The paper experimentally studies a palladium-catalysed C-C cross-coupling of aryl halides with terminal alkynes by separating a proposed dual-palladium mechanism into independently investigated elementary reactions. The authors propose a dissociative Pd-Pd transmetallation pathway initiated by PPh3 loss, followed by association through a cyclic intermediate containing bridging alkyne and iodide ligands; dissociation of a later Pd-Pd complex is proposed as the most likely rate-determining step of that stoichiometric process.
