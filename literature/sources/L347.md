# L347 — Diselenide-enabled photocatalytic hydroazolation of gem -difluoroalkenes

**SI reference 8; Tier 2.**

Mohammed K. Abd El-Gaber; Ryan M. Herrick; Pranaya Sudhakar; Ashutosh Rana; Brent A. Roach; Jeffrey E. Dick; Ryan A. Altman. Diselenide-enabled photocatalytic hydroazolation of gem -difluoroalkenes. Chemical Science 16(47), 22701-22710 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc04074d

[doi:10.1039/d5sc04074d](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc04074d)

## Chemical contribution

Diphenyl diselenide improves photocatalytic hydroazolation of gem-difluoroalkenes and is not consumed by a hydroselenolation side reaction under the reported conditions. Benzimidazole affords a CF2-retaining product in 87%, whereas several azaindoles instead form monofluorovinyl products. The authors propose that selenium-derived species mediate photocatalyst regeneration, deprotonation and hydrogen-atom transfer. This is Tier 2 evidence for intermediate fate and fluorination-endpoint selection, with a secondary role in considering competing endpoints. Selenium is a co-catalyst rather than the transferred nucleophile; the study is not a negative test of project PhSeH coupling.

### L347-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; source summary.

In the optimized hydroazolation, (PhSe)2 improved selectivity and avoided a hydroselenolation side reaction; reducing its loading from 20% to 5% improved performance. Photocatalyst, visible light and (PhSe)2 were required in the control experiments.

### L347-S003

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; source summary.

The authors proposed that PhSe-derived intermediates regenerate the photocatalyst, remove a proton and transfer a hydrogen atom, thereby diverting the radical intermediate from fluoride elimination. This is a mechanistic proposal supported by the reported physicochemical measurements.
