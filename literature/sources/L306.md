# L306 — Click Chemistry: Diverse Chemical Function from a Few Good Reactions

**SI reference 375; Tier 3.**

Hartmuth C. Kolb; M. G. Finn; K. Barry Sharpless. Click Chemistry: Diverse Chemical Function from a Few Good Reactions. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 40(11), 2004-2021 (2001). https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20010601)40:11<2004::aid-anie2004>3.0.co;2-5

[doi:10.1002/1521-3773(20010601)40:11](https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20010601)40:11)

## Chemical contribution

The authors report that many reactions meeting their click criteria proceed better in water than in organic solvent and that organic reactants can exhibit higher apparent rate constants in aqueous solution. For unsymmetrical aziridines, the nitrogen substituent and solvent can control ring-opening regioselectivity; N-sulfonyl aziridines usually favor attack at the less hindered center, while N-acyl aziridines favor the opposite regioisomer.
