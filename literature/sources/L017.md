# L017 — Unlocking Difluorocarbene as a Fluorocarbanion and a Fluorocarbon Radical Precursor for Sequential Catalytic Coupling

**SI reference 134; Tier 1.**

Shi-Ping Sun; Tong Mu; Xue-Ying Zhang; Xia-Ping Fu; Hai-Yang Zhao; Yanxia Zhang; Xiao-Song Xue; Xingang Zhang. Unlocking Difluorocarbene as a Fluorocarbanion and a Fluorocarbon Radical Precursor for Sequential Catalytic Coupling. Journal of the American Chemical Society 147(42), 38897-38906 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c15052

[doi:10.1021/jacs.5c15052](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c15052)

## Chemical contribution

The alkene-partner scope included heteroaryl- and aryl-substituted enones, aliphatic enones, cyclohexenone, linear enones without an alpha substituent, alpha,beta-unsaturated aldehydes, acrylates, and an aliphatic alkene; the aldehydes gave moderate yields and the aliphatic alkene gave a lower yield. The palladium catalyst, Xantphos, and hydroquinone were each essential under the tested conditions; Pd(dba)2 was a less effective alternative precatalyst. The authors propose that oxidative addition of an aryl iodide occurs before difluorocarbene migratory insertion to form an aryldifluoromethylpalladium intermediate.
