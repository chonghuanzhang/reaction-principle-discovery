# L020 — Prediction of Nucleophilicity and Electrophilicity Based on a Machine‐Learning Approach

**SI reference 137; Tier 3.**

Yidi Liu; Qi Yang; Junjie Cheng; Long Zhang; Sanzhong Luo; Jin‐Pei Cheng. Prediction of Nucleophilicity and Electrophilicity Based on a Machine‐Learning Approach. ChemPhysChem 24(14), e202300162 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/cphc.202300162

[doi:10.1002/cphc.202300162](https://doi.org/10.1002/cphc.202300162)

## Chemical contribution

The curated dataset contained 1115 nucleophiles and 285 electrophiles across 22 solvents. The nucleophile dataset was dominated by C- and N-nucleophiles; O- and S-nucleophiles were represented less frequently, with 86 O-nucleophiles and 25 S-nucleophiles. The nucleophilicity dataset excluded transition-metal complexes, solvent mixtures, multisite reactants, P-nucleophiles, and H-P nucleophiles because these entries were scarce.
