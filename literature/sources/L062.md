# L062 — The poisoning of palladium catalyst for the reaction between hydrogen and oxygen

**SI reference 173; Tier 4.**

G. A. Sergeant; A. F. F. Bartlett. The poisoning of palladium catalyst for the reaction between hydrogen and oxygen. Journal of Applied Chemistry 5(5), 208-213 (1955). https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.5010050502

[doi:10.1002/jctb.5010050502](https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.5010050502)

## Chemical contribution

Stibine gradually poisoned the palladium catalyst, but a stibine-poisoned catalyst regenerated over 120-200 degrees even while stibine remained present. Arsine-poisoned catalyst regenerated completely within 24 hours at 250 degrees after arsine removal, whereas regeneration remained slight after prolonged heating at 280-290 degrees while arsine remained present. The authors attribute recovery from carbon monoxide poisoning under the tested conditions to oxidation of carbon monoxide and note that higher carbon monoxide concentrations would introduce oxygen depletion.
