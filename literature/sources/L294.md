# L294 — Fluorine-Containing Butanolides and Butenolides. Vinylic Fluorine Displacement in 4,4-Dialkyl-2,3-difluoro-2-buten-4-olides and a Novel Rearrangement Induced by Organolithium Addition to a Carbonyl Group

**Online reference 25; Tier 1.**

Oldřich Paleta; Andrew Pelter; Josef Kebrle; Zdeněk Duda; Jan Hajduch. Fluorine-Containing Butanolides and Butenolides. Vinylic Fluorine Displacement in 4,4-Dialkyl-2,3-difluoro-2-buten-4-olides and a Novel Rearrangement Induced by Organolithium Addition to a Carbonyl Group. Tetrahedron 56(20), 3197-3207 (2000). https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8

[doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8)

## Chemical contribution

The O-nucleophile scope included simple alkoxides, protected polyhydroxy-compound and sugar alkoxides, and substituted phenoxides; phenoxide products 14–24 were obtained in moderate to good yields. The sodium/tert-butanol condition gave no detected conversion of butenolide 1, whereas the NaH/DME condition afforded product 9 in 53% yield. The authors formulate the oxyanion substitution as 1,4-addition followed by fluoride expulsion from a metal-enolate intermediate.
