# L283 — A Dichotomy in Cross-Coupling Site Selectivity in a Dihalogenated Heteroarene: Influence of Mononuclear Pd, Pd Clusters, and Pd Nanoparticles—the Case for Exploiting Pd Catalyst Speciation

**Online reference 354; Tier 4.**

Neil W. J. Scott; Mark J. Ford; Neda Jeddi; Anthony Eyles; Lauriane Simon; Adrian C. Whitwood; Theo Tanner; Charlotte E. Willans; Ian J. S. Fairlamb. A Dichotomy in Cross-Coupling Site Selectivity in a Dihalogenated Heteroarene: Influence of Mononuclear Pd, Pd Clusters, and Pd Nanoparticles—the Case for Exploiting Pd Catalyst Speciation. Journal of the American Chemical Society 143(25), 9682-9693 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.1c05294

[doi:10.1021/jacs.1c05294](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.1c05294)

## Chemical contribution

With Pd(PPh3)4, direct reaction with 2,4-dibromopyridine predominantly formed the C2 oxidative-addition regioisomer at 23 °C, and the major regioisomer was characterized by X-ray diffraction and NMR spectroscopy. For Pd3-cluster or nanoparticle catalysis, the authors propose organoboron activation before aryl-halide activation, potentially through a Pd3-hydroxo species or a boronate complex activated at a nanoparticle surface; they explicitly state that the mechanistic model requires independent investigation.
