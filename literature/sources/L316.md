# L316 — Kinetics and Mechanism of the Addition of Nucleophiles to α,β-Unsaturated Thiol Esters

**SI reference 384; Tier 3.**

Rosemarie F. Hartman; Seth D. Rose. Kinetics and Mechanism of the Addition of Nucleophiles to α,β-Unsaturated Thiol Esters. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 71(17), 6342-6350 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo060191+

[doi:10.1021/jo060191+](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo060191+)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction of compound 1 with N-acetyl-L-cysteine accelerated as solution pH increased. In an NMR experiment, compound 2 and one equivalent of N-acetylcysteamine showed no reaction before base addition, whereas addition of 0.1 equivalent of DBN produced cysteamine-thiol addition to the crotonate double bond within two minutes. The authors propose that N-acetyl-L-cysteine thiolate rapidly attacks the unsaturated thiol ester and that protonation of the resulting enolate by an acid such as neutral zwitterionic HEPES is rate-limiting.
