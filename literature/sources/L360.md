# L360 — Nucleophilic Substitution of gem-Difluoroalkenes with TMSNu Promoted by Catalytic Amounts of Cs2CO3

**Online reference 29; Tier 1.**

Ling-Feng Jiang; Bing-Tao Ren; Bin Li; Guang-Yi Zhang; Yi Peng; Zhen-Yu Guan; Qing-Hai Deng. Nucleophilic Substitution of gem-Difluoroalkenes with TMSNu Promoted by Catalytic Amounts of Cs2CO3. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 84(11), 6557-6564 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.9b00999

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.9b00999](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.9b00999)

## Chemical contribution

Catalytic Cs2CO3 promoted substitution of gem-difluoroalkenes with TMSCN or TMSCF3 to produce cyano- or trifluoromethyl-substituted monofluoroalkenes. The abstract attributes initiation to maintaining a sufficient concentration of nucleophilic CN− or CF3− and reports moderate to excellent stereoselectivity. This source connects precursor form and base-assisted donor activation to carbon transfer, while leaving the project-specific CF3-to-alkene activation and carbonyl compatibility to be established separately.
