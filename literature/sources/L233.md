# L233 — First‐Principles Prediction of Nucleophilicity Parameters for π Nucleophiles: Implications for Mechanistic Origin of Mayr’s Equation

**SI reference 311; Tier 3.**

Chen Wang; Yao Fu; Qing‐Xiang Guo; Lei Liu. First‐Principles Prediction of Nucleophilicity Parameters for π Nucleophiles: Implications for Mechanistic Origin of Mayr’s Equation. Chemistry – A European Journal 16(8), 2586-2598 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.200902484

[doi:10.1002/chem.200902484](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.200902484)

## Chemical contribution

The selected BH&HLYP/PCM-UAHF computational protocol gave an rms error of 1.9 kcal mol−1 after systematic correction and covered a reactivity range of about 30 kcal mol−1. Some calculated reactions had log k values above 9 that the authors characterized as theoretical only because they exceed the diffusion limit and cannot be measured experimentally. The authors proposed that the physical origin of the s parameter may arise from steric effects.
