# L016 — A Nucleophilicity Scale for the Reactivity of Diazaphospholenium Hydrides: Structural Insights and Synthetic Applications

**Online reference 133; Tier 3.**

Jingjing Zhang; Jin‐Dong Yang; Jin‐Pei Cheng. A Nucleophilicity Scale for the Reactivity of Diazaphospholenium Hydrides: Structural Insights and Synthetic Applications. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 58(18), 5983-5987 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201901456

[doi:10.1002/anie.201901456](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201901456)

## Chemical contribution

Experimental second-order rate constants and values calculated from the Mayr equation agreed within a factor of three for the reported calibration reactions. The reported hydrides have sN values of 0.34-0.68, lower than the cited values for C-H, B-H, and Si-H hydride donors; the authors interpret these low values as low electrophile selectivity among highly reactive hydride donors. Hydride 1a gave quantitative conversion with the tested electrophile set, including the weakly electrophilic alpha,beta-unsaturated esters 3e and 3f.
