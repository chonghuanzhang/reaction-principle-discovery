## Chemical contribution

The 2017 report establishes N-heterocyclic-carbene-catalysed C–S coupling of gem-difluoroalkenes with thiols to construct an olefinic carbon–sulfur bond. It adds an organocatalytic route to the sulfur-partner precedent set and shows that activation mode can differ for the same donor class. The accessible abstract and cited-reference record do not resolve full scope, individual yields or transfer to the upstream CF3-substrate activation used in this project.
