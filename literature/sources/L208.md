# L208 — Asymmetric Cyclopentannelation: Camphor-Derived Auxiliary

**Online reference 289; Tier 1.**

Paul E. Harrington; Tsuyoshi Murai; Chester Chu; Marcus A. Tius. Asymmetric Cyclopentannelation: Camphor-Derived Auxiliary. Journal of the American Chemical Society 124(34), 10091-10100 (2002). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja020591o

[doi:10.1021/ja020591o](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja020591o)

## Chemical contribution

A fused dihydropyran substrate gave product in only 29% yield with undetermined ee, and a beta,beta-disubstituted morpholino enamide gave cyclopentenone in 14% yield with substantial recovered starting amide. The paper investigates an enantioselective cyclopentannelation using allenyl ether-derived nucleophiles and a traceless camphor-derived chiral auxiliary. The authors reason that rapid auxiliary cleavage is needed to avoid rearrangement or proton-loss pathways and that alkoxyalkyl residues perform best because the departing cation is stabilized by adjacent oxygen donation.
