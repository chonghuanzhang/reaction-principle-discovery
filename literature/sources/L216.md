# L216 — Decomposition of Ruthenium Olefin Metathesis Catalysts

**Online reference 296; Tier 4.**

Soon Hyeok Hong; Anna G. Wenzel; Tina T. Salguero; Michael W. Day; Robert H. Grubbs. Decomposition of Ruthenium Olefin Metathesis Catalysts. Journal of the American Chemical Society 129(25), 7961-7968 (2007). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja0713577

[doi:10.1021/ja0713577](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja0713577)

## Chemical contribution

All tested phosphine-based ruthenium methylidene complexes generated methyltricyclohexylphosphonium salts as the major phosphine species upon decomposition. Attempts to prepare the bis(pyridine)-coordinated methylidene complex 24 failed because of instability; its methylidene protons were not observed even in situ. The authors favor decomposition by attack of dissociated free phosphine on the methylidene carbon, but state that the observed kinetics cannot experimentally distinguish this pathway from internal phosphine attack.
