# L200 — Palladium‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Alkylation of gem ‐Difluorocyclopropanes: Switching Regioselectivity via Simple Hydrazones

**SI reference 284; Tier 2.**

Leiyang Lv; Chao‐Jun Li. Palladium‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Alkylation of gem ‐Difluorocyclopropanes: Switching Regioselectivity via Simple Hydrazones. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 60(23), 13098-13104 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202102240

[doi:10.1002/anie.202102240](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202102240)

## Chemical contribution

The authors describe earlier palladium-catalyzed ring-opening reactions of gem-difluorocyclopropanes with N- and O-centered nucleophiles and subsequent reports involving C-C, C-N, or C-S bond formation, predominantly giving linear beta-fluoroalkenes. The proposed mechanism comprises oxidative addition into the strained cyclopropane C-C bond, beta-fluoride elimination to a fluorinated palladium pi-allyl complex, transmetalation with a deprotonated hydrazone-derived nucleophile, regioselective 3,3'-reductive elimination, and denitrogenative decomposition to product.
