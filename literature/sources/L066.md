# L066 — Novel Method for the Synthesis of Merocyanines: New Photophysical Possibilities for a Known Class of Fluorophores

**Online reference 86; Tier 1.**

Brunella Bardi; Katerina V. Vygranenko; Beata Koszarna; Olena Vakuliuk; Łukasz Dobrzycki; Daniel T. Gryko; Francesca Terenziani; Anna Painelli. Novel Method for the Synthesis of Merocyanines: New Photophysical Possibilities for a Known Class of Fluorophores. Chemistry – A European Journal 29(45), e202300979 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202300979

[doi:10.1002/chem.202300979](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202300979)

## Chemical contribution

A poorly reactive naphthalene substrate required xylenes at 160 °C for 72 h and furnished merocyanine 16 in 10% yield; its structure was confirmed by X-ray crystallography. The authors propose that the tandem process probably begins with Friedel–Crafts alkylation of the electron-rich phenol by the aldehyde formyl group, possibly catalyzed by a proton from the acidic tetrafluorophenol, followed by intramolecular SNAr and HF elimination.
