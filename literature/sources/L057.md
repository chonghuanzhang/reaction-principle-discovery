# L057 — The study of carbon acylation of highly delocalized enolates using catalytic DMAP on 3-phenyl benzofuranone derivatives

**Online reference 169; Tier 3.**

Xiguang Zhao. The study of carbon acylation of highly delocalized enolates using catalytic DMAP on 3-phenyl benzofuranone derivatives. Master’s thesis, Eastern Illinois University (2006). Repository/dissertation record 749. https://thekeep.eiu.edu/theses/749

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Across six 3-phenylbenzofuranone derivatives, the DMAP-catalyzed rearrangement gave isolated yields of 76-98%, while the observed color durations ranged from 93 seconds to 53 minutes. The proposed mechanism invokes DMAP attack on the ethyl carbonate to form an acylated pyridinium ion and release a delocalized PBF enolate, followed by carbon attack on the pyridinium acyl group to form the C-acylated product and regenerate DMAP.
