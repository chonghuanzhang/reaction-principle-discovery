# L021 — Ynone Promoted Deaminative Coupling of Gramines with C- and N-Nucleophiles

**Online reference 138; Tier 3.**

Iker Hernández; Guillermo Domínguez; Vadim A. Soloshonok; Aitor Landa; Mikel Oiarbide. Ynone Promoted Deaminative Coupling of Gramines with C- and N-Nucleophiles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 89(23), 17291-17309 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.4c01895

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.4c01895](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.4c01895)

## Chemical contribution

The hydrazone scope showed strong structure sensitivity: a p-tolylsulfonyl hydrazone gave only 35% N-coupling product, and an acetone-derived hydrazone lacking an aromatic C-terminal group failed to give the desired coupling product and instead formed an aza-Michael adduct. Competitive experiments showed that O-TBS ynone 2d reacted with gramine faster than ethyl propiolate, especially at lower temperature, and unlike ethyl propiolate-promoted reactions, reactions promoted by silyloxy ynones 2c/2d did not show detectable Michael-addition products from the C-nucleophile.
