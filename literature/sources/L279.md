# L279 — Effects of Hydrogen Bonding Solvation by Diverse Fluorinated Bulky Alcohols on the Reaction Rate and Selectivity in Crown Ether Mediated Nucleophilic Fluorination in an Aprotic Solvent

**SI reference 351; Tier 3.**

Eloah P. Ávila; Mauro V. de Almeida; Marcelo S. Valle; Josefredo R. Pliego. Effects of Hydrogen Bonding Solvation by Diverse Fluorinated Bulky Alcohols on the Reaction Rate and Selectivity in Crown Ether Mediated Nucleophilic Fluorination in an Aprotic Solvent. ACS Organic & Inorganic Au 5(1), 69-83 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acsorginorgau.4c00081

[doi:10.1021/acsorginorgau.4c00081](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsorginorgau.4c00081)

## Chemical contribution

Reaction rate increased with crown ether concentration: at 2 h, 2 equivalents of 18-crown-6 gave 46% conversion versus 31% with 1 equivalent, and 2 equivalents reached complete conversion in 22 h while 1 equivalent did not reach complete conversion in 48 h. Computed barriers from the KF-18C6-TBOH-F3 reference complex were 26.7 kcal mol−1 for SN2 and 31.4 kcal mol−1 for E2, giving a calculated 4.7 kcal mol−1 preference for SN2.
