# L222 — Silver-Catalyzed ( Z )-β-Fluoro-vinyl Iodonium Salts from Alkynes: Efficient and Selective Syntheses of Z -Monofluoroalkenes

**SI reference 69; Tier 2.**

Alexi T. Sedikides; Alastair J. J. Lennox. Silver-Catalyzed ( Z )-β-Fluoro-vinyl Iodonium Salts from Alkynes: Efficient and Selective Syntheses of Z -Monofluoroalkenes. Journal of the American Chemical Society 146(23), 15672-15680 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c03826

[doi:10.1021/jacs.4c03826](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c03826)

## Chemical contribution

Independently prepared alkynyl-iodonium intermediates failed to react without silver but underwent rapid hydrofluorination with catalytic AgBF4, forming the Z-FVI in good yield after 45 minutes; the corresponding iodoalkyne did not form iodofluoroalkene with or without Ag(I). The authors propose that Ag(I) has a dual mechanistic role in activating the alkyne toward alkynyl-iodonium formation and mediating anti beta-fluorination to control regioselectivity and Z stereoselectivity.
