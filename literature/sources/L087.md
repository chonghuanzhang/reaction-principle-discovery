# L087 — Recent advances in catalytic carbofluorination of unsaturated system via C–F bond reconstruction

**SI reference 193; Tier 2.**

Yaxin Zeng; Xufei Yan; Ying Xia. Recent advances in catalytic carbofluorination of unsaturated system via C–F bond reconstruction. Tetrahedron Chem 12, 100109 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tchem.2024.100109

[doi:10.1016/j.tchem.2024.100109](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tchem.2024.100109)

## Chemical contribution

The review reports Rh-catalyzed reactions of gem-difluorinated cyclopropanes with epoxides to form fluorinated ethers; methyleneazetidines are also competent under modified conditions and give bisfluorinated amines. Carbamoyl fluorides are reported to be less electrophilic than acyl fluorides and therefore require more stringent C–F activation conditions, creating a potential conflict with C–F bond reformation. For the Rh/epoxide reaction, the proposed mechanism involves a fluoroallyl-Rh species, oxygen attack to form an oxonium intermediate, ring opening to a carbocation, and fluoride capture.
