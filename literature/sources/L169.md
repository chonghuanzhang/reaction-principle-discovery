# L169 — Regioselective Synthesis of Emission Color‐Tunable Pyrazolo[1,5‐a]pyrimidines with β,β‐Difluoro Peroxides as 1,3‐Bis‐Electrophiles

**SI reference 259; Tier 1.**

Yangyang Ma; Yuanjin Chen; Leiyang Lv; Zhiping Li. Regioselective Synthesis of Emission Color‐Tunable Pyrazolo[1,5‐a]pyrimidines with β,β‐Difluoro Peroxides as 1,3‐Bis‐Electrophiles. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 363(13), 3233-3239 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202100298

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202100298](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202100298)

## Chemical contribution

Lowering the reaction temperature from 60 °C to 25 °C reduced the 3a yield to 54%, whereas 80 °C gave 74%, close to the 60 °C result. The authors propose that DABCO promotes peroxide rearrangement to a ketone, HF elimination forms a β-fluoro enone, and aminopyrazole then reacts by nucleophilic addition-elimination followed by cyclization and rearomatization; an alternative condensation pathway is also proposed for the minor regioisomer.

### L169-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Among the quoted solvent-screen entries, 1,4-dioxane with DABCO at 60 °C gave the best reported result, with table values of 77% for 3a, 73% in parentheses, and 8% for 3a'; the authors state that regioselectivity depends on the solvent.

Location: 2; Reaction-condition optimization

### L169-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

DABCO outperformed the other reported bases: TMEDA gave 54% 3a, while pyridine, triethylamine, DBU, and K2CO3 gave poor results, including no detected 3a with K2CO3.

Location: 2; Base optimization

Aryl-substituted β-perfluoro peroxides bearing electron-donating or electron-withdrawing groups at ortho, meta, or para positions were tolerated, and naphthalene- and furan-containing substrates gave products in 67% and 74% yield, respectively.
