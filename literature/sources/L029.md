# L029 — Catalytic conversion of fluoroalkyl alkyl ethers to carbonyl compounds

**Online reference 146; Tier 1.**

David C. England. Catalytic conversion of fluoroalkyl alkyl ethers to carbonyl compounds. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 49(21), 4007-4008 (1984). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00195a025

[doi:10.1021/jo00195a025](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00195a025)

## Chemical contribution

The conversion was subsequently performed catalytically, exothermally, at atmospheric pressure and low temperature in high yield for multiple related compounds; antimony pentafluoride was identified as one of several effective metal-halide Lewis acid catalysts. Ethyl ethers are within the demonstrated scope: catalytic removal of ethyl fluoride was reported for two examples. The author proposes that the mechanism may involve fluoride abstraction from a C-F bond to form an acid fluoride, a metal fluoride anion, and a methyl cation.
