# L336 — Competition of Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution, σ‐Bond Metathesis, and syn Hydrometalation in Titanium(III)‐Catalyzed Hydrodefluorination of Arenes

**Online reference 399; Tier 2.**

Juliane Krüger; Jakob Leppkes; Christian Ehm; Dieter Lentz. Competition of Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution, σ‐Bond Metathesis, and syn Hydrometalation in Titanium(III)‐Catalyzed Hydrodefluorination of Arenes. Chemistry – An Asian Journal 11(21), 3062-3071 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1002/asia.201601036

[doi:10.1002/asia.201601036](https://doi.org/10.1002/asia.201601036)

## Chemical contribution

A brominated substrate undergoes competing catalytic hydrodebromination with low turnover, and the absence of the second hydrodefluorination products seen for hexafluorobenzene was interpreted as evidence that the active species is poisoned. Pentafluoropyridine and perfluorotoluene undergo para hydrodefluorination smoothly at room temperature with 1.2 mol% catalyst loading. For hexafluorobenzene, the computed syn-hydrometallation pathway contains a hydrometallation intermediate followed by facile fluoride elimination; the authors further suggest that fluoride is trapped by excess silane and transferred to titanium.
