# L320 — Efficient One Step Procedure for the Synthesis of α-Trifluoromethylated Arylacetates

**Online reference 45; Tier 2.**

In Howa Jeong; Tae Won Park; Bum Tae Kim. Efficient One Step Procedure for the Synthesis of α-Trifluoromethylated Arylacetates. Synthetic Communications 28(11), 1981-1987 (1998). https://doi.org/10.1080/00397919808007172

[doi:10.1080/00397919808007172](https://doi.org/10.1080/00397919808007172)

## Chemical contribution

Alkoxide stoichiometry controlled the product distribution: one equivalent of sodium methoxide gave monosubstituted product 2a in 87% yield, two equivalents gave 2a and ester 3a in 40% and 41% yields, and three equivalents gave 3a in 75% yield. The authors propose that methoxide attack gives monosubstituted product 2a through addition and β-defluorination, after which dealkylation and another defluorination form ketene 6a; further methoxide reaction and aqueous quench then afford ester 3a.
