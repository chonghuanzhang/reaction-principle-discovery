# L209 — Regiodivergent Nucleophilic Fluorination under Hydrogen Bonding Catalysis: A Computational and Experimental Study

**SI reference 290; Tier 3.**

Matthew A. Horwitz; Alexander B. Dürr; Konstantinos Afratis; Zijun Chen; Julia Soika; Kirsten E. Christensen; Makoto Fushimi; Robert S. Paton; Véronique Gouverneur. Regiodivergent Nucleophilic Fluorination under Hydrogen Bonding Catalysis: A Computational and Experimental Study. Journal of the American Chemical Society 145(17), 9708-9717 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c01303

[doi:10.1021/jacs.3c01303](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c01303)

## Chemical contribution

The fluorination scope included varied aryl, amino, ester, and amide substituents; tertiary amines and saturated heterocycles were tolerated, while KF generally gave higher β:α ratios and often higher isolated yields than CsF. For asymmetric fluorination, the authors state that the origin of catalyst regiopreference remains unresolved and propose that distinct binding orientations of the enantiomeric aziridinium ions may expose different carbons to fluoride attack.

### L209-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Computations predicted that fluoride regioselectivity depends strongly on hydrogen-bond-donor coordination: free fluoride favored α-addition by 9 kJ/mol, while oxalic-acid coordination favored β-addition by 14 kJ/mol, with a linear correlation between predicted selectivity and fluoride charge.

Location: 3; Results and Discussion: computational analysis of HBD coordination and regioselectivity

### L209-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For the model dissymmetric aziridinium chloride, CsF without Schreiner's urea favored α-fluorination at 34% conversion, whereas 20 mol % urea gave quantitative fluorination and reversed the kinetic preference toward the β-fluoro regioisomer; KF reacted only in the presence of the urea catalyst and likewise favored β-fluorination.

Location: 3; Results and Discussion: experimental validation of catalyst-controlled regioselectivity

### L209-S005
