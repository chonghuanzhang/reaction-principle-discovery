# L152 — Copper-Catalyzed Fluoroolefination of Silyl Enol Ethers and Ketones toward the Synthesis of β-Fluoroenones

**SI reference 59; Tier 1.**

Yanlin Li; Jing Liu; Shuang Zhao; Xuzhao Du; Minjie Guo; Wentao Zhao; Xiangyang Tang; Guangwei Wang. Copper-Catalyzed Fluoroolefination of Silyl Enol Ethers and Ketones toward the Synthesis of β-Fluoroenones. Organic Letters 20(4), 917-920 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.7b03700

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.7b03700](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.7b03700)

## Chemical contribution

In the tested model reaction, the trimethylsilyl enol ether gave 3a in 13% yield with 54% acetophenone, whereas the TBS enol ether gave 3a in 79% yield; the authors suggested that poor trimethylsilyl-enol-ether stability might explain the low yield, and addition of TMSCl to trap trace moisture was associated with a 90% yield. TEMPO completely inhibited the reaction, and a TEMPO-CF2CO2Et adduct was isolated as the major product, providing direct experimental support for involvement of a radical species.
