# L149 — Synthetic Strategies to Access Biologically Important Fluorinated Motifs: Fluoroalkenes and Difluoroketones

**SI reference 38; Tier 2.**

Ming-Hsiu Yang. Synthetic Strategies to Access Biologically Important Fluorinated Motifs: Fluoroalkenes and Difluoroketones. Doctoral thesis, University of Kansas (2017).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Electron-rich, electron-neutral, and electron-deficient benzylic substrates reacted, but moderately electron-deficient substrates required higher catalyst loading and/or temperature, and strongly electron-withdrawing substrates gave lower yields even after optimization. Cu-based catalytic systems could reach full conversion but gave no or only trace desired allylation product, instead typically forming difluoroacetophenone and bicinnamyl isomers. The authors propose that electron-donating groups stabilize a Pd(π-benzyl)(α,α-difluoroenolate) intermediate and facilitate reaction, whereas electron-withdrawing groups destabilize that intermediate and retard reaction.
