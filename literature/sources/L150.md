# L150 — Solvent- and anion-dependent rearrangement of fluorinated carbene ligands provides access to fluorinated alkenes

**Online reference 246; Tier 2.**

Lewis M. Hall; Lucy M. Milner; Sam J. Hart; Adrian C. Whitwood; Jason M. Lynam; John M. Slattery. Solvent- and anion-dependent rearrangement of fluorinated carbene ligands provides access to fluorinated alkenes. Dalton Transactions 48(47), 17655-17659 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1039/c9dt04307a

[doi:10.1039/c9dt04307a](https://doi.org/10.1039/c9dt04307a)

## Chemical contribution

Changing the fluoride source to TREAT·HF selectively formed Z-2a, whereas the PPh3-containing analogue also underwent competing C-H activation; dppe complexes were therefore used subsequently to avoid that side reaction. The authors propose kinetically controlled protonation at the beta carbon to form 5a+, followed by solvent- and anion-dependent intermolecular proton migration through putative intermediate 8a+; chloride may then displace the alkene and provide a thermodynamic driving force by forming stable complex 6a.
