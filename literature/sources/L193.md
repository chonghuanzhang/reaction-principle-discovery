# L193 — Intramolecular Fluoroacylation Enabled by TrBF 4 -Catalyzed Fluoride Recycling

**Online reference 277; Tier 1.**

Ali McKnight; Yuriko H. Fujisato; Namrata Khanal; Christine M. Le. Intramolecular Fluoroacylation Enabled by TrBF4-Catalyzed Fluoride Recycling. Organic Letters 27(5), 1322-1326 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c00154

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c00154](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c00154)

## Chemical contribution

Experiments disfavored a simple fluoride-abstraction pathway: a Friedel-Crafts probe gave only trace product with 81% recovery of the acyl fluoride, and catalytically relevant NMR experiments did not detect trityl fluoride. The authors propose dual activation in which tritylium activates the acyl-fluoride carbonyl, BF4 supplies the initial fluoride, and collapse of a tetrahedral intermediate expels fluoride to regenerate TrBF4; cyclization and fluoride addition may be concerted or stepwise.
