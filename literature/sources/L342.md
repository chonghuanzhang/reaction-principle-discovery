# L342 — Phosphine-Catalyzed Intermolecular Acylfluorination of Alkynes via a P(V) Intermediate

**Online reference 56; Tier 1.**

Hayato Fujimoto; Takuya Kodama; Masahiro Yamanaka; Mamoru Tobisu. Phosphine-Catalyzed Intermolecular Acylfluorination of Alkynes via a P(V) Intermediate. Journal of the American Chemical Society 142(41), 17323-17328 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.0c08928

[doi:10.1021/jacs.0c08928](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.0c08928)

## Chemical contribution

Under the optimized conditions, acyl fluoride 4a and alkynoate 5a reacted with 30 mol% PCy3 in toluene at room temperature to give monofluoroalkene 6aa in 74% isolated yield as a 1:1.2 E/Z mixture. The authors propose a stepwise C-F-forming ligand-coupling pathway on a P(V) intermediate in which cleavage of an apical P-F bond allows fluorine migration to the equatorial beta-carbon to form zwitterionic INT2, followed by formation of the fluorinated product through PCy3 dissociation.
