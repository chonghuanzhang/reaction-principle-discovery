# L325 — Development of Copper(I)-Catalyzed Synthesis of Functionalized Organofluorine and Organoboron Compounds

**SI reference 390; Tier 2.**

Ryoto Kojima. Development of Copper(I)-Catalyzed Synthesis of Functionalized Organofluorine and Organoboron Compounds. Doctoral thesis, Hokkaido University (2018). Repository/dissertation record k13245. https://doi.org/10.14943/doctoral.k13245

[doi:10.14943/doctoral.k13245](https://doi.org/10.14943/doctoral.k13245)

## Chemical contribution

Under the reported Chapter 2 conditions, the sterically congested branched substrate (E)-1d gave 88% yield but 50% ee, while the tetra-substituted product 2e was detected only in trace amount; the author attributed the latter result probably to steric hindrance. For the Chapter 1 copper(I)/diboron system, the authors propose coordination and insertion of a gem-difluoroalkene into a borylcopper(I) species, followed by elimination and copper(I)-catalyzed protodeborylation; DFT calculations favor the transition state leading to the observed Z isomer, but the quotation does not directly establish every proposed catalytic intermediate.

### L325-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Under the Chapter 1 Z-selective conditions, gem-difluoroalkene 1a reacted with 1.2 equiv bis(pinacolato)diboron, 5 mol% CuCl/Xantphos, 1 equiv sodium tert-butoxide, and 1 equiv methanol in THF at 30 degrees C to give (Z)-2a in high yield with perfect Z selectivity; ligand omission gave only trace product and low substrate conversion.

Location: 18; Chapter 1 optimized Z-selective hydrodefluorination conditions and ligand dependence

### L325-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The reported optimized reaction of (E)-1a with bis(pinacolato)diboron used 5 mol% CuCl, 5 mol% (R,S)-Josiphos ligand L1, NaOMe, and THF at 30 degrees C and afforded (R)-2a in 91% NMR yield, 85% isolated yield, and 97% ee; the exact NaOMe loading is internally inconsistent between 1.2 and 1.5 equiv.

### L325-S010

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose that a borylcopper(I) species coordinates to the trifluoromethyl alkene, followed by insertion to form an alkylcopper(I) intermediate and beta-fluorine elimination to give the gem-difluoroallylboronate and a copper(I) fluoride species.

Location: 59; Chapter 2 proposed catalytic mechanism

### L325-S011

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Mechanistic experiments in Chapter 1 showed beta-deuterium incorporation from MeOD in the diboron system and copper-dependent deborylation of a separately prepared monofluoroalkenyl boronate, supporting participation of a borylated intermediate and a copper-catalyzed deborylation step without directly proving the full proposed catalytic cycle.

Location: 22; Chapter 1 mechanistic experiments relevant to a borylcopper/fluoride-elimination manifold
