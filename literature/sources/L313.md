# L313 — Fluorine Effects for Tunable C–C and C–S Bond Cleavage in Fluoro-Julia–Kocienski Intermediates

**SI reference 382; Tier 2.**

Lei Kang; Yongjia Lin; Zeng Gao; Jinlong Zhang; Huameng Yang; Jinlong Qian; Qian Peng; Gaoxi Jiang. Fluorine Effects for Tunable C–C and C–S Bond Cleavage in Fluoro-Julia–Kocienski Intermediates. CCS Chemistry 3(6), 1678-1689 (2021). https://doi.org/10.31635/ccschem.020.202000320

[doi:10.31635/ccschem.020.202000320](https://doi.org/10.31635/ccschem.020.202000320)

## Chemical contribution

Changing the sulfonyl substituent to trifluoromethyl switches the reaction to a C-S-cleavage/desulfonation pathway; the reported model product is formed in 89% yield, and additional aldehydes give products in 80-91% yields. Computational comparison predicts a 9.4 kcal/mol lower rate-determining barrier for the fluorinated transition state than for its hydrogen analogue, while experiments with nonfluorinated counterparts show low reactivity/selectivity, including less than 10% conversion with a complicated result or no reaction by NMR and GC-MS.
