# L363 — Copper-Catalyzed Selective Defluorinative Sulfuration of Trifluoropropanamides Leading to α-Fluorothioacrylamides

**SI reference 12; Tier 1.**

Xiao-Qin Shen; Sui-Qian Wang; Dongfeng Fan; Xing-Guo Zhang; Hai-Yong Tu. Copper-Catalyzed Selective Defluorinative Sulfuration of Trifluoropropanamides Leading to α-Fluorothioacrylamides. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 86(2), 1591-1600 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c02436

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.0c02436](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c02436)

## Chemical contribution

The author abstract reports N-chelation-assisted Cu-catalysed selective defluorinative sulfuration of trifluoropropanamides with disulfides to yield α-fluorothioacrylamides, incorporating both sulfur atoms of the disulfide into products. It contributes a direct substrate-family precedent and a monofluorinated sulfur-product endpoint. The name alone does not establish whether F and S occupy the same alkene carbon, and a disulfide reagent is distinct from the free thiol used in this project.
