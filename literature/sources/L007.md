# L007 — Barton–Zard Reaction of β-Fluoro-β-nitrostyrenes─a Selective Route to Functionalized 4-Fluoropyrroles

**Online reference 126; Tier 1.**

Roman V. Larkovich; Victoria E. Shambalova; Savva A. Ponomarev; Alexander S. Aldoshin; Konstantin A. Lyssenko; Mikhail S. Nechaev; Valentine G. Nenajdenko. Barton–Zard Reaction of β-Fluoro-β-nitrostyrenes─a Selective Route to Functionalized 4-Fluoropyrroles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(14), 10122-10136 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00935

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c00935](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00935)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction was demonstrated on several-gram scale: pyrrole 3a was isolated as 3 g in 73% yield from 3.1 g of substrate 1a. The reaction can follow competing pathways involving elimination of either HF or nitrous acid, making leaving-group chemoselectivity central to product identity. The authors attribute preference for the fluorinated pyrrole to kinetic control of cyclization and stereocontrolled anion abstraction, despite the calculated nitropyrrole product being thermodynamically lower in energy.
