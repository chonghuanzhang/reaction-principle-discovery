# L353 — Lewis acid-promoted hydrofluorination of alkynyl sulfides to generate α-fluorovinyl thioethers

**Online reference 71; Tier 1.**

Davide Bello; David O'Hagan. Lewis acid-promoted hydrofluorination of alkynyl sulfides to generate α-fluorovinyl thioethers. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry 11, 1902-1909 (2015). https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.11.205

[doi:10.3762/bjoc.11.205](https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.11.205)

## Chemical contribution

Lewis-acid-promoted hydrofluorination of preformed alkynyl sulfides gave α-fluorovinyl thioethers containing C(F)–SR, with both aryl and alkyl sulfur substituents. The source establishes local product-connectivity precedent across different sulfur substituents. Because the C–S bond is already present in the alkyne precursor, the result does not by itself demonstrate capture of a CF3-derived intermediate by a free thiol or compatibility with this project’s catalytic sequence.
