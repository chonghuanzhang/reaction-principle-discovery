# L088 — Synthesis of β-Fluoro-α,β-Unsaturated Amides from the Fragmentation of Morpholine 3,3,3-Trifluoropropanamide by Grignard Reagents

**SI reference 24; Tier 1.**

Amna T. Adam; Frank R. Fronczek; David A. Colby. Synthesis of β-Fluoro-α,β-Unsaturated Amides from the Fragmentation of Morpholine 3,3,3-Trifluoropropanamide by Grignard Reagents. Organic Letters 22(7), 2630-2633 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599)

## Chemical contribution

Isopropyl, cyclohexyl, and butyl Grignard reagents afforded products 8–10 in 62–71% yields with the expected (E)-stereoisomer as the major product; an acetal-containing alkyl reagent gave product 11 in 73% yield, and ortho-substituted and heteroaryl Grignard reagents also reacted. Addition of commonly used Grignard reagents to morpholine 3,3,3-trifluoropropanamide affords (E)-β-fluoro-α,β-unsaturated amides with high stereocontrol. Because the proposed enolate was not detected by 19F NMR, the authors suggested that fluoride elimination from that species is instantaneous.
