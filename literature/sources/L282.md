# L282 — Pd-Catalyzed Dearomative Spirocyclization of Bromophenols via[2+2+1] Strategy

**Online reference 353; Tier 1.**

Kunyu Li; Lu Bai; Xinjun Luan. Pd-Catalyzed Dearomative Spirocyclization of Bromophenols via[2+2+1] Strategy. Chinese Journal of Organic Chemistry 39(8), 2211–2217 (2019). https://doi.org/10.6023/cjoc201903065

[doi:10.6023/cjoc201903065](https://doi.org/10.6023/cjoc201903065)

## Chemical contribution

In the reported ligand screening in DMF, DPPP, BINAP, TFP, and DPPF gave isolated yields of 92%, 86%, 33%, and 18%, respectively. The optimized conditions were 5.0 mol% Pd(OAc)2, 6.0 mol% DPPP, 2.0 equivalents of K2CO3 in DMF at 110 °C for 18 hours. Para-bromophenol substrates bearing Me, OMe, iPr, tBu, F, Cl, or CF3 substituents reacted, including substitution at the 2, 3, 5, and 6 positions.
