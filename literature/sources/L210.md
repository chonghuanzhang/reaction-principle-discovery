# L210 — Palladium-Catalyzed Allylic C–F Bond Functionalization of Pentafluoroethyl Alkenes with Heteroatom Nucleophiles

**SI reference 291; Tier 2.**

Zhengjie Fu; Gavin Chit Tsui. Palladium-Catalyzed Allylic C–F Bond Functionalization of Pentafluoroethyl Alkenes with Heteroatom Nucleophiles. Organic Letters 27(36), 10141-10146 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03217

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03217](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03217)

## Chemical contribution

Condition screening showed strong dependence on catalyst/ligand, lithium base, solvent, and temperature: alternative solvents lowered performance, NaOH was ineffective, dppf outperformed dppbz, dppp, and dppe, 40 °C gave poor yield, and 100 °C reduced diastereoselectivity. Benzyl alcohol and ethyl acetoacetate were unreactive with 1,2-disubstituted alkene 5a under the reported standard conditions, with only trace products detected. The authors propose that increased steric hindrance in the internal alkenes lowers their reactivity toward weaker nucleophiles such as alcohols.
