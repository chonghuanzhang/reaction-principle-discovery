# L051 — Gold-Catalyzed Hydrofluorination of Electron-Deficient Alkynes: Stereoselective Synthesis of β-Fluoro Michael Acceptors

**SI reference 164; Tier 1.**

Thomas J. O’Connor; F. Dean Toste. Gold-Catalyzed Hydrofluorination of Electron-Deficient Alkynes: Stereoselective Synthesis of β-Fluoro Michael Acceptors. ACS Catalysis 8(7), 5947-5951 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.8b01341

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.8b01341](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.8b01341)

## Chemical contribution

Et3N·2HF, Et3N·HF, and pyridine·HF failed to produce the model monofluoroalkene, while increasing the amount of Et3N·3HF did not significantly affect the reaction. Dec-2-ynal was a selectivity boundary: it gave 72% yield and Z:E = 51:49 under standard conditions; at 5 °C it gave Z:E >98:2 and 22% yield after 24 hours, but after 96 hours the yield rose to 51% while Z:E declined to 70:30.
