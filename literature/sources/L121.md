# L121 — Synthesis of Difluoroalkylated Heteroarenes via Difluorocarbene

**SI reference 224; Tier 1.**

Alexey L. Trifonov; Alexander D. Dilman. Synthesis of Difluoroalkylated Heteroarenes via Difluorocarbene. Organic Letters 23(17), 6977-6981 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02603

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02603](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02603)

## Chemical contribution

In situ formed phosphonium salts gave low yields in attempted photoinduced reactions, whereas isolated salt 2e coupled under blue LED irradiation with a silyl enol ether, a vinyl trifluoroborate, or tert-butyl acrylate under partner-dependent photochemical conditions. The paper reports a three-fragment, C2-selective synthesis of difluoroalkyl-substituted heteroarenes from N-methoxyazinium salts, difluorocarbene, and electrophilic or radical reagents. The authors propose that an in situ generated difluorinated phosphorus ylide adds to N-methoxyazinium salts and, after methanol elimination, forms alpha-pyridyl-substituted phosphonium salts.
