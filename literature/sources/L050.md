# L050 — 2,2-difluorovinyl benzoates for diverse synthesis of gem-difluoroenol ethers by Ni-catalyzed cross-coupling reactions

**SI reference 76; Tier 2.**

Bingnan Du; Chun-Ming Chan; Pui-Yiu Lee; Leong-Hung Cheung; Xin Xu; Zhenyang Lin; Wing-Yiu Yu. 2,2-difluorovinyl benzoates for diverse synthesis of gem-difluoroenol ethers by Ni-catalyzed cross-coupling reactions. Nature Communications 12(1), 412 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20725-9

[doi:10.1038/s41467-020-20725-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20725-9)

## Chemical contribution

Benzoate functioned as the leaving group in C(vinyl)-O activation, supported by isolation of benzoic acid in 76% yield; an adamantanecarboxylate-derived substrate gave no coupling product under standard conditions. Ni(PPh3)4 remained partially active, giving 7c in 40% yield, whereas NiCl2 and Ni(OAc)2 were inactive and the starting materials were completely recovered. Competition experiments gave a Hammett slope of +0.82 (R² = 0.82), showing that electron-withdrawing benzoate substituents increased reaction rate.
