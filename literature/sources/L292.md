# L292 — Do general nucleophilicity scales exist?

**Online reference 362; Tier 3.**

Herbert Mayr; Armin R. Ofial. Do general nucleophilicity scales exist? Journal of Physical Organic Chemistry 21(7-8), 584-595 (2008). https://doi.org/10.1002/poc.1325

[doi:10.1002/poc.1325](https://doi.org/10.1002/poc.1325)

## Chemical contribution

For isocyanides, pseudo-first-order kinetic measurements furnished second-order rate constants, and trapping experiments established formation of nitrilium ions in the monitored reaction. A single reference electrophile provides only a limited kinetic comparison window; unreactive combinations and diffusion-controlled reactions cannot be ranked by their bond-forming rate constants. The authors attribute the high intrinsic barriers of trifluoromethanesulfonyl-substituted carbanions in DMSO primarily to carbanion desolvation.
