# L129 — β-Perfluoroalkyl Peroxides as Fluorinated C3-Building Blocks for the Construction of Benzo[4,5]imidazo[1,2-a]pyridines

**Online reference 231; Tier 1.**

Yangyang Ma; Leiyang Lv; Zhiping Li. β-Perfluoroalkyl Peroxides as Fluorinated C3-Building Blocks for the Construction of Benzo[4,5]imidazo[1,2-a]pyridines. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 87(2), 1564-1573 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c02589

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.1c02589](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c02589)

## Chemical contribution

An aliphatic decyl-substituted peroxide was substantially less effective, giving 3m in 31% yield with about 50% starting material remaining despite elevated temperature and prolonged reaction time; replacing DABCO with tBuOK or NaOH produced no observed 3m or 3m′. The proposed mechanism comprises DABCO-mediated peroxide rearrangement and HF elimination to β-fluoro enone B, SNV-type C-F cleavage by the carbanion of 2a to intermediate D, and intramolecular cyclocondensation to product 3.
