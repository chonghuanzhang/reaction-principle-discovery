# L194 — Chemical reactivity of quinclorac employing the HSAB local principle - Fukui function

**SI reference 278; Tier 3.**

Luis H. Mendoza Huizar; Clara H. Rios-Reyes; Nubia J. Olvera-Maturano; Juvencio Robles; Jose A. Rodriguez. Chemical reactivity of quinclorac employing the HSAB local principle - Fukui function. Open Chemistry 13(1), 000010151520150008 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1515/chem-2015-0008

[doi:10.1515/chem-2015-0008](https://doi.org/10.1515/chem-2015-0008)

## Chemical contribution

The study calculated global and local reactivity descriptors for quinclorac using B3LYP/6-311++G(2d,2p) and MP2/6-311++G(2d,2p), with PCM used to represent solvent effects. Based on the calculations, the authors proposed electrophilic attack at 8C followed by C-Cl scission, nucleophilic aromatic attack at 3C through an addition-elimination process replacing the carboxylic group, and radical hydrogen substitution at 2C; they cautioned that the pathway depends on the attacking species.
