# L339 — Recent Advance in Defluorinative Transformations of α‐Trifluoromethyl Carbene Intermediates

**Online reference 42; Tier 2.**

Kewei Chen; Abdulla Yusuf; Xinfang Xu. Recent Advance in Defluorinative Transformations of α‐Trifluoromethyl Carbene Intermediates. ChemCatChem 18(2), e01604 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.202501604

[doi:10.1002/cctc.202501604](https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.202501604)

## Chemical contribution

Thiols and selenols react under iron porphyrin catalysis to give gem-difluorovinyl sulfides and selenides in high yields, while copper-based systems enable analogous reactions of amines and alcohols to give gem-difluoroenamines and difluorovinyl ethers in moderate to good yields. The review describes the coupling sequence as initial formation of an organocopper species followed by carbene formation, migratory insertion, and β-fluoride elimination, but the supplied text does not independently establish each mechanistic step.

### L339-S010

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In the Rh(I)/arylboronate reaction, electron-rich arylboronates favor β-fluoride elimination to gem-difluoroolefins, whereas electron-deficient arylboronates can undergo direct protonation to trifluoromethylated arenes; one such product is reported in 78% yield.
