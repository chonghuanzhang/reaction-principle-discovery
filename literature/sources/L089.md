# L089 — BF3·Et2O-Promoted Decomposition of Cyclic α-Diazo-β-Hydroxy Ketones: Novel Insights into Mechanistic Aspects

**SI reference 194; Tier 1.**

Francesco Venturoni; Bruno Cerra; Maura Marinozzi; Emidio Camaioni; Antimo Gioiello; Roberto Pellicciari. BF3·Et2O-Promoted Decomposition of Cyclic α-Diazo-β-Hydroxy Ketones: Novel Insights into Mechanistic Aspects. Catalysts 8(12), 600 (2018). https://doi.org/10.3390/catal8120600

[doi:10.3390/catal8120600](https://doi.org/10.3390/catal8120600)

## Chemical contribution

For cyclobutyl substrate 20, n-pentane gave a complex mixture, whereas acetonitrile gave amide 23 in 77% yield as the major product together with fluorinated product 24 in 2% yield and diketone 25 in 5% yield; benzene instead gave solvent-derived adduct 26 as the main product in 55% yield. The authors propose that the cationic intermediates are trapped by solvent, fluoride, or hydroxide released from [BF3OH]−, accounting for multiple product classes.
