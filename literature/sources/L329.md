Synthesis of Substituted N' ‐(3,5,6,7,8‐Pentafluoro‐1,4‐Dioxo‐1,4‐Dihydronaphthalen‐2‐yl)Hydrazides by Condensation of Acyl Hydrazines and Perfluoro‐1,4‐Naphthoquinone. ## Chemical contribution

The paper summarizes prior reports in which hexafluoro-1,4-naphthoquinone reacted with nitrogen, oxygen, sulfur, phosphorus, and carbon nucleophile classes, although those prior transformations are not demonstrated by this paper's experiments. The proposed mechanism consists of nitrogen attack by the acyl hydrazine on a quinone double bond to form zwitterionic intermediate A, deprotonation to anion B, and fluoride-anion elimination to give product 11.
