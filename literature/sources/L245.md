# L245 — Nickel-Catalyzed Exhaustive Hydrodefluorination of Perfluoroalkyl Arenes

**Online reference 321; Tier 2.**

Ryohei Doi; Masashi Yasuda; Naoki Kajita; Kenta Koh; Sensuke Ogoshi. Nickel-Catalyzed Exhaustive Hydrodefluorination of Perfluoroalkyl Arenes. Journal of the American Chemical Society 145(20), 11449-11456 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c03471

[doi:10.1021/jacs.3c03471](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c03471)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction rate increased with ICy at Ni/ICy ratios below 2 but decreased when more than two equivalents of ICy were present relative to nickel, showing inhibition by excess ligand. For conversion of the proposed difluoroalkene intermediate, the authors consider two possible nickel pathways: direct oxidative addition of a vinylic C-F bond, or Ni-H insertion followed by beta-fluorine elimination; both pathways are proposed to pass through a monofluoroalkene before further defluorination.
