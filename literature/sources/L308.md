# L308 — Inhibition of a Gold-Based Catalyst in Benzyl Alcohol Oxidation: Understanding and Remediation

**Online reference 377; Tier 4.**

Emmanuel Skupien; Rob Berger; Vera Santos; Jorge Gascon; Michiel Makkee; Michiel Kreutzer; Patricia Kooyman; Jacob Moulijn; Freek Kapteijn. Inhibition of a Gold-Based Catalyst in Benzyl Alcohol Oxidation: Understanding and Remediation. Catalysts 4(2), 89-115 (2014). https://doi.org/10.3390/catal4020089

[doi:10.3390/catal4020089](https://doi.org/10.3390/catal4020089)

## Chemical contribution

A small amount of water used with K2CO3 prevented observable catalyst deactivation and gave conversion close to 100%, but water alone did not restore the deactivated catalyst; excessive water caused phase separation, catalyst agglomeration, and mass-transport limitation. Pre-addition of benzoic acid caused severe inhibition: despite introducing about 40 times less acid than aldehyde in the comparison experiment, hardly any benzyl alcohol conversion occurred, and only about 25% of the added acid was detected in solution.
