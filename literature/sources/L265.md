# L265 — Metal Hydride-Induced Hydrodefluorination of Fluorinated Alkenes and Allenes

**Online reference 338; Tier 2.**

Moritz F. Kühnel. Metal Hydride-Induced Hydrodefluorination of Fluorinated Alkenes and Allenes. Doctoral thesis, Freie Universität Berlin (2011).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Catalytic activity depended strongly on catalyst ligand electronics, silane substitution, and donor solvent: simple titanium fluorides were inactive, tertiary silanes failed to activate the catalyst, and catalytic hydrodefluorination with 18 proceeded only in donor solvents such as THF or diglyme. The proposed catalytic cycle uses a titanium hydride to transfer hydride to the fluoroalkene, forming hydrodefluorination products and titanocene(III) fluoride, which is proposed to regenerate the hydride by reaction with excess silane.
