# L128 — Silver-Catalyzed Regioselective Phosphorylation of para-Quinone Methides with P(III)-Nucleophiles

**Online reference 53; Tier 3.**

Shipan Xu; Jun Xie; Yu Liu; Weifeng Xu; Ke-Wen Tang; Biquan Xiong; Wai-Yeung Wong. Silver-Catalyzed Regioselective Phosphorylation of para-Quinone Methides with P(III)-Nucleophiles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 86(21), 14983-15003 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c01703

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.1c01703](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c01703)

## Chemical contribution

A water/deuterium oxide kinetic isotope effect of kH/kD = 4.0 was measured, and a phosphorus-31 NMR signal assigned only as a possible intermediate appeared at δ = 7.28 and gradually disappeared as starting material converted to product. The authors proposed, rather than established, a mechanism involving AgBF4 activation of the para-quinone-methide carbonyl, P(III) 1,6-conjugate addition/aromatization, a water-assisted Michaelis-Arbuzov-type rearrangement, hydrogen-atom transfer, and regeneration of silver catalyst.
