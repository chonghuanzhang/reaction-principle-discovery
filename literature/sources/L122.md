# L122 — Effect of 6,6′-Substituents on Bipyridine-Ligated Ni Catalysts for Cross-Electrophile Coupling

**Online reference 225; Tier 4.**

Haotian Huang; Jose L. Alvarez-Hernandez; Nilay Hazari; Brandon Q. Mercado; Mycah R. Uehling. Effect of 6,6′-Substituents on Bipyridine-Ligated Ni Catalysts for Cross-Electrophile Coupling. ACS Catalysis 14(9), 6897-6914 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.4c00827

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.4c00827](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.4c00827)

## Chemical contribution

The measured precatalyst-activation trends did not follow the electrochemical reduction potentials: although cyclic voltammetry indicated that Me2- and Mes2-substituted complexes were easier to reduce than the unsubstituted complex, only the unsubstituted complex was activated by TME under the catalytic test conditions. The authors infer that, after activation by TDAE, productive coupling fails because formation of or radical trapping by LnNi(Ar)X is too slow relative to alkyl-radical formation and decomposition; proposed radical side reactions include solvent hydrogen-atom abstraction and alkyl–alkyl coupling.

### L122-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In Ni/Co dual-catalytic coupling of an aryl bromide with an alkyl bromide, most tested Ni(II) precatalysts gave greater than 80% product after 24 hours, but (tBubpyMe)NiCl2 gave 46% cross-coupled product and approximately 50% biaryl side product.

### L122-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Changing the relative Ni/Co catalyst loadings improved the (tBubpyMe)NiCl2 system to greater than 80% cross-coupled product at 80 °C; equal 2.5 mol% Ni and Co loadings gave 91% product at 40 °C, and room-temperature coupling was possible with a 48-hour reaction time.

Location: 4; Optimization of catalyst loading and temperature for Ni/Co dual-catalytic XEC.

### L122-S008

For coupling of an aryl iodide with a benzylic Katritzky salt using the weak reductant TME, only the unsubstituted and mono-6-methyl precatalysts gave appreciable product; the other precatalysts gave essentially negligible product and left substantial aryl iodide unreacted.
