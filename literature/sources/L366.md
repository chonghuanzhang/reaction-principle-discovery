## Chemical contribution

The PubChem-transcribed patent abstract describes a disulfide reaction with CuI/K2CO3/PPh3/H2O in THF at 80 °C for 12 h. It contributes a sulfur-method patent-family clue and an explicit condition regime. Its translated substrate name, trifluoroacetamide, conflicts with trifluoropropanamide in the related paper, so the starting structure and product F/S topology remain unresolved. A/B publications count as one family rather than independent experimental demonstrations.
