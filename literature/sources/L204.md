# L204 — C−F bond activation enables synthesis of aryl difluoromethyl bicyclopentanes as benzophenone-type bioisosteres

**SI reference 80; Tier 2.**

Mingshuo Chen; Yuang Cui; Xiaoping Chen; Rui Shang; Xiaheng Zhang. C−F bond activation enables synthesis of aryl difluoromethyl bicyclopentanes as benzophenone-type bioisosteres. Nature Communications 15(1), 419 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44653-6

[doi:10.1038/s41467-023-44653-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44653-6)

## Chemical contribution

The paper cites a prior report titled “Construction of C–X (X = S, O, Se) bonds via Lewis acid-promoted functionalization of trifluoromethylarenes”; the supplied text provides no reaction conditions, scope, yields, selectivity, or mechanistic evidence for that report. The authors propose photoexcitation of an N-anionic catalyst followed by single-electron reduction of the trifluoromethylarene, formation of a difluorobenzylic radical, addition to propellane, hydrogen-atom transfer from γ-terpinene, and catalyst regeneration by a final single-electron transfer.
