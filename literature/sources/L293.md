# L293 — First-Principle Predictions of Absolute pKa's of Organic Acids in Dimethyl Sulfoxide Solution

**Online reference 363; Tier 3.**

Yao Fu; Lei Liu; Rui-Qiong Li; Rui Liu; Qing-Xiang Guo. First-Principle Predictions of Absolute pKa's of Organic Acids in Dimethyl Sulfoxide Solution. Journal of the American Chemical Society 126(3), 814-822 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja0378097

[doi:10.1021/ja0378097](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja0378097)

## Chemical contribution

Across 105 organic acids spanning approximately pKa 0 to 50 in DMSO, the reported MP2- and B3LYP-based predictions had standard deviations of 1.8 and 1.7 pKa units and correlation coefficients of approximately 0.99. The authors found that simple PCM calculations were also poor for strongly acidic molecules such as methanesulfonic acid and HCl and used the cluster-continuum method for those compounds.
