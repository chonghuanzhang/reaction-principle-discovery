# L267 — Synthetic, spectroscopic, structural, and electrochemical investigations of ferricenium derivatives with weakly coordinating anions: ion pairing, substituent, and solvent effects

**Online reference 340; Tier 3.**

Firoz Shah Tuglak Khan; Amy L. Waldbusser; Maria C. Carrasco; Hadi Pourhadi; Shabnam Hematian. Synthetic, spectroscopic, structural, and electrochemical investigations of ferricenium derivatives with weakly coordinating anions: ion pairing, substituent, and solvent effects. Dalton Transactions 50(21), 7433-7455 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1039/d1dt01192h

[doi:10.1039/d1dt01192h](https://doi.org/10.1039/d1dt01192h)

## Chemical contribution

Use of very strong oxidants such as nitrosonium salts or Magic Blue caused unspecified unwanted side reactions. In MeCN, electrolyte-anion identity had only a limited effect on half-wave potentials and peak separations, whereas larger electrolyte-dependent potential shifts occurred in lower-polarity DCM, especially for ketone-substituted derivatives. The authors attribute stabilization of ferricenium and shifts in low-polarity-solvent redox potentials to counterion-dependent ion pairing.
