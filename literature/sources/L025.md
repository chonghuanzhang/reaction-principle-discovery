# L025 — Room-Temperature Direct Arylation of Polyfluorinated Arenes under Biphasic Conditions

**Online reference 142; Tier 1.**

Olivier René; Keith Fagnou. Room-Temperature Direct Arylation of Polyfluorinated Arenes under Biphasic Conditions. Organic Letters 12(9), 2116-2119 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol1006136

[doi:10.1021/ol1006136](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol1006136)

## Chemical contribution

Ligand identity strongly affected performance: RuPhos gave 91% over 16 h, DavePhos enabled a 5 h reaction, and MePhos furnished product 3 in 90% isolated yield after 2 h. Tetrafluoro- and trifluoroarenes participated in the coupling; the described tetrafluorinated products were obtained in 91-98% yields, and the described trifluorinated arenes gave only monoarylation products in 81-99% yields. The authors attributed the role of silver(I) to plausible iodide abstraction from a Pd(II) complex, generating an electrophilic cationic palladium intermediate.
