# L314 — Deoxygenative gem-difluoroolefination of carbonyl compounds with (chlorodifluoromethyl)trimethylsilane and triphenylphosphine

**Online reference 84; Tier 2.**

Fei Wang; Lingchun Li; Chuanfa Ni; Jinbo Hu. Deoxygenative gem-difluoroolefination of carbonyl compounds with (chlorodifluoromethyl)trimethylsilane and triphenylphosphine. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry 10, 344-351 (2014). https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.10.32

[doi:10.3762/bjoc.10.32](https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.10.32)

## Chemical contribution

The aldehyde scope included diverse aromatic aldehydes, including examples bearing tert-butylthio, methoxy, or bromo substituents, and an enolizable aldehyde that furnished product in 47% yield. TMSCF2Br and TMSCF3 failed under the tested comparative conditions: TMSCF2Br was fully consumed without difluoroolefin formation, while TMSCF3 showed less than 5% conversion and no product with either tested NaI loading. Gem-difluoroolefin formation was accompanied in every reported case by Ph3PF2, HCF2Cl, fluorotrimethylsilane, and unidentified byproducts in variable yields.
