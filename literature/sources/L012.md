# L012 — Selective Quadruple C(sp3)-F Functionalization of Polyfluoroalkyl Ketones

**SI reference 129; Tier 1.**

Ting Xie; Guo-Qiang Wang; Ya-Wen Wang; Weidong Rao; Haiyan Xu; Shuhua Li; Zhi-Liang Shen; Xue-Qiang Chu. Selective Quadruple C(sp3)-F Functionalization of Polyfluoroalkyl Ketones. iScience 23(7), 101259 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2020.101259

[doi:10.1016/j.isci.2020.101259](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2020.101259)

## Chemical contribution

For the model reaction, 2-(perfluorobutyl)-3,4-dihydronaphthalen-1(2H)-one and 2-methylbenzimidazole gave product 3a in 74% NMR yield and 70% isolated yield using 10 mol% CoBr2, TBAB, Cs2CO3, DMSO, nitrogen, 70 °C, and 10 h. The authors propose a mechanism involving Cs2CO3-assisted HF elimination, nucleophilic addition/fluoride elimination to form a coupled species, further desaturation and aromatization, and intramolecular O-nucleophilic vinylic substitution that cleaves a fourth C-F bond; they state that a 5-endo-trig pathway cannot be excluded.
