# L242 — Farewell to the HSAB Treatment of Ambident Reactivity

**Online reference 30; Tier 4.**

Herbert Mayr; Martin Breugst; Armin R. Ofial. Farewell to the HSAB Treatment of Ambident Reactivity. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 50(29), 6470-6505 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201007100

[doi:10.1002/anie.201007100](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201007100)

## Chemical contribution

For thiocyanate, the review summarizes that kinetically controlled reactions generally yield products of S attack, whereas thermodynamically controlled reactions generally yield products of N attack. For enolates, the site of electrophilic attack depends on enolate structure, electrophile, solvent, and counterion, so selectivity cannot be assigned from nucleophile identity alone. The review reports that additions of nucleophiles to sp2 carbon electrophiles generally have lower intrinsic barriers than SN2 substitutions, where bond cleavage in the rate-determining step requires more structural reorganization.
