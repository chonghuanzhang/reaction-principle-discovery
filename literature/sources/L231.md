# L231 — Enantioselective Construction of Fluorinated Tertiary Stereocenters by Rh-Catalyzed [2 + 2 + 2] Cycloaddition of 1,6-Enynes with α-Fluoroacrylamides

**Online reference 309; Tier 2.**

Shintaro Hamada; Yu Sato; Yoshinobu Komiya; Ken Tanaka. Enantioselective Construction of Fluorinated Tertiary Stereocenters by Rh-Catalyzed [2 + 2 + 2] Cycloaddition of 1,6-Enynes with α-Fluoroacrylamides. Organic Letters 27(43), 12172-12177 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03987

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03987](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03987)

## Chemical contribution

The condition screen used 1,6-enyne 1, α-fluoroacrylamide 2, 10 mol % [Rh(cod)2]BF4, 12 mol % chiral ligand, and CH2Cl2; for substrate 1b, (R)-BINAP and (R)-P-Phos each afforded cycloadduct 3 in 82% isolated yield and >99% ee with less than 1% hydroalkenylation product 4. The authors propose that Rh(I) first forms rhodacyclopentene A with the 1,6-enyne, followed by α-fluoroacrylamide insertion to seven-membered rhodacycle B and reductive elimination; they further propose that amide-carbonyl coordination promotes insertion and suppresses β-fluoride elimination.
