# L140 — Cationic Rhodium(I) Tetrafluoroborate Catalyzed Intramolecular Carbofluorination of Alkenes via Acyl Fluoride C−F Bond Activation**

**Online reference 49; Tier 1.**

Tomoki Yoshida; Masaya Ohta; Tomoya Emmei; Takuya Kodama; Mamoru Tobisu. Cationic Rhodium(I) Tetrafluoroborate Catalyzed Intramolecular Carbofluorination of Alkenes via Acyl Fluoride C−F Bond Activation. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 62(23), e202303657 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202303657

[doi:10.1002/anie.202303657](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202303657)

## Chemical contribution

The tertiary alkyl fluoride product 2h underwent downstream C-F substitution with 4-bromobenzenethiol under B(C6F5)3 catalysis to give sulfide 8 in 78% yield; a separate dehydrofluorination followed by palladium-catalyzed arylboronic-acid 1,4-addition gave arylated product 9 in 60% overall yield. Acyl fluorides bearing a monosubstituted alkene or an internal alkene failed, whereas 1,1-disubstituted alkenes were widely tolerated; shortening the tether by one carbon gave phenol derivative 2m' instead of the targeted six-membered carbofluorination product 2m.

### L140-S008

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The proposed mechanism involves fluoride abstraction from the acyl fluoride by a Lewis-acidic rhodium cation, intramolecular interception of the resulting acylium cation by the tethered alkene, formation of a cyclic tertiary carbocation, fluoride transfer from BF4- to that carbocation, and catalyst regeneration through fluoride transfer involving BF3 and a rhodium-fluoride complex.
