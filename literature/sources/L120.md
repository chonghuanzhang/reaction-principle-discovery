# L120 — Kinetic and Theoretical Studies of 3,5-Dicyanothiophene Reactivity with Cyclic Secondary Amines in Water and Acetonitrile: Quantification, Correlation, and Prediction

**Online reference 223; Tier 3.**

Rim Hamdi; Amira Ghabi; Salah Bouazizi; Laouiti Anouar; Jean-François Longevial; Michéle Sindt; Sahbi Ayachi; Taoufik Boubaker. Kinetic and Theoretical Studies of 3,5-Dicyanothiophene Reactivity with Cyclic Secondary Amines in Water and Acetonitrile: Quantification, Correlation, and Prediction. The Journal of Physical Chemistry A 129(4), 1168-1179 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.4c07795

[doi:10.1021/acs.jpca.4c07795](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.4c07795)

## Chemical contribution

Brønsted plots of log k1 against amine conjugate-acid pKaH were linear with correlation coefficients above 0.96, giving βnuc values of 0.39 in water and 0.61 in acetonitrile. The authors propose that increased first-shell coordination may account for the lower reactivity of pyrrolidine in water. The authors interpret the Brønsted coefficients as supporting a common σ-complexation mechanism in which C−N bond formation is partially developed in the transition state and zwitterionic σ-adduct formation is rate determining.
