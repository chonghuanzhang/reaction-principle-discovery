# L002 — Selective Defluorination of Trifluoromethyl Substituents by Conformationally Induced Remote Substitution

**SI reference 122; Tier 2.**

Mehul H. Jesani; Maria Schwarz; Shiwhu Kim; Finlay L. Evans; Alexander White; Alex Browning; Roman Abrams; Jonathan Clayden. Selective Defluorination of Trifluoromethyl Substituents by Conformationally Induced Remote Substitution. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 63(24), e202403477 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202403477

[doi:10.1002/anie.202403477](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202403477)

## Chemical contribution

Nucleophile identity and conformational preorganization strongly affected cyclization: an alcohol substrate gave only traces at 100 °C, a gem-dimethyl analogue gave 33%, and primary or secondary amine nucleophiles gave moderate to good yields; the 4d cyclization showed no evidence of polymerization. The authors propose that benzylic deprotonation and fluoride loss generate a difluoroquinomethide, which is captured by the pendent amine to form an α,α-difluorobenzyl anion before protonation gives the cyclized product.

### L002-S010

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The reported transformation is a base-induced intramolecular substitution in which a tethered nucleophilic atom replaces fluoride remotely, producing a cyclized difluoromethyl product that can subsequently be converted to a selectively defluorinated product.

Location: 1; Abstract and introduction defining the reaction class and overall sequence.
