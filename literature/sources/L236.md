# L236 — Au(I) Catalyzed HF Transfer: Tandem Alkyne Hydrofluorination and Perfluoroarene Functionalization

**Online reference 314; Tier 1.**

Daniel Mulryan; Jack Rodwell; Nicholas A. Phillips; Mark R. Crimmin. Au(I) Catalyzed HF Transfer: Tandem Alkyne Hydrofluorination and Perfluoroarene Functionalization. ACS Catalysis 12(6), 3411-3419 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c05474

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.1c05474](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c05474)

## Chemical contribution

The authors developed an Au(I)-catalyzed tandem reaction that transfers an equivalent of HF from a pentafluoropyridine/4-methoxyphenol pair to diphenylacetylene. Using 10 mol % [Au(IPr)NiPr2] in toluene at 120 °C gave the biaryl ether and fluoroalkene in greater than 80% spectroscopic yield, with complete trans selectivity in alkyne hydrofluorination. Fluoroarene reactivity declined for less electron-deficient substrates, and the reported fluoroarene scope was limited to systems susceptible to SNAr.

### L236-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

### L236-S006

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose a nucleophile-dependent catalytic network in which cSNAr is expected to be slow and turnover-limiting for phenol-based nucleophiles, whereas the hydrofluorination sequence is slow and likely turnover-limiting for aniline-based nucleophiles. They further suggest that direct protodeauration with HX may be a minor pathway and that reaction of [Au(IPr)F] with HX could provide a bypass that generates HF.

Location: 6; Results and Discussion: Catalytic Network

### L236-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For pentafluoropyridine, 4-methoxyphenol, and diphenylacetylene, products 1a and 2a formed concurrently and no HF buildup was observed. With 4-methoxyaniline, formation of 1h occurred independently of formation of 2a, and the authors identified HF as a reaction intermediate from a broad, concentration-dependent 19F NMR resonance at δ 150.5-152.5 ppm.

Location: 6; Results and Discussion: Catalytic Network

### L236-S011

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

No HF transfer occurred without the Au(I) catalyst, and 4-methoxyphenol and pentafluoropyridine did not react after 16 h at 120 °C without the catalyst; the authors conclude that Au(I) participates in both hydrofluorination and SNAr.
