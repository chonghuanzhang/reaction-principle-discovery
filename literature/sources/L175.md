# L175 — Recent development in preparation reactivity and biological activity of enaminoketones and enaminothiones and their utilization to prepare heterocyclic compounds

**SI reference 264; Tier 3.**

Giuseppina Negri; Concetta Kascheres; Albert J. Kascheres. Recent development in preparation reactivity and biological activity of enaminoketones and enaminothiones and their utilization to prepare heterocyclic compounds. Journal of Heterocyclic Chemistry 41(4), 461-491 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1002/jhet.5570410402

[doi:10.1002/jhet.5570410402](https://doi.org/10.1002/jhet.5570410402)

## Chemical contribution

Enaminones prepared from 4-ethoxy-1,1,1-trifluorobut-3-en-2-one and amines reacted with Grignard reagents to give alpha,beta-unsaturated trifluoromethyl ketones in good yield through 1,4-addition followed by elimination. Reactions of enaminones with nucleophiles can occur by nucleophilic attack at the beta carbon. The reviewed amidine reaction was proposed to begin with attack at the enaminone beta carbon, followed by cyclization and water elimination.
