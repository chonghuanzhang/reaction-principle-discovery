# L188 — Catalytic Markovnikov and anti‐Markovnikov Functionalization of Alkenes and Alkynes: Recent Developments and Trends

**Online reference 40; Tier 3.**

Matthias Beller; Jayasree Seayad; Annegret Tillack; Haijun Jiao. Catalytic Markovnikov and anti‐Markovnikov Functionalization of Alkenes and Alkynes: Recent Developments and Trends. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 43(26), 3368-3398 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200300616

[doi:10.1002/anie.200300616](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200300616)

## Chemical contribution

For 3,3,3-trifluoropropene, [PdCl2(dppf)]/SnCl2 hydrocarboxylation at 115 °C and 100 bar CO gives 4,4,4-trifluorobutyric acid in 93% yield and 99% regioselectivity, whereas [PdCl2(PPh3)2]-catalyzed hydroesterification gives ethyl 2-methyl-3,3,3-trifluoropropionate in 96% yield and 79% regioselectivity. The reviewed authors proposed hydroxycarbonylpalladium(II) intermediates for hydrocarboxylation and alkylpalladium(II)/acylpalladium(II) intermediates for hydroesterification of the fluorinated olefins, while the review authors considered ligand denticity a more likely determinant of selectivity.
