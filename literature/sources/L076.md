# L076 — Persulfate-Based Advanced Oxidation: Critical Assessment of Opportunities and Roadblocks

**Online reference 183; Tier 4.**

Jaesang Lee; Urs von Gunten; Jae-Hong Kim. Persulfate-Based Advanced Oxidation: Critical Assessment of Opportunities and Roadblocks. Environmental Science & Technology 54(6), 3064-3081 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.9b07082

[doi:10.1021/acs.est.9b07082](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.9b07082)

## Chemical contribution

The review finds that reported rapid degradation and pH trends for several substrates contradict established singlet-oxygen kinetics, weakening assignments of singlet oxygen as the primary oxidant. It also distinguishes peroxymonosulfate from peroxydisulfate: peroxymonosulfate is more susceptible to nucleophilic attack and oxygen-atom transfer to anionic nucleophiles, including halides, cyanide, azide and bicarbonate. These observations constrain interpretation of oxidation-based activation and competing reactions.
