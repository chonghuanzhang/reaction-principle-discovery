# L335 — New Syntheses and Chemistry of Hexafluorotropone

**SI reference 398; Tier 1.**

Yan Lou; Yigang He; John T. Kendall; David M. Lemal. New Syntheses and Chemistry of Hexafluorotropone. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 68(10), 3891-3895 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo020723w

[doi:10.1021/jo020723w](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo020723w)

## Chemical contribution

A previously reported aqueous sodium hydroxide procedure produced three hydroxy derivatives in 65% yield and a 15:65:20 ratio, with the 2-position described as the least favored site of hydroxide attack. The lithium hydroxide procedure favored pentafluorotropolone formation over the alternative hydroxy regioisomers: less than 10% of the 3-hydroxy derivative and none of the 4-hydroxy derivative were present. The authors attribute the improved regioselectivity of the lithium hydroxide synthesis to coordination by the carbonyl group directing hydroxide attack toward neighboring carbons.
