# L346 — Use of trifluoroacetaldehyde N-tfsylhydrazone as a trifluorodiazoethane surrogate and its synthetic applications

**Online reference 23; Tier 2.**

Xinyu Zhang; Zhaohong Liu; Xiangyu Yang; Yuanqing Dong; Matteo Virelli; Giuseppe Zanoni; Edward A. Anderson; Xihe Bi. Use of trifluoroacetaldehyde N-tfsylhydrazone as a trifluorodiazoethane surrogate and its synthetic applications. Nature Communications 10(1), 284 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z

[doi:10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z)

## Chemical contribution

Benzeneselenol gave the selenodifluoroalkene 25 in 66% yield under the TFHZ-Tfs-derived coupling conditions. Two fluorines remain in this product, so it is an alternative fluorination endpoint to the project target. The gem-difluorovinyl sulfide 9 underwent CuCl/B2pin2-mediated monodefluorination to the (Z)-monofluorovinyl sulfide 66 in 67% yield, while excess p-methoxyphenol/NaH replaced both fluorines to give product 67 in 42% yield.
