# L039 — Sulfonamide Synthesis through Electrochemical Oxidative Coupling of Amines and Thiols

**Online reference 51; Tier 4.**

Gabriele Laudadio; Efstathios Barmpoutsis; Christiane Schotten; Lisa Struik; Sebastian Govaerts; Duncan L. Browne; Timothy Noël. Sulfonamide Synthesis through Electrochemical Oxidative Coupling of Amines and Thiols. Journal of the American Chemical Society 141(14), 5664-5668 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b02266

[doi:10.1021/jacs.9b02266](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b02266)

## Chemical contribution

Batch operation was possible but required 24 hours and increased electrolyte loading, while flow was used in most cases for optimal yields; batch processing was useful when longer reaction times or insoluble starting materials were involved. Acid identity and concentration, electrolyte loading, and anode material affect reaction performance: omitting or reducing acid lowered isolated yields, sulfuric acid was slightly inferior to hydrochloric acid, higher electrolyte concentration lowered yields, and other anode materials were less efficient.

### L039-S001

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose that an aminium radical reacts with the disulfide to form a sulfenamide, followed by two oxidation steps through a sulfinamide intermediate to the sulfonamide; isolation and resubjection of the sulfenamide supports, but does not independently establish, the complete proposed sequence.

### L039-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors report direct electrochemical oxidative coupling of thiols and amines without an additional catalyst or sacrificial reagent; the flow transformation can be completed in 5 minutes and produces hydrogen at the counter electrode.

### L039-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Kinetic experiments showed complete conversion of the thiol to the corresponding disulfide within the first 20 seconds; disulfides were competent coupling partners, and the disulfide was consumed within 5 minutes as sulfonamide formed.

Location: 2; Kinetic mechanistic experiments
