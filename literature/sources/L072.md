# L072 — Photo/Ni dual-catalyzed radical defluorinative sulfonylation to synthesize gem -difluoro allylsulfones

**Online reference 181; Tier 2.**

Yiran Xu; Shengchun Wang; Zhao Liu; Mian Guo; Aiwen Lei. Photo/Ni dual-catalyzed radical defluorinative sulfonylation to synthesize gem -difluoro allylsulfones. Chemical Communications 59(25), 3707-3710 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1039/d2cc05934g

[doi:10.1039/d2cc05934g](https://doi.org/10.1039/d2cc05934g)

## Chemical contribution

DCE gave a much lower yield, THF failed, and the reaction failed when air replaced nitrogen. Para- and meta-substitution on the trifluoromethylated alkene was broadly tolerated, but ortho-substituted trifluoromethylated alkenes failed; several cyclic aromatic and heteroaromatic motifs were also suitable. The authors propose sulfonyl-radical generation by photoexcited Ru(II), radical addition, capture by Ni(II) to form an alkyl-Ni(III) species, beta-fluoride elimination to product, and single-electron transfer that regenerates the catalysts.
