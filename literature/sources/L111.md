# L111 — Preparation and Addition−Elimination Reactions of Benzyl α,β,β-Trifluoroacrylate. A New Stereoselective Approach to (Z)-β-Substituted α,β-Difluoroacrylates

**SI reference 214; Tier 1.**

Shigeyuki Yamada; Mayumi Noma; Kazunori Hondo; Tsutomu Konno; Takashi Ishihara. Preparation and Addition−Elimination Reactions of Benzyl α,β,β-Trifluoroacrylate. A New Stereoselective Approach to (Z)-β-Substituted α,β-Difluoroacrylates. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 73(2), 522-528 (2008). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo701975y

[doi:10.1021/jo701975y](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo701975y)

## Chemical contribution

Diisopropylzinc furnished the product in 97% isolated yield with E/Z = 2:98, whereas dimethylzinc and diphenylzinc gave no expected product; the authors also reported that dialkylzinc reagents prepared in situ by literature methods were inefficient for this reaction. The authors propose that an in situ generated organocuprate forms a π-complex involving the alkene and carbonyl oxygen, undergoes oxidative addition to an enolate-like intermediate, and then eliminates metal fluoride before reductive elimination gives the Z product.

### L111-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Benzyl α,β,β-trifluoroacrylate undergoes carbon-carbon bond-forming addition-elimination with Grignard or dialkylzinc reagents in the presence of a copper(I) salt, producing β-substituted α,β-difluoroacrylates in high yields with high Z selectivity; trialkylaluminum reagents also give addition-elimination products in good yields but with lower stereoselectivity.

Location: 1; Abstract summary of the addition-elimination reaction classes and stereoselectivity.

### L111-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For substrate 1 and diethylzinc under the reported conditions, no desired product was detected with diethylzinc alone or with the tested CuCl, CuBr, CuI, or CuCN systems, whereas CuCN·2LiCl with TMSCl afforded 2q in 80% yield with E/Z = 6:94 at −78 °C.

Location: 4; Copper salt and LiCl dependence of the diethylzinc reaction and organozinc scope.

### L111-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Allylmagnesium chloride does not follow the desired selective addition-elimination pathway, instead furnishing a 1,2-adduct and a 1,2:1,4-bis-adduct in 74% and 22% yields, respectively.

### L111-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For phenylmagnesium bromide, 0.13 equivalents of CuBr substantially improved product yield and Z selectivity: product 2a was obtained in 94% NMR yield, 90% isolated yield, and E/Z = 14:86, compared with 5% yield and E/Z = 43:57 without CuBr.

Location: 4; Optimization of reactions with PhLi and PhMgBr in the absence or presence of CuBr.

<a id="statement-l111-s007"></a>

### L111-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Trialkylaluminum reactions are strongly solvent- and temperature-dependent and generally less Z-selective: triethylaluminum gave no product in THF or ether at -78 °C, but gave 75% yield and E/Z = 43:57 in hexane at -20 °C; trimethylaluminum failed, while triisobutyl- and tri-n-octylaluminum reacted.

Location: 5; Solvent, temperature, copper-additive, and nucleophile effects in trialkylaluminum reactions.

<a id="statement-l111-s008"></a>

### L111-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The tested Grignard scope included aryl, alkyl, benzyl, alkenyl, and naphthyl partners, with partner-dependent efficiency: 2-methoxyphenyl gave no arylated product and 4-trifluoromethylphenyl gave 6% yield. For several initially inefficient partners, modified conditions using 5.0 equivalents of Grignard reagent and 0.25 equivalents of CuBr improved the yields.

Location: 4; Grignard reagent scope and loading-dependent rescue of less efficient partners.

<a id="statement-l111-s009"></a>

### L111-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Under the typical phenylmagnesium bromide procedure, 0.13 equivalents of CuBr and 1.3 equivalents of PhMgBr were used in THF at -78 °C for 1 hour, and chromatographic isolation furnished benzyl 2,3-difluoro-3-phenylacrylate in 90% yield.

Location: 6; Experimental section, typical procedure for the CuBr-catalyzed PhMgBr reaction.
