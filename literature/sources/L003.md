# L003 — Nucleophilicities of Nitroalkyl Anions

**SI reference 123; Tier 4.**

Thorsten Bug; Tadeusz Lemek; Herbert Mayr. Nucleophilicities of Nitroalkyl Anions. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 69(22), 7565-7576 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo048773j

[doi:10.1021/jo048773j](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo048773j)

## Chemical contribution

Solvent strongly changed nitronate nucleophilicity: all nitronates were less nucleophilic in water than in DMSO, with aliphatic nitronates decreasing by roughly a factor of one million, while aromatic nitronates showed a smaller decrease. Consequently, aliphatic nitronates were stronger nucleophiles in DMSO but weaker nucleophiles in water. The authors proposed that hydrogen-bonding solvation of the more localized charges of aliphatic nitronates may explain their particularly large loss of nucleophilicity in water.

### L003-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The kinetic data show that pKaH is not a reliable predictor of nitronate nucleophilicity: aliphatic nitronates in water were about two orders of magnitude less reactive than expected from pKaH, and species with comparable pKaH values could differ in nucleophilic reactivity by a factor of 10^4.
