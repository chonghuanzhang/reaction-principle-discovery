# L045 — Accelerated Reaction Kinetics in Microdroplets: Overview and Recent Developments

**Online reference 159; Tier 3.**

Zhenwei Wei; Yangjie Li; R. Graham Cooks; Xin Yan. Accelerated Reaction Kinetics in Microdroplets: Overview and Recent Developments. Annual Review of Physical Chemistry 71(1), 31-51 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-physchem-121319-110654

[doi:10.1146/annurev-physchem-121319-110654](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-physchem-121319-110654)

## Chemical contribution

The review reports that microdroplet or thin-film environments accelerate diverse organic reactions, including C-C, C-N, and C-O bond formation, and that rates increase by orders of magnitude as droplet size or film thickness decreases. Mass-spectrometric product-to-reactant signal ratios may not directly represent concentration ratios because reactants and products can have different ionization efficiencies and ion-loss behavior; isolated reactant/product controls can correct for these effects.
