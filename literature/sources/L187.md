# L187 — The influence of acceptor nucleophilicity on the glycosylation reaction mechanism

**SI reference 273; Tier 3.**

S. van der Vorm; T. Hansen; H. S. Overkleeft; G. A. van der Marel; J. D. C. Codée. The influence of acceptor nucleophilicity on the glycosylation reaction mechanism. Chemical Science 8(3), 1867-1875 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1039/c6sc04638j

[doi:10.1039/c6sc04638j](https://doi.org/10.1039/c6sc04638j)

## Chemical contribution

In the benzylidene glucose system, reactive nucleophiles predominantly gave beta-linked products, whereas less reactive O-, C-, and deuterium nucleophiles preferentially gave alpha-glucosyl products. The benzylidene mannose system was beta-selective across the acceptor range overall, but selectivity decreased for weak O-nucleophiles and the HFIP reaction showed moderate alpha-selectivity. The authors propose that reactive carbohydrate acceptors displace anomeric alpha-triflates with substantial SN2 character, whereas weaker nucleophiles react with species having greater carbocation character.
