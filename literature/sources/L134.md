# L134 — Phase-Transfer and Ion-Pairing Catalysis of Pentanidiums and Bisguanidiniums

**Online reference 235; Tier 3.**

Lili Zong; Choon-Hong Tan. Phase-Transfer and Ion-Pairing Catalysis of Pentanidiums and Bisguanidiniums. Accounts of Chemical Research 50(4), 842-856 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.accounts.6b00604

[doi:10.1021/acs.accounts.6b00604](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.accounts.6b00604)

## Chemical contribution

Halogenated pentanidiums 2i or 2j supported sulfur alkylation across sulfenate precursors and alkyl halides; reported alkyl groups included benzyl, alkyl, allylic, propargylic, and 2-naphthylmethyl groups, and thienyl or benzothienyl sulfenates gave sulfoxides in high yields and excellent enantioselectivities. Optimization of the sulfenate benzylation included precursor, solvent, inorganic base, and temperature, but enantioselectivity initially remained moderate; introducing halogen substituents on pentanidium increased enantioselectivity, whereas OMe-substituted side arms diminished it.
