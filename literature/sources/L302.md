# L302 — Sequential Addition and Cyclization Processes of α,β-Ynones and α,β-Ynoates Containing Proximate Nucleophiles

**Online reference 371; Tier 3.**

Antonio Arcadi; Giorgio Abbiati; Fabio Marinelli; Elisabetta Rossi. Sequential Addition and Cyclization Processes of α,β-Ynones and α,β-Ynoates Containing Proximate Nucleophiles. Synthesis 46(06), 687-721 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0033-1338594

[doi:10.1055/s-0033-1338594](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0033-1338594)

## Chemical contribution

The palladium reaction provides evidence that catalyst and ligand composition affect productive coupling: Pd2(dba)3/dppe gave nearly the same yield as Pd(OAc)2/dppe, whereas Pd(PPh3)4 was much less effective. The standard system used Pd(OAc)2 and dppe. The authors’ calculations supported electronic control of regioselectivity, proposing greater palladium affinity for the more electrophilic β-carbon. This comparison distinguishes observed catalyst effects from their mechanistic interpretation.
