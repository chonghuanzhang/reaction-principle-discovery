# L189 — Chemoselective catalytic hydrodefluorination of trifluoromethylalkenes towards mono-/gem-di-fluoroalkenes under metal-free conditions

**Online reference 28; Tier 2.**

Jingjing Zhang; Jin-Dong Yang; Jin-Pei Cheng. Chemoselective catalytic hydrodefluorination of trifluoromethylalkenes towards mono-/gem-di-fluoroalkenes under metal-free conditions. Nature Communications 12(1), 2835 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3

[doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3)

## Chemical contribution

Mono-HDF used 5 mol% diazaphospholene catalyst, 0.33 equivalents of PhSiH3, and acetonitrile under argon; substrate 2a gave gem-difluoroalkene 3a in 99% yield after 12 hours at 70 °C. The authors propose a catalytic sequence in which diazaphospholene hydride transfer forms hydrophosphination intermediate A, beta-fluoride elimination gives mono-HDF product 3 and 1-F, PhSiH3 regenerates the catalyst, and a second HDF gives monofluoroalkene 4.
