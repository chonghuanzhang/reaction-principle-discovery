# L260 — Thiol Ester−Boronic Acid Cross-Coupling. Catalysis Using Alkylative Activation of the Palladium Thiolate Intermediate

**SI reference 335; Tier 4.**

Cecile Savarin; Jiri Srogl; Lanny S. Liebeskind. Thiol Ester−Boronic Acid Cross-Coupling. Catalysis Using Alkylative Activation of the Palladium Thiolate Intermediate. Organic Letters 2(20), 3229-3231 (2000). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol000231a

[doi:10.1021/ol000231a](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol000231a)

## Chemical contribution

The corresponding iodo substrate coupled with phenylboronic acid without NaI to give benzophenone in 56% yield. Without catalytic NaI, the bromobutyl-derived thiol ester produced only traces of benzophenone under otherwise specified palladium-catalyzed conditions. The authors propose that NaI generates the alkyl iodide from the alkyl bromide and suggest that iodide might also form an anionic acylpalladate with increased electron density for alkylative activation.
