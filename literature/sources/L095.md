# L095 — 3d/4f Coordination Clusters as Cooperative Catalysts for Highly Diastereoselective Michael Addition Reactions

**Online reference 199; Tier 3.**

Kieran Griffiths; Athanassios C. Tsipis; Prashant Kumar; Oliver P. E. Townrow; Alaa Abdul-Sada; Geoffrey R. Akien; Amgalanbaatar Baldansuren; Alan C. Spivey; George E. Kostakis. 3d/4f Coordination Clusters as Cooperative Catalysts for Highly Diastereoselective Michael Addition Reactions. Inorganic Chemistry 56(16), 9563-9573 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.inorgchem.7b01011

[doi:10.1021/acs.inorgchem.7b01011](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.inorgchem.7b01011)

## Chemical contribution

The supplied table reports, at 2.5 mol% loading after 15 minutes, quantitative yield for 1ZnY, 94% for 1ZnDy, 85% for the entry listed as 1CuY, 29% for 1NiDy, and 34% for 1CoDy; the table heading instead names 1CuDy. The authors propose that barbiturate coordinates to Zn after dissociation of coordinated DMF, while trans-beta-nitrostyrene coordinates to Y, enabling cooperative activation followed by proton transfer and carbon-carbon bond-forming nucleophilic attack.
