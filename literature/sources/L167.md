# L167 — The Reactions of Azines on Treatment with 1,3-Bifunctional Nucleophiles

**SI reference 258; Tier 3.**

Valery N Charushin; Oleg N Chupakhin. The Reactions of Azines on Treatment with 1,3-Bifunctional Nucleophiles. Russian Chemical Reviews 53(10), 956-970 (1984). https://doi.org/10.1070/rc1984v053n10abeh003134

[doi:10.1070/rc1984v053n10abeh003134](https://doi.org/10.1070/rc1984v053n10abeh003134)

## Chemical contribution

The paper is a review of cyclisation reactions between azines and 1,3-bifunctional nucleophiles that annelate five-membered heterocycles to aromatic aza-rings. Regioselectivity in competitive nucleophilic addition to azines can change with kinetic versus thermodynamic control: C(2) is generally more susceptible to attack, whereas thermodynamic control favors more stable C(4)-linked sigma-adducts. The mechanism of the quinoxalinium-enolate cyclisations remains unresolved; the authors state that further quantum-chemical analysis and experimental evidence are required.
