# L042 — Recent Advances in Transition Metal‐Catalyzed Functionalization of gem‐Difluoroalkenes

**SI reference 35; Tier 2.**

Suvajit Koley; Ryan A. Altman. Recent Advances in Transition Metal‐Catalyzed Functionalization of gem‐Difluoroalkenes. Israel Journal of Chemistry 60(3-4), 313-339 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1002/ijch.201900173

[doi:10.1002/ijch.201900173](https://doi.org/10.1002/ijch.201900173)

## Chemical contribution

A base-promoted intramolecular nitrogen attack on a gem-difluoroalkene followed by beta-fluoride elimination generated N-substituted 2-fluoroindoles, but formation of the required amino-difluorovinyl precursor first used a Pd-catalyzed Buchwald-Hartwig coupling. Transition-metal-catalyzed functionalization of gem-difluoroalkenes commonly proceeds through beta-fluoroalkylmetal intermediates followed by beta-fluoride elimination to form monofluoroalkene products; formation of a strong metal-fluoride bond provides a thermodynamic driving force. The review proposes that the Cu defluorinative borylation and silylation mechanisms likely involve regioselective olefin insertion followed by syn-coplanar beta-fluoride elimination.
