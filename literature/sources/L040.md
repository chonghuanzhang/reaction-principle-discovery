# L040 — Copper-Catalyzed Asymmetric Defluoroborylation of 1-(Trifluoromethyl)Alkenes

**SI reference 155; Tier 2.**

Pan Gao; Chengkai Yuan; Yue Zhao; Zhuangzhi Shi. Copper-Catalyzed Asymmetric Defluoroborylation of 1-(Trifluoromethyl)Alkenes. Chem 4(9), 2201-2211 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chempr.2018.07.003

[doi:10.1016/j.chempr.2018.07.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chempr.2018.07.003)

## Chemical contribution

The reported standard scope conditions use 10 mol% CuI, 11 mol% L5, 2 equivalents each of NaOtBu, B2pin2, and MeOH in CH3CN for 12 hours under argon while warming from 0 °C to room temperature. The authors propose formation of an active copper species, transmetalation with an anionic B2pin2 adduct, enantioselective organocopper-intermediate formation, and cis beta-fluoride elimination to release product and a CuF-like species; this catalytic sequence is proposed rather than directly established.
