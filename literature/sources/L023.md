# L023 — Total Syntheses of Perenniporides

**Online reference 140; Tier 1.**

Masao Morita; Ken Ohmori; Keisuke Suzuki. Total Syntheses of Perenniporides. Organic Letters 17(22), 5634-5637 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5b02906

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5b02906](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5b02906)

## Chemical contribution

Product 14 was formed as an inseparable 3:2 isomer mixture by silica chromatography; preparative separation gave 14a in 40% yield and 14b in 26% yield from a run with 68% combined yield. Crude-mixture proton NMR showed that cycloadduct I, rather than product 14, predominated after the high-pressure reaction; HF elimination to 14 occurred only after acid treatment. Increasing diene 18 to 3.0 equivalents did not cause the double reaction; only product 14 was obtained, in 65% yield.
