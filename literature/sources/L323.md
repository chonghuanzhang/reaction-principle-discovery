## Chemical contribution

The C-N substitution of heteroaryl fluoride 24 used a tenfold molar amount of p-methoxybenzylamine in 1-methyl-2-pyrrolidinone at 95 °C for 18 hours and afforded intermediate 25 in 31% yield after purification. Fluorinated heteroaryl intermediate 24 underwent displacement of fluorine by PMB amine, with concomitant removal of the N1-Boc protecting group, to provide intermediate 25. Heating intermediate 25 in TFA at 60 °C for seven hours afforded the TFA salt of target compound 8e in 30% yield.
