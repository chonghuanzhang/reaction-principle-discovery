# L092 — Quantification of the Ambident Electrophilicities of Halogen-Substituted Quinones

**Online reference 197; Tier 1.**

Xingwei Guo; Herbert Mayr. Quantification of the Ambident Electrophilicities of Halogen-Substituted Quinones. Journal of the American Chemical Society 136(32), 11499-11512 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja505613b

[doi:10.1021/ja505613b](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja505613b)

## Chemical contribution

With cyclic silyl ketene acetals, fluoranil showed competing mono- and disubstitution or selective substitution: reaction with 2d gave mainly C-1 addition, a trace of monosubstitution, and a disubstitution product isolated in 27% yield, whereas reaction with 2e gave only the substitution product. The reaction of fluoranil 1d with enamine 2h gave a complex mixture of products that was not analyzed.

### L092-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.
