# L132 — Palladium-Catalyzed meta-Selective C–H Functionalization by Noncovalent H-Bonding Interaction

**Online reference 234; Tier 1.**

Guoshuai Li; Yifei Yan; Pengfei Zhang; Xiaohua Xu; Zhong Jin. Palladium-Catalyzed meta-Selective C–H Functionalization by Noncovalent H-Bonding Interaction. ACS Catalysis 11(16), 10460-10466 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c02974

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.1c02974](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c02974)

## Chemical contribution

L9 increased product formation and meta selectivity under the reported conditions: no more than 20% olefinated product formed without L9 after 20 h, close to 65% formed with 1.0 equivalent L9, and 0.5 equivalent L9 gave 53% yield with o/m/p = 1/3.3/1. Scope limitations included sharply reduced meta selectivity for meta-substituted or bulky-substituted substrates and only trace olefination for substrates bearing additional NO2, CF3, or ester electron-withdrawing groups.
