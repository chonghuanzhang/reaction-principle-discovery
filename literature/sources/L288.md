# L288 — Global and Local Electrophilicity Patterns of Diazonium Ions and Their Reactivity toward π-Nucleophiles

**Online reference 359; Tier 3.**

Patricia Pérez. Global and Local Electrophilicity Patterns of Diazonium Ions and Their Reactivity toward π-Nucleophiles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 68(15), 5886-5889 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo030125x

[doi:10.1021/jo030125x](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo030125x)

## Chemical contribution

For the diazonium ions, positive atomic charge was concentrated at N1, while the largest electrophilic Fukui-function values occurred preferentially at terminal N2, making N2 the more electrophilic site. For the analyzed substituted alkenes, the less-substituted C1 sites had the largest nucleophilic Fukui-function values and were identified as the sites consistent with the stated Markovnikov regioselectivity rule. The diazonium–alkene addition was interpreted as forming a proposed azocarbocation intermediate through an interaction predicted to be more orbital-controlled than charge-controlled.
