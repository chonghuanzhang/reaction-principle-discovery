# L044 — How Constant Are Ritchie's “Constant Selectivity Relationships”? A General Reactivity Scale for n-, π-, and σ-Nucleophiles

**Online reference 158; Tier 3.**

Shinya Minegishi; Herbert Mayr. How Constant Are Ritchie's “Constant Selectivity Relationships”? A General Reactivity Scale for n-, π-, and σ-Nucleophiles. Journal of the American Chemical Society 125(1), 286-295 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja021010y

[doi:10.1021/ja021010y](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja021010y)

## Chemical contribution

Some nucleophile-electrophile combinations had unfavorable product-formation equilibria or incomplete conversion: semicarbazide required relatively high concentration for 90% conversion, aqueous azide gave less than 5% carbocation loss with two electrophiles, and other aqueous azide reactions were incomplete but too fast for rate determination. Attempts to obtain aqueous phenoxide kinetics failed because the carbocation decays were not exponential; p-nitrophenoxide was too unreactive to provide precise rate constants with the tested electrophiles.
