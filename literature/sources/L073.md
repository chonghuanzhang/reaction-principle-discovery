# L073 — Copper-Catalyzed Regioselective Monodefluoroborylation of Polyfluoroalkenes en Route to Diverse Fluoroalkenes

**Online reference 19; Tier 2.**

Hironobu Sakaguchi; Yuta Uetake; Masato Ohashi; Takashi Niwa; Sensuke Ogoshi; Takamitsu Hosoya. Copper-Catalyzed Regioselective Monodefluoroborylation of Polyfluoroalkenes en Route to Diverse Fluoroalkenes. Journal of the American Chemical Society 139(36), 12855-12862 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.7b08343

[doi:10.1021/jacs.7b08343](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.7b08343)

## Chemical contribution

At 80 °C, the difluorovinyl arene reaction gave no product without added base, whereas 0.2 equivalents of KOAc gave 77% yield; CuOAc and PCy3 could also promote the reaction without separately added KOAc. The authors concluded that substrate–diboron matching matters, and reported that (Bnep)2 was less effective for substrates bearing an electron-withdrawing group, including para-methoxycarbonyl-substituted 1f. The authors propose an addition-elimination pathway in which substrate-dependent beta-fluorine elimination controls regioselectivity.
