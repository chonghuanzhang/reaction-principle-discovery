# L077 — Nitrogen-embedding heterocyclic nucleophiles in catalytic asymmetric vinylogous Mannich reactions

**SI reference 184; Tier 3.**

Beatrice Ranieri. Nitrogen-embedding heterocyclic nucleophiles in catalytic asymmetric vinylogous Mannich reactions. Doctoral thesis, Università degli Studi di Parma (2013).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

The three-component protocol accepted a variety of alkyl and hydroxyalkyl aldehydes with generally good yields, excellent anti diastereoselectivity, and up to 96% ee, but aromatic and alpha,beta-unsaturated aldehydes gave lower yields and only modest stereoselectivity. Nucleophile structure imposed scope boundaries: the N-benzyl TBS derivative gave only 6% isolated yield, the N-allyl derivative gave a racemic product in 14% yield, and N-methyl silyloxypyrroles failed to provide the desired Mannich adduct.
