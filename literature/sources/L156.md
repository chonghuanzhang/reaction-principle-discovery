# L156 — Nickel‐Catalyzed Selective C(sp2)−F Bond Alkylation of Industrially Relevant Hydrofluoroolefin HFO‐1234yf

**Online reference 249; Tier 2.**

Yun‐Cheng Luo; Ming‐Kuan Wang; Ling‐Chao Yu; Xingang Zhang. Nickel‐Catalyzed Selective C(sp2)−F Bond Alkylation of Industrially Relevant Hydrofluoroolefin HFO‐1234yf. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 62(37), e202308690 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202308690

[doi:10.1002/anie.202308690](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202308690)

## Chemical contribution

Primary alkylzinc reagents gave moderate to good yields, and the reaction tolerated silylether, benzyloxy, arylether, ester, phosphate, acetyl, amide, acetal, pyrrole, alkyl borate, and aryl bromide functionality. Initial Ni(cod)2/bipyridine conditions in THF at room temperature gave only trace product; MeCN raised the yield to 19%, whereas dioxane, DMF, CH2Cl2, and toluene gave less than 2%, and defluorination remained significant. The authors propose a Ni(0)/Ni(II) cycle involving HFO coordination, lithium-assisted selective C(sp2)-F oxidative addition to an alkenylnickel(II) species, transmetallation, and reductive elimination.
