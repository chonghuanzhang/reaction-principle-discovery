# L138 — Synthetic methodologies for the chemical recycling of fluorocarbons

**Online reference 239; Tier 2.**

Shannon E. S. Farley; Mark R. Crimmin. Synthetic methodologies for the chemical recycling of fluorocarbons. Nature Reviews Chemistry 10(4), 277-287 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41570-026-00813-y

[doi:10.1038/s41570-026-00813-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41570-026-00813-y)

## Chemical contribution

Catalytic fluoride shuttling from ordinary aliphatic C(sp3)-F bonds or perfluoroalkanes is essentially unknown, and successful defluorination can generate unstable radicals or cations that undergo undesired side reactions. The review cites phosphine-catalyzed intermolecular acylfluorination of alkynes proceeding via a P(V) intermediate. Computational studies suggest that base-mediated defluorination of a vicinal H-C-C-F unit proceeds through rate-limiting deprotonation followed by fast beta-fluoride elimination to form anhydrous KF.
