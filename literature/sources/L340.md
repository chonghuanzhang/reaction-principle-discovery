# L340 — Halide Effects in Transition Metal Catalysis

**Online reference 402; Tier 3.**

Keith Fagnou; Mark Lautens. Halide Effects in Transition Metal Catalysis. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 41(1), 26-47 (2002). https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20020104)41:1<26::aid-anie26>3.0.co;2-9

[doi:10.1002/1521-3773(20020104)41:1](https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20020104)41:1)

## Chemical contribution

In a palladium reaction of an unsaturated carbonyl system, excess bromide suppressed beta-hydride elimination and enabled protonolysis, whereas low bromide loading gave the normal beta-hydride-elimination product; LiF did not reproduce the suppression. The proposed explanation for halide-mediated protonolysis is that excess halide occupies the free coordination site, donates electron density to palladium to polarize the Pd-C bond, and permits a palladium-enolate/oxoallylpalladium equilibrium that facilitates protonation.
