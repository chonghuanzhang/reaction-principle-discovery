# L297 — Sterically congested boronate and silane synthesis via electronically controlled protoboration and protosilylation

**SI reference 366; Tier 2.**

Cheng-Qiang Wang; Yi Li; Chao Feng. Sterically congested boronate and silane synthesis via electronically controlled protoboration and protosilylation. Cell Reports Physical Science 2(6), 100461 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2021.100461

[doi:10.1016/j.xcrp.2021.100461](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2021.100461)

## Chemical contribution

Under standard protoboration conditions, nonfluorinated terminal allene 1am reacted only weakly and gem-dibromoallene 1an gave no reaction; Xantphos enabled a different terminal-allene outcome but did not alter the model gem-difluoroallene regioselectivity. The standard protoboration scope used allene 1, B2pin2, CuI, NaOtBu, and MeOH in DCE at 28 °C for 12 h under nitrogen, followed by NaOH/H2O2 oxidation; reported scope yields were isolated product yields.
