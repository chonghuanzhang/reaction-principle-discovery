# L321 — Regio-, stereo-, and enantioselective ipso- and migratory defluorinative olefin cross-coupling to access highly functionalized monofluoroalkenes

**Online reference 18; Tier 2.**

Daning Zeng; Zihao Liu; Guoce Huang; You Wang; Shaolin Zhu. Regio-, stereo-, and enantioselective ipso- and migratory defluorinative olefin cross-coupling to access highly functionalized monofluoroalkenes. Nature Communications 15(1), 7645 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52054-6

[doi:10.1038/s41467-024-52054-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52054-6)

## Chemical contribution

For migratory coupling, L1* alone gave low reactivity and moderate regioselectivity, whereas adding an achiral chain-walking ligand improved catalytic efficiency and regioselectivity without significant loss of enantioselectivity; the dual-ligand system supported terminal and internal unactivated alkenes. The authors interpret styrene-isomer accumulation as evidence that alkene isomerization is faster than asymmetric hydroalkenylation, and interpret isotope-labeling results as indicating that the achiral ligand participates in isomerization but minimally, if at all, in styrene hydroalkenylation.

### L321-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The reported general procedure uses 10 mol% NiI2·xH2O, 12 mol% L1*, 1 equivalent KI, 6 mol% TBAF, DMA, 1.5 equivalents of styrene, 1 equivalent of gem-difluoroalkene, and 2 equivalents of DEMS at 25 °C for up to 10 hours under nitrogen.

Location: 3; General procedure A

<a id="statement-l321-s003"></a>

### L321-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A catalytic amount of TBAF was critical for reaction initiation, Na3PO4 and NaF were comparably effective, THF harmed both reactivity and enantioselectivity, and operation at 0 °C lowered the yield somewhat.

Location: 1; Condition-selection boundaries

### L321-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A Ni(0) catalyst did not produce the desired model product, and an electron-deficient CF3-substituted radical acceptor did not furnish the proposed radical-trapping/β-fluoride-elimination product, including when the gem-difluoroalkene coupling partner was absent.

### L321-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors report a NiH-catalyzed ipso- and migratory defluorinative cross-coupling of olefins with gem-difluoroalkenes under mild conditions, producing structurally diverse monofluoroalkenes bearing a tertiary allylic stereogenic center.

### L321-S007

### L321-S008

### L321-S011
