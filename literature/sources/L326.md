# L326 — Photocatalytic Dual-Defluorination Thiolation of Trifluoromethyl Hydrazones

**SI reference 391; Tier 2.**

Wen-Jie Pan; Wei-Guo Cao; Ji-Chang Xiao; Jin-Hong Lin. Photocatalytic Dual-Defluorination Thiolation of Trifluoromethyl Hydrazones. Organic Letters 27(20), 5275-5280 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c01492

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c01492](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c01492)

## Chemical contribution

No product formed without light; omission of the photocatalyst gave dramatically lower yield and poor E/Z selectivity; TEMPO almost completely inhibited the reaction. The authors propose a radical-carbanion relay in which base-generated hydrazone carbanion is oxidized by photoexcited Ru complex, proceeds through radical and diazo species, loses nitrogen, is reduced to an anion, and undergoes beta-fluoride elimination to form a gem-difluoroolefin.
