# L164 — Cytotoxicity of new n-butylamino and sulfur-containing derivatives of polyfluorinated 1,4-naphthoquinone

**SI reference 41; Tier 1.**

Ol'ga A. Zakharova; Leonid I. Goryunov; Nadezhda M. Troshkova; Ludmila P. Ovchinnikova; Vitalij D. Shteingarts; Georgy A. Nevinsky. Cytotoxicity of new n-butylamino and sulfur-containing derivatives of polyfluorinated 1,4-naphthoquinone. European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry 45(1), 270-274 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejmech.2009.10.006

[doi:10.1016/j.ejmech.2009.10.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejmech.2009.10.006)

## Chemical contribution

Reaction of quinone 7 with a stated 1.5-fold excess of n-butylamine in 1,4-dioxane at room temperature gave predominantly the monosubstituted product 1, which was isolated in 63% yield. Compounds 1–6 were synthesized by amino-defluorination of hexafluoro-1,4-naphthoquinone with n-butylamine, 2-methylthioethylamine, or 2,2′-dithiodiethylamine, establishing C–N bond formation with loss of fluorine on a polyfluorinated quinone electrophile. Reaction of two equivalents of quinone 7 with 2,2′-dithiodiethylamine at room temperature for 15 h gave compound 6 in 75% isolated yield.
