# L248 — Palladium-Catalyzed Domino Heck/Intermolecular C–H Bond Functionalization: Efficient Synthesis of Alkylated Polyfluoroarene Derivatives

**SI reference 324; Tier 1.**

Xin-Xing Wu; Wen-Long Chen; Yi Shen; Si Chen; Peng-Fei Xu; Yong-Min Liang. Palladium-Catalyzed Domino Heck/Intermolecular C–H Bond Functionalization: Efficient Synthesis of Alkylated Polyfluoroarene Derivatives. Organic Letters 18(8), 1784-1787 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.6b00447

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.6b00447](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.6b00447)

## Chemical contribution

For the model reaction, SPhos outperformed PPh3, XPhos, Xantphos, and DPEphos and gave product 3a in 71% yield. A substrate containing a CF3 substituent afforded product 3p in 51% yield, whereas an amide bearing a free N−H bond failed to give the desired product 3q. The authors propose oxidative addition of Pd(0) to the substrate C−I bond, intramolecular Heck formation of an alkylpalladium intermediate, silver-mediated iodide abstraction, concerted metalation–deprotonation of the polyfluoroarene, and reductive elimination.
