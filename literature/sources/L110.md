# L110 — A facile synthesis of 2,3-disubstituted furo[2,3-b]pyridines

**SI reference 213; Tier 1.**

Gregory L. Beutner; Jeffrey T. Kuethe; Nobuyoshi Yasuda. A facile synthesis of 2,3-disubstituted furo[2,3-b]pyridines. Tetrahedron Letters 50(7), 781-784 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2008.11.125

[doi:10.1016/j.tetlet.2008.11.125](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2008.11.125)

## Chemical contribution

Ketones 22 and 23 were instead obtained from amide 21b using PhLi, MeLi, or MeMgBr; the authors report excellent or similarly high yields and no observable impurities for the organolithium reactions. For formation of ester-containing product 12a, sodium hydride and ethyl glycolate in THF at 60 °C for 12 hours afforded a 50% yield; intermediate 11a formed rapidly and cyclized more slowly.

### L110-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors report a sequence from 2-fluoropyridines and carboxylic acids in which acylation with a Weinreb amide is followed by a one-pot, base-induced displacement and cyclization to give 2,3-carbon-disubstituted furo[2,3-b]pyridines.

Location: 1; Description of the reported synthetic protocol and accessible product classes.

### L110-S003

### L110-S009
