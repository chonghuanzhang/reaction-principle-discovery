# L015 — An efficient access to 3,6-disubstituted 1H-pyrazolo[3,4-b]pyridines via a one-pot double SNAr reaction and pyrazole formation

**Online reference 132; Tier 1.**

Yong-Li Zhong; Matthew G. Lindale; Nobuyoshi Yasuda. An efficient access to 3,6-disubstituted 1H-pyrazolo[3,4-b]pyridines via a one-pot double SNAr reaction and pyrazole formation. Tetrahedron Letters 50(20), 2293-2297 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2009.02.142

[doi:10.1016/j.tetlet.2009.02.142](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2009.02.142)

## Chemical contribution

The reported transformation is a tandem sequence comprising selective nucleophilic substitution of the 6-fluoride of a 2,6-difluoro-3-ketopyridine, hydrazine substitution of the remaining 2-fluoride, and pyrazole formation, furnishing 3,6-disubstituted 1H-pyrazolo[3,4-b]pyridines. The authors tentatively attributed the solvent-dependent regioselectivity to hydrogen bonding: carbonyl-amine hydrogen bonding in nonpolar solvent was proposed to direct substitution toward C-2, whereas amine-solvent hydrogen bonding in polar solvent was proposed to favor substitution at the less hindered C-6 position.

### L015-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Regioselectivity in the initial tert-butylamine SNAr step was strongly solvent-dependent: DMAc gave the 6-substituted and 2-substituted intermediates in a 4:1 ratio, while toluene reversed the preference and gave the 2-substituted intermediate selectively at a reported 16.7:1 ratio over the 6-substituted intermediate.

Location: 2; Solvent effects on regioselectivity of the first nucleophilic aromatic substitution.

### L015-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The general tandem procedure used 3 equivalents of the initial nucleophile in DMAc at 0-5 °C for 0.5-3 h, followed by 4 equivalents of hydrazine monohydrate at 0-5 °C and subsequent stirring at room temperature for 1-5 h.

### L015-S004
