# L163 — Selectively Tunable Synthesis of α-Trifluoromethyl Ketones

**Online reference 255; Tier 1.**

Pan Zhou; Shanya Lu; Xiaoqiang Wu; Wei Zhong; Tao XU. Selectively Tunable Synthesis of α-Trifluoromethyl Ketones. Organic Letters 25(13), 2344-2348 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00725

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00725](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00725)

## Chemical contribution

Under the standard conditions, the model reaction gave the target product in 88% yield and a dehaloprotonation side product in 11% yield. Control experiments indicated that nickel, photocatalyst, light, and base were important, whereas omission of dtbpy still gave 78% product yield. The authors propose a Ni(I)-involving cycle in which photoexcited TBADT generates an acyl radical from the aldehyde, Ni(I) captures that radical, an alkyl radical oxidizes the resulting acyl–nickel intermediate to Ni(III), and reductive elimination forms the C–C bond.
