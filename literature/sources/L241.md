# L241 — Cu-Catalyzed Stereoselective Borylation of gem-Difluoroalkenes with B2pin2

**Online reference 318; Tier 2.**

Juan Zhang; Wenpeng Dai; Qingyun Liu; Song Cao. Cu-Catalyzed Stereoselective Borylation of gem-Difluoroalkenes with B2pin2. Organic Letters 19(12), 3283-3286 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.7b01430

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.7b01430](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.7b01430)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction tolerated methylthio, acetoxy, chloro, bromo, and iodo substituents; naphthalene- and thiophene-containing substrates gave products in modest yields, and a difluorodiene substrate gave its product in moderate yield. The proposed catalytic cycle involves formation of a phosphine-coordinated Cu(I)-boryl complex, difluoroalkene insertion into the Cu–B bond to give a beta-borylalkyl copper species, conformational selection, beta-fluoride elimination to form the (Z)-fluorinated alkenylboronate, and regeneration of the Cu-boryl catalyst.
