# L304 — In Situ Studies of Arylboronic Acids/Esters and R3SiCF3 Reagents: Kinetics, Speciation, and Dysfunction at the Carbanion–Ate Interface

**Online reference 373; Tier 4.**

Andrés García-Domínguez; Andrew G. Leach; Guy C. Lloyd-Jones. In Situ Studies of Arylboronic Acids/Esters and R3SiCF3 Reagents: Kinetics, Speciation, and Dysfunction at the Carbanion–Ate Interface. Accounts of Chemical Research 55(9), 1324-1336 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.accounts.2c00113

[doi:10.1021/acs.accounts.2c00113](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.accounts.2c00113)

## Chemical contribution

For CF3 addition to the ketone, excess ketone produced autoacceleration, whereas excess TMSCF3 progressively slowed the reaction; O-silylation of enolizable ketones was the only major side reaction reported. Kinetic analysis supports a mechanism in which the siliconate serves as a reservoir and TMSCF3 inhibits CF3 transfer by sequestering free trifluoromethanide; calculations disfavor direct CF3 transfer from the siliconate and support predissociation of CF3 anion.
