# L185 — Anion−π Complexes of Halides with p ‑Benzoquinones: Structures, Thermodynamics, and Criteria of Charge Transfer to Electron Transfer Transition

**Online reference 271; Tier 1.**

Slade Kepler; Matthias Zeller; Sergiy V. Rosokha. Anion−π Complexes of Halides with p ‑Benzoquinones: Structures, Thermodynamics, and Criteria of Charge Transfer to Electron Transfer Transition. Journal of the American Chemical Society 141(23), 9338-9348 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b03277

[doi:10.1021/jacs.9b03277](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b03277)

## Chemical contribution

Computational optimization placed fluoride at oxygen-, halogen-, or cyano-substituted benzoquinone carbons; some starting arrangements gave σ-complexes, while arrangements over halogen-substituted carbons gave halogen substitution by fluorine. Mixing p-benzoquinone acceptors with chloride, bromide, or iodide produced anion−π complexes, whereas fluoride produced structurally distinct σ-complexes. The authors propose that anion−π complexation facilitates halide/benzoquinone electron transfer by enabling interaction with a second halide and decreasing the electron-transfer barrier.
