# L171 — Visible‐Light‐Promoted Oxy‐difluoroalkylation of Aryl Alkynes for the Synthesis of β‐Fluoroenones and α‐Difluoroalkyl Ketones

**SI reference 46; Tier 1.**

Zhong Zhang; Xiangqian Li; Dayong Shi. Visible‐Light‐Promoted Oxy‐difluoroalkylation of Aryl Alkynes for the Synthesis of β‐Fluoroenones and α‐Difluoroalkyl Ketones. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 363(13), 3348-3353 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202100289

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202100289](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202100289)

## Chemical contribution

Methanol was tested as an oxygen nucleophile and generated product 7 in 47% yield, demonstrating capture by an oxygen nucleophile other than water under this reaction manifold. The authors propose photoinduced single-electron formation of a CF2CO2Et radical, radical addition to the alkyne, oxidation of the resulting vinyl radical to a vinyl carbocation, capture by water or alcohol to form the α-difluoroalkyl ketone, and base-promoted E2 elimination to form the (E)-β-fluoroenone.
