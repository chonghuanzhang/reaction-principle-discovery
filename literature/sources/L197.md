# L197 — Enantioselective Epoxidation of α,β‐Enones by Electrophilic Activation with a BINOL–Zinc Catalyst

**Online reference 281; Tier 3.**

Ana Minatti; Karl Heinz Dötz. Enantioselective Epoxidation of α,β‐Enones by Electrophilic Activation with a BINOL–Zinc Catalyst. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2006(1), 268-276 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200500606

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.200500606](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200500606)

## Chemical contribution

CMHP generally gave higher yields and shorter reaction times than TBHP; hydrogen peroxide and mCPBA gave no epoxide, and mCPBA instead afforded a small amount of chalcone enol ester. The isolated precipitate was catalytically competent, giving complete conversion, 99% yield, and 90% ee, whereas the filtrate was inactive. The proposed oxygen-transfer step involves attack of weakly nucleophilic hydroperoxide at the activated β-carbon, followed by oxygen transfer to form the epoxy ketone and an alcohol.
