# L160 — Mechanochemical Defluorinative Acylation of ortho‐Hydroxyarylenaminones by CF3‐Compounds: Synthesis of 3‐Acylchromones

**SI reference 75; Tier 2.**

Satenik Mkrtchyan; Vishal B. Purohit; Spartak Khutsishvili; Jela Nociarová; Muhammad Yar; Tariq Mahmood; Khurshid Ayub; Šimon Budzák; Marek Skoršepa; Viktor O. Iaroshenko. Mechanochemical Defluorinative Acylation of ortho‐Hydroxyarylenaminones by CF3‐Compounds: Synthesis of 3‐Acylchromones. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 365(12), 2026-2035 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202300260

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202300260](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202300260)

## Chemical contribution

DABCO and the identity of the bicarbonate base strongly affected reaction efficiency: alternative organic bases lowered the yield, while replacing NaHCO3 with KHCO3 gave 39% rather than 91% yield under the tabulated conditions. The authors propose that chemoselective activation of the CF3 group by ytterbia in the presence of DABCO forms a carbon-centered *CF2- radical, which attacks the enaminone; the resulting radical intermediate is then proposed to undergo oxidation to a carbocation followed by pyrone-ring formation.
