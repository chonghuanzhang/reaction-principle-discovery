# L358 — Stereoselective Synthesis of Monofluoroalkenylphosphine Oxides via Photoinduced Decarboxylative Coupling of α-Fluoroacrylic Acids with P(O)H Compounds

**Online reference 72; Tier 1.**

Xiao-Yu Lu; Hong-Ye Pan; Rui Huang; Kang Yang; Xiang Zhang; Zi-Zhen Wang; Qian-Qian Tao; Gui-Xian Yang; Xiao-Juan Wang; Hai-Pin Zhou. Stereoselective Synthesis of Monofluoroalkenylphosphine Oxides via Photoinduced Decarboxylative Coupling of α-Fluoroacrylic Acids with P(O)H Compounds. Organic Letters 25(14), 2476-2481 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00631

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00631](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00631)

## Chemical contribution

Photoinduced decarboxylative/dehydrogenative coupling of α-fluoroacrylic acids with phosphine oxides and phosphonates formed monofluorovinyl phosphorus products. The abstract reports satisfactory yields and excellent E selectivity, with analogous access to monofluorovinyl silanes. This supplies product-class and donor-form precedent for P(O)H chemistry, while the fluorine is already present in the acid precursor; it is not evidence for selective removal of fluorines from a CF3 substrate.

### L358-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; reaction or product precedent.

Photoinduced decarboxylative/dehydrogenative coupling of α-fluoroacrylic acids with P(O)H compounds formed monofluorovinyl phosphine oxides and phosphonates. It establishes C(F)–P(O) product precedent but does not involve CF3-substrate defluorination.
