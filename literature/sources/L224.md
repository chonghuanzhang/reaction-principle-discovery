# L224 — Copper(I)-Catalyzed [4 + 1] Cycloaddition of Silyl Dienol Ethers with Sodium Bromodifluoroacetate: Access to β,β-Difluorocyclopentanone Derivatives

**SI reference 302; Tier 1.**

Kohei Fuchibe; Tatsuya Aono; Ji Hu; Junji Ichikawa. Copper(I)-Catalyzed [4 + 1] Cycloaddition of Silyl Dienol Ethers with Sodium Bromodifluoroacetate: Access to β,β-Difluorocyclopentanone Derivatives. Organic Letters 18(18), 4502-4505 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.6b02085

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.6b02085](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.6b02085)

## Chemical contribution

The typical reaction used copper(I) complex 5d and sodium bromodifluoroacetate in acetonitrile, with silyl dienol ether added at room temperature followed by heating at 50 °C for 12 h; product 2a was isolated in 70% yield. The proposed catalytic cycle invokes formation of copper(I) difluorocarbene complex 8d+Br−, nucleophilic attack by the silyl dienol ether either at the CF2 carbon or at copper followed by migratory insertion, Michael-type ring closure, and regeneration of the difluorocarbene complex.
