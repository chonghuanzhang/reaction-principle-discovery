# L046 — Ambident Reactivity of the Thiocyanate Anion Revisited: Can the Product Ratio Be Explained by the Hard Soft Acid Base Principle?

**Online reference 160; Tier 3.**

Robert Loos; Shinjiro Kobayashi; Herbert Mayr. Ambident Reactivity of the Thiocyanate Anion Revisited: Can the Product Ratio Be Explained by the Hard Soft Acid Base Principle? Journal of the American Chemical Society 125(46), 14126-14132 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja037317u

[doi:10.1021/ja037317u](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja037317u)

## Chemical contribution

The authors conclude that the selectivity of carbocation reactions with thiocyanate cannot be explained by the hard-soft acid-base principle. The kinetic experiments examined reactions of benzhydrylium ions with tetra-n-butylammonium thiocyanate in acetonitrile at 20 °C. The authors propose that the lower intrinsic barrier for sulfur attack may arise from the Principle of Least Nuclear Motion.
