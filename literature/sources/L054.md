# L054 — Alkenyl Thioetherification Enabled by Nickel Catalysis

**SI reference 167; Tier 2.**

Bing Xu; Huan Xiang; Yuqiong Tan; Ze Li; Shijun Li; Xiang-Yang Ye; Yang Ye. Alkenyl Thioetherification Enabled by Nickel Catalysis. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(7), 4592-4605 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00049

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c00049](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00049)

## Chemical contribution

For the reported inactivated alkenyl bromide scope, the authors identified a modified condition using 150 mol% tBuONa instead of KOAc; primary, secondary, and tertiary alkyl thiols were reported to couple with (E)-(4-bromobut-3-en-1-yl)benzene under this scope. The authors tentatively propose a Ni(0)/Ni(II) cycle involving Zn reduction of the Ni(II) precatalyst, oxidative addition of the alkenyl halide, thiol coordination and deprotonation, and reductive elimination; this mechanism is proposed rather than directly established.

### L054-S006

### L054-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In the model-reaction optimization, omission of Ni-1 gave trace product, omission of Zn gave 17%, omission of KOAc gave 33%, and reactions at 30 or 40 degrees C gave 41% or 60%, respectively; changing the ligand/nickel system gave yields ranging from 19% to 84%.

Location: 2; Results and Discussion: Table 1 optimization data
