# L079 — Transition metal-catalyzed C(sp 2 /sp 3 )–H α-fluoroalkenylation from gem -(bromo/di)fluoroalkenes to monofluoroalkenes: scope, mechanisms, and synthetic applications

**Online reference 186; Tier 2.**

Essam M. Eliwa; Ahmed H. Bedair; Jean-Pierre Djukic. Transition metal-catalyzed C(sp 2 /sp 3 )–H α-fluoroalkenylation from gem -(bromo/di)fluoroalkenes to monofluoroalkenes: scope, mechanisms, and synthetic applications. Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry 22(34), 6860-6904 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob01044b

[doi:10.1039/d4ob01044b](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob01044b)

## Chemical contribution

For Rh(III)-catalyzed quinazolinone fluoroalkenylation with a gem-difluoroalkene, H/D exchange showed reversible C–H activation, electron-rich gem-difluoroalkenes outperformed electron-poor analogues in competition, and a KIE of 3.3 suggested possible participation of C–H cleavage in the rate-determining step. A Pd-free CuI/dppe/LiOtBu protocol operates in anhydrous 1,4-dioxane under argon at 110 °C for 12 h and gives (Z)-monofluoroalkenes from 1,3-diazoles, but alkylated or tetrasubstituted gem-bromofluoroalkenes are unreactive and ortho-EWG substrates are unstable.

### L079-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Rh(III)Cp* catalysis provides regio- and stereoselective C(sp2)–H α-fluoroalkenylation of N-directed (hetero)arenes with gem-difluoroalkenes; one optimized example used cationic &#91;RhCp*(CH3CN)3&#93;(SbF6)2 in methanol at 80 °C for 16 h under air, while imine-, anilide-, and ketone-directed partners were unsuitable.

### L079-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.
