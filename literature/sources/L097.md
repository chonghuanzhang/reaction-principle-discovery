# L097 — Stereoselective One‐Pot Sequential Dehydrochlorination/trans‐Hydrofluorination Reaction of β‐Chloro‐α,β‐unsaturated Aldehydes or Ketones: Facile Access to (Z)‐β‐Fluoro‐β‐arylenals/β‐Fluoro‐β‐arylenones

**SI reference 201; Tier 1.**

Jingli Zhang; Liran Liu; Junxin Duan; Lianghu Gu; Bifeng Chen; Taolei Sun; Yuefa Gong. Stereoselective One‐Pot Sequential Dehydrochlorination/trans‐Hydrofluorination Reaction of β‐Chloro‐α,β‐unsaturated Aldehydes or Ketones: Facile Access to (Z)‐β‐Fluoro‐β‐arylenals/β‐Fluoro‐β‐arylenones. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 359(24), 4348-4358 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.201700981

[doi:10.1002/adsc.201700981](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.201700981)

## Chemical contribution

Silver salts were required for hydrofluorination of the model alkynone under the tested catalytic conditions: no fluorination occurred without silver salts, Pd(OAc)2 gave no observed product, and CuI gave only alkynone 5a. The authors propose that enals react through base-promoted alkynal formation, nucleophilic HF2- attack, an alkenyl anion, and protonation, while enones react through an alkynyl ketone-silver pi complex, backside fluoride attack to a vinyl-silver intermediate, and protonolysis.

### L097-S009
