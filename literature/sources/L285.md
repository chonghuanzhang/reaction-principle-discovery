# L285 — Synthesis of 6,13-difluoropentacene

**Online reference 356; Tier 1.**

Matthias W Tripp; Ulrich Koert. Synthesis of 6,13-difluoropentacene. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry 16, 2136-2140 (2020). https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.16.181

[doi:10.3762/bjoc.16.181](https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.16.181)

## Chemical contribution

The complete route from 1,4-difluorobenzene gave 6,13-difluoropentacene in 7% yield over seven steps, with Friedel–Crafts acylations and ortho-directed lithiation identified as key steps. Initial attempts to prepare 6,13-difluoropentacene through a route beginning with 6,13-pentacenedione failed, leading the authors to investigate a route in which the central ring already carried both fluorine substituents. The authors proposed that an intramolecular Friedel–Crafts-type acylation through acylium-cation intermediate 6 could form the indicated C5,5a bond.
