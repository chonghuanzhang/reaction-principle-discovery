# L211 — Design, synthesis, and activity of achiral analogs of 2-quinolones and indoles as non-thiol farnesyltransferase inhibitors

**SI reference 292; Tier 1.**

Qun Li; Keith W. Woods; Weibo Wang; Nan-Horng Lin; Akiyo Claiborne; Wen-zhen Gu; Jerry Cohen; Vincent S. Stoll; Charles Hutchins; David Frost; Saul H. Rosenberg; Hing L. Sham. Design, synthesis, and activity of achiral analogs of 2-quinolones and indoles as non-thiol farnesyltransferase inhibitors. Bioorganic & Medicinal Chemistry Letters 15(8), 2033-2039 (2005). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.02.062

[doi:10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.02.062](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.02.062)

## Chemical contribution

A second Suzuki coupling used 5-bromoindole and 1-naphthylboronic acid to prepare descyano compound 37a in 37% yield. The study synthesized farnesyltransferase inhibitors by transposing the D-ring of tipifarnib to the imidazole and modifying the 2-quinolone motif. The reported Suzuki conditions used 1-naphthylboronic acid, Pd(PPh3)4, NaHCO3, ethanol/toluene, and reflux for 3 hours, with a reported yield range of 37–46%.
