# L345 — Palladium-Catalyzed Selenylative Alkoxycarbonylation of Alkynes toward β-Selenated Cinnamic Acid Esters

**SI reference 7; Tier 4.**

Fengxiang Zhu; Huanan Wu; Xiao-Feng Wu. Palladium-Catalyzed Selenylative Alkoxycarbonylation of Alkynes toward β-Selenated Cinnamic Acid Esters. Organic Letters 27(28), 7714-7718 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c02491

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c02491](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c02491)

## Chemical contribution

PhSeH is productive in Pd-catalysed selenylative alkoxycarbonylation of phenylacetylene, ethanol and CO: the optimized reaction gives 88% GC yield. Added bases lower the reported yield to 35% with Na2CO3, 33% with EtONa, 28% with t-BuOK and 22% with Et3N; DMF and DMSO give negligible product. Dimethyl diselenide gives less than 5%, whereas PhSeSePh affords the carbonylated product in 91%. These observations show donor-form and condition dependence. Formation of the productive diselenide cannot by itself be treated as catalyst deactivation. The reaction does not test a fluorinated carbonyl electrophile.

### L345-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; supporting literature evidence.

Replacing the solvent with DMF or DMSO in the PhSeH/Pd carbonylation system gave almost no target product, showing a condition-dependent failure rather than an inability to form C–Se bonds.
