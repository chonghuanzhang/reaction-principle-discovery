# L144 — A Variation of the Fischer Indolization Involving Condensation of Quinone Monoketals and Aliphatic Hydrazines

**SI reference 242; Tier 1.**

Jinzhu Zhang; Zhiwei Yin; Patrick Leonard; Jing Wu; Kate Sioson; Che Liu; Robert Lapo; Shengping Zheng. A Variation of the Fischer Indolization Involving Condensation of Quinone Monoketals and Aliphatic Hydrazines. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 52(6), 1753-1757 (2013). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201207533

[doi:10.1002/anie.201207533](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201207533)

## Chemical contribution

The reported hydrazine scope included N-n-alkylhydrazines, N-sec-alkylhydrazines, cyclopentylhydrazine, and cycloheptylhydrazine; the authors described the corresponding yields as moderate to good or similar to that of 7ac. Treatment of quinone monoketal 1a with methylhydrazine or N,N-dimethylhydrazine gave only polar intractable products, and the desired nonpolar alkylaryldiazene was not detected. The authors suggest that general acid catalysis facilitates dehydration of a carbinolhydrazine intermediate and that steric hindrance in bulkier hydrazines may disfavor competing 1,4-addition.
