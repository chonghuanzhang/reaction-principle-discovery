# L063 — Preparation of [ 18 F]Alkenyl Fluorides Using No-Carrier-Added [ 18 F]AgF via Silver-Mediated Direct Radiofluorination of Alkynes

**Online reference 174; Tier 1.**

Xinyan Gao; Kehao Gong; Mingwei Wang; Bo Xu; Junbin Han. Preparation of [ 18 F]Alkenyl Fluorides Using No-Carrier-Added [ 18 F]AgF via Silver-Mediated Direct Radiofluorination of Alkynes. Organic Letters 24(35), 6438-6442 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02553

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02553](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02553)

## Chemical contribution

[18F]KF·K222 alone did not produce the model product, adding AgOTf to that fluoride source gave a lower yield, water was required as the proton source, and a larger amount of water sharply reduced reactivity. The proposed mechanism consists of trans-addition of silver [18F]fluoride to the alkyne to form silver intermediate A, followed by AcOH-mediated protodemetalation to give the fluoroalkene.
