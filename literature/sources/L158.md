# L158 — Fluoroalkylations and Fluoroalkenylations with Iodonium Salts

**SI reference 251; Tier 2.**

Ferenc Béke; János T. Csenki; Zoltán Novák. Fluoroalkylations and Fluoroalkenylations with Iodonium Salts. The Chemical Record 23(9), e202300083 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/tcr.202300083

[doi:10.1002/tcr.202300083](https://doi.org/10.1002/tcr.202300083)

## Chemical contribution

Palladium-catalyzed Z-selective fluoroalkenylation of aromatic compounds used 7.5 mol% Pd(OAc)2, two equivalents of TFA, DCM or DCE, ambient temperature, and 5–24 hours; electron-deficient substrates failed or reacted only at low conversion. Primary amines added to a trifluoropropenyl iodonium salt to form trifluoromethyl aziridines; sodium carbonate increased the reported yield from 42% to 82%, while a free hydroxy group disturbed the reaction and required prior silylation.

### L158-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Primary and secondary amines, alcohols, carboxylates, thiols, enolates, and enol ethers react with an electrophilic trifluoroethyl iodonium reagent under transition-metal-free conditions; the reported N-, O-, and S-trifluoroethylations occur in dichloromethane at 25 °C and give high yields.

Location: 4; Section 2.2, reactions of trifluoroethyl(aryl)iodonium salts with nucleophiles

### L158-S010

Palladium-catalyzed cross-coupling of arylboronic acids with trifluoroethyl(mesityl)iodonium salt was highly condition-dependent: Pd2(dba)3/K3PO4 in acetonitrile gave trifluoroethylated products from electron-rich substrates, whereas Pd[P(t-Bu)3]2/Cs2CO3 in DMF gave arylated products from electron-rich or electron-poor substrates.
