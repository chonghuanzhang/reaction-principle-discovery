# L301 — Defluorinative Carboimination of Trifluoromethyl Ketones

**Online reference 370; Tier 2.**

Xiaolong Zhang; Yongquan Ning; Zhaohong Liu; Shuang Li; Giuseppe Zanoni; Xihe Bi. Defluorinative Carboimination of Trifluoromethyl Ketones. ChemRxiv (2022). https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-5mcgj

[doi:10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-5mcgj](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-5mcgj)

## Chemical contribution

An N-allyl difluoroenamine intermediate was isolated in 75% yield after stopping the reaction at 40 minutes; resubjection to standard conditions gave product 3 in 92% yield, compared with 60% without silver catalyst. The reaction showed regioselectivity for the less hindered terminal double bond of N-allylbut-2-en-1-amine, affording product 36 in 75% yield. The authors propose that steric hindrance in the internal-alkene substrate changes the N-allyl difluoroenamine rearrangement outcome and enables a competing intramolecular [2+2] cycloaddition.
