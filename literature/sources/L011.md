# L011 — Visible-Light-Initiated Sequential Trifluoromethylation/Remote C(sp3)–H Alkynylation of Vinyl Azides by Radical Relay

**Online reference 128; Tier 1.**

Zirui Chen; Yunnan Duan; Shitao Sun; Bin Lin; Xiaozu Liu; Peijun Liu. Visible-Light-Initiated Sequential Trifluoromethylation/Remote C(sp3)–H Alkynylation of Vinyl Azides by Radical Relay. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(7), 4687-4693 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00170

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c00170](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c00170)

## Chemical contribution

For the model reaction, 4CzIPN under 40 W blue-light irradiation in MeCN, argon, and room temperature gave product 3aa in 56% isolated yield after 8 hours. For substrates 1v and 1w, only gamma-alkynylated products were observed, which the authors interpreted as a strong preference for 1,5-HAT over 1,6-HAT. Control experiments gave no product without 4CzIPN or when the reaction was performed in the dark, establishing that both photocatalyst and visible light were required under the tested conditions.
