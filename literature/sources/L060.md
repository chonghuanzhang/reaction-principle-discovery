# L060 — Fluoroketenes. 10. Synthesis and chemistry of a perfluoroacylketene and a related perfluorovinyl ketone

**SI reference 171; Tier 1.**

David C. England. Fluoroketenes. 10. Synthesis and chemistry of a perfluoroacylketene and a related perfluorovinyl ketone. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 46(1), 147-153 (1981). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00314a032

[doi:10.1021/jo00314a032](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00314a032)

## Chemical contribution

The paper describes the synthesis and chemistry of a perfluoroacylketene 12 and a related perfluorovinyl ketone 5, both prepared from a hexafluoropropene dimer and characterized as thermally stable but highly reactive. The authors interpret stereospecific vinyl-ketone additions as indicating a concerted mechanism and suggest that at least part of the nonstereospecific acylketene reaction may proceed through a zwitterionic intermediate.
