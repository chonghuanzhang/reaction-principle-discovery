# L041 — Glutathione catalysis and the reaction mechanisms of glutathione-dependent enzymes

**Online reference 156; Tier 3.**

Marcel Deponte. Glutathione catalysis and the reaction mechanisms of glutathione-dependent enzymes. Biochimica et Biophysica Acta (BBA) - General Subjects 1830(5), 3217-3266 (2013). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbagen.2012.09.018

[doi:10.1016/j.bbagen.2012.09.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbagen.2012.09.018)

## Chemical contribution

A reported failure boundary is that MGST1 had no significant activity with the Michael acceptor ethacrynic acid; the review also notes that another Michael acceptor can inhibit PTGES1 rather than act as a substrate. For GST-catalyzed Michael addition, the author presents a model in which enzyme-activated GSH attacks the electrophile, a transition state and enolate intermediate are stabilized, and the substrate is subsequently protonated.
