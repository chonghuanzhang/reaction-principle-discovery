# L107 — Fluoroketenes. 11. Synthesis and chemistry of a perfluoroacylketene and related compounds containing a perfluoroisopropyl sulfide group

**Online reference 210; Tier 1.**

David C. England. Fluoroketenes. 11. Synthesis and chemistry of a perfluoroacylketene and related compounds containing a perfluoroisopropyl sulfide group. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 46(1), 153-157 (1981). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00314a033

[doi:10.1021/jo00314a033](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00314a033)

## Chemical contribution

Weak amide bases promoted self-condensation of acylketene 14 with loss of carbon dioxide to give pyrone 43, whose structure was confirmed by X-ray analysis; the corresponding reaction was not observed for acylketene 15. The authors proposed that the hydrazoic-acid reaction could initially form an acid azide, followed by Curtius rearrangement to an isocyanate and subsequent cyclization or addition of hydrazoic acid.
