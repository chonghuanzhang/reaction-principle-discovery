# L259 — Synthesis of β-fluoroenones and their reductive rearrangement in aqueous media

**SI reference 334; Tier 1.**

Yan He; Nana Shen; Xuesen Fan; Xinying Zhang. Synthesis of β-fluoroenones and their reductive rearrangement in aqueous media. Tetrahedron 69(41), 8818-8823 (2013). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tet.2013.07.065

[doi:10.1016/j.tet.2013.07.065](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tet.2013.07.065)

## Chemical contribution

Substrates bearing a methyl group at the internal or terminal position of the allene moiety required longer reaction times; an unsaturated ester moiety was tolerated, and the tested simple alkyl-substituted substrate gave only a trace amount of the desired product. Reactions run in refluxing dichloromethane, diethyl ether, acetonitrile, or THF gave complicated mixtures, whereas with 3 equivalents of KF, formation of β-fluoroenone 2a was not observed even over a prolonged reaction period.

### L259-S007
