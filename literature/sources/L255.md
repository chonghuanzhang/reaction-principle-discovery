# L255 — 1,2‐Diazetidines − Structure, Synthesis, and Functionalization

**SI reference 330; Tier 3.**

Stefan Roesner. 1,2‐Diazetidines − Structure, Synthesis, and Functionalization. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 29(19), e70442 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.70442

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.70442](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.70442)

## Chemical contribution

The palladium-catalyzed cyclization has a sharp aryl-iodide electronic scope boundary: electron-deficient aryl iodides give diazetidines in high yields, while electron-rich aryl iodides give five-membered dihydropyrazoles exclusively. A competing [4 + 2] cycloaddition can divert an alkene/azodicarbonyl reaction to oxadiazine derivatives rather than the desired formal [2 + 2] products. Thermal alkene/azodicarbonyl formation of 1,2-diazetidines is proposed to proceed through a dipolar or diradical intermediate rather than a concerted pathway.
