# L254 — Regioselectivity and High Electrophilicity in the σ Complexation of Aromatic Triflones

**SI reference 329; Tier 3.**

Nizar El Guesmi; Taoufik Boubaker; Régis Goumont; François Terrier. Regioselectivity and High Electrophilicity in the σ Complexation of Aromatic Triflones. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2014(30), 6774-6786 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201402607

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.201402607](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201402607)

## Chemical contribution

Acidification of isolated 1,1-adduct 3e fully regenerated anisole 1e, directly demonstrating reversible σ complexation. Decreasing pKa is associated with dramatic changes in electrophilic potential. The authors assign the observed transient to the 1,3-dimethoxy adduct 2e based primarily on the similar approximately 430 nm UV/Vis maxima of the two detected adducts and their proposed common activation pattern.
