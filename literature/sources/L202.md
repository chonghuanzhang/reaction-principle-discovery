# L202 — An anionic phosphenium complex as an ambident nucleophile

**Online reference 286; Tier 3.**

B. Stadelmann; J. Bender; D. Förster; W. Frey; M. Nieger; D. Gudat. An anionic phosphenium complex as an ambident nucleophile. Dalton Transactions 44(13), 6023-6031 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1039/c5dt00008d

[doi:10.1039/c5dt00008d](https://doi.org/10.1039/c5dt00008d)

## Chemical contribution

The low-temperature NMR data support assignment of the methyl group in intermediate 8 to phosphorus rather than iron, including its chemical shifts, inequivalent isopropyl environments, and NOESY correlations. The authors propose that 8 is a phosphine-bound iron tricarbonyl complex with an empty or solvent-occupied coordination site and may be the initial product of MeI attack; they attribute its conversion to 6 and 7 to ligand extrusion or redistribution.

### L202-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Low-temperature NMR of Na[2] with MeI showed species 8 as approximately 90% of the phosphorus-containing material at −70 °C; it remained unchanged below −50 °C and then irreversibly converted to 6 and 7 on warming to ambient temperature, with no other spectroscopically observed intermediates.
