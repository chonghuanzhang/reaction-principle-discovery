# L056 — Highlighting the Underrated Side of the High Profile Nitroso Entity as a Nucleophile

**SI reference 168; Tier 3.**

George N. Pairas; Petros G. Tsoungas. Highlighting the Underrated Side of the High Profile Nitroso Entity as a Nucleophile. ChemistrySelect 8(41), e202302671 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/slct.202302671

[doi:10.1002/slct.202302671](https://doi.org/10.1002/slct.202302671)

## Chemical contribution

Computational analyses identify both nitroso nitrogen and oxygen as nucleophilic centers, but predict different preferred termini depending on the descriptor, hardness or softness treatment, charge model, and molecular structure. For the reported reaction with terminal alkynes, the authors describe formation of an intermediate by N-nucleophilic nitroso attack followed by intramolecular cycloaddition, but explicitly state that a radical pathway cannot be excluded.

### L056-S009

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

For reactions involving sulfimides and nitrile oxides, the review attributes different products to conformation-dependent N- versus O-cyclization of in situ generated nitroso species; sterically congested nitrile oxides give both products in relatively low yields, whereas unsubstituted or poorly substituted nitrile oxides give one product exclusively.
