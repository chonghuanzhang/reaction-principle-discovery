# L176 — C-F bond activation under transition-metal-free conditions

**SI reference 79; Tier 2.**

Han-Jun Ai; Xingxing Ma; Qiuling Song; Xiao-Feng Wu. C-F bond activation under transition-metal-free conditions. Science China Chemistry 64(10), 1630-1659 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11426-021-1040-2

[doi:10.1007/s11426-021-1040-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11426-021-1040-2)

## Chemical contribution

In the sequential HDF protocol, tertiary amides and pentafluoropropanamides reacted under both mono- and di-HDF conditions; reduction was selective for C-F bonds alpha to the carbonyl while a separate CF3 group remained inert. The degree of hydrodefluorination of trifluoroacetamides and acetates was condition-dependent: DMAP-BH3/TBHN, thiophenol, and NaH2PO4·2H2O furnished mono-HDF products, whereas increased DMAP-BH3 and Na2HPO4·12H2O in 1,4-dioxane selectively furnished di-HDF products. Calculations for phosphinylation of nonactivated aryl fluorides suggested a nucleophile-dependent mechanism involving both concerted and stepwise SNAr pathways.
