# L318 — Ring-Restructuring of gem-Difluorocyclopropanes: Diverse Access to Four- to Nine-Membered Ring Skeletons

**SI reference 386; Tier 2.**

Yan Sun; Chao Yang; Liliang Huang; Huangdi Feng. Ring-Restructuring of gem-Difluorocyclopropanes: Diverse Access to Four- to Nine-Membered Ring Skeletons. Pharmaceutical Fronts 08(01), e23-e39 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2787-1940

[doi:10.1055/a-2787-1940](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2787-1940)

## Chemical contribution

A Pd-IPent-catalyzed reaction of gem-difluorocyclopropanes with enamides constructs multisubstituted N-H pyrroles through cleavage of a cyclopropane C-C bond, two C-F bonds, and a C-N bond; the products were reported in good-to-excellent yields. The review identifies a scope limitation: current gem-difluorocyclopropane methods predominantly use monosubstituted substrates, while sterically demanding disubstituted and multisubstituted derivatives remain a challenge. The review states that gem-difluorinated metallocyclobutane intermediates are proposed for transition-metal-catalyzed cyclizations, but the precise mechanisms remain unresolved and require further experimental and computational study.
