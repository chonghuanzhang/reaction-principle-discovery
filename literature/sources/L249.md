# L249 — Synthesis of fluorinated pyrrolizidine, indolizidine and quinolizidine derivatives

**Online reference 325; Tier 2.**

Anton Klipkov; Igor Gerus; Alexander Sorochinsky. Synthesis of fluorinated pyrrolizidine, indolizidine and quinolizidine derivatives. Ukrainica Bioorganica Acta 17(1), 22-39 (2022). https://doi.org/10.15407/bioorganica2022.01.022

[doi:10.15407/bioorganica2022.01.022](https://doi.org/10.15407/bioorganica2022.01.022)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction was reported to have broad scope across both dihydroisoquinoline acid derivatives and alpha-(trifluoromethyl)styrenes, excellent functional-group tolerance, and high diastereo- and regioselectivity; the supplied text does not enumerate individual examples or quantitative selectivity values. The proposed mechanism involves addition of the alpha-amino radical to the alpha-(trifluoromethyl)styrene, reduction of the resulting alpha-CF3 alkyl radical to a carbanion, and beta-fluoride elimination to form a difluoroalkene intermediate.
