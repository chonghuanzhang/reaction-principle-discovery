# L127 — 8‐Fluoro‐6‐(methoxymethoxy)quinoline: Synthesis and regioselective functionalization via reaction with organolithium compounds

**Online reference 230; Tier 1.**

Josef Stadlwieser; Pierre Barbier; Sven Taylor. 8‐Fluoro‐6‐(methoxymethoxy)quinoline: Synthesis and regioselective functionalization via reaction with organolithium compounds. Helvetica Chimica Acta 81(5-8), 1088-1094 (1998). https://doi.org/10.1002/hlca.19980810522

[doi:10.1002/hlca.19980810522](https://doi.org/10.1002/hlca.19980810522)

## Chemical contribution

tert-Butyllithium in THF at -78 degrees gave only the isolable 1,2-addition-derived compound 8b in moderate yield. With BuLi in THF at -78 degrees, the product attributed to 1,2-addition predominated; adding TMEDA changed the outcome so that directed ortho-metalation predominated. The organolithium reaction outcomes were quantified after trimethylsilyl triflate trapping and oxidation of dihydroquinoline 1,2-addition products with activated manganese dioxide, using isolated yields after chromatography.
