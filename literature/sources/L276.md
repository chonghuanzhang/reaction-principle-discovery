# L276 — Supramolecular catalysis. Part 1: non-covalent interactions as a tool for building and modifying homogeneous catalysts

**Online reference 348; Tier 3.**

Matthieu Raynal; Pablo Ballester; Anton Vidal-Ferran; Piet W. N. M. van Leeuwen. Supramolecular catalysis. Part 1: non-covalent interactions as a tool for building and modifying homogeneous catalysts. Chem. Soc. Rev. 43(5), 1660-1733 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1039/c3cs60027k

[doi:10.1039/c3cs60027k](https://doi.org/10.1039/c3cs60027k)

## Chemical contribution

The review organizes supramolecular catalyst applications into bidentate ligand construction, di- or oligonuclear complex construction, alteration of a metal catalyst's coordination sphere, and control of substrate reactivity. For the palladium-catalyzed allylation of N-heterocycles with allylic alcohols, the authors propose that a ligand N-H group hydrogen-bonds to the substrate hydroxyl group and improves its leaving-group ability; the mechanism is not clearly established.
