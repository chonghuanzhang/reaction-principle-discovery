# L284 — Synthesis of substituted 5,6,7,8‐tetrafluoro‐1H‐benzo[f]indol‐4,9‐diones based on the reaction of hexafluoro‐1,4‐napthoquinone with methyl 3‐aminocrotonates

**SI reference 355; Tier 1.**

Ekaterina N. Kudryavtseva; Boris V. Lichitsky; Andrey N. Komogortsev; Constantine V. Milyutin; Evgeny V. Tretyakov. Synthesis of substituted 5,6,7,8‐tetrafluoro‐1H‐benzo[f]indol‐4,9‐diones based on the reaction of hexafluoro‐1,4‐napthoquinone with methyl 3‐aminocrotonates. Journal of Heterocyclic Chemistry 61(10), 1554-1563 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1002/jhet.4872

[doi:10.1002/jhet.4872](https://doi.org/10.1002/jhet.4872)

## Chemical contribution

At room temperature for 8 h, ether and toluene gave no reaction because of low quinone solubility, while acetonitrile, dichloromethane, and dioxane gave complex unidentified mixtures; methanol afforded precipitated 8a in 41% yield. The authors propose initial enamine addition to a quinone double bond followed by HF elimination to intermediate 8, acid-promoted carbonyl protonation and intramolecular nitrogen cyclization, and final deprotonation and HF elimination to product 9.
