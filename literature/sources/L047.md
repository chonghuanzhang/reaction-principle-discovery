# L047 — Deprotonative Metalation of Aromatic Compounds by Using an Amino‐Based Lithium Cuprate

**SI reference 161; Tier 1.**

Tan Tai Nguyen; Nada Marquise; Floris Chevallier; Florence Mongin. Deprotonative Metalation of Aromatic Compounds by Using an Amino‐Based Lithium Cuprate. Chemistry – A European Journal 17(37), 10405-10416 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201100990

[doi:10.1002/chem.201100990](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201100990)

## Chemical contribution

Diphenyl disulfide trapped the pyridyl cuprate to give a C-S coupling product in 67% yield, whereas several other sulfur electrophiles acted as oxidants or gave negligible trapping product. The reported attempts to use activated alkenes failed. The authors proposed that the higher yields with electron-deficient aryl bromides could reflect an addition-elimination pathway rather than classical oxidative addition.
