# L220 — Evidence That Protons Can Be the Active Catalysts in Lewis Acid Mediated Hetero‐Michael Addition Reactions

**Online reference 300; Tier 4.**

Tobias C. Wabnitz; Jin‐Quan Yu; Jonathan B. Spencer. Evidence That Protons Can Be the Active Catalysts in Lewis Acid Mediated Hetero‐Michael Addition Reactions. Chemistry – A European Journal 10(2), 484-493 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.200305407

[doi:10.1002/chem.200305407](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.200305407)

## Chemical contribution

Catalytic activity generally correlated with cation hydrolysis constants: readily hydrolyzing cations gave good yields, borderline cations gave poor conversion, and weakly hydrolyzing cations gave no reaction, with stated rare-earth exceptions. Water loading had a nonmonotonic effect: adding up to two equivalents relative to Pd catalyst accelerated the aza-Michael reaction, whereas larger amounts slowed it because water acted as a Brønsted base.
