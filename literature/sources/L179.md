# L179 — Defluorinative thio-functionalization: direct synthesis of methyl-dithioesters from trifluoromethylarenes

**Online reference 52; Tier 2.**

Marcus Söderström; Esther Olaniran Håkansson; Luke R. Odell. Defluorinative thio-functionalization: direct synthesis of methyl-dithioesters from trifluoromethylarenes. Chemical Communications 61(1), 145-148 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1039/d4cc05540c

[doi:10.1039/d4cc05540c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4cc05540c)

## Chemical contribution

A free NH piperazine substrate was not tolerated and formed an insoluble black solid, while acetyl protection enabled isolation of the corresponding product in 40% yield. A low-temperature, substoichiometric BF3SMe2 experiment did not furnish proposed intermediate I; aqueous workup instead gave thioester 1x in 22% NMR yield and dithioacetal 1y in 9%, while NaBH4 treatment of the crude mixture gave significant 1y and only traces of 1x.
