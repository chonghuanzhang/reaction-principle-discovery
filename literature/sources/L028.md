# L028 — Synthesis and antibacterial activity of 5-methoxy- and 5-hydroxy-6-fluoro-1,8-naphthyridone-3-carboxylic acid derivatives

**SI reference 145; Tier 1.**

T. Matthew Hansen; Yu-Gui Gu; Tamara M. Rehm; Peter J. Dandliker; Linda E. Chovan; Mai H. Bui; Angela M. Nilius; Bruce A. Beutel. Synthesis and antibacterial activity of 5-methoxy- and 5-hydroxy-6-fluoro-1,8-naphthyridone-3-carboxylic acid derivatives. Bioorganic & Medicinal Chemistry Letters 15(11), 2716-2719 (2005). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.04.007

[doi:10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.04.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2005.04.007)

## Chemical contribution

Displacement of the C7 fluorine by each selected amine proceeded smoothly in acetonitrile with Hünig's base and gave compounds 10a–c in high yield. The authors prepared and evaluated a series of 5-methoxy- and 5-hydroxy-6-fluoro-1,8-naphthyridone-3-carboxylic acid derivatives. Scheme 2 specifies amine, DIPEA, acetonitrile, and 60 °C for the C7 substitution and reports 90–100% yield.
