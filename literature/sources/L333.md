**SI reference 397; Tier 2.**

Xin Xu. Exploring the mechanisms of nickel/rhodium-catalyzed cross-coupling and rhodium-catalyzed olefin hydroboration reactions, and rhenium/tantalum metalacyclic complex formation : a theoretical study. Doctoral thesis, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2023). https://doi.org/10.14711/thesis-991013202156403412

[doi:10.14711/thesis-991013202156403412](https://doi.org/10.14711/thesis-991013202156403412)

## Chemical contribution

The calculated oxidative-addition barrier is about 27.2 kcal/mol, and oxidative addition was identified as the rate-determining step in the modeled reaction. The reported reaction couples 2,2-difluorovinyl benzoates with arylboronic acids under nickel catalysis to produce gem-difluoroenol ethers. The DFT-supported proposed catalytic sequence is oxidative addition of the vinyl C-O bond, base exchange, transmetalation with the arylboronic acid, and reductive elimination.
