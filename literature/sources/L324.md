# L324 — Highly E-Selective Synthesis of α-Fluoro-β-arylalkenyl Sulfones from gem-Difluoroalkenes with Sodium Sulfinates

**SI reference 67; Tier 2.**

Yuxiu Li; Xiangqian Li; Xiaowei Li; Dayong Shi. Highly E-Selective Synthesis of α-Fluoro-β-arylalkenyl Sulfones from gem-Difluoroalkenes with Sodium Sulfinates. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 86(9), 6983-6993 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c00490

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.1c00490](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c00490)

## Chemical contribution

A variety of sodium sulfinates underwent the reaction in moderate to good yields with excellent stereoselectivity, and sodium alkylsulfinates gave products 3ao-av in 75-97% yields with excellent E/Z selectivity. Addition of TEMPO, BHT, or 1,1-diphenylethylene did not obviously inhibit the reaction; the authors interpreted this result as ruling out a radical process. The authors propose nucleophilic addition of a sulfinate-derived anion to the gem-difluoroalkene, followed by preferential anti-β-fluoride elimination from a favored conformational model to produce the E-selective product.
