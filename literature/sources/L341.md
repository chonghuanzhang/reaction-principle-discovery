# L341 — Palladium-Catalyzed Carbothiolation of Alkenes and Alkynes for the Synthesis of Heterocycles

**SI reference 403; Tier 4.**

Tristan Delcaillau; Hendrik L. Schmitt; Philip Boehm; Eric Falk; Bill Morandi. Palladium-Catalyzed Carbothiolation of Alkenes and Alkynes for the Synthesis of Heterocycles. ACS Catalysis 12(10), 6081-6091 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.2c01178

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.2c01178](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.2c01178)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction scope was examined using 25 mol% IPr because lower ligand loading caused decreased yield under several tested conditions. Alkyne products showed isomerization behavior: N-acetyl derivative 46 was observed only as the isomerized indole derivative in the crude mixture, while product 47 partially isomerized during silica-gel chromatography. The authors propose that reversible C-S oxidative addition produces self-correcting selectivity, and they regard formation of cyclized amide 41 in 87% yield as support for that proposal.
