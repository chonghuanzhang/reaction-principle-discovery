# L162 — Copper‐Catalyzed [3+2] Annulation of Pyridinium Ylides with α‐CF 3 Ketones: Synthesis of Functionalized 2‐Fluoroindolizines

**Online reference 254; Tier 1.**

Shiming Shu; Leilei Wang; Leiyang Lv; Zhiping Li. Copper‐Catalyzed [3+2] Annulation of Pyridinium Ylides with α‐CF3 Ketones: Synthesis of Functionalized 2‐Fluoroindolizines. Asian Journal of Organic Chemistry 12(5), e202300075 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202300075

[doi:10.1002/ajoc.202300075](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202300075)

## Chemical contribution

α-CF3 esters and amides failed under the standard conditions, giving only trace annulation products because the substrates decomposed. The proposed mechanism forms a pyridinium ylide and a difluorovinyl ketone by deprotonation, followed by stepwise or concerted [3+2] annulation, deprotonation, β-fluoride elimination, and copper-catalyzed aerobic oxidative aromatization; the authors explicitly state that an alternative ordering of oxidation, deprotonation, and β-fluoride elimination cannot be excluded.
