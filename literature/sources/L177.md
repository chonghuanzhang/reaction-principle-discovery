# L177 — Effects of Reduced Sulfur Compounds on Pd-Catalytic Hydrodechlorination of Trichloroethylene in Groundwater by Cathodic H2under Electrochemically Induced Oxidizing Conditions

**SI reference 265; Tier 4.**

Songhu Yuan; Mingjie Chen; Xuhui Mao; Akram N. Alshawabkeh. Effects of Reduced Sulfur Compounds on Pd-Catalytic Hydrodechlorination of Trichloroethylene in Groundwater by Cathodic H2 under Electrochemically Induced Oxidizing Conditions. Environmental Science & Technology 47(18), 10502–10509 (2013). https://doi.org/10.1021/es402169d

[doi:10.1021/es402169d](https://doi.org/10.1021/es402169d)

## Chemical contribution

Radical-scavenging experiments supported an oxidation pathway parallel to hydrodechlorination: ascorbic acid reduced TCE decay toward the no-sulfite result, while methanol caused only slight inhibition, suggesting little contribution from hydroxyl and sulfate radicals. The proposed mechanism assigns low-sulfite enhancement to SO3 radical anion formation followed by rapid conversion to SO5 radical anion, with both radicals, especially SO5 radical anion, proposed to oxidize TCE intermediates that are subsequently hydrodechlorinated to ethane.

### L177-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In the presence of 1 g/L Pd/Al2O3, TCE was efficiently reduced in the undivided electrolytic cell over a wide pH range, with ethane accumulation supporting Pd-catalytic hydrodechlorination as the dominant pathway; the approximately 80% TCE/ethane mass balance indicated unquantified intermediates.

Location: 3; Results and Discussion: Pd-Catalytic TCE Hydrodechlorination and H2O2 Accumulation in the Electrolytic System

<a id="statement-l177-s002"></a>

### L177-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Removing anodic oxygen by using the cathodic compartment increased the observed TCE-decay rate constant to 0.071 min−1, about five times the value measured with anodic oxygen at 100 mA; the authors attributed oxygen inhibition to competition for reactive hydrogen and chemisorbed oxygen blocking TCE reaction on Pd.

Location: 3; Results and Discussion: effect of anodic oxygen
