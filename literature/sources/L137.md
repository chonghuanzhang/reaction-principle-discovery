# L137 — The Reaction of Oligomers of Tetrafluoroethylene II. The Reaction of the Tetrafluoroethylene Pentamer with Allyl Alcohol and the Chemical Transformation of Reaction Products

**SI reference 238; Tier 1.**

Li-Fo Chen; Jun-Huan Wang. The Reaction of Oligomers of Tetrafluoroethylene II. The Reaction of the Tetrafluoroethylene Pentamer with Allyl Alcohol and the Chemical Transformation of Reaction Products. Acta Chimica Sinica 41(4), 375–379 (1983).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Compound 2 reacted with dimethylamine to form the nitrogen-containing compound 8; the assigned product lacked an allyloxy 1H NMR signal and showed N-methyl resonances at 2.73 and 2.67 ppm, with MS, IR, and elemental analysis reported as consistent with the assigned structure. The authors propose that dimethylamine first attacks the terminal alkene of compound 2 to form an unstable intermediate, followed by fluoride attack at the allyl group and cleavage of an oxygen-containing bond to form compound 8.
