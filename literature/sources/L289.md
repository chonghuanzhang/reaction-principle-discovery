# L289 — Quantitative Differences in Sulfur Poisoning Phenomena over Ruthenium and Palladium: An Attempt To Deconvolute Geometric and Electronic Poisoning Effects Using Model Catalysts

**Online reference 360; Tier 4.**

Amy Kolpin; Glenn Jones; Simon Jones; Weiran Zheng; James Cookson; Andrew P. E. York; Paul J. Collier; Shik Chi Edman Tsang. Quantitative Differences in Sulfur Poisoning Phenomena over Ruthenium and Palladium: An Attempt To Deconvolute Geometric and Electronic Poisoning Effects Using Model Catalysts. ACS Catalysis 7(1), 592-605 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.6b02765

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.6b02765](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.6b02765)

## Chemical contribution

For Pd(PVP), first-stage alkyne hydrogenation showed no significant coverage-dependent rate change, including at theoretical MEA coverages above one monolayer, and cis-alkene selectivity remained above 94% across the reported coverage range. Sulfur modification progressively inhibited the examined second-stage Pd-catalyzed conversion of cis-3-hexen-1-ol; 30% sulfur coverage caused more than a 70% reduction in initial activity, and the rate of further deactivation slowed beyond approximately 55%–60% coverage.
