# L280 — Regioselective C–F Bond Transformations of Silyl Difluoroenolates

**Online reference 37; Tier 2.**

Ryohei Doi; Koki Kajiwara; Taiki Negoro; Kenta Koh; Sensuke Ogoshi. Regioselective C–F Bond Transformations of Silyl Difluoroenolates. Organic Letters 25(29), 5542-5547 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02057

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02057](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02057)

## Chemical contribution

The reported scope tolerated aryl, alkyl, alkoxy, aniline, and fluorine substituents on the organozinc-derived coupling component; benzylation and methylation also proceeded, and silyl difluoroenolates containing oxygen, nitrogen, or fluorine were applicable. The scope figure reports Z/E greater than 50:1 unless otherwise stated. The authors propose that steric differences between the two fluorine atoms cause lithium to coordinate to the less-congested fluorine and thereby determine selective C−F cleavage and E/Z selectivity.
