# L256 — A review of Ni based powder catalyst for urea oxidation in assisting water splitting reaction

**Online reference 331; Tier 4.**

Jiaxin Li; Shuli Wang; Jinfa Chang; Ligang Feng. A review of Ni based powder catalyst for urea oxidation in assisting water splitting reaction. Advanced Powder Materials 1(3), 100030 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmate.2022.01.003

[doi:10.1016/j.apmate.2022.01.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmate.2022.01.003)

## Chemical contribution

The review reports that urea electrooxidation occurs after formation of Ni(III) species on the electrode surface and that active sites are regenerated during continued adsorption and oxidation. The review identifies Ni-based catalysts as promising for urea oxidation because of cost, structural tunability, compatibility, and facile active-phase formation. A catalyst-regeneration mechanism was proposed in which Ni(OH)2 is electrochemically oxidized to NiOOH, urea is chemically oxidized, and NiOOH is reduced back to Ni(OH)2.
