# L170 — Pyridinium-Masked Enol as a Precursor for Constructing Alpha-Fluoromethyl Ketones

**SI reference 260; Tier 1.**

Jijun Xu; Yi Li; Xuanyu Zhu; Shisong Lv; Yiming Xu; Tanyu Cheng; Guohua Liu; Rui Liu. Pyridinium-Masked Enol as a Precursor for Constructing Alpha-Fluoromethyl Ketones. Organic Letters 25(33), 6211-6216 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02419

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02419](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02419)

## Chemical contribution

The trifluoromethylation scope included substituted aromatic ketones in good to very good yields, while a thienyl substrate gave a diminished 45% yield; benzyl- and alkyl-derived substrates gave products in 73% and 76% yields, respectively. The authors proposed two catalytic pathways and explicitly favored oxidation of CF3SO2Na followed by SO2 loss to form a CF3 radical, while acknowledging that they had no experimental evidence distinguishing this pathway.
