# L264 — Addition of Ester Enolates to N-Alkyl-2-fluoropyridinium Salts: Total Synthesis of (±)-20-Deoxycamptothecin and (+)-Camptothecin

**Online reference 337; Tier 1.**

M.-Lluïsa Bennasar; Ester Zulaica; Cecília Juan; Yolanda Alonso; Joan Bosch. Addition of Ester Enolates to N-Alkyl-2-fluoropyridinium Salts: Total Synthesis of (±)-20-Deoxycamptothecin and (+)-Camptothecin. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 67(21), 7465-7474 (2002). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo026173j

[doi:10.1021/jo026173j](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo026173j)

## Chemical contribution

The isolated fluorinated C-4 adducts underwent acid hydrolysis of the C-F bond with 1 N HCl; subsequent desulfurization gave products in 75-80% overall yield, and related fluorinated adducts gave dihydropyridones in good yield by acid hydrolysis. The authors propose that C-4 regioselectivity is controlled by steric factors and is favored by increasing the bulk of both the pyridinium N-substituent and the enolate.
