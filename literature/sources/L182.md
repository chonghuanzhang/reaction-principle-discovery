# L182 — Tautomeric equilibria and pKa values for 'sulfurous acid' in aqueous solution: a thermodynamic analysis

**SI reference 268; Tier 3.**

J. Peter Guthrie. Tautomeric equilibria and pKa values for 'sulfurous acid' in aqueous solution: a thermodynamic analysis. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 57(4), 454-457 (1979). https://doi.org/10.1139/v79-074

[doi:10.1139/v79-074](https://doi.org/10.1139/v79-074)

## Chemical contribution

The paper estimates that correcting apparent rate constants for the minor reactive bisulfite tautomer would require a factor of less than 100. The calculated hydration free energy was considered consistent with an earlier infrared-derived limit indicating less than one part in thirty sulfurous acid in solution. The author proposes that interpretation of bisulfite nucleophilic reactions may need correction if the sulfur-lone-pair reactive species is a minor tautomer rather than the major monoanion.
