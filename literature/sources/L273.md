# L273 — Mild metal-catalyzed C–H activation: examples and concepts

**SI reference 346; Tier 4.**

T. Gensch; M. N. Hopkinson; F. Glorius; J. Wencel-Delord. Mild metal-catalyzed C–H activation: examples and concepts. Chemical Society Reviews 45(10), 2900-2936 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1039/c6cs00075d

[doi:10.1039/c6cs00075d](https://doi.org/10.1039/c6cs00075d)

## Chemical contribution

The review reports mild C–N bond formation with electrophilic amine reagents under Rh, Ru, or Ir catalysis at room temperature to 40 °C without an external oxidant, while describing nucleophilic substitution at nitrogen only as a likely mechanism. The paper is a review of metal-catalyzed C–H activation transformations conducted under comparatively mild conditions, specifically emphasizing reactions at or below ambient temperature, without strongly acidic or basic additives, or without strong oxidants.
