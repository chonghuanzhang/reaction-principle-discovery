# L174 — Hydrogen-bond promoted nucleophilic fluorination: concept, mechanism and applications in positron emission tomography

**SI reference 263; Tier 3.**

Ji-Woong Lee; Maria Teresa Oliveira; Hyeong Bin Jang; Sungyul Lee; Dae Yoon Chi; Dong Wook Kim; Choong Eui Song. Hydrogen-bond promoted nucleophilic fluorination: concept, mechanism and applications in positron emission tomography. Chemical Society Reviews 45(17), 4638-4650 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1039/c6cs00286b

[doi:10.1039/c6cs00286b](https://doi.org/10.1039/c6cs00286b)

## Chemical contribution

For fluorination of alkyl mesylate 3 with KF, acetonitrile at 100 °C gave hardly any product after 24 h and crown ether gave only 40% yield, whereas aqueous [bmim][BF4] completed the reaction within 1.5 h and afforded product 4 in up to 92% yield. Competition experiments in the reported Friedel-Crafts benzylation showed significant reactivity only for benzylic C-F bonds when related C-Cl, C-Br, and C-OH substrates were present.
