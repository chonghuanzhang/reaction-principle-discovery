# L207 — Fluorinated Para ‐Quinone Monoacetal: Versatile Reactivity for Constructing Functionalized Scaffolds

**Online reference 288; Tier 1.**

Kohei Azami; Masao Morita; Keisuke Suzuki; Ken Ohmori. Fluorinated Para ‐Quinone Monoacetal: Versatile Reactivity for Constructing Functionalized Scaffolds. Chemistry – An Asian Journal 20(24), e00921 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1002/asia.202500921

[doi:10.1002/asia.202500921](https://doi.org/10.1002/asia.202500921)

## Chemical contribution

Carbon-nucleophile outcome depended strongly on precursor class: sodium enolates of 1,3-dicarbonyl compounds gave products in moderate yields, Meldrum's acid and acetonitrile were unreactive, acetone enolate gave a moderate-yield 1,4-adduct, and ethyl acetate predominantly underwent competing 1,2-addition–dehydration with only 18% desired product. Acetamide and benzamide reacted through nitrogen to give products in 64% and 39% yield, respectively; N-methylacetamide, succinimide, acetic acid, and benzoic acid were unreactive under the stated comparison conditions.
