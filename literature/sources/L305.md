# L305 — Enantioconvergent halogenophilic nucleophilic substitution (SN2X) reaction by chiral phase-transfer catalysis

**SI reference 374; Tier 3.**

Xin Zhang. Enantioconvergent halogenophilic nucleophilic substitution (SN2X) reaction by chiral phase-transfer catalysis. Doctoral thesis, Nanyang Technological University (2019). https://doi.org/10.32657/10220/48077

[doi:10.32657/10220/48077](https://doi.org/10.32657/10220/48077)

## Chemical contribution

A separately prepared proposed intermediate, benzoylsulfenyl bromide, reacted with the corresponding carbanion under PN6 catalysis to afford tertiary thioester in 66% yield and 82% ee along with racemic tertiary bromide in 12% yield; the author described this as evidence that sulfenyl bromide is a plausible ambident electrophilic intermediate. Carbanion-exchange experiments generated mixtures of crossover thioesters from two substrate combinations, providing experimental support for exchangeable anionic intermediates.
