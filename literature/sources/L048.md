# L048 — Enolizable β-Fluoroenones: Synthesis and Asymmetric 1,2-Reduction

**SI reference 162; Tier 1.**

Lukas Zygalski; Christoph Middel; Klaus Harms; Ulrich Koert. Enolizable β-Fluoroenones: Synthesis and Asymmetric 1,2-Reduction. Organic Letters 20(16), 5071-5074 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.8b02435

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.8b02435](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.8b02435)

## Chemical contribution

The optimized hydrofluorination generally gave good-to-excellent Z/E selectivity and tolerated multiple functional groups, but substrate 7b gave no hydrofluorination product; the exact structure of 7b and the identities of the tolerated groups are not established by the extracted text. The authors rationalize furan 11 formation through ynone-to-allenone rearrangement followed by Ag-mediated cyclization via intermediate 14; this is a proposed pathway rather than a directly established mechanism.

### L048-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For ynone 7a, the best reported AgF hydrofluorination conditions used 3 equivalents of AgF, 0.45 equivalent of 2,2′-biphenol, t-BuOH/DMF, 70 °C, and 5 h, affording 10a in 75% yield with a 10.0:1 Z/E ratio and 2% furan 11.
