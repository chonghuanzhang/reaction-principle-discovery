# L223 — Palladium‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of 1‐Aryl‐2,2‐Difluoroalkenes and Boronic Acids: Stereoselective Synthesis of Monofluorostilbenes

**SI reference 27; Tier 2.**

Richard T. Thornbury; F. Dean Toste. Palladium‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of 1‐Aryl‐2,2‐Difluoroalkenes and Boronic Acids: Stereoselective Synthesis of Monofluorostilbenes. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 55(38), 11629-11632 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201605651

[doi:10.1002/anie.201605651](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201605651)

## Chemical contribution

The conditions were suited to boronic acids and potassium tetrafluoroborate salts, whereas boronic esters reacted sluggishly and arylstannanes and arylsilanes were inert both with and without added fluoride. The authors propose migratory insertion of the difluoroalkene into a palladium-aryl bond to form an alkylpalladium intermediate, followed by beta-fluoride elimination and transmetalation with an aryl boronic acid; they further interpret the probe results as favoring beta-fluoride elimination from a palladium alkyl intermediate rather than simple base-mediated elimination.
