# L084 — Identification and Elimination of an Unexpected Catalyst Poison in Suzuki Coupling

**Online reference 191; Tier 4.**

Jing Liu; Kyle Kimmel; Kimkim Dao; Yang Liu; Ming Qi. Identification and Elimination of an Unexpected Catalyst Poison in Suzuki Coupling. Organic Process Research & Development 22(1), 111-116 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.oprd.7b00342

[doi:10.1021/acs.oprd.7b00342](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.oprd.7b00342)

## Chemical contribution

A stalled coupling could be rescued with additional catalyst and ligand, but higher catalyst loading increased residual palladium in product 1 and adversely affected downstream reactions. The failed Suzuki coupling closely resembled a control in which Pd(OAc)2 was omitted, initially implicating palladium catalyst poisoning. The authors propose that elemental sulfur contamination most likely arose during an SOCl2/DMF esterification through chemistry involving decomposition of SOCl2 and reaction of SCl2 with DMF.
