# L228 — Metal‐Free Photoinduced Defluorinative Carboxylation of Trifluoromethylalkenes with Formate

**SI reference 306; Tier 2.**

Fang‐Ming Du; Lu‐Yu Yan; Ming‐Chen Fu. Metal‐Free Photoinduced Defluorinative Carboxylation of Trifluoromethylalkenes with Formate. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 26(41), e202300883 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.202300883

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.202300883](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.202300883)

## Chemical contribution

Photocatalyst and solvent choice strongly affected feasibility: an iridium photocatalyst gave 8% yield, 4CzIPN gave no isolated desired product, and THF, acetone, and acetonitrile failed to give product 2. The authors propose a radical/polar crossover mechanism in which a thiyl radical generates the carbon dioxide radical anion from formate, radical addition to the alpha-trifluoromethylstyrene forms a benzyl radical, single-electron reduction forms a benzyl anion, and beta-fluoride elimination gives the product.
