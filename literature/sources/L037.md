# L037 — Applications of the Controlled Oxidation of Pyrroles

**Online reference 153; Tier 4.**

Kieran Josef Rihak. Applications of the Controlled Oxidation of Pyrroles. Doctoral thesis, University of Tasmania (2017). https://doi.org/10.25959/100.00028372

[doi:10.25959/100.00028372](https://doi.org/10.25959/100.00028372)

## Chemical contribution

Using a larger excess of Deoxofluor converted fluorohydrin 233 into competing elimination and difluorination products; direct treatment of diol 162 under the discussion conditions gave fluoroalkene 242 and difluorinated product 234 in reported yields of 25% and 20%, respectively. X-ray crystallography established that fluorohydrin 233 resulted from deoxyfluorination at the C3 alcohol with inversion of stereochemistry, leaving fluorine trans to the remaining alcohol.
