# L131 — Synthesis of isoxazoles via cyclization of β-fluoro enones with sodium azide

**SI reference 233; Tier 1.**

Liangkui Li; Shiqing Huang; Kuantao Mao; Leiyang Lv; Zhiping Li. Synthesis of isoxazoles via cyclization of β-fluoro enones with sodium azide. Tetrahedron Letters 71, 153052 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2021.153052

[doi:10.1016/j.tetlet.2021.153052](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2021.153052)

## Chemical contribution

DFT calculations place the path-A cyclization transition state 2.2 kcal/mol above path B and compute a 27.4 kcal/mol barrier and −36.5 kcal/mol reaction free energy for the path-B product-forming step. The authors propose that azide first substitutes the vinyl fluorine to form a vinyl azide, followed either by azirine formation and rearrangement or by direct 5-exo-trig cyclization through enolate oxygen attack; nitrogen release is proposed as the driving force.
