# L043 — Synthesis of Polyfluorinated Nitrogen‐Containing Heterocycles from Hemifluorinated Enones or Organofluorosilicon Building Blocks as Synthetic Equivalents

**Online reference 157; Tier 1.**

Frédéric Chanteau; Benoît Didier; Boniface Dondy; Pascale Doussot; Richard Plantier‐Royon; Charles Portella. Synthesis of Polyfluorinated Nitrogen‐Containing Heterocycles from Hemifluorinated Enones or Organofluorosilicon Building Blocks as Synthetic Equivalents. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2004(7), 1444-1454 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300671

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.200300671](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300671)

## Chemical contribution

Hemiperfluoroenones reacted with amidinium salts under mild basic conditions to form fluorinated pyrimidines; among the tested conditions for aromatic enones, suspended KOH in dichloromethane gave the best yields. Heating the aromatic-substituted benzothiazepine at 120 °C efficiently extruded sulfur, whereas similar treatment of carbohydrate-derived starting materials 6f and 6g caused degradation. The authors propose that a β-enaminone-type intermediate can cyclize by intramolecular nucleophilic attack either at the β-carbon through Michael-type addition or at the carbonyl carbon through condensation.
