# L133 — Peroxide-Initiated Hydrophosphinylation of gem-Difluoroalkenes

**SI reference 21; Tier 2.**

Andrew J. Intelli; Ryan T. Lee; Ryan A. Altman. Peroxide-Initiated Hydrophosphinylation of gem-Difluoroalkenes. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(19), 14012-14021 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562)

## Chemical contribution

Diethyl phosphite was an unsuccessful phosphorus partner, giving only trace product despite further optimization of time, solvent, temperature, and peroxide. The authors propose a free-radical chain mechanism in which a methyl radical abstracts hydrogen from diphenylphosphine oxide, the resulting phosphorus-centered radical adds regioselectively at the difluorinated alkene carbon, and the carbon-centered radical abstracts hydrogen from another equivalent of diphenylphosphine oxide to form product and propagate the chain.
