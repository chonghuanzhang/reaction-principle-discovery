# L113 — Fluorine-containing β,β-disubstituted trimethylsilyl vinyl ethers: synthesis and reactions with N-(1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethyl)dimethylamine

**Online reference 216; Tier 1.**

A. Yu. Volkonskii; E. M. Kagramanova; E. I. Mysov; N. D. Kagramanov. Fluorine-containing β,β-disubstituted trimethylsilyl vinyl ethers: synthesis and reactions with N-(1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethyl)dimethylamine. Russian Chemical Bulletin 59(3), 569-576 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11172-010-0134-z

[doi:10.1007/s11172-010-0134-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11172-010-0134-z)

## Chemical contribution

The slow 1c reaction required sequential treatment at 20 °C for 79 h, 40 °C for 5 h, and 50-60 °C for 7 h; fractional distillation then afforded approximately 96% pure 3c in 79.4% yield. The authors attribute the limited success of heating the slow 1c reaction to fluoride-ion elimination by the proposed fluorinated enolate anion 4c, resulting in by-products and reduced target-product yield.
