# L118 — Making and Breaking C‒F Bonds via Palladium-Catalysis

**SI reference 221; Tier 2.**

Richard Tresslar Thornbury. Making and Breaking C‒F Bonds via Palladium-Catalysis. Doctoral thesis, University of California, Berkeley (2018). https://escholarship.org/uc/item/60h323qp

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

The reaction was highly selective for mono-coupling, but substrate/product 3m showed over-reaction side products; lowering the boronic acid loading remedied this issue and allowed isolation in moderate yield. When 1,1-difluoro-1,2-diphenylethane was subjected to the standard conditions, the corresponding monofluoroalkene was not observed. The authors interpret the negative saturated-substrate control as suggesting that beta-fluoride elimination is not simply base-mediated and instead occurs from a palladium-alkyl intermediate.
