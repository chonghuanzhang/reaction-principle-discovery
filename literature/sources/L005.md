# L005 — Basicity, nucleophilicity, and nucleofugality in the elimination–substitution reactions of β-phenylmercaptoethyl phenolates in DMSO–ethanol media

**SI reference 125; Tier 3.**

Hai-Qi Xie; Nguyen Truong; Erwin Buncel; J. Garfield Purdon. Basicity, nucleophilicity, and nucleofugality in the elimination–substitution reactions of β-phenylmercaptoethyl phenolates in DMSO–ethanol media. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 72(2), 448-453 (1994). https://doi.org/10.1139/v94-066

[doi:10.1139/v94-066](https://doi.org/10.1139/v94-066)

## Chemical contribution

Hammett analysis gave the best fit with sigma-minus parameters, which the authors interpreted as being consistent with substantial negative charge development on aryloxy oxygen in the rate-determining transition state. The authors proposed that the preference for elimination arises mainly from the phenyl substituent on sulfur, the relatively poor phenolate leaving groups, and increased ethoxide basicity in DMSO.
