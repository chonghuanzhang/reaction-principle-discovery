# L159 — Rhodium‐Catalyzed Enantio‐ and Regioselective Allylation of Indoles with gem ‐Difluorinated Cyclopropanes

**Online reference 252; Tier 2.**

Hui Yang; Yaxin Zeng; Xiangyu Song; Lin Che; Zhong‐Tao Jiang; Gang Lu; Ying Xia. Rhodium‐Catalyzed Enantio‐ and Regioselective Allylation of Indoles with gem ‐Difluorinated Cyclopropanes. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 63(22), e202403602 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202403602

[doi:10.1002/anie.202403602](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202403602)

## Chemical contribution

The authors report a rhodium-catalyzed branched-regioselective and enantioselective allylic coupling of gem-difluorinated cyclopropanes with indoles; the model reaction gave 3a in 93% yield, 93% ee, and a 38:1 branched-to-linear ratio. The authors propose a simplified mechanism involving C-C activation by oxidative addition, C-F activation by beta-fluoride elimination, and C-C formation by allylic coupling; they speculate that allylic coupling is net stereoretentive and suggest either an inner-sphere metalation/reductive-elimination sequence or allyl-Rh stereoinversion followed by outer-sphere substitution.
