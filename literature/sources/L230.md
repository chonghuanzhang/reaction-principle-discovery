# L230 — Exploring Intrinsic Bond Properties with the Fukui Matrix from Conceptual Density Matrix Functional Theory

**Online reference 308; Tier 3.**

Bin Wang; Paul Geerlings; Farnaz Heidar-Zadeh; Paul W. Ayers; Frank De Proft. Exploring Intrinsic Bond Properties with the Fukui Matrix from Conceptual Density Matrix Functional Theory. Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation 21(4), 1695-1708 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01627

[doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01627](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01627)

## Chemical contribution

MBIS and AVH partitioning produced consistent numerical results and were preferred by the authors for describing bond behavior upon electron attachment or detachment, although a dihalogen test gave the desired sign only with Mulliken partitioning. For conjugated functional groups such as methyl propionate, the ground-state wave function alone was insufficient for accurate bond-rupture prediction; combining constrained DFT with conceptual DFT accessed higher-energy electronic configurations and facilitated cleavage prediction.
