# L278 — Unexpected Nickel Complex Speciation Unlocks Alternative Pathways for the Reactions of Alkyl Halides with dppf-Nickel(0)

**SI reference 350; Tier 4.**

Megan E. Greaves; Thomas O. Ronson; Guy C. Lloyd-Jones; Feliu Maseras; Stephen Sproules; David J. Nelson. Unexpected Nickel Complex Speciation Unlocks Alternative Pathways for the Reactions of Alkyl Halides with dppf-Nickel(0). ACS Catalysis 10(18), 10717-10725 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.0c02514

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.0c02514](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.0c02514)

## Chemical contribution

Added free dppf significantly accelerated reactions of [Ni(COD)(dppf)] with alkyl halides. With added dppf, the alkyl-halide reactions were pseudo-first-order in the nickel precursor, first-order in alkyl halide and dppf, and inhibited by COD. The authors use the contrasting reactivity of [Ni(dppf)2] and [Ni(dppe)2] to favor a mechanism requiring ligand opening and a vacant coordination site over outer-sphere electron transfer.
