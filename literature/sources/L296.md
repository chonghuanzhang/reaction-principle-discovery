# L296 — Cyclopentadiene bisadduct of tetrafluoro-p-benzoquinone: its stereochemistry, reactivity toward nucleophiles, and photochemistry

**SI reference 365; Tier 1.**

R. Marshall Wilson. Cyclopentadiene bisadduct of tetrafluoro-p-benzoquinone: its stereochemistry, reactivity toward nucleophiles, and photochemistry. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 48(5), 707-711 (1983). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00153a017

[doi:10.1021/jo00153a017](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00153a017)

## Chemical contribution

The tetrafluoro-p-benzoquinone cyclopentadiene bisadduct undergoes facile addition of oxygen nucleophiles at a carbonyl group, followed by reaction of the carbonyl adduct with the double bond of the endo-bicycloheptene moiety. Elemental-composition data exclude assignment of the hydroxide and methoxide adducts as Favorskii rearrangement products. The authors propose initial oxygen-nucleophile attack on an electron-deficient carbonyl to form tetrahedral intermediate 8, followed by a proximity-favored process involving the endo-bicycloheptene double bond.
