# L275 — Preparation of 5-Fluoropyrazoles from Pyrazoles and N-Fluorobenzenesulfonimide (NFSI)

**SI reference 347; Tier 1.**

Vitalina Levchenko; Yurii V. Dmytriv; Andriy V. Tymtsunik; Konstantin Liubchak; Alexander Rudnichenko; Anton V. Melnyk; Stanislav Y. Veselovych; Yurii V. Borodulin; Oleksandr M. Otsalyuk; Andrei A. Tolmachev; Pavel K. Mykhailiuk. Preparation of 5-Fluoropyrazoles from Pyrazoles and N-Fluorobenzenesulfonimide (NFSI). The Journal of Organic Chemistry 83(6), 3265-3274 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.8b00199

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.8b00199](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.8b00199)

## Chemical contribution

Among tested N-aryl pyrazoles 2-9, products from all substrates except 7 were isolated in 60-65% yield; the reaction mixtures also contained starting materials and PhSO2-pyrazole side products. Increasing reaction time, NFSI amount, or reaction temperature did not improve conversion of the model reaction. The authors attributed the lack of NFSI reaction by anion A from pyrazole 11 to its sterically bulky tert-butyl substituent.
