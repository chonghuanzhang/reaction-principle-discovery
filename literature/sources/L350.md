# L350 — A Brønsted acid–base approach for the net monoselective C–F substitution of (trifluoromethyl)alkanes

**SI reference 10; Tier 1.**

Nicholas J. Coradi; Jeffrey S. Bandar. A Brønsted acid–base approach for the net monoselective C–F substitution of (trifluoromethyl)alkanes. Chemical Science 17(19), 9644-9652 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c

[doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

## Chemical contribution

The acid–base study includes a Ph2PH-derived fluorovinyl phosphine in 37% yield before unsuccessful subsequent HF addition. The failed downstream step must remain separate from successful initial C–P bond formation. Its aliphatic CF3 substrate differs from the project carbonyl scaffold. This positive monofluorovinyl observation constrains any unfavorable P-donor assessment.
