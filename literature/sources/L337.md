# L337 — Defluorinative Alkylboration of Alkenes Enabled by Dual Photoredox and Copper Catalysis

**SI reference 400; Tier 2.**

Yanmin Fan; Zhonghou Huang; Yi Lu; Shengqing Zhu; Lingling Chu. Defluorinative Alkylboration of Alkenes Enabled by Dual Photoredox and Copper Catalysis. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 63(5), e202315974 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202315974

[doi:10.1002/anie.202315974](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202315974)

## Chemical contribution

Reaction efficiency was sensitive to the copper source and solvent: other Cu(I) or Cu(II) salts and less-polar solvents gave dramatically lower yields. Under the optimized conditions, the model product was obtained in 86% yield with excellent regioselectivity; only trace side products were reported. The authors proposed SET reduction of an in situ PhCF3/[HO-B2pin2]- species followed by fluoride elimination to form a difluoroalkyl radical, radical addition to the alkene, copper capture, a Cu(III) intermediate, and reductive elimination.
