# L081 — Holistic Prediction of the pKa in Diverse Solvents Based on a Machine‐Learning Approach

**Online reference 188; Tier 3.**

Qi Yang; Yao Li; Jin‐Dong Yang; Yidi Liu; Long Zhang; Sanzhong Luo; Jin‐Pei Cheng. Holistic Prediction of the pKa in Diverse Solvents Based on a Machine‐Learning Approach. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 59(43), 19282-19291 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202008528

[doi:10.1002/anie.202008528](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202008528)

## Chemical contribution

On the same testing set, the six-solvent holistic model HM-6S performed better than all six corresponding single-solvent models. For a series of commonly used secondary aminocatalysts, experimental pKaH values ranged from 10.54 to 24.02, and pKaH was described as a rough estimate of aminocatalyst nucleophilicity. The authors proposed that internal hydrogen bonding may enhance BINOL-type catalyst acidity and that the model likely missed this feature because suitable examples were lacking from the training set.
