# L086 — Ring Opening of Diketene in Superacidic Media

**SI reference 192; Tier 1.**

Sebastian Steiner; Ulli Blumenschein; Zurwa M. Shafiq; Christoph Jessen; Alexander Nitzer; Christoph Taeschler; Andreas J. Kornath. Ring Opening of Diketene in Superacidic Media. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 90(1), 672-681 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.4c02601

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.4c02601](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.4c02601)

## Chemical contribution

Diketene reacted with anhydrous HF at −60 °C to form acetoacetyl fluoride; the authors initially report the keto tautomer as the only tautomer under these conditions. The authors propose ring opening through protonation at either the lactone oxygen or the C=C bond, formation of an acyl cation, and subsequent fluoride addition to the carbonyl moiety; they favor C=C protonation because calculations identify it as the more basic site.
