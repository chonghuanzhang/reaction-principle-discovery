# L135 — Rhodium-Catalyzed Asymmetric Arylation/Defluorination of 1-(Trifluoromethyl)alkenes Forming Enantioenriched 1,1-Difluoroalkenes

**Online reference 236; Tier 2.**

Yinhua Huang; Tamio Hayashi. Rhodium-Catalyzed Asymmetric Arylation/Defluorination of 1-(Trifluoromethyl)alkenes Forming Enantioenriched 1,1-Difluoroalkenes. Journal of the American Chemical Society 138(38), 12340-12343 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6b07844

[doi:10.1021/jacs.6b07844](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6b07844)

## Chemical contribution

Arylboroxines bearing alkoxy, methyl, bromo, or trifluoromethyl substituents at the 3- or 4-position of phenyl were successfully incorporated in high yields, while a bulky aryl group gave a slightly lower yield. An aryl-substituted trifluoromethylalkene performed poorly under the arylboroxine conditions, giving 9% arylation/defluorination product and 3% hydroarylation product; raising the temperature improved yields only partially and did not provide high product selectivity.
