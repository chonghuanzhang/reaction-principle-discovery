# L142 — Magnesium‐Mediated Reductive Defluorinative Cross‐Electrophile Coupling of α‐Trifluoromethyl Styrenes with CO2 and Acyl Imidazoles

**SI reference 241; Tier 2.**

Jianjun Hao; Lu Ren; Jingwen Tan; Yulin Peng; Linhai Jing; Pan Han. Magnesium‐Mediated Reductive Defluorinative Cross‐Electrophile Coupling of α‐Trifluoromethyl Styrenes with CO2 and Acyl Imidazoles. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 367(6), e202401594 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202401594

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202401594](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202401594)

## Chemical contribution

The carboxylation tolerated multiple aryl substituents, including fluoro, chloro, methyl, methoxy, trifluoromethyl, trifluoromethoxy, methylthio, tert-butyl, trimethylsilyl, fused-ring, heterocyclic, and heteroatom-substituted examples; electron-donating substituents generally gave higher yields than electron-withdrawing substituents. A 7.0 mmol reductive defluorinative carboxylation was reported to proceed without a significant decrease in yield. The authors suggest that strongly electron-withdrawing substituents suppress product formation by decreasing the nucleophilicity of an anionic intermediate.
