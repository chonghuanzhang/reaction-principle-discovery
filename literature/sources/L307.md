# L307 — Rational design of nucleophilic amine sites via computational probing of steric and electronic effects

**SI reference 376; Tier 3.**

Anton De Vylder; Jeroen Lauwaert; Maarten K. Sabbe; Marie-Françoise Reyniers; Jeriffa De Clercq; Pascal Van Der Voort; Joris W. Thybaut. Rational design of nucleophilic amine sites via computational probing of steric and electronic effects. Catalysis Today 334, 96-103 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cattod.2019.01.026

[doi:10.1016/j.cattod.2019.01.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cattod.2019.01.026)

## Chemical contribution

The trimethylborane-basicity scale failed to explain the inactivity of R-pipecolic acid despite its high calculated basicity, indicating that an initial-attack steric probe does not capture all later mechanistic reactivity, including enamine nucleophilicity. The authors limit kinetic predictions from thermodynamic basicity rankings to small sets of structurally similar Lewis bases that differ only in steric or electronic effects around the amine and for which a linear free-energy relationship is expected.
