# L026 — Cyclopropylboronic acid: synthesis and Suzuki cross-coupling reactions

**SI reference 143; Tier 1.**

Debra J. Wallace; Cheng-yi Chen. Cyclopropylboronic acid: synthesis and Suzuki cross-coupling reactions. Tetrahedron Letters 43(39), 6987-6990 (2002). https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4039(02)01606-4

[doi:10.1016/s0040-4039(02)01606-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4039(02)01606-4)

## Chemical contribution

Condition screening used 1.2 equivalents of cyclopropylboronic acid; water significantly accelerated the coupling, and 5 mol% palladium acetate with 10 mol% tricyclohexylphosphine gave the best conversion among the reported screening conditions. Under separately explored Buchwald aryl-chloride conditions, 1-chloro-4-nitrobenzene gave 20% product after 24 hours, while 4-chlorobenzaldehyde gave only trace product after 48 hours; the stated conditions used 10 mol% 2-(di-tert-butylphosphino)biphenyl, 5 mol% palladium acetate, three equivalents of potassium fluoride, THF, and 45 °C.

### L026-S004

### L026-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The isolated cyclopropylboronic acid typically contained 5-10% boric acid, which was not detrimental to the Suzuki reactions, and it showed no noticeable decomposition or activity loss over several months in a capped vial at room temperature.

Location: 1; Reagent purity, storage stability, and retained coupling activity.

### L026-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Under the optimized conditions, 1.3 equivalents of cyclopropylboronic acid coupled with 2-, 3-, 4-, and polysubstituted aryl bromides and tolerated ketone, ester, aldehyde, amine, nitrile, fluoro, and ether functionality; the phenol substrate showed no reaction.

Location: 2; Aryl bromide scope and functional-group compatibility.

<a id="statement-l026-s008"></a>

### L026-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Pyridines, quinolines, a furan, 2-bromonaphthoquinone, and a vinyl bromide gave the desired coupling products, whereas chlorobenzene, 1-chloro-4-nitrobenzene, and phenyl triflate did not react under the current conditions.

Location: 2; Heteroaryl and vinyl bromide scope, with chloride and triflate failure boundaries.

### L026-S009

Cyclopropylboronic acid showed no reaction with phenols or amines under the examined copper-catalyzed Chan-Evans coupling methods.
