# L085 — Simple and efficient synthesis of 2,2-disubstituted-1,1-difluorophosphonates and phosphonothioates

**Online reference 55; Tier 2.**

Serge R. Piettre. Simple and efficient synthesis of 2,2-disubstituted-1,1-difluorophosphonates and phosphonothioates. Tetrahedron Letters 37(13), 2233-2236 (1996). https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-4039(96)00239-0

[doi:10.1016/0040-4039(96)00239-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-4039(96)00239-0)

## Chemical contribution

For the model reaction, staged tert-butyl peroxypivalate initiation after freeze-thaw degassing gave an NMR-estimated 88:8:0:3 mixture of desired adduct, regioisomer, by-product, and unconsumed olefin; the preparative procedure at 62 °C gave 97% starting-material consumption and isolated the desired product in 66% yield with 8% regioisomer. The authors proposed that by-product 7b forms through a 1,5-hydrogen-atom shift in radical adduct 11, followed by beta-scission, acetaldehyde loss, quenching by diethyl phosphite, and oxidation.
