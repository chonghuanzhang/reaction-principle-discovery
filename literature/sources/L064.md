# L064 — Stability of Pyridiniumylquinones to Aqueous Media: The Formation of Pyridinium−Oxy Zwitterionic Quinones

**SI reference 175; Tier 1.**

Andrew S. Koch; Warren Grant Harbison; Joshua M. Hubbard; Martin de Kort; Barbara A. Roe. Stability of Pyridiniumylquinones to Aqueous Media: The Formation of Pyridinium−Oxy Zwitterionic Quinones. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 61(17), 5959-5963 (1996). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo9600924

[doi:10.1021/jo9600924](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo9600924)

## Chemical contribution

Products from reactions of pyridines with 2-chloro-1,4-benzoquinone and 2,5-dichloro-1,4-benzoquinone appeared unstable toward moisture. Pyridiniumylanthraquinone 3 showed substantially greater water and thermal stability than the pyridiniumbenzoquinones, with no decomposition observed in boiling water and decomposition beginning only at 255 °C. The authors proposed that pyridine substitution of the quinone most probably proceeds by an SNAr mechanism and that the first pyridinium substituent electronically enhances reaction at the adjacent carbon sufficiently to outweigh steric effects.
