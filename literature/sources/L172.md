# L172 — Chiral phase-transfer catalysis in the asymmetric α-heterofunctionalization of prochiral nucleophiles

**Online reference 261; Tier 3.**

Johannes Schörgenhumer; Maximilian Tiffner; Mario Waser. Chiral phase-transfer catalysis in the asymmetric α-heterofunctionalization of prochiral nucleophiles. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry 13, 1753-1769 (2017). https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.13.170

[doi:10.3762/bjoc.13.170](https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.13.170)

## Chemical contribution

In asymmetric α-trifluoromethylthiolation, catalyst performance depended on substrate ring size: simple cinchona alkaloids were effective for indanone-derived substrates but gave no more than 10% yield for larger rings, while chiral phase-transfer catalysts enabled the larger-ring products in satisfying yields and high enantioselectivities. The review authors consider asymmetric phase-transfer-catalyzed α-amination to have been less systematically explored than other α-heterofunctionalizations and to remain far from solved, especially for unprotected α-aminocarbonyl targets.

### L172-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Attempts at asymmetric phase-transfer-catalyzed α-bromination or α-iodination showed counterion oxidation and α-halogenation but no asymmetric induction under the tested conditions, including tests with N-bromosuccinimide and multiple chiral catalysts.

Location: 6; α-Bromination and α-iodination; failed selectivity and side reaction

### L172-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

An early asymmetric α-fluorination of β-ketoesters used NFSI, a cinchona-derived quaternary ammonium catalyst, solid K2CO3 or Cs2CO3, biphasic conditions, and room temperature, reaching enantiomeric ratios up to 85:15.

Location: 2; α-Fluorinations

### L172-S008
