# L165 — HFIP-Catalyzed Difluoroalkylation of Propargylic Alcohols to Access Tetrasubstituted Difluoroalkyl Allenes

**SI reference 256; Tier 1.**

Jinshan Li; Wenxue Xi; Saimei Liu; Chenxi Ruan; Xiaochun Zheng; Jianguo Yang; Lei Wang; Zhiming Wang. HFIP-Catalyzed Difluoroalkylation of Propargylic Alcohols to Access Tetrasubstituted Difluoroalkyl Allenes. Organic Letters 23(18), 7264-7269 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02659

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02659](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02659)

## Chemical contribution

The reported scope includes propargylic alcohols and difluoroenoxysilanes bearing electron-donating and electron-withdrawing substituents; substituted diaryl-ketone-derived propargylic alcohols gave products 3h-3p in 64-87% yields, and a heteroaryl-substituted propargylic alcohol was tolerated. For formation of fluorinated cyclopentanone 12 from difluoroalkyl allene 3m under separate downstream conditions, the authors propose hydroxide attack, keto-enol tautomerism, beta-fluoride elimination to a fluorinated pent-2-ene-1,4-dione intermediate, and intramolecular nucleophilic cyclization.
