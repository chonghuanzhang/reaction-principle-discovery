# L226 — A new method for the determination of the relative acidities of alcohols in alcoholic solutions. The nucleophilicities and competitive reactivities of alkoxides and phenoxides

**Online reference 304; Tier 3.**

Wilkins Reeve; Charles M. Erikson; Patrick F. Aluotto. A new method for the determination of the relative acidities of alcohols in alcoholic solutions. The nucleophilicities and competitive reactivities of alkoxides and phenoxides. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 57(20), 2747-2754 (1979). https://doi.org/10.1139/v79-444

[doi:10.1139/v79-444](https://doi.org/10.1139/v79-444)

## Chemical contribution

With n-butyl bromide, the competitive reactions were run at 26 °C; approximately 25 hours were required for 50% reaction, less than 3% of the electrophile formed 1-butene, and no measurable reaction occurred without base. The authors interpret the invariance to alcohol ratio, mixture composition, and added water as indicating that ion pairing, dielectric-constant changes, and other solvent effects are small under their experimental conditions.
