# L334 — Reductive quenching-initiated catalyst-controlled divergent alkylation of α-CF3-olefins

**Online reference 64; Tier 2.**

Yunxiao Zhang; Yizhi Zhang; Youyuan Guo; Shanshan Liu; Xiao Shen. Reductive quenching-initiated catalyst-controlled divergent alkylation of α-CF3-olefins. Chem Catalysis 2(6), 1380-1393 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.checat.2022.03.021

[doi:10.1016/j.checat.2022.03.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.checat.2022.03.021)

## Chemical contribution

The eosin Y hydroalkylation branch furnished trifluoromethyl alkanes from various α-CF3 olefins in 35–94% yield; electron-neutral and electron-rich substrates generally gave high alkane-to-difluoroalkene ratios of 96:4 to greater than 99:1. The authors propose that alkyl-radical addition to the α-CF3 olefin forms a benzylic radical; stronger reduction by the iridium system gives a benzylic anion followed by β-fluoride elimination, whereas eosin Y favors hydrogen-atom transfer to retain the CF3 group.
