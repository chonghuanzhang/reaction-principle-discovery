# L108 — The photochemical nucleophile–olefin combination, aromatic substitution (photo-NOCAS) reaction (Part 4): methanol–olefins, methyl 4-cyanobenzoate

**Online reference 211; Tier 3.**

Kevin McMahon; Donald R. Arnold. The photochemical nucleophile–olefin combination, aromatic substitution (photo-NOCAS) reaction (Part 4): methanol–olefins, methyl 4-cyanobenzoate. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 71(4), 450-468 (1993). https://doi.org/10.1139/v93-066

[doi:10.1139/v93-066](https://doi.org/10.1139/v93-066)

## Chemical contribution

Cyclohexene gave trans photo-NOCAS adduct 27 in 8% yield, while the major isolated product was a 1:1 olefin-aromatic adduct in 12% yield. The reported acceptor scope was narrow: among the listed substituted benzonitriles and cyanocarbonyl compounds, photo-NOCAS products were observed only with 1,4-dicyanobenzene and methyl 4-cyanobenzoate. A proposed key step is attack of a beta-alkoxyalkyl radical on the methyl 4-cyanobenzoate radical anion, potentially at either the cyano-bearing ipso position or the carboxyl carbonyl.
