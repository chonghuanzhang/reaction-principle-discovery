# L246 — Silver‐Assisted Difunctionalization of Terminal Alkynes: Highly Regio‐ and Stereoselective Synthesis of Bromofluoroalkenes

**Online reference 322; Tier 1.**

Yibiao Li; Xiaohang Liu; Deyun Ma; Bifu Liu; Huanfeng Jiang. Silver‐Assisted Difunctionalization of Terminal Alkynes: Highly Regio‐ and Stereoselective Synthesis of Bromofluoroalkenes. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 354(14-15), 2683-2688 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.201200250

[doi:10.1002/adsc.201200250](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.201200250)

## Chemical contribution

One bromofluoroalkene, 2f, reacted with phenylboronic acid to give a fluorinated alkene product in 91% yield. Common fluoride sources and several Ag- or Pd-containing systems were ineffective or produced only traces, while the CsF/AgNO3 system mainly formed phenylethynyl bromide. The authors propose Ag-catalyzed formation of a bromoalkyne, silver coordination, trans-addition of AgF to form a vinyl-silver intermediate, and protonation to furnish the Z bromofluoroalkene; they state that an alternative fluoride-addition pathway cannot be excluded.
