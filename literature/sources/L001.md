# L001 — Copper-catalysed oxy-alkylation of styrenes enabled by halogen-atom transfer

**SI reference 121; Tier 1.**

Qiujian Tan; Xiang Lyu; Yu Zhao; Huaquan Fang; Xianxiu Xu; Zhongyan Hu. Copper-catalysed oxy-alkylation of styrenes enabled by halogen-atom transfer. Chemical Science 16(37), 17316-17324 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc03774c

[doi:10.1039/d5sc03774c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc03774c)

## Chemical contribution

Radical trapping inhibited or reduced product formation and produced characterized difluoroalkyl and alpha-aminoalkyl adducts; a radical-clock substrate gave a ring-expanded product in 40% yield together with product 3 in 33% yield. The authors propose that Cu(II) oxidizes Cy2NMe, deprotonation gives an alpha-aminoalkyl radical, XAT generates an ethyl difluoroacetate radical, radical addition to styrene and oxygen/copper recombination produce intermediate 30, and Cy2NMe-mediated dehydrofluorination then forms the Z-beta-fluoroenone.

### L001-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

CuCl2 and Cy2NMe were essential under the tested conditions; omission of either gave no product. PPh3 improved but was not essential, and air was required: nitrogen gave no reaction while pure oxygen lowered the yield.
