# L261 — Photocatalytic Alkyl Radical Addition Tandem Oxidation of Alkenyl Borates

**SI reference 336; Tier 1.**

Yu-Jie Li; De-Guang Liu; Jin-Hu Ren; Tian-Jun Gong; Yao Fu. Photocatalytic Alkyl Radical Addition Tandem Oxidation of Alkenyl Borates. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(7), 4325-4333 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02923

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02923](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02923)

## Chemical contribution

Pure oxygen decreased the yield of 3a to 46%, which the authors attributed to accelerated direct oxidation of the alkenyl borate substrate. TEMPO strongly inhibited or shut down the reaction, and an alkyl-TEMPO adduct was detected by GC-MS. The authors propose that photoexcited Ir(ppy)3 reduces the alkyl halide to an alkyl radical and oxygen to superoxide; radical addition to the alkenyl borate gives an alpha-boryl radical, followed by reaction with superoxide and possible migratory rearrangements to product.
