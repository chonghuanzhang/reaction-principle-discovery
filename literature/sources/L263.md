# L263 — Pinpoint-fluorinated polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (F-PAHs) and their heteroaromatic analogs: syntheses, reactivities, and properties

**Online reference 33; Tier 2.**

Kohei Fuchibe; Takeshi Fujita; Junji Ichikawa. Pinpoint-fluorinated polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (F-PAHs) and their heteroaromatic analogs: syntheses, reactivities, and properties. Bulletin of the Chemical Society of Japan 97(3), uoad024 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1093/bulcsj/uoad024

[doi:10.1093/bulcsj/uoad024](https://doi.org/10.1093/bulcsj/uoad024)

## Chemical contribution

Under PdCl2/AgX and BF3·OEt2 in HFIP, 1,1,2-trifluoro-1-alkenes cyclized to vic-difluorinated phenacenes; a gram-scale example gave pure 13,14-difluoropicene in 54% yield after sublimation. The reported Pd(II)-catalyzed difluoroalkene cyclization includes larger F-phenacenes and uses a cationic Pd(II) species generated from PdCl2 and AgOTf for F-chrysenes and F-picenes. The proposed cycloalkylpalladium intermediate undergoes BF3-assisted beta-fluorine elimination, and BF4− formation is proposed to regenerate the active cationic Pd(II) species.
