# L008 — Catalytic defluorinative ketyl–olefin coupling by halogen-atom transfer

**SI reference 32; Tier 2.**

Peter Bellotti; Huan-Ming Huang; Teresa Faber; Ranjini Laskar; Frank Glorius. Catalytic defluorinative ketyl–olefin coupling by halogen-atom transfer. Chemical Science 13(26), 7855-7862 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1039/d2sc02732a

[doi:10.1039/d2sc02732a](https://doi.org/10.1039/d2sc02732a)

## Chemical contribution

Omission controls established that photocatalyst, silanol reagent, KOAc, and light were each necessary under the tested conditions; replacing the silanol with Et3N or DIPEA gave no product, while tribenzylamine conditions gave 20% yield. A range of substituted α-trifluoromethylstyrenes was coupled successfully, but electron-withdrawing substituents promoted substantial trifluoromethylated side products through competitive protonation of the proposed anionic intermediate IX. The ring-opening experiment was interpreted by the authors as suggesting a benzyl-radical intermediate.
