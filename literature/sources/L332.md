# L332 — Comparison of Monophosphine and Bisphosphine Precatalysts for Ni-Catalyzed Suzuki–Miyaura Cross-Coupling: Understanding the Role of the Ligation State in Catalysis

**SI reference 396; Tier 4.**

Julia E. Borowski; Samuel H. Newman-Stonebraker; Abigail G. Doyle. Comparison of Monophosphine and Bisphosphine Precatalysts for Ni-Catalyzed Suzuki–Miyaura Cross-Coupling: Understanding the Role of the Ligation State in Catalysis. ACS Catalysis 13(12), 7966-7977 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.3c01331

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.3c01331](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.3c01331)

## Chemical contribution

With 2-thiophenylboronic acid, added thiophene or increased boronic-acid loading decreased the yield of the monophosphine system, whereas added 2-phenylthiophene product had no effect and the bisphosphine systems were unaffected by these additives. The authors propose that thiophene formed by protodeboronation deactivates the more labile monophosphine system, while P2Ni-favoring bisphosphines protect the catalyst; direct quantification of thiophene and discrimination between catalyst-mediated and base-catalyzed formation were unsuccessful.
