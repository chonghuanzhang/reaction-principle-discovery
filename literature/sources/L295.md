# L295 — Ion pairing and reactivity of β-ketoenolates

**Online reference 364; Tier 3.**

E. M. Arnett; V. DePalma; S. Maroldo; L. S. Small. Ion pairing and reactivity of β-ketoenolates. Pure and Applied Chemistry 51(1), 131-137 (1979). https://doi.org/10.1351/pac197951010131

[doi:10.1351/pac197951010131](https://doi.org/10.1351/pac197951010131)

## Chemical contribution

The study compares purified salts of completely ionized symmetrical diketones and esters to examine ion pairing and enolate reactivity. Conductance of lithium dibenzoylmethide and lithium dipivaloylmethide was barely detectable with the stated 1% bridge. The proposed conformational account balances chelation stabilization of a U-shaped enolate against dipolar repulsion between parallel carbonyl groups, linking counterion association and donor conformation rather than treating an enolate as a single invariant species.
