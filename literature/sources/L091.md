# L091 — Functionalization by metallation of fluoropyrazine. Diazines XXI

**Online reference 196; Tier 1.**

Nelly Plé; Alain Turck; Arnault Heynderickx; Guy Quéguiner. Functionalization by metallation of fluoropyrazine. Diazines XXI. Tetrahedron 54(19), 4899-4912 (1998). https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(98)00173-2

[doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(98)00173-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(98)00173-2)

## Chemical contribution

LTMP with a five-minute metallation time gave the best tested result for reaction of fluoropyrazine with acetaldehyde, affording the product in 86% yield. The electrophile scope included aldehydes, benzophenone, ethyl formate, a sulfur electrophile, iodine, cyanogen bromide, and N,N-dimethyl amides, with reported yields ranging from 28% to 90% in Table 2. The authors proposed that bulky Ph2C(OH) or SiMe3 substituents could block approach of LTMP to the C5 position.
