# L225 — Hypercoordinate Silicon Compounds in Organic Synthesis: Improved Methods for the Synthesis of Aryl(Trialkoxy)Silane Derivatives; and Trimethylsilyl Cyanide as a Cyanide Source for Nucleophilic Substitution

**SI reference 303; Tier 3.**

Amy Slover Manoso. Hypercoordinate Silicon Compounds in Organic Synthesis: Improved Methods for the Synthesis of Aryl(Trialkoxy)Silane Derivatives; and Trimethylsilyl Cyanide as a Cyanide Source for Nucleophilic Substitution. Doctoral thesis, University of Maryland, College Park (2004).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Secondary halides and sulfonates reacted more slowly than primary substrates, and competing alkene elimination reduced nitrile yields; the tested secondary octyl iodide, bromide, and tosylate nevertheless gave nitrile in reported yields of 76-83%. Cyclohexyl bromide failed as a cyanide-displacement substrate and instead gave alkene, while menthyl chloride was unreactive; steric hindrance was offered as the presumed explanation for the latter result.

### L225-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Primary alkyl iodide, bromide, chloride, and mesylate substrates were efficiently converted to nitriles, with the observed leaving-group reactivity order I approximately equal to OMs, greater than Br, greater than Cl.

### L225-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Across the tested primary and secondary alkyl halides and sulfonates, tetrabutylammonium cyanide reacted 1.5–9 times faster than the cyanosilicate reagent, while the two methods gave identical reported product yields.

Location: 182; Chapter 2, mechanistic comparison with tetrabutylammonium cyanide

### L225-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The Me3SiCN/TBAF system converted a range of primary and secondary alkyl halides and sulfonates to nitriles; the general procedure used a 1:1.5:1.5 substrate/Me3SiCN/TBAF molar ratio in acetonitrile unless otherwise noted.

Location: 174; Chapter 2, Results and Discussion, cyanide-displacement scope table

<a id="statement-l225-s005"></a>

### L225-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For benzyl-bromide cyanation with 1.5 equivalents of Me3SiCN in acetonitrile at room temperature, 100, 50, and 25 mol% TBAF gave 89%, 42%, and 15% phenylacetonitrile, respectively, after one hour.

Location: 178; Chapter 2, fluoride-stoichiometry experiment

### L225-S006

The author proposed as a plausible mechanism that fluoride first causes transient formation of a hypercoordinate cyanofluorosilicate, followed by rapid disproportionation to dicyano- and difluorotrimethylsilicates.

### L225-S008
