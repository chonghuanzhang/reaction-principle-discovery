# L112 — Catalytic Asymmetric Synthesis of Butenolides and Butyrolactones

**SI reference 215; Tier 3.**

Bin Mao; Martín Fañanás-Mastral; Ben L. Feringa. Catalytic Asymmetric Synthesis of Butenolides and Butyrolactones. Chemical Reviews 117(15), 10502-10566 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00151

[doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00151](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00151)

## Chemical contribution

For the reported transformation, a range of unsaturated carboxylic acids bearing aryl, heteroaryl, or alkynyl substituents and different radical precursors was evaluated, affording functionalized butenolides with tetrasubstituted stereogenic centers in good yields and excellent enantioselectivities. For copper-catalyzed oxytrifluoromethylation of unsaturated carboxylic acids, mechanistic studies suggested that enantiodetermining C-O bond formation proceeds through Cu-C bond formation between a Cu(II) center and a prochiral alkyl radical, followed by stereoretentive reductive elimination from a Cu(III) complex.
