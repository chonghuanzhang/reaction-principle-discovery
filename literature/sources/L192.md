# L192 — Fluorine-Directed Nazarov Cyclizations: A Controlled Synthesis of Cross-Conjugated 2-Cyclopenten-1-ones

**SI reference 276; Tier 1.**

Junji Ichikawa; Shinji Miyazaki; Masaki Fujiwara; Toru Minami. Fluorine-Directed Nazarov Cyclizations: A Controlled Synthesis of Cross-Conjugated 2-Cyclopenten-1-ones. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 60(8), 2320-2321 (1995). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00113a005

[doi:10.1021/jo00113a005](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00113a005)

## Chemical contribution

Acyclic and cyclic fluorinated divinyl ketones 3b-g reacted rapidly under the selected conditions to afford the corresponding cyclopentenones in good to excellent yields. In a fluorine-control experiment, fluorinated substrate 3a gave a single product, whereas its nonfluorinated counterpart 7 gave a mixture of three products 8-10. The authors propose that carbonyl-oxygen silylation generates a cyclopentenyl cation, which collapses with loss of fluoride and a proton to form the endocyclic and exocyclic double bonds.
