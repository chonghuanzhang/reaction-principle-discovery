# L195 — Carborane acids. New “strong yet gentle” acids for organic and inorganic chemistry

**Online reference 279; Tier 3.**

Christopher A. Reed. Carborane acids. New “strong yet gentle” acids for organic and inorganic chemistry. Chem. Commun. (13), 1669-1677 (2005). https://doi.org/10.1039/b415425h

[doi:10.1039/b415425h](https://doi.org/10.1039/b415425h)

## Chemical contribution

A carborane acid cleanly and reversibly protonates C60 in dry halocarbon solvents, allowing quantitative isolation of the protonated fullerene salt, whereas traditional acids caused oxidative or nucleophilic decomposition. The author proposes that excess Lewis acid can lower substrate basicity through adduct formation, forcing protonation to compete with Lewis-acid binding and potentially increasing the required Brønsted acidity by several orders of magnitude.
