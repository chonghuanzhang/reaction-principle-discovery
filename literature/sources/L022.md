# L022 — Electron-Transfer-Enabled Concerted Nucleophilic Fluorination of Azaarenes: Selective C–H Fluorination of Quinolines

**SI reference 139; Tier 1.**

Li Zhang; Jiyao Yan; Dilgam Ahmadli; Zikuan Wang; Tobias Ritter. Electron-Transfer-Enabled Concerted Nucleophilic Fluorination of Azaarenes: Selective C–H Fluorination of Quinolines. Journal of the American Chemical Society 145(37), 20182-20188 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c07119

[doi:10.1021/jacs.3c07119](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c07119)

## Chemical contribution

Electron-deficient and electron-neutral aryl substituents are tolerated, whereas electron-rich aryl substituents give lower yields; several substrates showed incomplete conversion that the authors attribute as likely due to product inhibition. The authors interpret the observed C4 selectivity and calculated LUMOs as being consistent with protonated quinoline acting as the electrophile; they propose that Lewis-acid coordination can improve C4 selectivity by sterically disfavoring C2 reaction.
