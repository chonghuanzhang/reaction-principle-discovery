# L181 — Development of Halogenase Enzymes for Use in Synthesis

**Online reference 267; Tier 4.**

Jonathan Latham; Eileen Brandenburger; Sarah A. Shepherd; Binuraj R. K. Menon; Jason Micklefield. Development of Halogenase Enzymes for Use in Synthesis. Chemical Reviews 118(1), 232-269 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00032

[doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00032](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00032)

## Chemical contribution

A sequential chemoenzymatic example used tryptophan-7-halogenase-generated chloropacidamycin and Suzuki-Miyaura chemistry to prepare 7-aryl pacidamycin derivatives. PrnA and RebH did not accept the tested N-methylated or other N-functionalized tryptophans, whereas SttH accepted N-methylated tryptophan. Reported one-pot systems separated charged biocatalysts and chemocatalysts with hydrophobic PDMS membranes while allowing a nonpolar aryl halide intermediate to diffuse between compartments.
