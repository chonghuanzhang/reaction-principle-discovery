## Chemical contribution

Hydrofluorination of ynamides provides α-fluoroenamides bearing sulfonamide or imide nitrogen, establishing that these nitrogen substituents can occur in fluorovinyl product motifs. The source therefore informs product-connectivity comparisons for nitrogen candidates. Its route starts from a preformed ynamide and introduces fluorine, so the precedent does not independently establish N–H donor activation or selective defluorinative coupling of a trifluoroethyl carbonyl substrate.
