# L349 — β-Acyloxyl alkenyl amide synthesis via multiple defluorination: α-Trifluoromethyl ketone–amine as synergystic peptide coupling reagent

**Online reference 66; Tier 1.**

Sufang Cao; Haijin Guo; Zukang Zhong; Demao Chen; Wenli Song; Yunyun Liu; Jie-Ping Wan. β-Acyloxyl alkenyl amide synthesis via multiple defluorination: α-Trifluoromethyl ketone–amine as synergystic peptide coupling reagent. Science Advances 11(43), eaea4120 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aea4120

[doi:10.1126/sciadv.aea4120](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aea4120)

## Chemical contribution

The authors proposed that PhCOCH2CF3 undergoes HF elimination, amine addition and a second HF elimination to a fluoroenaminone intermediate, supported by HRMS. Subsequent chemistry removes the remaining fluorine and forms a β-acyloxy alkenyl amide. This source contributes an adjacent carbonyl/CF3 activation sequence and a competing complete-defluorination endpoint; detection of a monofluorinated intermediate does not establish its isolation or accumulation as the final target product.
