## Chemical contribution

The author abstract describes preparation and synthetic use of (trimethylsilyl)dimethylphosphinite, Me2P–O–SiMe3, to obtain dimethylphosphinoyl-derived nitriles, amines and carbonyl-containing compounds. This P(III) precursor has no P–H bond and is distinct from Ph2PH and P(O)H reagents. The source informs precursor-form distinctions. No failed target coupling, reaction-specific attack site or individual yield was verified from the accessible abstract.
