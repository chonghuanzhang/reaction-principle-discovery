# L328 — Xanthone Natural Products via N-Heterocyclic Carbene Catalysis: Total Synthesis of Atroviridin

**Online reference 44; Tier 1.**

Yumiko Suzuki; Yoshinori Fukuta; Shinya Ota; Masayo Kamiya; Masayuki Sato. Xanthone Natural Products via N-Heterocyclic Carbene Catalysis: Total Synthesis of Atroviridin. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 76(10), 3960-3967 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo200303c

[doi:10.1021/jo200303c](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo200303c)

## Chemical contribution

The experimental procedure combined sodium hydride, 1,3-dimethylimidazolium iodide 7, 2,4,5-trifluoronitrobenzene 5, and aldehyde 6 in dehydrated DMF, then stirred the mixture for 10 min at 0 °C and 1 h at room temperature; the supplied excerpt reports a 76% yield for the characterized product, although the product name or number is omitted at that point in the quotation. The authors propose that a Breslow intermediate is part of the NHC-catalyzed aroylation pathway.
