# L287 — Versatile Fluorinated Building Blocks by Stereoselective (Per)fluoroalkenylation of Ketones

**SI reference 358; Tier 1.**

Fei Ye; Shaoke Zhang; Zhihong Wei; Florian Weniger; Anke Spannenberg; Christoph Taeschler; Stefan Ellinger; Haijun Jiao; Helfried Neumann; Matthias Beller. Versatile Fluorinated Building Blocks by Stereoselective (Per)fluoroalkenylation of Ketones. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2020(1), 70-81 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201901717

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.201901717](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201901717)

## Chemical contribution

Adding three equivalents of TEMPO to the model reaction left only traces of diene 3a; the authors interpret this inhibition as support for a radical process. The authors assign new fluorine NMR signals near -68 ppm to a proposed charge-transfer complex G and consider conversion of G to iminium intermediate D to be rate determining; these mechanistic identities and the rate-determining-step assignment remain proposed interpretations.

### L287-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Five- and six-membered cyclic ketones gave products in good yields, while a seven-membered ketone gave lower yield; cyclic ketones containing oxygen, sulfur, or nitrogen and substrates bearing ester, amide, nitrile, ketal, alkyl, or phenyl substituents were reported as compatible.

### L287-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

DFT calculations found the proposed neutral intermediate H1 to be 2.5 kJ/mol more stable than H2 and the Z diene to be 17.3 kJ/mol more stable than the E isomer; the authors use these calculations to support their proposed stereoselective fluoride-elimination pathway.

Location: 5; Results and Discussion, DFT calculations and mechanistic interpretation.

### L287-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Products 4a, 4f, 4n, and 4y were treated with benzamidine, hydroxylamine, or hydrazine to furnish the reported heterocycles in good to high isolated yields. An estrone-derived product 11 was obtained in 50% yield by a nucleophilic addition reaction. These were downstream transformations of products 4 rather than the initial ketone/perfluoroalkyl-halide reaction.

Location: 5; Results and Discussion, downstream valorization of isolated fluoroenones.

### L287-S007

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose that cyclopentanone and pyrrolidine first condense to enamine A, which is converted to α-perfluoroalkyl-substituted iminium iodide D through either radical-radical cross-coupling or radical transfer, followed by elimination to form multisubstituted diene 3.

Location: 3; Results and Discussion, proposed mechanism.

### L287-S011

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For the model reaction, the best first-step conditions used Cs2CO3, 4 Å molecular sieves, THF, and 80 °C. Pyrrolidine was the best promoter, and a 2:2.2:1 ratio of ketone/pyrrolidine/perfluoroalkyl halide gave 82% yield, while product 4a was isolated in 67% yield.
