# L155 — Regional Electrophilic and Nucleophilic Fukui Functions Efficiently Highlight the Lewis Acidic/Basic Regions in Ionic Liquids

**Online reference 248; Tier 3.**

Andrea Cerda-Monje; Rodrigo Ormazábal-Toledo; Carlos Cárdenas; Patricio Fuentealba; Renato Contreras. Regional Electrophilic and Nucleophilic Fukui Functions Efficiently Highlight the Lewis Acidic/Basic Regions in Ionic Liquids. The Journal of Physical Chemistry B 118(13), 3696-3701 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1021/jp5009994

[doi:10.1021/jp5009994](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp5009994)

## Chemical contribution

For cited Diels–Alder reactions, BF4−, NTf2−, OTf−, and PF6− ionic liquids were reported to give the best yields and reaction rates, while Br− and CF3CO2− ionic liquids gave lower yields; the authors associate the best performance with bifunctional Lewis acidity/basicity. For the cited SNAr reaction between 1-fluoro-4-nitrobenzene and p-anisidine, ionic liquids bearing CF3CO2− or CH3SO3− gave yields above 70%, whereas BF4−, NTf2−, OTf−, and PF6− ionic liquids gave yields below 5%.
