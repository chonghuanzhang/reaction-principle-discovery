# L300 — Vicarious nucleophilic substitution of hydrogen (VNS) in 1,4-naphthoquinone derivatives—competition between VNS and vinylic nucleophilic substitution (SNV)

**Online reference 369; Tier 1.**

Mieczysław Mąkosza; Shamil Nizamov. Vicarious nucleophilic substitution of hydrogen (VNS) in 1,4-naphthoquinone derivatives—competition between VNS and vinylic nucleophilic substitution (SNV). Tetrahedron 57(47), 9615-9621 (2001). https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(01)00962-0

[doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(01)00962-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(01)00962-0)

## Chemical contribution

Strongly basic tert-BuOK/THF conditions gave negative results and were associated with decomposition of the putative VNS products, whereas DBU enabled dimethyl chloromalonate and ethyl 2-chloroacetoacetate to react smoothly with substrate 1a to give the expected VNS products in good yields. The authors proposed a two-step substitution pathway involving initial SNV formation of an intermediate, followed by cine substitution through carbon addition at the adjacent hydrogen-bearing position, proton transfer, chloride elimination, and base-induced beta-elimination.
