# L100 — Multiscale modeling of reaction rates: application to archetypal S N 2 nucleophilic substitutions

**SI reference 204; Tier 3.**

Jonathan Campeggio; Marco Bortoli; Laura Orian; Mirco Zerbetto; Antonino Polimeno. Multiscale modeling of reaction rates: application to archetypal S N 2 nucleophilic substitutions. Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 22(6), 3455-3465 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1039/c9cp03841h

[doi:10.1039/c9cp03841h](https://doi.org/10.1039/c9cp03841h)

## Chemical contribution

The sulfur-containing case study is the SN2 reaction of methylthiolate with dimethyl disulfide in water. The authors identify accurate treatment of specific solute-solvent interactions as the principal computational difficulty and note that a counterion may influence the IRC profile. The computed energy profile for the methylthiolate/dimethyl-disulfide reaction is approximately symmetric and has an activation energy of 16.1 kBT.
