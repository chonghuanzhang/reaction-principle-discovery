# L109 — Highly Enantioselective Synthesis of Monofluoroalkenes by Rhodium-Catalyzed Asymmetric Arylation/Defluorination of Allyl Difluorides

**Online reference 212; Tier 2.**

Yuhang Pan; Xiaosa Lu; Huayu Qiu; Tamio Hayashi; Yinhua Huang. Highly Enantioselective Synthesis of Monofluoroalkenes by Rhodium-Catalyzed Asymmetric Arylation/Defluorination of Allyl Difluorides. Organic Letters 22(21), 8413-8418 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c03044

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c03044](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c03044)

## Chemical contribution

In the reported optimization of 1a with 3-MeOC6H4B(OH)2, KOH gave only a trace of target product and most 1a underwent ester hydrolysis, whereas Cs2CO3 gave Z-3aa in 99% yield with Z-3aa/E-4aa selectivity >50:1. The authors propose syn arylrhodation of the alkene to form a beta,beta-difluoroalkylrhodium intermediate, followed by syn beta-fluoride elimination to form the monofluoroalkene and a Rh-F species, with transmetalation by the arylboronic acid regenerating the aryl-Rh species.

### L109-S002

### L109-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A series of arylboronic acids gave products in good to high yields, and meta- or para-substituted electron-deficient arylboronic acids generally improved Z/E selectivity; an ortho-methoxy arylboronic acid was insufficiently reactive.

Location: 3; Arylboronic-acid scope and steric/electronic effects

<a id="statement-l109-s004"></a>

### L109-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Catalyst-ligand and solvent choice were consequential: binap and segphos gave less than 5% yield, other chiral dienes gave lower yields and selectivity, and MTBE/water outperformed dioxane, THF, and toluene mixtures for the selected chiral catalyst.

Location: 2; Ligand and solvent optimization

<a id="statement-l109-s005"></a>

### L109-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A ketone-containing allyl difluoride gave product in 62% yield, greater than 99% ee, and 24:1 Z/E; a tertiary amide gave 21% yield, greater than 99% ee, and 1:1 Z/E, while a secondary amide did not react.

Location: 3; Ketone and amide scope boundaries

### L109-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The reported standard conditions use E-allyl difluoride, two equivalents of arylboronic acid, 5 mol% Rh as [RhCl((R,R)-Fc-tfb*)]2, three equivalents of Cs2CO3, MTBE/water, 80 °C, and 14 hours.

Location: 3; Scheme 2 standard reaction conditions

### L109-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Reactivity depended strongly on the group attached to the fluorinated carbon: the corresponding trifluoromethylalkene gave only trace product, and a substrate lacking a carbonyl group at that position was completely unreactive.

Location: 3; Functional-group dependence and activation boundary

### L109-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

E-Allyl difluorides containing a CF2COR2 unit react with arylboronic acids under chiral diene-rhodium catalysis to give chiral monofluoroalkenes in high yields, with greater than 99% ee in all reported cases and Z/E selectivity reported as high as 20:1.

Location: 1; Abstract reaction definition and headline performance

<a id="statement-l109-s009"></a>

### L109-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Isolated pure Z and E monofluoroalkene products did not isomerize under the reaction conditions or in the presence of NaOEt, supporting the conclusion that the observed Z and E products are formed kinetically rather than by post-reaction equilibration.

Location: 4; Product-isomerization control experiments
