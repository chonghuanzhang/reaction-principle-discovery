# L106 — Carbofluorination of Alkenes with gem-Difluorinated Cyclopropanes as Bifunctional Reagents Enabled by Well-Define Rhodium Catalysts

**SI reference 209; Tier 2.**

Yaxin Zeng; Zhong-Tao Jiang; Yulei Zhu; Jinqi Chen; Han Zhang; Ying Xia. Carbofluorination of Alkenes with gem-Difluorinated Cyclopropanes as Bifunctional Reagents Enabled by Well-Define Rhodium Catalysts. ChemRxiv (2023). https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2023-8mj8n

[doi:10.26434/chemrxiv-2023-8mj8n](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2023-8mj8n)

## Chemical contribution

Mechanistic probes observed both benzyl and tertiary alkyl fluorides from a rearrangement-capable alkene and found that only tetrafluoroborate counter-anion allowed the reaction to proceed; these observations support, but do not independently prove, carbocation involvement and a special role for BF4-. The authors propose oxidative addition of the gem-DFCP to rhodium, beta-fluoride elimination to a fluoroallyl-rhodium species, outer-sphere alkene attack to form a benzylic cation, and fluoride abstraction from BF4- to form product, with BF3/BF4- turnover; they explicitly characterize this mechanism as preliminary.
