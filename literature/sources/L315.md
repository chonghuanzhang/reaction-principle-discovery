# L315 — Organo-photoredox catalyzed gem -difluoroallylation of ketone-derived dihydroquinazolinones via C(sp 3 )–C bond and C(sp 3 )–F bond cleavage

**Online reference 383; Tier 2.**

Yue Zhang; Tianshuai Zhu; Yuqian Lin; Xian Wei; Xinyu Xie; Ruofan Lin; Zhijie Zhang; Weiwei Fang; Jing-Jing Zhang; Yue Zhang; Meng-Yang Hu; Lingchao Cai; Zhen Chen. Organo-photoredox catalyzed gem -difluoroallylation of ketone-derived dihydroquinazolinones via C(sp 3 )–C bond and C(sp 3 )–F bond cleavage. Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry 22(27), 5561-5568 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob00671b

[doi:10.1039/d4ob00671b](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob00671b)

## Chemical contribution

Ketone-derived dihydroquinazolinones bearing secondary, tertiary, primary, or heteroatom-stabilized alkyl residues furnished gem-difluoroalkenes in 27-99% yields, and cyclic-ketone-derived substrates also reacted in moderate yields. The authors propose SET oxidation and C-C cleavage of the dihydroquinazolinone to an alkyl radical, radical addition to the alpha-trifluoromethyl alkene, reduction to an alpha-CF3 carbanion, and beta-fluoride elimination to the gem-difluoroalkene; stabilization of the carbanion by a strong electron-withdrawing group is proposed to favor protonation instead.
