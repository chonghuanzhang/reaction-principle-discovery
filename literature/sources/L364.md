# L364 — Nickel-catalysed chelation-assisted reductive defluorinative sulfenylation of trifluoropropionic acid derivatives

**SI reference 13; Tier 1.**

Yu-Qiu Guan; Jia-Fan Qiao; Yu-Feng Liang. Nickel-catalysed chelation-assisted reductive defluorinative sulfenylation of trifluoropropionic acid derivatives. Chemical Communications 60(17), 2405-2408 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1039/d3cc06041a

[doi:10.1039/d3cc06041a](https://doi.org/10.1039/d3cc06041a)

## Chemical contribution

The author abstract reports Ni-catalysed chelation-assisted reductive coupling of trifluoropropanamides or esters with disulfides, cleaving all three C–F bonds and producing thioacrylamides or esters with high E selectivity. It defines a successful complete-defluorination endpoint within the relevant substrate family. This supports explicit fluorine-count accounting and comparison of donor/catalyst regimes, but it cannot establish formation of the project’s monofluorinated target or be labelled a failed literature reaction.
