# L258 — Direct relationship between nucleophilicity and basicity in SN2 reactions of fluorenyl anions with benzyl chloride in dimethyl sulfoxide solution

**SI reference 333; Tier 3.**

Frederick G. Bordwell; David L. Hughes. Direct relationship between nucleophilicity and basicity in SN2 reactions of fluorenyl anions with benzyl chloride in dimethyl sulfoxide solution. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 45(16), 3314-3320 (1980). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo01304a035

[doi:10.1021/jo01304a035](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo01304a035)

## Chemical contribution

At 25 °C in Me2SO, the tabulated conjugate-acid pKa values span 8.3 to 24.35 and the second-order rate constants for benzyl chloride vary substantially; the 9-tert-butyl anion is much slower than less basic members despite its high basicity. The authors propose that the solvent rate order reflects both stabilization of the fluorenyl anion and stabilization of incipient chloride in the SN2 transition state.
