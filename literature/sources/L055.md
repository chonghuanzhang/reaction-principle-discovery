# L055 — Difluoroenol Phosphinates as Versatile Difluoroenolate Surrogates: Synthesis and Applications in Defluorination and Deoxygenative Coupling

**SI reference 1; Tier 2.**

Miki B. Kurosawa; Shuhei Shimoyama; Hiroki Tanaka; Kenta Kato; Junichiro Yamaguchi. Difluoroenol Phosphinates as Versatile Difluoroenolate Surrogates: Synthesis and Applications in Defluorination and Deoxygenative Coupling. ChemRxiv (2025). https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-1rklr

[doi:10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-1rklr](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-1rklr)

## Chemical contribution

Separately prepared addition product 5A did not form 3A when treated with potassium carbonate at 80 °C, excluding 5A as a productive intermediate under that test. The authors propose Pudovik addition, phospha-Brook rearrangement, and beta-fluoride elimination to form an enol phosphinate and HF; they further propose that HF trapping favors the phosphinate while protonation without a trapping agent gives the difluoromethyl ketone.
