# L053 — Electrophilic Reactivities of 1,2‐Diaza‐1,3‐dienes

**SI reference 166; Tier 3.**

Tanja Kanzian; Simona Nicolini; Lucia De Crescentini; Orazio A. Attanasi; Armin R. Ofial; Herbert Mayr. Electrophilic Reactivities of 1,2‐Diaza‐1,3‐dienes. Chemistry – A European Journal 16(39), 12008-12016 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201000828

[doi:10.1002/chem.201000828](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201000828)

## Chemical contribution

Linear dependence of observed rate constants on carbanion concentration was found for all studied diazadiene/carbanion reactions; the authors identified nucleophilic attack as rate limiting and obtained second-order rate constants from the slopes. The study measured reactions of 1,2-diaza-1,3-dienes with acceptor-substituted carbanions at 20 °C, found second-order kinetics, and derived electrophilicity parameters using the linear free-energy relationship log k = s(N+E).
