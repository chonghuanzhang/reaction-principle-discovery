# L271 — Nickel-Catalyzed Carbonylation of Secondary Trifluoromethylated, Difluoromethylated, and Nonfluorinated Aliphatic Electrophiles with Arylboronic Acids under 1 atm of CO

**Online reference 344; Tier 1.**

Ran Cheng; Hai-Yang Zhao; Shu Zhang; Xingang Zhang. Nickel-Catalyzed Carbonylation of Secondary Trifluoromethylated, Difluoromethylated, and Nonfluorinated Aliphatic Electrophiles with Arylboronic Acids under 1 atm of CO. ACS Catalysis 10(1), 36-42 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.9b04038

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.9b04038](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.9b04038)

## Chemical contribution

Both electron-rich and electron-deficient arylboronic acids reacted, and the reported partner scope tolerated amine, morpholine, thioether, ester, enolizable ketone, silyl, furan, thiophene, dibenzofuran, and ortho-methyl substitution. The authors proposed that transmetalation could generate a nickel-nucleophile species, followed by CO insertion and formal oxidative addition of the trifluoromethylated alkyl iodide; reductive elimination from the resulting high-valent nickel species was proposed to produce the product while suppressing beta-fluoride elimination.

### L271-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In stoichiometric experiments, an arylnickel complex reacted with CO to give an acylnickel complex in 71% yield, and treatment of that complex with the trifluoromethylated secondary alkyl iodide afforded ketone 3a in 52% yield without observation of the beta-fluoride-elimination byproduct.

Location: 2; Stoichiometric support for an acylnickel intermediate and fluorine retention
