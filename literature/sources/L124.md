# L124 — Chemo‐, Regio‐, and Stereoselective Diels–Alder Reaction of Ambident Dienophilic Monothiomaleimide

**SI reference 227; Tier 3.**

Yoshinao Tamaru; Hiroto Harayama; Hiroya Sakata; Hironobu Konishi; Keigo Fugami; Masanari Kimura; Shuji Tanaka; Toshiya Okajima; Yoshimasa Fukazawa. Chemo‐, Regio‐, and Stereoselective Diels–Alder Reaction of Ambident Dienophilic Monothiomaleimide. Liebigs Annalen 1997(5), 907-923 (1997). https://doi.org/10.1002/jlac.199719970520

[doi:10.1002/jlac.199719970520](https://doi.org/10.1002/jlac.199719970520)

## Chemical contribution

Parent 1,3-butadiene and most donor-substituted dienes produced comparable amounts of C=S and C=C adducts, whereas conjugated or electron-withdrawing dienes strongly favored C=S addition; 1,3-cyclohexadiene was an exception that gave only the C=C adduct. The authors attribute exclusive C=S addition by the C-4 methyl-substituted dienophile to steric hindrance of diene approach to its C=C bond, while stating that the source of its high endo selectivity is unclear.
