# L083 — Formation of Quaternary Carbon Center with a Trifluoromethyl Group Using a Pd-Catalyzed Allylation Reaction

**Online reference 190; Tier 1.**

Kazuyuki Sato; Yu Takiguchi; Yosuke Yoshizawa; Kayoko Iwase; Yoshie Shimizu; Atsushi Tarui; Masaaki Omote; Itsumaro Kumadaki; Akira Ando. Formation of Quaternary Carbon Center with a Trifluoromethyl Group Using a Pd-Catalyzed Allylation Reaction. Chemical and Pharmaceutical Bulletin 55(11), 1593-1596 (2007). https://doi.org/10.1248/cpb.55.1593

[doi:10.1248/cpb.55.1593](https://doi.org/10.1248/cpb.55.1593)

## Chemical contribution

Pd(OAc)2 with PPh3 also furnished the product in good yield, whereas the supplied table reports no reaction for PdCl2, PdCl2/PPh3, Pd(OAc)2 alone, or PPh3 alone under their respective tested conditions. The authors proposed a catalytic sequence involving oxidative addition of palladium into the allyl carbonate, decarboxylation to a pi-allyl alkoxy palladium complex, deprotonation of ketone 3, formation of intermediate 9, and reductive elimination to product 5 with regeneration of Pd(0).

### L083-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

An alpha-trifluoromethyl ketone reacted with allyl ethyl carbonate under palladium catalysis at room temperature to form the allylated quaternary-carbon product in excellent yield.
