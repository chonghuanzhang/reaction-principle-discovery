# L080 — Highly Stereoselective Intramolecular Carbofluorination of Internal α,β-Ynones Promoted by Selectfluor

**Online reference 187; Tier 1.**

Bingbin Zhu; Hang Han; Wei-Ke Su; Boan Yan; Zhi Li; Chuanming Yu; Xinpeng Jiang. Highly Stereoselective Intramolecular Carbofluorination of Internal α,β-Ynones Promoted by Selectfluor. Organic Letters 23(11), 4488-4492 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c01441

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.1c01441](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c01441)

## Chemical contribution

Under the optimized conditions, model substrate 1a gave product 2a in 90% yield with a 99:1 E/Z ratio; the table specifies Selectfluor, water, anhydrous DCE, 110 °C, nitrogen, and 10 h for this entry. The authors propose electrostatic association of Selectfluor with the ketone, stereodifferentiated tetrafluoroborate fluoride transfer assisted by aldehyde-oxygen chelation, formation of a zwitterionic intermediate by Michael addition, intramolecular cyclization, and final hydrolysis.
