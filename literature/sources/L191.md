# L191 — Mechanochemical Defluorinative Arylation of Trifluoroacetamides: An Entry to Aromatic Amides

**SI reference 275; Tier 2.**

Satenik Mkrtchyan; Mohanad Shkoor; Mandalaparthi Phanindrudu; Miroslav Medved′; Olena Sevastyanova; Viktor O. Iaroshenko. Mechanochemical Defluorinative Arylation of Trifluoroacetamides: An Entry to Aromatic Amides. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 88(2), 863-870 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02197

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02197](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02197)

## Chemical contribution

For the arylboronic-acid model reaction, PdCl2 gave a 72% isolated yield, DABCO outperformed K2CO3, CoCl2 failed, CuCl or CuCl2 sharply reduced yield, and NiBr2 with DABCO gave the highest isolated yield of 87%; Dy2O3 was the best lanthanide-oxide additive tested. The dimethyl(aryl)sulfonium model afforded the desired amide in 86% yield; replacing DABCO with Na2CO3 sharply decreased yield, and pinacol diborane was indispensable for both the iodonium and sulfonium routes.

### L191-S006
