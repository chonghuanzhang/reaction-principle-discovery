# L143 — Synthesis of internal fluorinated alkenes via facile aryloxylation of substituted phenols with aryl trifluorovinyl ethers

**Online reference 43; Tier 2.**

Justin D. Moody; Don VanDerveer; Dennis W. Smith Jr.; Scott T. Iacono. Synthesis of internal fluorinated alkenes via facile aryloxylation of substituted phenols with aryl trifluorovinyl ethers. Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry 9(13), 4842–4849 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1039/c1ob05041a

[doi:10.1039/c1ob05041a](https://doi.org/10.1039/c1ob05041a)

## Chemical contribution

The isolated sodium ethoxide reaction used approximately equimolar sodium ethoxide and aryl TFVE 1 and afforded (Z)/(E)-11 in 86% isolated yield. A literature-inspired Pd-catalyzed strategy was attempted for the phenol/aryl-TFVE addition but produced only unreacted starting materials rather than hydrofluorinated product 10. Attempts to trap the proposed carbanion with D2O or CF3SO2Cl, including trials at −78 °C in THF or diethyl ether, did not furnish trapped products; the observed products were the vinyl ether and a trace hydrofluorinated adduct.
