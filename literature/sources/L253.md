# L253 — Nucleophilic additions to a para-benzyne derived from an enediyne: Exploring the non-radical reactivity of a diradical

**SI reference 328; Tier 3.**

Gabriel J. Reyes-Rodríguez. Nucleophilic additions to a para-benzyne derived from an enediyne: Exploring the non-radical reactivity of a diradical. Doctoral thesis, University of California, San Diego (2013). Repository/dissertation record UMI 3605959.

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

GC-MS gave a mass matching the expected diiodo byproduct. In an experiment with added I2 and Bu4NI, 1,4-diiodotetrahydronaphthalene was observed before heating at 14% conversion, and its yield was 91% after 10 hours of heating. The authors propose that reduced solvent polarity facilitates neutralization of intermediate 3 by decreasing its solvation, accounting for the approximately fivefold greater reactivity of CH3CN than DMSO.
