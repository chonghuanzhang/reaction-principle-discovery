# L190 — Branched-Selective Cross-Electrophile Coupling of 2-Alkyl Aziridines and (Hetero)aryl Iodides Using Ti/Ni Catalysis

**Online reference 274; Tier 4.**

Wendy L. Williams; Neyci E. Gutiérrez-Valencia; Abigail G. Doyle. Branched-Selective Cross-Electrophile Coupling of 2-Alkyl Aziridines and (Hetero)aryl Iodides Using Ti/Ni Catalysis. Journal of the American Chemical Society 145(44), 24175-24183 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c08301

[doi:10.1021/jacs.3c08301](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c08301)

## Chemical contribution

Control experiments established that omission of Ni and ligand prevented C-C coupling and produced reductive ring-opened products, while omission of Ti gave only 32% aziridine conversion and trace cross-coupled product. The authors propose coupled Ti and Ni cycles in which TiIII coordinates the aziridine and enables reversible radical ring-opening, the resulting radical adds to an arylnickel(II) intermediate, and radical addition to Ni or reductive elimination is likely the regioselectivity-determining step.

### L190-S004

### L190-S009

TiIII-mediated reductive ring-opening produced both branched and linear ring-opened products, and changing to a larger alkyl substituent gave a similar ratio, indicating that radical ring-opening itself is feasible for both regioisomers and does not explain the substrate-size selectivity trend.
