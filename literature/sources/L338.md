# L338 — Stereoselective synthesis of iodofluoroalkenes by iodofluorination of alkynes using IF5-pyridine-HF

**Online reference 401; Tier 1.**

Hitoshi Ukigai; Shoji Hara. Stereoselective synthesis of iodofluoroalkenes by iodofluorination of alkynes using IF5-pyridine-HF. Tetrahedron Letters 57(12), 1379-1381 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2016.02.063

[doi:10.1016/j.tetlet.2016.02.063](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2016.02.063)

## Chemical contribution

Reductant choice strongly affected iodofluorination selectivity: KI produced no desired 2a and instead gave diiodoalkene 3a and iodoalkyne 4a, whereas catechol and hydroquinone suppressed those reported byproducts and gave 2a in 75% and 82% NMR yield, respectively. The authors propose that hydroquinone reduces IF5-pyridine-HF to an IF species that adds to the alkyne, but state that the actual active species responsible for selectivity cannot be identified because the IF species is unstable in solution.
