# L078 — HP(O)Ph 2 -Mediated Hydrodefluorination of Polyfluoro(hetero)arenes

**Online reference 185; Tier 2.**

Xue-Ying Huang; Xian-Xian Mao; Chenshu Dai; Ming-Yang Gu; Danhua Ge; Zhi-Liang Shen; Kai Guo; Chao-Jun Li; Xue-Qiang Chu. HP(O)Ph 2 -Mediated Hydrodefluorination of Polyfluoro(hetero)arenes. JACS Au 6(3), 1602-1616 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.5c01460

[doi:10.1021/jacsau.5c01460](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.5c01460)

## Chemical contribution

PPh3, H3PO2 and NaH2PO2 each gave 0% target product in the same hydrodefluorination window, while hydroxy by-products were observed. An attempted catalytic variant using 10 mol% HP(O)Ph2 and PhSiH3 gave only 14% product and substantial substrate decomposition. Simple monofluoroarenes represented by 3-fluorobenzonitrile and 1-fluoro-4-methoxybenzene failed to react, defining specific reagent and substrate boundaries in that reported system.
