# L348 — Synthesis of N-(α-Fluorovinyl)azoles by the Reaction of Difluoroalkenes with Azoles

**SI reference 63; Tier 1.**

Yang Xiong; Xuxue Zhang; Tao Huang; Song Cao. Synthesis of N-(α-Fluorovinyl)azoles by the Reaction of Difluoroalkenes with Azoles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 79(14), 6395-6402 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo5005845

[doi:10.1021/jo5005845](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo5005845)

## Chemical contribution

K3PO4-promoted reaction of (2,2-difluorovinyl)arenes with N–H heterocycles at 25 °C gave N-(α-fluorovinyl)azoles. The record contributes a direct precedent for an azole nitrogen donor and a monofluorovinyl–N product class after substitution at a gem-difluoroalkene. It supports considering N–H heterocycles as potential partners while keeping alkene activation separate from the preceding steps required to access such an intermediate from a trifluoroethyl carbonyl compound.
