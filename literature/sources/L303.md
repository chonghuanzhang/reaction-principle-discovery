# L303 — Mechanistic Studies of Palladium-Catalyzed Aminocarbonylation of Aryl Chlorides with Carbon Monoxide and Ammonia

**Online reference 372; Tier 4.**

Justin Y. Wang; Alexandra E. Strom; John F. Hartwig. Mechanistic Studies of Palladium-Catalyzed Aminocarbonylation of Aryl Chlorides with Carbon Monoxide and Ammonia. Journal of the American Chemical Society 140(25), 7979-7993 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.8b04073

[doi:10.1021/jacs.8b04073](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.8b04073)

## Chemical contribution

Oxidative addition of aryl chlorides and bromides was determined to proceed through rate-limiting reaction of the haloarene with a three-coordinate Pd(0) species bearing a bidentate phosphine and one CO ligand. Arylpalladium and phenacylpalladium halide intermediates were synthesized, and their formation and reactions were studied kinetically. A primary carbon-13 kinetic isotope effect was interpreted as suggesting carbon-halogen bond cleavage during the rate-limiting oxidative-addition step.
