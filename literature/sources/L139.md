# L139 — 4‐Dimethylaminopyridine‐Boryl Radical Promoted Monodefluorinative Alkylation of 3,3‐Difluorooxindoles

**SI reference 240; Tier 2.**

Tesfaye Tebeka Simur; Fentahun Wondu Dagnaw; You‐Jie Yu; Feng‐Lian Zhang; Yi‐Feng Wang. 4‐Dimethylaminopyridine‐Boryl Radical Promoted Monodefluorinative Alkylation of 3,3‐Difluorooxindoles. Chinese Journal of Chemistry 40(5), 577-581 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.202100784

[doi:10.1002/cjoc.202100784](https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.202100784)

## Chemical contribution

Reaction performance depends strongly on the Lewis base-borane complex, additive, and radical initiator: 4-cyanopyridine-BH3 and NHC-BH3 did not promote product formation, water reduced efficiency, KH2PO4 plus water gave only trace product, omission of additive produced a complex reaction, and omission of radical initiator gave no reaction. The authors propose that the high activation energy for DMAP-boryl-radical addition to α-monofluoroamides, attributed to an increased LUMO-SOMO gap, accounts for exclusive monodefluorination.
