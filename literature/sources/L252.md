# L252 — Reaction Pathways in Carbonates and Esters

**SI reference 327; Tier 3.**

Pietro Tundo; Fabio Aricò. Reaction Pathways in Carbonates and Esters. ChemSusChem 16(23), e202300748 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/cssc.202300748

[doi:10.1002/cssc.202300748](https://doi.org/10.1002/cssc.202300748)

## Chemical contribution

Dimethyl carbonate methylates aryl and alkyl thiols under several mildly basic process modes, while aliphatic alcohols instead form carboxymethyl derivatives; acidic NaY enables chemoselective S-methylation in substrates containing both OH and SH groups. The authors propose a theoretical pilot model in which BAc2 and BAl2 activation energies are closer for dialkyl carbonates than for esters, while acknowledging that comprehensive carbonate-reactivity and thermodynamic data are lacking.
