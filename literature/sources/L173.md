# L173 — Modular, Concise, and Efficient Synthesis of Highly Functionalized 5-Fluoropyridazines by a [2 + 1]/[3 + 2]-Cycloaddition Sequence

**Online reference 262; Tier 1.**

Gaël Tran; Domingo Gomez Pardo; Tomoki Tsuchiya; Stefan Hillebrand; Jean-Pierre Vors; Janine Cossy. Modular, Concise, and Efficient Synthesis of Highly Functionalized 5-Fluoropyridazines by a [2 + 1]/[3 + 2]-Cycloaddition Sequence. Organic Letters 17(14), 3414-3417 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5b01370

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5b01370](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5b01370)

## Chemical contribution

A meta-CF3 aryl alkyne gave a modest 33% yield, while a para-NMe2 aryl alkyne underwent only degradation. Lewis-basic heteroaromatic alkynes presented a compatibility boundary with difluorocarbene: 2-ethynylpyridine gave a low yield, and 3-ethynylpyridine underwent only degradation during difluorocyclopropenation. The authors proposed that a dihydropyridazine intermediate would spontaneously aromatize by HF elimination and that the fused cyclopropanopyrazoline would readily expand because distal fluorine atoms weaken the fused bond.
