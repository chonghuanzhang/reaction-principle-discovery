# L114 — Sulfonyl Fluoride Synthesis through Electrochemical Oxidative Coupling of Thiols and Potassium Fluoride

**Online reference 217; Tier 4.**

Gabriele Laudadio; Aloisio de A. Bartolomeu; Lucas M. H. M. Verwijlen; Yiran Cao; Kleber T. de Oliveira; Timothy Noël. Sulfonyl Fluoride Synthesis through Electrochemical Oxidative Coupling of Thiols and Potassium Fluoride. Journal of the American Chemical Society 141(30), 11832-11836 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b06126

[doi:10.1021/jacs.9b06126](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.9b06126)

## Chemical contribution

The optimized representative reaction gave the targeted sulfonyl fluoride in 74% isolated yield using KF, pyridine, a biphasic acetonitrile/1 M HCl medium, and graphite/stainless-steel electrodes. A 1-fluoro-pyridinium reagent produced only traces of product, whereas KF combined with either HCl-pyridine or HCl-Et3N gave sulfonyl fluoride in good yields; the authors interpreted this as evidence for nucleophilic fluorination. The authors tentatively assigned observed fluorinated species as sulfenyl fluoride and sulfinyl fluoride intermediates; these species were not isolated.
