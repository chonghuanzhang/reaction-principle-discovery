# L018 — Kinetics of hydride-transfer reactions from hydrosilanes to carbenium ions. Substituent effects in silicenium ions

**SI reference 135; Tier 3.**

Herbert Mayr; Nils Basso; Gisela Hagen. Kinetics of hydride-transfer reactions from hydrosilanes to carbenium ions. Substituent effects in silicenium ions. Journal of the American Chemical Society 114(8), 3060-3066 (1992). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00034a044

[doi:10.1021/ja00034a044](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00034a044)

## Chemical contribution

An amino-substituted silane did not provide a measurable hydride-transfer rate under the tested conditions: addition of HSiMe2(NMe2) to An2CH+OTf at -70 °C instead produced amino-group transfer. The authors attribute the BCl3-associated acceleration to rapid formation of HBCl2, proposed to act as an efficient hydride donor in the presence of BCl3; boron NMR observations are described as supporting this interpretation.

### L018-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Hydrosilane structure strongly affected hydride-transfer rates: successive replacement of hydrogen by hexyl increased reactivity by about two orders of magnitude per hexyl group, whereas the analogous phenyl substitutions increased reactivity by about one order of magnitude, leaving trihexylsilane almost two orders of magnitude more reactive than triphenylsilane.

Location: 3; Variation of silanes; comparison of alkyl and phenyl substituent effects
