# L075 — Kinetic and mechanistic studies of the reactions between 2-nitrothiazole, 2-nitrobenzothiazole and some nucleophiles

**Online reference 48; Tier 3.**

Luciano Forlani. Kinetic and mechanistic studies of the reactions between 2-nitrothiazole, 2-nitrobenzothiazole and some nucleophiles. Journal of the Chemical Society, Perkin Transactions 2 (11), 1699–1702 (1991). https://doi.org/10.1039/p29910001699

[doi:10.1039/p29910001699](https://doi.org/10.1039/p29910001699)

## Chemical contribution

2-Nitrothiazole and 2-nitrobenzothiazole react with methoxide, tert-butoxide, and piperidine to produce the corresponding 2-alkoxy or 2-N-piperidyl heterocycles in almost quantitative yields. No anionic or zwitterionic complexes were detected during reactions of 2-nitrothiazoles, even at high initial alkoxide concentration; Meisenheimer adducts were observed instead for 5-nitrothiazole derivatives. The authors propose that the piperidine rate enhancement in weakly polar solvent arises from a substrate–nucleophile molecular complex that is more reactive than free substrate.
