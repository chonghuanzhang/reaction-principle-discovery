# L102 — Modular Synthesis of a Semibuckminsterfullerene

**Online reference 206; Tier 1.**

Cody F. Dickinson; Justin K. Yang; Glenn P. A. Yap; Marcus A. Tius. Modular Synthesis of a Semibuckminsterfullerene. Organic Letters 24(28), 5095-5098 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c01880

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.2c01880](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c01880)

## Chemical contribution

Octabromide 30 was generated by benzylic bromination and used without purification; refluxing it with aqueous sodium hydroxide in dioxane for 1.5 hours afforded semibuckminsterfullerene 31 in 40-60% overall yield across the two steps. The alumina-mediated C-F activation procedure failed for dibenzochrysenes bearing phenolic hydroxyl or phenolic methyl ether groups; the authors attribute this failure to preferential interaction of oxygen with the alumina surface, inhibiting C-C bond formation.
