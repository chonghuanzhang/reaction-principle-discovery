# L205 — Reactivities of allenic and olefinic Michael acceptors towards phosphines

**SI reference 54; Tier 3.**

Feng An; Harish Jangra; Yin Wei; Min Shi; Hendrik Zipse; Armin R. Ofial. Reactivities of allenic and olefinic Michael acceptors towards phosphines. Chemical Communications 58(20), 3358-3361 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1039/d1cc06786a

[doi:10.1039/d1cc06786a](https://doi.org/10.1039/d1cc06786a)

## Chemical contribution

Allenoate substitution substantially affected phosphine-addition rates: a tert-butyl ester reduced reactivity by a factor of 3 relative to the ethyl ester, a 2-substituent reduced reactivity by a factor of 32 for 1j relative to 1f, and an electron-accepting group on that substituent partially restored reactivity. Carbon-phosphorus bond formation between phosphines and allenoates 1a-1j was monitored spectroscopically; vinyl phosphonium triflates were spectroscopically characterized for the subset 1a, 1d, 1f, and 1i.
