# L290 — Titanium‐Catalyzed C–F Activation of Fluoroalkenes

**Online reference 361; Tier 2.**

Moritz F. Kühnel; Dieter Lentz. Titanium‐Catalyzed C–F Activation of Fluoroalkenes. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 49(16), 2933-2936 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200907162

[doi:10.1002/anie.200907162](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200907162)

## Chemical contribution

Changing the solvent from diglyme to toluene strongly suppressed the hexafluoropropene reaction under the reported conditions, giving only traces of products, 76% recovered substrate, and TON below 1. Hydrodefluorination of 3,3,3-trifluoropropene was substantially less selective and slower, producing hydrogenation product and secondary hydrodefluorination products before complete starting-material consumption. The authors attribute the hydrogenation side product to hydrogen generated by competing silane dehydrocoupling catalyzed by titanocene difluoride.
