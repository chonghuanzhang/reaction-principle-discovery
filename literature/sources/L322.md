# L322 — Fluoroolefins-Mediated Deoxygenative Substitution of Alcohols: Chemo- and Enantiospecific Construction of C–S, C–N, C–O, and C–Se Bonds

**SI reference 388; Tier 2.**

Hengyuan Li; Linqian Qin; Jia Nie; Huanfeng Jiang; Chuanle Zhu. Fluoroolefins-Mediated Deoxygenative Substitution of Alcohols: Chemo- and Enantiospecific Construction of C–S, C–N, C–O, and C–Se Bonds. Organic Letters 25(29), 5431-5436 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01713

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01713](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01713)

## Chemical contribution

Isolated O-tethered monofluoroalkenes 7 and 9 reacted with thiol 3a to produce thioether 4a in 89% and 96% yield, respectively, with 99% ee, directly supporting their competence as substitution intermediates. Both base and fluoroolefin activator were required: no reaction occurred when either component was omitted. The authors propose that defluorinative reaction of the alcohol with the fluoroolefin forms O-tethered monofluoroalkene A, followed by SN2 substitution with inversion of configuration.
