# L117 — A new convergent route to 1-substituted ellipticines

**Online reference 220; Tier 1.**

F. Marsais; P. Pineau; F. Nivolliers; M. Mallet; A. Turck; A. Godard; G. Queguiner. A new convergent route to 1-substituted ellipticines. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 57(2), 565-573 (1992). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00028a032

[doi:10.1021/jo00028a032](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00028a032)

## Chemical contribution

The lithio derivative formed from bromopyridine 35 or 36 failed to react effectively with acetaldehyde and instead mainly gave debrominated product in 70% yield. The reported successful C-C-forming step is a palladium(0)-catalyzed cross-coupling of bromopyridine 35 with tributylvinylstannanes, producing 4-vinylpyridines 40 and 41 in high yields under refluxing toluene. The authors attribute the failure of alkynyl coupling on compound 35 to steric hindrance associated with the linear coupling moiety and regard the successful vinylstannane coupling as consistent with that explanation.
