# L312 — Quantitative Structure–Reactivity Relationships for Synthesis Planning: The Benzhydrylium Case

**Online reference 381; Tier 3.**

Maike Eckhoff; Johannes V. Diedrich; Maike Mücke; Jonny Proppe. Quantitative Structure–Reactivity Relationships for Synthesis Planning: The Benzhydrylium Case. The Journal of Physical Chemistry A 128(1), 343-354 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.3c07289

[doi:10.1021/acs.jpca.3c07289](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.3c07289)

## Chemical contribution

For olefin–benzhydrylium reactions, the authors report that experimental rate constants have average deviations below one order of magnitude, whereas standard DFT calculations can deviate by one to two orders of magnitude at 20 °C. The authors hypothesized that the examined structural descriptors were too high-dimensional to map directly onto the limited available reactivity data and therefore proposed a two-step QSRR workflow using quantum molecular properties as surrogate quantities.
