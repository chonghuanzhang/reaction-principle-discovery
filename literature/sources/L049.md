# L049 — Bird’s-Eye View: Current Understanding and Future Perspectives on the Biodefluorination of Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS)

**SI reference 163; Tier 2.**

Shun Che; Huaqing Liu; Shucai Zhang; Yaochun Yu. Bird’s-Eye View: Current Understanding and Future Perspectives on the Biodefluorination of Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS). Water Research X 28, 100356 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wroa.2025.100356

[doi:10.1016/j.wroa.2025.100356](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wroa.2025.100356)

## Chemical contribution

Unsaturated perfluorinated compounds showed no aerobic biotransformation in the cited activated-sludge incubation, whereas unsaturated polyfluorinated carboxylic acids showed structure-dependent biodefluorination. In chlorinated PFAS, hydrolytic dechlorination produced fluoroalcohol moieties that underwent spontaneous HF elimination, and defluorination increased from below 10% for CTFE2 to approximately 80% for CTFE4 as chlorine substitution increased. The authors present alpha-carbon C-H substitution as a hypothesized structural requirement for the initial enzymatic step in aerobic defluorination.
