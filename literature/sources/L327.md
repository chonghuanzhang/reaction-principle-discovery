# L327 — Nucleophilicity of Glutathione: A Link to Michael Acceptor Reactivities

**SI reference 392; Tier 3.**

Robert J. Mayer; Armin R. Ofial. Nucleophilicity of Glutathione: A Link to Michael Acceptor Reactivities. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 58(49), 17704-17708 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201909803

[doi:10.1002/anie.201909803](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201909803)

## Chemical contribution

For each reference electrophile E2-E10, glutathione and the less functionalized cysteine derivatives AcCys or Cys had rate constants agreeing within a factor of 2.5, which the authors interpreted as detection of thiolate reactivity. S-benzhydrylation was observed for GSH with E1, while competing attack by the gamma-glutamyl amino group was not detected; the cited rate ratio for sulfur versus amino attack on acrylonitrile was greater than 100:1.

### L327-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

At the stated pH, the thiol groups of glutathione and N-acetylcysteine were more than 99% deprotonated; the resulting thiolates added directly to cationic electrophiles E2-E4 or underwent conjugate addition to neutral Michael acceptors E5-E10.
