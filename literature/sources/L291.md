# L291 — Morpholine-mediated defluorinative cycloaddition of gem -difluoroalkenes and organic azides

**SI reference 39; Tier 2.**

Tzu-Yu Huang; Mario Djugovski; Sweta Adhikari; Destinee L Manning; Sudeshna Roy. Morpholine-mediated defluorinative cycloaddition of gem -difluoroalkenes and organic azides. Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry 19, 1545-1554 (2023). https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.19.111

[doi:10.3762/bjoc.19.111](https://doi.org/10.3762/bjoc.19.111)

## Chemical contribution

A transition-metal catalyst was unnecessary: the catalyst-free reaction gave 31% yield at 50 °C and 70% yield after increasing the temperature to 75 °C. Organic azide thermal decomposition to anilines was the only observed byproduct class and reduced isolated yield; no other byproducts were detected by TLC or crude 1H NMR. The authors propose initial base-mediated nucleophilic addition–elimination of morpholine to form INT-1, followed either by an aminoalkyne cycloaddition pathway or by vinylic azide substitution, cyclization, and aromatization.
