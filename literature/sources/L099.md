# L099 — Inhibition of (dppf)nickel-catalysed Suzuki–Miyaura cross-coupling reactions by α-halo-N-heterocycles

**Online reference 203; Tier 4.**

Alasdair K. Cooper; Megan E. Greaves; William Donohoe; Paul M. Burton; Thomas O. Ronson; Alan R. Kennedy; David J. Nelson. Inhibition of (dppf)nickel-catalysed Suzuki–Miyaura cross-coupling reactions by α-halo-N-heterocycles. Chemical Science 12(42), 14074-14082 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1039/d1sc04582b

[doi:10.1039/d1sc04582b](https://doi.org/10.1039/d1sc04582b)

## Chemical contribution

Complex 3-Cl did not react with excess phenylboronic acid and potassium phosphate and was recovered unchanged after treatment with PhSn(n-Bu)3. Alternative nickel precatalysts bearing PPh3, PCy3, dppe, or XantPhos produced less than 2% yield of 2-phenylpyridine in attempted Suzuki–Miyaura coupling of 2-chloropyridine. The authors propose that the butterfly complex can re-enter catalysis only if it transmetalates with a sufficiently nucleophilic organometallic reagent.
