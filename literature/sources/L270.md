# L270 — Rhodium-Catalyzed C(sp2)–O Cross Couplings of Diazo Quinones with Phenols to Construct Diaryl Ethers

**Online reference 343; Tier 1.**

Zhen Fu; XiaoKun Wang; Xiaoyu Ren; Zhen Guo; Chengming Wang; Cong-Ying Zhou. Rhodium-Catalyzed C(sp2)–O Cross Couplings of Diazo Quinones with Phenols to Construct Diaryl Ethers. Organic Letters 26(1), 292-297 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c03969

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c03969](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c03969)

## Chemical contribution

Ortho-diazo quinones did not undergo the cross-coupling reaction, whereas diazonaphthoquinone analogues were reactive and gave diaryl ethers in moderate to high yield. For the model reaction, Rh2(esp)2 was the most effective tested catalyst; DCM at 40 °C gave 89% NMR yield, whereas room temperature gave 77%. The proposed mechanism invokes Rh2(esp)2-mediated diazo quinone decomposition to a Rh(II)-quinoid carbene, followed by either O–H insertion or oxonium-ylide formation, proton transfer, aromatization, diaryl ether formation, and catalyst regeneration.
