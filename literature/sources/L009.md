# L009 — Three-Component Bisannulation for the Synthesis of Trifluoromethylated Tetracyclic Aza-Aromatics through Six C(sp3)–F Bond Cleavage and Four C–N Bond Formation

**Online reference 127; Tier 1.**

Qing-Dong Wang; Ya-Wen Wang; Ting Xie; Yan-Ying Cui; Mengtao Ma; Zhi-Liang Shen; Xue-Qiang Chu. Three-Component Bisannulation for the Synthesis of Trifluoromethylated Tetracyclic Aza-Aromatics through Six C(sp3)–F Bond Cleavage and Four C–N Bond Formation. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 86(12), 8236-8247 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c00695

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.1c00695](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.1c00695)

## Chemical contribution

Aryl benzamidines bearing electron-donating or electron-withdrawing substituents gave products in 45-87% yield with high regiocontrol, and OMe, F, Cl, Br, and CF3 substituents were tolerated; a heteroaryl amidine gave product in 45% yield. The authors suggest that fluoroenone 6 may be a key intermediate and propose a sequence involving base-promoted successive defluorination, amidine condensation, intramolecular defluorinative amination, a second amidine condensation, and nucleophilic substitution with further C-F cleavage; an HF-elimination/electrocyclization pathway is offered as an alternative.
