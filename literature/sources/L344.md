# L344 — NiH‐Catalyzed Enantio‐ and Regioselective Hydroselenylation of Unactivated Alkenes

**Online reference 6; Tier 4.**

Hyung‐Joon Kang; Jaehun Kim; Sungwoo Hong. NiH‐Catalyzed Enantio‐ and Regioselective Hydroselenylation of Unactivated Alkenes. Advanced Science, e77070 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77070

[doi:10.1002/advs.77070](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77070)

## Chemical contribution

PhSeH gives no desired β-selenide in NiH-catalysed hydroselenylation of nonfluorinated N-phenylbut-3-enamide, whereas PhSeSePh gives 91% 1H NMR yield. Removing BnBr lowers the yield to 11%. The authors interpret these effects through selenium-source activation and interception of strongly coordinating selenolate. This is direct donor- and additive-dependent evidence within the reported NiH system, without a calibrated zero yield for PhSeH. It supports a compatibility question for the target reaction, not a universal Se exclusion or a direct test of RCOCH2CF3.
