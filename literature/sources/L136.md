# L136 — Radical Additions of TEMPO to Ketenes: Correlation of Free Radical and Nucleophilic Reactivity

**Online reference 237; Tier 3.**

Annette D. Allen; Bernice Cheng; Michael H. Fenwick; Wen-wei Huang; Sharif Missiha; Daryoush Tahmassebi; Thomas T. Tidwell. Radical Additions of TEMPO to Ketenes: Correlation of Free Radical and Nucleophilic Reactivity. Organic Letters 1(5), 693-696 (1999). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol990628i

[doi:10.1021/ol990628i](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol990628i)

## Chemical contribution

TEMPO reacts with a variety of ketenes by rate-limiting attack at the carbonyl carbon, producing 1,2-bis(adducts). The assignment of adduct 10a was supported by IR and proton NMR data and confirmed by X-ray crystallography. The authors propose that TEMPO first attacks the carbonyl carbon of phenylketene to form an enolic radical, which is then captured by a second TEMPO molecule.
