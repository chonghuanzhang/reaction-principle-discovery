# L214 — Cooperative Photoredox and N‐Heterocyclic Carbene Catalyzed Fluoroaroylation for the Synthesis of α‐Trifluoromethyl‐Substituted Ketones

**SI reference 73; Tier 1.**

Xiaoye Yu; Anirban Maity; Armido Studer. Cooperative Photoredox and N‐Heterocyclic Carbene Catalyzed Fluoroaroylation for the Synthesis of α‐Trifluoromethyl‐Substituted Ketones. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 62(48), e202310288 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202310288

[doi:10.1002/anie.202310288](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202310288)

## Chemical contribution

Activated aryl-substituted gem-difluoroalkenes bearing alkoxy, pyrazolyl, or alkylthiyl groups gave products in 50–82% yield, whereas para-cyano, para-nitro, and para-acyl substrates were inactive; the authors attributed inactivity to failure of oxidation under the applied conditions. The proposed mechanism comprises SET oxidation of the gem-difluoroalkene, regioselective fluoride trapping to form a benzylic radical, NHC-derived acylazolium reduction to a ketyl-type radical, and radical–radical cross-coupling followed by NHC fragmentation.

### L214-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The α-trifluoromethylated ketone 3a underwent a Pd-catalyzed defluorination/arylation with phenylboronic acid to give fluorinated α,β-unsaturated ketone 5 in 76% yield with high Z-selectivity. The reported conditions were Pd(PPh)3Cl2 (5 mol%), K3PO4 (3 equivalents), toluene, and 110 °C.

Location: 3; Synthetic utility and direct Pd/organoboron defluorinative C–C bond formation

### L214-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The optimized model reaction used PC-I (2 mol%), NHC precursor N1 (20 mol%), Cs2CO3 (12.5 mol%), acetonitrile, and 405 nm irradiation for 24 h, affording 3a in 96% NMR yield and 83% isolated yield, with side-product formation nearly fully suppressed.

Location: 2; Reaction optimization

<a id="statement-l214-s006"></a>

### L214-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A preformed acylazolium reacted with gem-difluoroalkene 1a in the absence of NHC catalyst when supplied with fluoride donor 2a or ZnF2, affording 3a; 2a gave the better result. Stern–Volmer studies also showed that both 1a and the acylazolium quenched excited PC-I, with 1a quenching more efficiently.

Location: 5; Intermediate-competence and fluorescence-quenching experiments
