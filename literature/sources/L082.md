# L082 — Synthesis and Characterization of a Diazirine-Based Photolabel of the Nonanesthetic Fropofol

**SI reference 189; Tier 1.**

E. Railey White; David M. Leace; Victoria M. Bedell; Natarajan V. Bhanu; Benjamin A. Garcia; William P. Dailey; Roderic G. Eckenhoff. Synthesis and Characterization of a Diazirine-Based Photolabel of the Nonanesthetic Fropofol. ACS Chemical Neuroscience 12(1), 176-183 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.0c00667

[doi:10.1021/acschemneuro.0c00667](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.0c00667)

## Chemical contribution

Conversion of ketone 3 to AziFo began with hydroxylamine hydrochloride in pyridine under reflux for 1 hour, followed by tosyl chloride treatment in pyridine under reflux for 1 hour. The authors propose that the different photolabeling preferences of AziFo and AziPm may arise from different binding poses and the position of the reactive carbene relative to residues in the HSAF pocket.
