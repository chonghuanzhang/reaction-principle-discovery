# L123 — Organocatalysis: Fundamentals and Comparisons to Metal and Enzyme Catalysis

**Online reference 226; Tier 3.**

Pierre Vogel; Yu-hong Lam; Adam Simon; Kendall Houk. Organocatalysis: Fundamentals and Comparisons to Metal and Enzyme Catalysis. Catalysts 6(9), 128 (2016). https://doi.org/10.3390/catal6090128

[doi:10.3390/catal6090128](https://doi.org/10.3390/catal6090128)

## Chemical contribution

The review identifies additions of carbon and heteroatom nucleophiles to polar unsaturated compounds, including electron-deficient alkenes bearing CF3, as important synthetic reactions. For anionic nucleophilic substitution, protic solvents can suppress nucleophilicity through specific hydrogen-bonding solvation, and tight association with a counter-cation makes an anion less reactive than the free ion. The authors predict that effective nucleophilic-addition catalysts can combine electrophilic activation of a carbonyl or imine with assistance in dissociating the nucleophilic reagent on one template.
