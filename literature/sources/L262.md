# L262 — Catalyst-free carbosilylation of alkenes using silyl boronates and organic fluorides via selective C-F bond activation

**Online reference 83; Tier 2.**

Jun Zhou; Bingyao Jiang; Yamato Fujihira; Zhengyu Zhao; Takanori Imai; Norio Shibata. Catalyst-free carbosilylation of alkenes using silyl boronates and organic fluorides via selective C-F bond activation. Nature Communications 12(1), 3749 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-24031-w

[doi:10.1038/s41467-021-24031-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-24031-w)

## Chemical contribution

The experiments support the authors' proposal that radical species are involved: TEMPO significantly inhibited the model reaction, and another experiment gave a silylated ring-opened product as the major product in 55% yield, while the expected three-component product was detected only in trace quantity by GCMS. Alkene scope has explicit boundaries: aryl and conjugated alkenes react, non-aryl alkenes are unreactive, electron-deficient acrylates and acrylamides are unsuitable, and the tested internal styrenes did not react.

### L262-S004

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose a KOtBu-triggered single-electron-transfer radical cascade in which a silyl radical adds to the alkene, potassium and a borate species assist C-F activation, and C-C bond formation generates the three-component product together with KF.

### L262-S010
