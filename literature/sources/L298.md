# L298 — Frustrated Lewis pair catalyzed C–F activation of α-trifluoromethylstyrenes

**Online reference 367; Tier 2.**

Chakyu Richard Chan; Dipendu Mandal; Rowan D. Young. Frustrated Lewis pair catalyzed C–F activation of α-trifluoromethylstyrenes. Tetrahedron Chem 8, 100050 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tchem.2023.100050

[doi:10.1016/j.tchem.2023.100050](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tchem.2023.100050)

## Chemical contribution

For substrate 1a and P(o-Tol)3, ACF gave quantitative conversion at 20 mol% under forcing conditions and 90% and 84% yields at 10 and 5 mol%, respectively; B(C6F5)3 and omission of Lewis acid failed to generate product. Aryl-substituted styrene substrates furnished products in reported yields ranging from 97% to 20%, while β-substituted styrenes such as stilbenes failed to produce product. The authors propose that γ-substituted 5a may be an intermediate en route to α-substituted 4a.
