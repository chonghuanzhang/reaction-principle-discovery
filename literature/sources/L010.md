# L010 — Palladium‐Catalyzed C–S Bond Formation of Stable Enamines with Arene/Alkanethiols: Highly Regioselective Synthesis of β‐Amino Sulfides

**SI reference 50; Tier 4.**

Yaojia Jiang; Gaohui Liang; Cong Zhang; Teck‐Peng Loh. Palladium‐Catalyzed C–S Bond Formation of Stable Enamines with Arene/Alkanethiols: Highly Regioselective Synthesis of β‐Amino Sulfides. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2016(20), 3326-3330 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201600588

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.201600588](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201600588)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction was demonstrated on gram scale and furnished the corresponding product in 75% yield under the standard catalytic conditions. The thiol scope includes substituted benzenethiols, naphthalene-1-thiol, and an alkanethiol; the alkanethiol example required PhI(OAc)2 instead of Cu(OAc)2 and gave 68% yield. The authors propose a Heck-type pathway in which oxidized benzenethiol forms a palladium–sulfur complex, followed by enamine coordination, deprotonation, reduction, elimination, and palladium reoxidation; only the disulfide substitution control directly supports part of this proposal.
