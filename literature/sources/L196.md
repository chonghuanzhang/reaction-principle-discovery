# L196 — Asymmetric Catalysis by Architectural and Functional Molecular Engineering: Practical Chemo- and Stereoselective Hydrogenation of Ketones

**Online reference 280; Tier 3.**

Ryoji Noyori; Takeshi Ohkuma. Asymmetric Catalysis by Architectural and Functional Molecular Engineering: Practical Chemo- and Stereoselective Hydrogenation of Ketones. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 40(1), 40-73 (2001). https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20010105)40:1<40::aid-anie40>3.0.co;2-5

[doi:10.1002/1521-3773(20010105)40:1](https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3773(20010105)40:1)

## Chemical contribution

Adding ethylenediamine and KOH reversed the inherent hydrogenation preference between an aldehyde and an olefin, making aldehyde hydrogenation 1500 times faster and changing the C=O/C=C selectivity profile by a factor of 375,000. The authors propose that ketone reduction occurs in the outer coordination sphere without a ketone–ruthenium complex or productive ruthenium alkoxide intermediate, and attribute the high rate and carbonyl selectivity to this nonclassical pathway.
