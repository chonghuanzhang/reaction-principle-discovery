## Chemical contribution

The author-abstract record available on 15 October 2026 reports base-promoted defluorinative thiolation of gem-difluoroalkenes with thiols, without a metal catalyst, to give monofluorovinyl thioethers. It supplies a recent direct precedent for a free-thiol donor and a single-fluorine alkene endpoint. Individual substrate scope, yields, stereochemical details and operational conditions remain limited by abstract-only access, and the precursor is already a gem-difluoroalkene.
