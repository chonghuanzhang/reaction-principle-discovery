# L277 — Quantification of the Lewis Basicities and Nucleophilicities of 1,3,5‐Tris(dialkylamino)benzenes

**SI reference 349; Tier 3.**

Gabriele Micheletti; Robert J. Mayer; Silvia Cino; Carla Boga; Andrea Mazzanti; Armin R. Ofial; Herbert Mayr. Quantification of the Lewis Basicities and Nucleophilicities of 1,3,5‐Tris(dialkylamino)benzenes. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2021(46), 6347-6357 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.202100939

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.202100939](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.202100939)

## Chemical contribution

Equilibrium constants for association of 1,3,5-tris(dialkylamino)benzenes with benzhydrylium tetrafluoroborates were measured photometrically in 10-4 M dichloromethane at 20 °C; combinations with very high conversion yielded only lower limits for K. The authors proposed that even if N-attack can initially be faster in some cases, rapid retroaddition and rearrangement to the thermodynamically more stable Wheland complex can account for exclusive C-attack under reversible conditions.
