# L229 — Visible Light-Driven α-Diazoketones as Denitrogenated Synthons: Synthesis of Fluorinated N-Heterocycles via Multicomponent Cyclization Reactions

**Online reference 307; Tier 1.**

Peng Zhao; Lingfeng Wang; Xiaoshuang Guo; Jiayi Chen; Yanbo Liu; Lei Wang; Yongmin Ma. Visible Light-Driven α-Diazoketones as Denitrogenated Synthons: Synthesis of Fluorinated N-Heterocycles via Multicomponent Cyclization Reactions. Organic Letters 25(18), 3314-3318 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01145

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01145](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c01145)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction did not proceed in the dark and gave low yields of 4a when either PPh3 or the photocatalyst was omitted. Additional nitrogen-containing nucleophiles gave perfluoroalkylated heterocycles in 42–75% yield, and IC3F7, IC5F11, and IC6F13 gave the desired products in 47–76% yield. The authors propose that a pentafluoroethyl radical adds to the α-diazoketone, the resulting radical intermediate undergoes single-electron reduction, and the resulting fluorocarbon anion undergoes β-fluoride elimination to form fluoroolefin II before cyclization with the nitrogen nucleophile.
