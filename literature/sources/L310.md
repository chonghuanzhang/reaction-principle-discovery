# L310 — Nickel-Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of Aliphatic Aldehydes with Trifluoromethyl Alkenes

**Online reference 379; Tier 2.**

Jichao Xiao; John Montgomery. Nickel-Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of Aliphatic Aldehydes with Trifluoromethyl Alkenes. ACS Catalysis 12(4), 2463-2471 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c05801

[doi:10.1021/acscatal.1c05801](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.1c05801)

## Chemical contribution

Electron-rich and electron-deficient 1,1-trifluoromethylstyrenes reacted under mild, base-free conditions in 71-93% yield, and several ester, amide, sulfonyl, nitrile, sulfide, and heterocyclic functionalities were compatible. An alkyl-substituted trifluoromethyl alkene was a poor coupling partner: most starting alkene was recovered, enol ether and reduced silyl ether side products formed competitively, and only 6% desired product was detected by GC-MS. The aldehyde scope included unhindered, beta-branched, and alpha-branched aliphatic aldehydes, although branched substrates reacted with diminished efficiency.
