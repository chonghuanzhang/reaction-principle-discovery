# L096 — Exploring the Nature of Anion−π Interactions: Complexes of π-Acceptors with Fluoro- or Oxoanions Compared to the Associations with Halides

**Online reference 200; Tier 1.**

Favour E. Odubo; Matthias Zeller; Sergiy V. Rosokha. Exploring the Nature of Anion−π Interactions: Complexes of π-Acceptors with Fluoro- or Oxoanions Compared to the Associations with Halides. The Journal of Physical Chemistry A 127(28), 5851-5859 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.3c02704

[doi:10.1021/acs.jpca.3c02704](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.3c02704)

## Chemical contribution

NBO calculations gave charge transfer values of 0.05–0.22 e for halide complexes, while values for oxo- and fluoroanion complexes were about an order of magnitude lower. Energy decomposition analysis indicated that complexes with polyatomic fluoro- and oxoanions are predominantly electrostatic, whereas analogous halide complexes have significant orbital-interaction contributions. The polyatomic fluoro- and oxoanions studied are characterized as very weak bases, poor nucleophiles, and poor reducing agents.
