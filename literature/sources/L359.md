# L359 — Nickel-Catalyzed Defluorinative Reductive Cross-Coupling Reaction of gem- Difluoroalkenes with Thiosulfonate or Selenium Sulfonate

**SI reference 22; Tier 1.**

Jian Li; Weidong Rao; Shun-Yi Wang; Shun-Jun Ji. Nickel-Catalyzed Defluorinative Reductive Cross-Coupling Reaction of gem- Difluoroalkenes with Thiosulfonate or Selenium Sulfonate. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 84(18), 11542-11552 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387)

## Chemical contribution

Ni-catalysed reductive cross-coupling of gem-difluoroalkenes with thiosulfonates or selenosulfonates formed sulfur- or selenium-substituted monofluoroolefins through C–F cleavage and C–S/C–Se formation. The author abstract reports high E selectivity and mild conditions. It establishes a productive selenium-containing donor class, but a selenosulfonate is distinct from phenylselenol or diphenyl diselenide, and detailed conditions and exact project-target substitution patterns are not supplied by the abstract.
