# L235 — Selective Metalation and Functionalization of Fluorinated Nitriles Using 2,2,6,6-Tetramethylpiperidyl Bases

**Online reference 313; Tier 1.**

Thiago dos Santos; Henrique P. Orenha; Valter E. Murie; Ricardo Vessecchi; Giuliano C. Clososki. Selective Metalation and Functionalization of Fluorinated Nitriles Using 2,2,6,6-Tetramethylpiperidyl Bases. Organic Letters 23(19), 7396-7400 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02572

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02572](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.1c02572)

## Chemical contribution

During formylation of the metalated 4-fluorobenzonitrile derivative, fluorine was displaced by in situ generated magnesium dimethylamide, furnishing an arylamine in 91% yield; changing the formylating agent gave a different outcome. The authors report regioselective deprotometalation of aromatic and heteroaromatic nitriles with magnesium- and zinc-derived TMP bases, followed by electrophile trapping to furnish highly functionalized nitriles. The authors rationalize the observed metalation regioselectivity using DFT calculations and attribute the accessible sites to aromatic hydrogen acidity and the inductive effect of fluorine.
