[doi:10.1002/ejoc.201701004](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201701004)

## Chemical contribution

Fluorenone gave the desired 1-iodo product in 52% yield, with competitive formation of a keto-alcohol side product. Xanthone was converted to its 1-iodo derivative in 72% yield using ZnCl2·TMEDA, LiTMP, THF, -55 °C for 15 minutes, and iodine. The authors propose that regioselective deprotonation adjacent to the ketone may result from lithium coordination by the ketone, a favored base approach, and/or stabilization.
