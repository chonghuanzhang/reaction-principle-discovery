# L013 — Unprecedented Nucleophilic Additions of Highly Polar Organometallic Compounds to Imines and Nitriles Using Water as a Non‐Innocent Reaction Medium

**Online reference 130; Tier 3.**

Giuseppe Dilauro; Marzia Dell'Aera; Paola Vitale; Vito Capriati; Filippo Maria Perna. Unprecedented Nucleophilic Additions of Highly Polar Organometallic Compounds to Imines and Nitriles Using Water as a Non‐Innocent Reaction Medium. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 56(34), 10200-10203 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201705412

[doi:10.1002/anie.201705412](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201705412)

## Chemical contribution

Organolithium scope with imines included MeLi, s-BuLi, t-BuLi, n-BuLi, and PhLi across substituted and sterically hindered imines, with reported isolated yields ranging from 45% to 97% depending on substrate, reagent, and equivalents. Sequential treatment of benzonitrile in water with s-BuLi and n-BuLi produced only ketone products, whereas sequential s-BuLi and allylmagnesium chloride furnished tertiary carbinamine 4a in 95% yield with less than 5% ketone byproduct.
