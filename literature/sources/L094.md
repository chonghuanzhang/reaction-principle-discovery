# L094 — Preparation of Polyfunctional Arylzinc Organometallics in Toluene by Halogen/Zinc Exchange Reactions

**Online reference 198; Tier 1.**

Moritz Balkenhohl; Dorothée S. Ziegler; Alexandre Desaintjean; Leonie J. Bole; Alan R. Kennedy; Eva Hevia; Paul Knochel. Preparation of Polyfunctional Arylzinc Organometallics in Toluene by Halogen/Zinc Exchange Reactions. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 58(37), 12898-12902 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201906898

[doi:10.1002/anie.201906898](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201906898)

## Chemical contribution

The method also supported Br/Zn exchange: 4-bromobenzonitrile reacted with 1c in toluene at 25 °C for 5 hours, and iodine quenching afforded 4-iodobenzonitrile in 77% yield. NMR monitoring showed that Et2Zn alone was inert toward exchange with 2-iodoanisole; adding one equivalent of LiOR gave 23% conversion after 10 minutes, whereas two equivalents gave 95% conversion. The authors propose that nitrogen-donor alkoxide coordination to lithium may explain the alkoxide effect because alkoxides lacking nitrogen donors may form less-reactive, higher oligomeric lithium zincates.
