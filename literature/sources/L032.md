# L032 — Hydrofluorination of Alkynes Catalysed by Gold Bifluorides

**Online reference 148; Tier 1.**

Fady Nahra; Scott R. Patrick; Davide Bello; Marcel Brill; Alan Obled; David B. Cordes; Alexandra M. Z. Slawin; David O'Hagan; Steven P. Nolan. Hydrofluorination of Alkynes Catalysed by Gold Bifluorides. ChemCatChem 7(2), 240-244 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.201402891

[doi:10.1002/cctc.201402891](https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.201402891)

## Chemical contribution

For alkynyl sulfides, elevated temperatures were avoided because multiple product isomers formed and some substrates were thermally unstable; decomposition could also affect room-temperature yields, and NH4BF4 was not beneficial in these cases. Alkynyl sulfides that failed under a range of literature hydrofluorination procedures gave the desired fluorovinyl thioethers in good yields and excellent stereoselectivities under the authors' protocol at room temperature. The authors proposed steric effects as the reason ortho-substituted alkynes were unreactive.
