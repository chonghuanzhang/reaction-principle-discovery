# L071 — Transition‐Metal‐Free Coupling of Polyfluorinated Arenes and Functionalized, Masked Aryl Nucleophiles

**SI reference 81; Tier 1.**

Lucie Finck; Martin Oestreich. Transition‐Metal‐Free Coupling of Polyfluorinated Arenes and Functionalized, Masked Aryl Nucleophiles. Chemistry – A European Journal 27(43), 11061-11064 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202101731

[doi:10.1002/chem.202101731](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202101731)

## Chemical contribution

For functionalized polyfluoroarenes, DMF gave better regiocontrol than ethereal solvents; reactions in DMF generally produced para- or beta-substituted products with good-to-moderate selectivity, and regioisomerically pure products were obtained after chromatography. Competing pathways included bisarylation when less fluoroarene was used and degradation of diazene 1a to the corresponding arylsilane upon Lewis-base activation. The authors propose stabilized Meisenheimer-type intermediates in the stepwise SNAr pathway.
