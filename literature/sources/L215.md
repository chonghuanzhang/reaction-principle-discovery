# L215 — Reaction of the Ambident Electrophile Dimethyl Carbonate with the Ambident Nucleophile Phenylhydrazine

**Online reference 295; Tier 3.**

Anthony E. Rosamilia; Fabio Aricò; Pietro Tundo. Reaction of the Ambident Electrophile Dimethyl Carbonate with the Ambident Nucleophile Phenylhydrazine. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 73(4), 1559-1562 (2008). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo701818d

[doi:10.1021/jo701818d](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo701818d)

## Chemical contribution

The observed reaction-rate order was KO-t-Bu > NaOMe > Cs2CO3 > K2CO3, and K2CO3 gave no reaction. Methanol removal with 5 Å molecular sieves suppressed methanolysis and gave carbazate 8 with greater than 90% selectivity, although less than 10% carbazate 10 was still produced. The authors propose that KO-t-Bu activates N-1 either by weakening its N-H bond through proton coordination or by deprotonation; they favor the phenylhydrazinide-anion pathway based on the reported pKa comparison.

### L215-S005
