# L250 — Recent advances in visible-light-induced defluorinative functionalization of α-trifluoromethyl arylalkenes

**SI reference 36; Tier 2.**

Yanyan He; Leiyang Lv; Zhenhua Jia; Teck-Peng Loh. Recent advances in visible-light-induced defluorinative functionalization of α-trifluoromethyl arylalkenes. Organic Chemistry Frontiers 13(7), 2178-2200 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1039/d5qo01732g

[doi:10.1039/d5qo01732g](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5qo01732g)

## Chemical contribution

The review reports C–S bond formation through photo/Ni dual-catalyzed radical defluorinative sulfonylation of α-trifluoromethyl arylalkenes, producing gem-difluoroallylsulfones in moderate to good yields in MeCN at room temperature under blue light. In the β,γ-unsaturated-oxime example, productive defluorinative reaction was substrate-dependent and efficient only for α-trifluoromethyl arylalkenes bearing electron-donating or weakly electron-withdrawing aryl substituents. The proposed sulfonylation mechanism involves sulfonyl-radical addition to the α-trifluoromethyl arylalkene, capture by Ni(II), and β-fluoride elimination from an alkyl-Ni(III) intermediate.
