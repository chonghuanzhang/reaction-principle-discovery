# L244 — Ni-Catalyzed Distal-Selective gem-Difluorovinylation of Unactivated Alkenes with 2,2-Difluorovinyl Benzoates

**Online reference 320; Tier 2.**

Mingyu Luo; Linya Xu; Xin Gao; Chun-Ming Chan; Qiushi Shen; Bingnan Du; Wing-Yiu Yu. Ni-Catalyzed Distal-Selective gem-Difluorovinylation of Unactivated Alkenes with 2,2-Difluorovinyl Benzoates. JACS Au 5(9), 4224-4232 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.5c00522

[doi:10.1021/jacsau.5c00522](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.5c00522)

## Chemical contribution

Both (E)- and (Z)-directed internal alkenes gave the same product in 82% and 71% yields, and several longer, substituted, cycloalkyl-containing, and primary-chloroalkyl substrates also coupled in 63–75% yields. The optimized coupling was completely linear-selective in the reported experiments: only linear product was observed, with no branched product. The reaction showed a significant hydrogenation side reaction for N-(pent-4-enyl)picolinimide: coupled product was obtained in 52% yield together with nearly 40% N-pentylpicolinamide.
