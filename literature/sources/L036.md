# L036 — Towards a General Scale of Nucleophilicity?

**Online reference 152; Tier 3.**

Thanh Binh Phan; Martin Breugst; Herbert Mayr. Towards a General Scale of Nucleophilicity? Angewandte Chemie International Edition 45(23), 3869-3874 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200600542

[doi:10.1002/anie.200600542](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.200600542)

## Chemical contribution

Charged and neutral nucleophiles reacting with iodomethane in methanol gave a similar correlation; across the sulfonium-ion and iodomethane correlations, absolute deviations were generally below one order of magnitude despite the variety of nucleophiles. Although the benzhydrylium-derived N and sN parameters were relevant to this SN2 reaction, Equation (3) was not directly applicable: changing nucleophiles affected reactivity toward sulfonium ion 1 by only 60% of the effect observed with benzhydrylium ions.
