# L266 — An Improved Chiral Auxiliary for the Allene Ether Version of the Nazarov Cyclization

**Online reference 339; Tier 1.**

Derrick B. delos Santos; April R. Banaag; Marcus A. Tius. An Improved Chiral Auxiliary for the Allene Ether Version of the Nazarov Cyclization. Organic Letters 8(12), 2579-2582 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol060816q

[doi:10.1021/ol060816q](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol060816q)

## Chemical contribution

Across the Table 1 reactions of Li-14, optical purities ranged from 85% to 93% ee and yields ranged from 53% to 85%. The reported transformation combines an enamide and a lithioallenyl ether to produce a cyclopentenone through the allene ether version of the Nazarov cyclization. The authors proposed that LiCl disrupts aggregation of the allenyl anion.
