# L178 — Nucleophilicity Prediction via Multivariate Linear Regression Analysis

**SI reference 266; Tier 4.**

Manuel Orlandi; Margarita Escudero-Casao; Giulia Licini. Nucleophilicity Prediction via Multivariate Linear Regression Analysis. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 86(4), 3555-3564 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c02952

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.0c02952](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c02952)

## Chemical contribution

Steric descriptors affected modeled nucleophilicity less than electronic descriptors, and the reported steric relationship is specific to reactions with benzhydrylium cations or related quinone methides rather than a general electrophile-independent rule. The authors propose that proton affinity and solvation energies are the principal nucleophilicity descriptors and that thermodynamics is a key contributor to reaction rates, while smaller solvation, steric, and electrostatic terms may affect intrinsic activation energies.
