# L213 — Pd-Catalyzed Mizoroki-Heck Reactions Using Fluorine-Containing Agents as the Cross-Coupling Partners

**Online reference 294; Tier 2.**

Jing Yang; Hua-Wen Zhao; Jian He; Cheng-Pan Zhang. Pd-Catalyzed Mizoroki-Heck Reactions Using Fluorine-Containing Agents as the Cross-Coupling Partners. Catalysts 8(1), 23 (2018). https://doi.org/10.3390/catal8010023

[doi:10.3390/catal8010023](https://doi.org/10.3390/catal8010023)

## Chemical contribution

For fluorine-containing electron-deficient olefins coupled with aryldiazonium salts, reaction efficiency depended strongly on the electron-withdrawing group: an alpha,beta-unsaturated ester gave very low yield, vinylphosphonate and vinylsulfone were unreactive, and a nitroalkene gave a complicated reaction with neither starting material nor desired product detected. The review interprets the trifluoromethyl group in the cyclization substrate as crucial and suggests that it activates the terminal vinylic carbon and stabilizes the cyclized palladium intermediate.

### L213-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In a Pd-catalyzed cyclization of a trifluoromethyl-substituted alkene bearing an O-acyloxime, beta-fluorine elimination was reported to occur exclusively over beta-hydrogen elimination; hydrogen- and methyl-substituted analogues gave no cyclized product under similar conditions.

Location: 14; Heck-type cyclization of 2-(trifluoromethyl)allyl ketone oximes

### L213-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In a Pd(OAc)2-catalyzed reaction of aryl bromide or iodide with vinylidene difluoride, the expected beta-hydride-elimination product was formed only in a very small amount, while alpha-fluorostyrene was the major product.

Location: 3; Fluoroalkenes as cross-coupling participants

### L213-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Intramolecular Heck cyclizations of 1,1-difluoroalkene substrates furnished ring-fluorinated products through reported insertion and beta-fluorine elimination, while monofluoroalkene, fluorine-free, dichloroalkene, and dibromoalkene analogues did not cyclize under the same conditions.

Location: 6; Heck-type 5-endo-trig cyclization promoted by vinylic fluorines

### L213-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The review reports an oxidative Heck coupling between diverse arylboronic acids and 2,3,3,3-tetrafluoroprop-1-ene that gives Z-beta-fluoro-beta-(trifluoromethyl)styrene derivatives in good yields with broad substrate scope and functional-group tolerance.

Location: 13; Oxidative Heck reactions of fluorinated olefins with arylboronic acids

### L213-S008

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The review presents the conventional Mizoroki-Heck cycle as oxidative addition to Pd(0), alkene addition, elimination of hydridopalladium halide, and base-assisted regeneration of Pd(0); this is explicitly described as a possible mechanism.

Location: 1; Introduction, possible Mizoroki-Heck mechanism

### L213-S009

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

Sulfur-containing olefins are identified as a catalyst-compatibility risk because they may poison palladium catalysts through stable metal-sulfur complexes; only a handful of successful sulfoxide-based Heck reactions had been reported.

Location: 8; Fluorine-containing vinyl sulfur compounds and catalyst poisoning risk

### L213-S010

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A carbonyl-containing monofluoroalkene, 3-fluoro-3-buten-2-one, underwent Pd-catalyzed Heck coupling with aryl iodides using Pd(OAc)2, triphenylphosphine, triethylamine, and DMF, giving Z products in good yields without observed conjugate-addition or fluoride-elimination products.

Location: 4; Heck coupling of a fluorinated enone
