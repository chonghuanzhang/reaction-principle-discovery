# L269 — Rhodium(I)-Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of Boronic Acids with Monofluoroalkenes

**Online reference 342; Tier 2.**

Yuwei Zong; Yihan Tang; Gavin Chit Tsui. Rhodium(I)-Catalyzed Defluorinative Coupling of Boronic Acids with Monofluoroalkenes. Organic Letters 24(35), 6380-6385 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02294

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02294](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c02294)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction tolerated multiple arylboronic acids, including electron-rich, electron-poor, halogenated, naphthyl, furyl, and thienyl examples, generally furnishing Z-configured trisubstituted alpha,beta-unsaturated esters; ortho substitution and an alkenyl boronic acid reduced performance. Cyclohexylboronic acid, 3-pyridinylboronic acid, and 2-bromophenylboronic acid failed to give the desired products. The authors propose a catalytic sequence involving Rh(I)-phenyl formation by transmetalation, alkene coordination and carborhodation to an alpha-Rh ester, followed by syn-beta-F elimination to release the major Z product.
