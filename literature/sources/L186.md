# L186 — Heterogeneous Gold and Palladium Based Catalysts for Solvent-Free Oxidation of Toluene

**Online reference 272; Tier 4.**

Mohd Izham Bin Saiman. Heterogeneous Gold and Palladium Based Catalysts for Solvent-Free Oxidation of Toluene. Doctoral thesis, Cardiff University (2012).

DOI not assigned in the available citation

## Chemical contribution

Supported Au-Pd/TiO2 catalyzed solvent-free toluene oxidation with TBHP at 80 °C; after 4 hours, the impregnated 5 wt% Au-Pd/TiO2 catalyst gave 3.0% conversion with 27.6% benzyl alcohol, 70.4% benzaldehyde, and 2.1% benzoic acid selectivity, compared with 0.3% conversion in the catalyst-free TBHP reaction. Longer reaction times increased toluene conversion but decreased benzyl alcohol and benzaldehyde selectivity because of consecutive oxidation to benzoic acid.

### L186-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

For the catalyst-free reaction, conversion increased as the temperature was raised from 60 to 90 °C. In the catalytic reaction, conversion was slightly lower at 90 °C, which the author attributed to TBHP decomposition beginning at that temperature; greater overoxidation to benzoic acid was observed at 90 °C, and 80 °C was selected for subsequent studies.

Location: 80; Chapter 4, temperature effects

### L186-S005

The author proposes that TBHP decomposes on the Au-Pd surface to form radical species that abstract hydrogen from toluene and lead to benzyl alcohol; benzyl alcohol is then proposed to oxidize to benzaldehyde, followed by further oxidation to benzoic acid.

### L186-S006

### L186-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

EPR detected a surface superoxide signal after treating Au-Pd/TiO2 with TBHP; the signal disappeared after subsequent toluene addition, supporting reaction of toluene with that surface-bound radical species. Hydroperoxy and other oxygen-centered peroxy radicals were not detected.

Location: 117; Chapter 4, EPR identification of radical species
