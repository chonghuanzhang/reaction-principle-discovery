# L116 — Lewis Acid-mediated Carbon-Fluorine Bond Transformation: Substitution of Fluorine and Insertion into a Carbon-Fluorine Bond

**SI reference 219; Tier 2.**

Yoshihiro Nishimoto; Makoto Yasuda. Lewis Acid-mediated Carbon-Fluorine Bond Transformation: Substitution of Fluorine and Insertion into a Carbon-Fluorine Bond. Journal of Synthetic Organic Chemistry, Japan 80(11), 1000-1010 (2022). https://doi.org/10.5059/yukigoseikyokaishi.80.1000

[doi:10.5059/yukigoseikyokaishi.80.1000](https://doi.org/10.5059/yukigoseikyokaishi.80.1000)

## Chemical contribution

Lower indium loading gave only 23% yield; the authors attributed this loss to conversion of InI3 by eliminated fluoride into less Lewis-acidic InFxI3-x. Addition of ZnI2 enabled 10 mol% InI3 to give 2a in 72% yield, whereas Me3SiI, BF3·OEt2, and Bu4NI were ineffective fluoride traps. The B(C6F5)3-catalyzed vinylic C-F transformation accepted several substituted alpha-fluorostyrenes and ester- or thioester-derived silyl ketene acetals, but an aliphatic fluoroalkene gave no desired compound.

### L116-S009

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The proposed photoredox mechanism invokes single-electron reduction, benzylic fluoride elimination, trapping of fluoride by tin fluoride to suppress the retro reaction, radical addition to the allylic stannane, and product-forming loss of a stannyl cation. Stern-Volmer quenching experimentally supports the otherwise thermodynamically unfavorable SET step.
