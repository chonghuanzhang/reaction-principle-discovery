# L219 — The Structural Proliferation of 2,6‐Difluoropyridine through Organometallic Intermediates

**SI reference 299; Tier 1.**

Manfred Schlosser; Thierry Rausis. The Structural Proliferation of 2,6‐Difluoropyridine through Organometallic Intermediates. European Journal of Organic Chemistry 2004(5), 1018-1024 (2004). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300649

[doi:10.1002/ejoc.200300649](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300649)

## Chemical contribution

High-yield isolation of aldehyde 7 required neutralization of the N,N-dimethylformamide adduct at -75 °C; at higher temperatures, formation of dimethylamino-substituted fluoropyridine aldehydes competed and gave a 1:10 regioisomeric mixture. The authors report that nucleophilic displacement of fluorine from the difluoropyridine aldehyde was regioselective. The authors assign the competing C-N bond-forming pathway to reversible dissociation of an O-lithiated hemiaminal into aldehyde 7 and lithium dimethylamide, followed by nucleophilic heteroaromatic substitution of fluoride.
