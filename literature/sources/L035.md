# L035 — Isotope effects in nucleophilic substitution reactions. VI. The effect of ion pairing on the transition state structure of SN2 reactions

**Online reference 151; Tier 3.**

Kenneth Charles Westaway; Zhu-Gen Lai. Isotope effects in nucleophilic substitution reactions. VI. The effect of ion pairing on the transition state structure of SN2 reactions. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 66(5), 1263-1271 (1988). https://doi.org/10.1139/v88-205

[doi:10.1139/v88-205](https://doi.org/10.1139/v88-205)

## Chemical contribution

The UV absorption maxima of lithium, sodium, and cesium thiophenoxides were independent of cation in DMSO, DMF, and methanol, supporting assignment of the ion-pair reactant in those solvents as solvent-separated; sodium thiophenoxide also appeared solvent-separated in diglyme. The secondary alpha-deuterium kinetic isotope effect changed in every solvent where thiophenoxide changed between free-ion and ion-pair forms, whereas it did not change with concentration in diglyme, where the reactant remained ion-paired.

### L035-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Adding 15-crown-5 ether under conditions where sodium thiophenoxide would normally be ion-paired changed the isotope effect to the value observed for the free-ion reaction; a control at free-ion concentrations indicated that crown ether itself did not cause the change.

Location: 3; Results and discussion; crown-ether intervention and control
