# L070 — Recent Advances in Indazole-Containing Derivatives: Synthesis and Biological Perspectives

**Online reference 180; Tier 1.**

Shu-Guang Zhang; Chao-Gen Liang; Wei-Hua Zhang. Recent Advances in Indazole-Containing Derivatives: Synthesis and Biological Perspectives. Molecules 23(11), 2783 (2018). https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules23112783

[doi:10.3390/molecules23112783](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules23112783)

## Chemical contribution

A Cu/Pd cooperatively catalyzed tandem C-N/C-P bond-forming reaction of 2-alkynyl azobenzenes is reported to produce phosphorated 2H-indazoles in moderate to good yields with good functional-group tolerance. The review reports ligand-free palladium-catalyzed intramolecular C-H amination of aminohydrazones to form 1H-indazoles; the described transformation does not identify a trifluoroethyl carbonyl electrophile, organoboron partner, or selective defluorination. For an iodine-mediated 2H-indazole synthesis, mechanistic studies suggested iodine-assisted hydrogen transfer, while DFT calculations were reported to support a radical-chain mechanism.
