# L240 — Friedel−Crafts-Type Cyclization of 2,2-Difluorovinyl Ketones via α-Fluorocarbocations and Its Application in Domino Cyclizations

**SI reference 34; Tier 1.**

Junji Ichikawa; Mikio Kaneko; Misaki Yokota; Masaaki Itonaga; Takaharu Yokoyama. Friedel−Crafts-Type Cyclization of 2,2-Difluorovinyl Ketones via α-Fluorocarbocations and Its Application in Domino Cyclizations. Organic Letters 8(15), 3167-3170 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol060912r

[doi:10.1021/ol060912r](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol060912r)

## Chemical contribution

Among the tested Lewis acids, the trimethylsilylating reagents were especially effective; TMSB(OTf)4 drove the reaction to completion without HFIP, and triflic acid also furnished product in 77% yield. In the intermolecular variant, m-dimethoxybenzene replaced one or two vinylic fluorines under acidic conditions, whereas anisole did not react. The authors propose that carbonyl substitution assists alpha-fluorocarbocation formation and that the cyclization proceeds through carbonyl-oxygen silylation followed by aromatic vinylation.
