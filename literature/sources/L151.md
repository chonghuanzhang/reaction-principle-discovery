# L151 — A Multi‐Component Reaction of Hexafluoro‐1,4‐naphthoquinone with N‐Heterocycles and Active Methylene Compounds: A Straightforward Approach to Polyfluorinated Zwitterions

**SI reference 247; Tier 1.**

Ekaterina N. Kudryavtseva; Boris V. Lichitsky; Evgeny V. Tretyakov. A Multi‐Component Reaction of Hexafluoro‐1,4‐naphthoquinone with N‐Heterocycles and Active Methylene Compounds: A Straightforward Approach to Polyfluorinated Zwitterions. Asian Journal of Organic Chemistry 14(4), e202400751 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202400751

[doi:10.1002/ajoc.202400751](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202400751)

## Chemical contribution

Acetylacetone gave polycyclic product 4a in moderate yield, and the corresponding zwitterionic product could not be isolated; the authors report formation of polycyclic products 4a–c as a common feature of the tested active methylene compounds bearing an acetyl group. The reaction of 2,6-lutidine, quinone 1, and malononitrile gave salt 10 instead of the expected betaine; subsequent reaction of salt 10 with quinone 1 resulted in substitution of only one fluorine atom.
