# L217 — Identification of Catalytic Active Sites for Durable Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cell: Catalytic Degradation and Poisoning Perspectives

**SI reference 297; Tier 4.**

Syed Shoaib Ahmad Shah; Tayyaba Najam; Muhammad Sohail Bashir; Muhammad Sufyan Javed; Aziz‐ur Rahman; Rafael Luque; Shu‐Juan Bao. Identification of Catalytic Active Sites for Durable Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cell: Catalytic Degradation and Poisoning Perspectives. Small 18(18), 2106279 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.202106279

[doi:10.1002/smll.202106279](https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.202106279)

## Chemical contribution

Operando and end-of-test spectroscopy found that two FeNx site populations degraded differently: S1 transformed into iron oxides, whereas S2 remained structurally and numerically unchanged and dominated ORR activity after 50 hours. The active-site configuration of pyrolyzed M-N-C ORR catalysts remains unresolved; metal-coordinated pyridinic nitrogen is favored by most results, but several alternative structures have been suggested and the available data are insufficient for a complete assignment.
