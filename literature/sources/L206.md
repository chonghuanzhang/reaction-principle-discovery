# L206 — Nickel-Catalyzed Enantio- and Diastereoselective Synthesis of Fluorine-Containing Vicinal Stereogenic Centers

**Online reference 287; Tier 2.**

Uttam Dhawa; Lara Lavrencic; Xile Hu. Nickel-Catalyzed Enantio- and Diastereoselective Synthesis of Fluorine-Containing Vicinal Stereogenic Centers. ACS Central Science 10(8), 1657-1666 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.4c00819

[doi:10.1021/acscentsci.4c00819](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.4c00819)

## Chemical contribution

TEMPO suppressed formation of the coupled product and produced an alkyl-TEMPO adduct, while a cyclopropyl radical-clock substrate gave ring-opened products. The reaction displays a strong fluoroalkene stereochemical boundary: (Z)-1a reacts without observed isomerization, whereas (E)-1a is largely unreactive and pure (E)-1a gives no 3aa. The proposed mechanism invokes single-electron activation of the alkyl halide, formation of a Ni-H species, enantioselective hydrometalation of the fluoroalkene to a Ni(II) alpha-fluoroalkyl complex, radical capture, and reductive elimination from Ni(III).
