# L184 — Synthesis of Polycyclic Furan and Chromene Derivatives via Cascade Reactions Enabled by Cleavage of Multiple C(sp3)−F Bonds

**Online reference 270; Tier 1.**

Ting Xie; Chen Zhang; Si‐Xuan Zhang; Weidong Rao; Haiyan Xu; Zhi‐Liang Shen; Xue‐Qiang Chu. Synthesis of Polycyclic Furan and Chromene Derivatives via Cascade Reactions Enabled by Cleavage of Multiple C(sp3)−F Bonds. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 362(21), 4736-4743 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202000660

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202000660](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202000660)

## Chemical contribution

Additive effects were substrate- or context-dependent: absence of TBAB inhibited formation of 5c and gave compound 23 in 27% yield, but TBAI produced 5c in 75% yield, whereas LiBr and CoBr2 did not promote the desired cyclization. Under basic conditions, substrate 1a eliminated HF to form defluorinated alkene intermediate 22 as a 1:1 E/Z mixture; unsaturated compound 23 was subsequently converted to product 5c in 52% yield under the standard conditions.
