# L272 — Resting State and Elementary Steps of the Coupling of Aryl Halides with Thiols Catalyzed by Alkylbisphosphine Complexes of Palladium

**Online reference 345; Tier 4.**

Elsa Alvaro; John F. Hartwig. Resting State and Elementary Steps of the Coupling of Aryl Halides with Thiols Catalyzed by Alkylbisphosphine Complexes of Palladium. Journal of the American Chemical Society 131(22), 7858-7868 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja901793w

[doi:10.1021/ja901793w](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja901793w)

## Chemical contribution

Base strongly accelerated reaction of the palladium bis-thiolate complex with an aryl chloride: 99% thioether formed after 40 minutes with NaOtPent, compared with 32% after 25 hours without base. The authors propose that the palladium bis-thiolate reacts through reversible reductive elimination of disulfide followed by irreversible oxidative addition of aryl bromide; the kinetic data are consistent with this pathway.
