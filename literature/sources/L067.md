# L067 — Iron-Catalyzed Photochemical Defluorinative Functionalization of Polyfluorinated Aromatics

**SI reference 177; Tier 1.**

Ke Wang; Jia-Lin Tu; Ao-Men Hu; Mei Li; Lin Guo; Chao Yang; Wujiong Xia. Iron-Catalyzed Photochemical Defluorinative Functionalization of Polyfluorinated Aromatics. Organic Letters 27(36), 10049-10053 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03114

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03114](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.5c03114)

## Chemical contribution

For the alkane reaction, measured parallel and competitive kinetic isotope effects were 1.9 and 1.4, respectively; the authors interpreted these results as indicating that hydrogen-atom abstraction was not rate-determining. The authors propose an LMCT pathway in which excited FeCl3 generates a chlorine radical, hydrogen-atom transfer forms an alkyl radical, radical addition to the polyfluoroarene is followed by single-electron reduction, and heterolytic C–F cleavage eliminates fluoride to form product.
