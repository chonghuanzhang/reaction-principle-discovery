**SI reference 317; Tier 3.**

J. Peter Guthrie. Hydrolysis of esters of oxy acids: pKa values for strong acids; Brønsted relationship for attack of water at methyl; free energies of hydrolysis of esters of oxy acids; and a linear relationship between free energy of hydrolysis and pKa holding over a range of 20 pK units. Canadian Journal of Chemistry 56(17), 2342-2354 (1978). https://doi.org/10.1139/v78-385

[doi:10.1139/v78-385](https://doi.org/10.1139/v78-385)

## Chemical contribution

The Brønsted analysis of the unobservable reverse ester-formation reaction is weakly constrained because too few data points are available to specify the line well, and the derived ester-formation rate constants have uncertainties of at least an order of magnitude. Combining literature evidence produced an internally consistent set of strong mineral-acid and arenesulfonic-acid pKa values referenced to dilute aqueous solution, with an expected uncertainty of 0.5 log unit.
