# L183 — Philicity and Fugality Scales for Organic Reactions

**SI reference 269; Tier 3.**

Rodrigo Ormazábal-Toledo; Renato Contreras. Philicity and Fugality Scales for Organic Reactions. Advances in Chemistry 2014, 1-13 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/541547

[doi:10.1155/2014/541547](https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/541547)

## Chemical contribution

The regional nucleophilicity index assigns the greatest nucleophilicity to expected reactive atoms, with heteroatom N, O, or S sites identified as the most nucleophilic centers in the evaluated set. In the morpholine substitution of 1-X-2,4-dinitrobenzenes, the reported experimental leaving-group order is F > Cl > Br > I. For aryl benzoate substitution, the authors propose that electron-attracting groups on the leaving group may enhance the rate constant by destabilizing the intermediate from which the leaving group departs.
