# L030 — Carbon–fluorine bond activation for the synthesis of functionalized molecules

**Online reference 47; Tier 2.**

Teresa A. Unzner; Thomas Magauer. Carbon–fluorine bond activation for the synthesis of functionalized molecules. Tetrahedron Letters 56(7), 877-883 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2014.12.145

[doi:10.1016/j.tetlet.2014.12.145](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tetlet.2014.12.145)

## Chemical contribution

Installing a 2-phenylsulfonyl electron-withdrawing group on 3-alkylated 3,3-difluoropropenes enabled mild reactions with sodium alkoxides, phenoxides, thiolates, neutral amines, and Grignard reagents; organolithium reagents were not tolerated because of excessive reactivity. In platinum-catalyzed amination of cyclic 3,3-difluoropropenes, cyclic and acyclic secondary amines were compatible, whereas primary amines and allylamine inhibited the catalyst. The organolithium SN2′ reaction was proposed to be promoted by a C–F···Li+ interaction that increases fluoride leaving-group ability, with LiF formation suggested as an additional driving force.
