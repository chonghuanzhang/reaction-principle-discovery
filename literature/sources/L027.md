# L027 — Atom Recombination of Difluorocarbene Enables 3-Fluorinated Oxindoles from 2-Aminoarylketones

**Online reference 144; Tier 2.**

Guan Zhang; Qianqian Shi; Mengyuan Hou; Kai Yang; Shihui Wang; Shuai Wang; Wangyang Li; Chaokun Li; Jian Qiu; Hetao Xu; Lu Zhou; Cece Wang; Shi-Jun Li; Yu Lan; Qiuling Song. Atom Recombination of Difluorocarbene Enables 3-Fluorinated Oxindoles from 2-Aminoarylketones. CCS Chemistry 4(5), 1671-1679 (2022). https://doi.org/10.31635/ccschem.021.202100979

[doi:10.31635/ccschem.021.202100979](https://doi.org/10.31635/ccschem.021.202100979)

## Chemical contribution

Alternative difluorocarbene precursors HCF2Cl, TMSCF2Br, and ethyl iododifluoroacetate also reacted, but gave lower yields than ethyl bromodifluoroacetate. The proposed mechanism involves amine attack on in situ generated difluorocarbene, intramolecular carbon-carbon bond formation with the carbonyl, bromide-mediated substitution, intramolecular cleavage of a neighboring C-F bond to form an epoxide, and rearrangement by a 1,2-fluorine shift; DFT assigns the epoxide-forming substitution a calculated barrier of 23.5 kcal/mol and describes it as rate-limiting.
