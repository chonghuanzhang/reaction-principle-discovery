# L052 — Scales of Nucleophilicity and Electrophilicity: A System for Ordering Polar Organic and Organometallic Reactions

**SI reference 165; Tier 3.**

Herbert Mayr; Matthias Patz. Scales of Nucleophilicity and Electrophilicity: A System for Ordering Polar Organic and Organometallic Reactions. Angewandte Chemie International Edition in English 33(9), 938-957 (1994). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.199409381

[doi:10.1002/anie.199409381](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.199409381)

## Chemical contribution

The authors identify the principal domain of their correlations as reactions of cationic carbon electrophiles with nucleophiles in which one bond is formed in the rate-determining step, without cycloaddition or sigma-bond cleavage, while noting that some hydride-transfer and organometallic cleavage reactions also follow the relationships. The graphical reactivity scale provides only a rough first approximation of whether a reaction will occur at 20 °C; the full equation is required for more precise prediction.
