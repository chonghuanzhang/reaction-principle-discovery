# L257 — Deactivation Mechanism and Anti-Deactivation Measures of Metal Catalyst in the Dry Reforming of Methane: A Review

**Online reference 332; Tier 4.**

Bo Yuan; Tao Zhu; Yiwei Han; Xueli Zhang; Meidan Wang; Chen Li. Deactivation Mechanism and Anti-Deactivation Measures of Metal Catalyst in the Dry Reforming of Methane: A Review. Atmosphere 14(5), 770 (2023). https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos14050770

[doi:10.3390/atmos14050770](https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos14050770)

## Chemical contribution

In DRM, carbon deposition can deactivate metal catalysts by covering active sites, blocking reactant access, obstructing pores, impairing diffusion, and eventually causing mechanical damage or bed blockage. The paper gives conflicting generalizations about whether noble metals are less or more susceptible to sulfur poisoning than non-noble or transition-metal catalysts. This is a 2023 review article in Atmosphere titled "Deactivation Mechanism and Anti-Deactivation Measures of Metal Catalyst in the Dry Reforming of Methane: A Review," with DOI 10.3390/atmos14050770.
