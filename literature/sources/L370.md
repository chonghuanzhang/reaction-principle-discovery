# L370 — Palladium-Catalyzed Regio- and Enantioselective Hydrophosphination of gem-Difluoroallenes

**Online reference 9; Tier 2.**

Ji, D.; Qi, Z.; Li, X. Palladium-Catalyzed Regio- and Enantioselective Hydrophosphination of gem-Difluoroallenes. Organic Letters 2023, 25, 5957–5962. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02031.

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02031](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c02031)

## Chemical contribution

Pd-catalysed hydrophosphination of gem-difluoroallenes gives a CF2-retaining allylic phosphorus product from Ph2PH in 83% isolated yield after oxidation. Cy2PH and tBu2PH are unreactive under the examined conditions, and Ph2PH gives no desired coupling with the nonfluorinated terminal-allene control. The authors suggest inhibition for the dialkylphosphine failures. These controls support activation- and donor-specific transfer constraints; they do not demonstrate failure of Ph2PH with RCOCH2CF3.
