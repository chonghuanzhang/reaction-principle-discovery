# L234 — Fluoroketenes. VI. Cycloadditions of cumulenes to bis(trifluoromethyl)ketene

**SI reference 312; Tier 1.**

David C. England; Carl G. Krespan. Fluoroketenes. VI. Cycloadditions of cumulenes to bis(trifluoromethyl)ketene. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 35(10), 3322-3327 (1970). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00835a030

[doi:10.1021/jo00835a030](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00835a030)

## Chemical contribution

Butylethylketene reacted with 1 in hexane to give dione 6b in 87% yield, whereas diphenylketene did not react with 1 at 100°C over one week. Ketene and methylketene cycloadd to bis(trifluoromethyl)ketene in good yield to give beta-lactones derived only from addition across the carbon-carbon double bond of the nonfluorinated ketene. For cyclobutanedione formation, the authors state that their evidence cannot distinguish a discrete intermediate from an essentially concerted mechanism.
