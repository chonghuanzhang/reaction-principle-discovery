# L299 — Rapid synthesis of pomalidomide-conjugates for the development of protein degrader libraries

**Online reference 368; Tier 1.**

Duncan K. Brownsey; Ben C. Rowley; Evgueni Gorobets; Benjamin S. Gelfand; Darren J. Derksen. Rapid synthesis of pomalidomide-conjugates for the development of protein degrader libraries. Chemical Science 12(12), 4519-4525 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1039/d0sc05442a

[doi:10.1039/d0sc05442a](https://doi.org/10.1039/d0sc05442a)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction of propargylamine and 4-fluorothalidomide in DMF initially produced the desired compound 2a in 25% yield and an appreciable amount of byproduct 3, whose identity was confirmed by single-crystal X-ray diffraction; byproduct 3 was difficult to separate from 2a. The authors propose that DMF undergoes transformylation or decomposition in the presence of a primary amine at elevated temperature, releasing dimethylamine that competes as a nucleophile to form byproduct 3.
