# L201 — Rhodium‐Catalyzed Regioselective 3‐Fluoroallylic Cyanation

**SI reference 285; Tier 1.**

Christoph Middel; Lukas Zygalski; Jannick Meinecke; Ulrich Koert. Rhodium‐Catalyzed Regioselective 3‐Fluoroallylic Cyanation. Advanced Synthesis & Catalysis 363(4), 1028-1032 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202001324

[doi:10.1002/adsc.202001324](https://doi.org/10.1002/adsc.202001324)

## Chemical contribution

Fluorinated substrates gave exclusively the distal regioisomer in the reported scope, whereas the corresponding nonfluorinated substrate produced a 2:1 distal/proximal mixture, supporting a decisive fluorine substituent effect on regioselectivity. The reaction tolerated a variety of functional groups and diprotected amines, but steric hindrance alpha to the R1 residue appeared to suppress reaction, whereas alpha steric hindrance at R2 had no significant reactivity effect.
