# L237 — Addition of Carboxylic Acids to gem-Difluoroalkenes for the Synthesis of gem-Difluoromethylenated Compounds

**Online reference 315; Tier 2.**

Yuwei Zong; Gavin Chit Tsui. Addition of Carboxylic Acids to gem-Difluoroalkenes for the Synthesis of gem-Difluoromethylenated Compounds. Organic Letters 26(6), 1261-1264 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.4c00095

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.4c00095](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.4c00095)

## Chemical contribution

A trisubstituted gem-difluoroalkene reacted with p-toluenesulfonic acid and camphor-10-sulfonic acid to form RSO3-CF2 products in moderate yields, whereas tetrasubstituted substrate 1a did not react with those sulfonic acids; a BINOL-derived phosphoric acid gave no reaction with either substrate. The authors propose a concerted mechanism in which the carboxylic acid protonates the substrate ester while its oxygen attacks the beta carbon, followed by tautomerization of an enol intermediate; they suggest that this pathway avoids beta-fluoride elimination.
