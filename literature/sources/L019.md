# L019 — Au(I) Fluorido Phosphine Complexes: Tools for the Hydrofluorination of Alkynes

**Online reference 136; Tier 1.**

Simon G. Rachor; Ruben Jaeger; Thomas Braun. Au(I) Fluorido Phosphine Complexes: Tools for the Hydrofluorination of Alkynes. European Journal of Inorganic Chemistry 2022(17), e202200158 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejic.202200158

[doi:10.1002/ejic.202200158](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejic.202200158)

## Chemical contribution

Internal-alkyne reactivity depended strongly on substrate and conditions: diphenylacetylene required excess substrate and 50 °C; 3-hexyne gave 50% conversion at 50 °C and decomposed upon further heating; 2-butyne reacted, whereas hexafluorobutyne did not produce an observed vinyl complex even at elevated temperature. The authors propose that hydrofluorination proceeds through alkyne coordination to a cationic gold center, outer-sphere nucleophilic fluoride addition to form a vinyl species, and subsequent protodeauration.

### L019-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Gold was required for catalytic hydrofluorination under the tested conditions. Among the compared non-optimized systems, [Au(Cl)(SIPr)]/AgBF4 gave the highest yield and regioisomer ratio, whereas [Au(I)(SPhos)] was inactive unless AgBF4 was added.
