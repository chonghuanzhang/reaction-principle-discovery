# L033 — Rapid access to diverse, trifluoromethyl-substituted alkenes using complementary strategies

**SI reference 149; Tier 1.**

James P. Phelan; Rebecca J. Wiles; Simon B. Lang; Christopher B. Kelly; Gary A. Molander. Rapid access to diverse, trifluoromethyl-substituted alkenes using complementary strategies. Chemical Science 9(12), 3215-3220 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1039/c7sc05420c

[doi:10.1039/c7sc05420c](https://doi.org/10.1039/c7sc05420c)

## Chemical contribution

In Ni/photoredox coupling of the masked substrates, carbonate and other basic additives gave only partial conversion, whereas KF was identified as the most effective additive and enabled products in good yield. Lewis-basic sites impeded TMSOTf-mediated Peterson elimination: some substrates required super-stoichiometric TMSOTf or protonation of a basic site, while less-basic amines underwent elimination with catalytic TMSOTf. The authors state that these observations support Lewis-base-mediated deactivation of TMSOTf.

### L033-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Unmasking showed explicit limitations: the free-indole substrate underwent undesired side reactions, alcohol 2s gave only partial conversion even under forcing conditions, and product 3k required KHF2 treatment after concomitant silyl-mediated defluorination to return the material to the trifluoroborate salt.

Location: 4; Failed or difficult Peterson-elimination scope and side reactions

### L033-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Direct Suzuki cross-coupling of potassium trifluoromethylvinyl trifluoroborate with multiple aryl and heteroaryl bromides was demonstrated using palladium and phosphine ligands, including functionalized and drug-like substrates; some substrates required variation of the phosphine ligand.

Location: 5; Direct access to alpha-trifluoromethyl alkenes using potassium trifluoromethylvinyl trifluoroborate

### L033-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The paper reports two complementary routes to alpha-trifluoromethyl alkenes: diversification of alpha-trifluoromethyl-beta-silyl alcohols followed by Lewis-acid-promoted elimination, and direct cross-coupling with an alpha-trifluoromethylvinyl trifluoroborate reagent.

Location: 1; Abstract and overview of the two synthetic strategies
