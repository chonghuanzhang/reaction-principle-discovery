# L147 — Revealing the unusual role of bases in activation/deactivation of catalytic systems: O–NHC coupling in M/NHC catalysis

**Online reference 78; Tier 4.**

Victor M. Chernyshev; Oleg V. Khazipov; Maxim A. Shevchenko; Andrey Yu. Chernenko; Alexander V. Astakhov; Dmitry B. Eremin; Dmitry V. Pasyukov; Alexey S. Kashin; Valentine P. Ananikov. Revealing the unusual role of bases in activation/deactivation of catalytic systems: O–NHC coupling in M/NHC catalysis. Chemical Science 9(25), 5564-5577 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1039/c8sc01353e

[doi:10.1039/c8sc01353e](https://doi.org/10.1039/c8sc01353e)

## Chemical contribution

Base response depended on metal identity: Pt complexes 5a and 5b were almost unreactive toward KOH in dioxane over 20 h but gave azolone yields of 42% and 65% with tert-BuOK under otherwise matching conditions. The product boundary depended on NHC heterocycle stability: benzimidazole-derived complexes gave the highest azolone yields, whereas most imidazole- and 1,2,4-triazole-derived complexes gave only trace azolones because those products underwent instability and solvolysis.

### L147-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.
