# L218 — Chemoselective reactions of electrophiles with quinazoline amides; 3-alkylquinazoline-2,4-(1H,3H)-diones, and 2-arylquinazoline-4(3H)ones and their thioamide regioisomers

mohamed Abd El-Moneim; mohammed ibrahim megahed; Ibrahim Ahmed ali; walid fathalla. Chemoselective reactions of electrophiles with quinazoline amides; 3-alkylquinazoline-2,4-(1H,3H)-diones, and 2-arylquinazoline-4(3H)ones and their thioamide regioisomers. [doi:10.21608/ajbas.2025.399039.1263](https://doi.org/10.21608/ajbas.2025.399039.1263)

## Chemical contribution

3-Alkylquinazoline-2,4-diones 2a-c reacted with ethyl chloroacetate, dimethyl sulfate, or benzyl chloride in the presence of potassium carbonate to give chemoselective N-substituted products 3-5(a-c) in good yield; product assignments were based on NMR and physicochemical analysis. The authors propose that conjugation involving the lactim oxygen and an almost planar 2-aryl group raises the oxygen-centered HOMO and makes oxygen sufficiently soft to react with soft electrophiles.

### L218-S008
