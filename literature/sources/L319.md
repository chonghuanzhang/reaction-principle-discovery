# L319 — Mixed organofluorine-organosilicon chemistry. 3. A highly efficient and convenient synthesis of aryl perfluoroalk-1-enyl ketones from perfluoroalkyl iodides and aroylsilanes

**Online reference 387; Tier 1.**

Boniface Dondy; Charles Portella. Mixed organofluorine-organosilicon chemistry. 3. A highly efficient and convenient synthesis of aryl perfluoroalk-1-enyl ketones from perfluoroalkyl iodides and aroylsilanes. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 58(24), 6671-6674 (1993). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00076a028

[doi:10.1021/jo00076a028](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00076a028)

## Chemical contribution

Evidence supporting an enoxysilane intermediate included isolation of a sterically hindered perfluoroenoxysilane and isolation of the hydrolysis product corresponding to the unisolated trimethylsilyl analogue. The authors propose a multistep mechanism in which nucleophilic addition is followed by a Brook rearrangement, beta-fluoride elimination to a perfluoroenoxysilane, and fluoride attack to form the enone; whether the final step is concerted or proceeds through an enolate was stated to be unresolved.
