# L251 — Cytotoxicity of new alkylamino- and phenylamino-containing polyfluorinated derivatives of 1,4-naphthoquinone

**Online reference 326; Tier 1.**

Ol'ga D. Zakharova; Ludmila P. Ovchinnikova; Leonid I. Goryunov; Nadezhda M. Troshkova; Vitalij D. Shteingarts; Georgy A. Nevinsky. Cytotoxicity of new alkylamino- and phenylamino-containing polyfluorinated derivatives of 1,4-naphthoquinone. European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry 45(6), 2321-2326 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejmech.2010.02.009

[doi:10.1016/j.ejmech.2010.02.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejmech.2010.02.009)

## Chemical contribution

Reaction of quinone 6 with aniline at room temperature for 15 h gave approximately 90% compound 4 and approximately 10% further-transformation products in the product mixture; compound 4 was isolated in 62% yield. With tert-butylamine, the reaction plateaued at approximately 80% compound 1 and 20% starting material after 20 h; longer reaction times did not improve the major-product percentage and produced further-transformation products. Compound 1 was isolated in 62% yield.
