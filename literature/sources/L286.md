# L286 — Oxidative [3+2] Annulation of Pyridinium Salts with gem‐Difluoroalkenes: Synthesis of 2‐Fluoroindolizines

**Online reference 357; Tier 1.**

Li–Miao Yang; You‐Ya Zhang; Jing‐Tong Deng; Ai‐Jun Ma; Xiang‐Zhi Zhang; Shu‐Yu Zhang; Jin‐Bao Peng. Oxidative [3+2] Annulation of Pyridinium Salts with gem‐Difluoroalkenes: Synthesis of 2‐Fluoroindolizines. Asian Journal of Organic Chemistry 10(7), 1679-1682 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202100253

[doi:10.1002/ajoc.202100253](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajoc.202100253)

## Chemical contribution

For the model substrates, the optimized reported conditions used DBU, THF, and 90 °C and afforded product 3aa in 86% isolated yield; the table footnote specifies 2.0 equivalents of base and a 5 h reaction time. The proposed mechanism involves base-generated pyridinium ylide A, stepwise anionic or concerted [3+2] annulation with the gem-difluoroalkene to tetrahydroindolizine B, beta-fluoride elimination, and oxidative aromatization under air.
