# L309 — Au-Catalyzed Cross-Coupling of Arenes via Double C–H Activation

**Online reference 378; Tier 1.**

Xacobe C. Cambeiro; Nanna Ahlsten; Igor Larrosa. Au-Catalyzed Cross-Coupling of Arenes via Double C–H Activation. Journal of the American Chemical Society 137(50), 15636-15639 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5b10593

[doi:10.1021/jacs.5b10593](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5b10593)

## Chemical contribution

Near-stoichiometric substrate ratios retained cross-selectivity: 1.5 equivalents of pentafluorobenzene gave 70% yield, and 1.5 equivalents of tetrafluoropyridine gave 63% yield, without observed homocoupling in the former experiment. Stoichiometric bases, with or without Ag(I) salts, completely inhibited the cross-coupling, whereas omission of stoichiometric base and use of substoichiometric AgOPiv enabled catalytic turnover. The authors state that the exact mechanism is unclear; they interpret electron-rich-arene scope and regioselectivity as consistent with Au(III) C-H activation and suggest possible Ag involvement in electron-poor-arene C-H activation.
