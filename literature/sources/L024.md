# L024 — A Copper(I)‐Catalyzed Enantioselective γ‐Boryl Substitution of Trifluoromethyl‐Substituted Alkenes: Synthesis of Enantioenriched γ,γ‐ gem ‐Difluoroallylboronates

**SI reference 141; Tier 2.**

Ryoto Kojima; Sota Akiyama; Hajime Ito. A Copper(I)‐Catalyzed Enantioselective γ‐Boryl Substitution of Trifluoromethyl‐Substituted Alkenes: Synthesis of Enantioenriched γ,γ‐ gem ‐Difluoroallylboronates. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 57(24), 7196-7199 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201803663

[doi:10.1002/anie.201803663](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201803663)

## Chemical contribution

Alkenes bearing hexyl or methylcyclohexyl groups gave products in 89-90% yield and 96-97% ee; a more sterically congested branched alkene still gave 88% yield but only 50% ee. The authors anticipated that the present transformation could proceed through a mechanism analogous to asymmetric allylic boryl substitution, involving enantioselective addition of a borylcopper intermediate followed by elimination; this mechanism was proposed rather than directly established on the supplied pages.
