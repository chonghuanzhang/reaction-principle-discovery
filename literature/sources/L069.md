# L069 — New routes to hexafluorocyclopentadiene and related compounds

**Online reference 179; Tier 1.**

Richard R. Soelch; George W. Mauer; David M. Lemal. New routes to hexafluorocyclopentadiene and related compounds. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 50(26), 5845-5852 (1985). https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00350a079

[doi:10.1021/jo00350a079](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00350a079)

## Chemical contribution

The 2,5-dienone 4 was substantially less reactive than the 2,4-isomer: it gave only 10% hexafluorocyclopentadiene at 625 °C, while higher temperatures increased product formation but also increased unidentified impurities. The authors propose that pyrolysis of dienone 3 may proceed through electrocyclic ring opening to an alpha-fluoro ketene, decarbonylation to a carbene, and cyclization either directly or through hexafluorovinylcyclopropene; the cyclopropene intermediate was not observed and could not be ruled out.

### L069-S001

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In an alternative ring-expansion route, bromotrifluoroethylene and tetrafluorocyclopropene gave 4- and 3-bromocyclopentenes in an 84:16 ratio, but only 31% purified yield; subsequent zinc/zinc chloride reduction in diglyme furnished hexafluorocyclopentadiene in 92% yield and 98% purity.
