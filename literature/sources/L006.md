# L006 — Carbodefluorination of fluoroalkyl ketones via a carbene-initiated rearrangement strategy

**SI reference 74; Tier 2.**

Linxuan Li; Xinyu Zhang; Yongquan Ning; Xiaolong Zhang; Binbin Liu; Zhansong Zhang; Paramasivam Sivaguru; Giuseppe Zanoni; Shuang Li; Edward A. Anderson; Xihe Bi. Carbodefluorination of fluoroalkyl ketones via a carbene-initiated rearrangement strategy. Nature Communications 13(1), 4280 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-31976-z

[doi:10.1038/s41467-022-31976-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-31976-z)

## Chemical contribution

Isolated intermediate 209 gave product 3 in 91% yield under the reaction conditions and in 64% yield without silver catalyst. Pre-prepared ether 210 failed to produce rearrangement product 44 under the standard conditions, excluding the tested O-H carbene-insertion ether pathway. The authors propose that steric hindrance during electrophilic attack may explain the failure of tertiary alcohols.
