# L311 — Catalytic asymmetric defluorinative allylation of silyl enol ethers

**Online reference 380; Tier 2.**

Jordi Duran; Javier Mateos; Albert Moyano; Xavier Companyó. Catalytic asymmetric defluorinative allylation of silyl enol ethers. Chemical Science 14(26), 7147-7153 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1039/d3sc01498c

[doi:10.1039/d3sc01498c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d3sc01498c)

## Chemical contribution

The optimized asymmetric reaction used (DHQD)2PHAL in a THF/CH2Cl2 mixture and furnished product 3a in 75% yield, 14:1 branched-to-linear selectivity, greater than 20:1 syn diastereoselectivity, and 95:5 enantiomeric ratio after 72 hours. With DABCO, an ammonium intermediate was quantitatively formed from allyl fluoride 2b and fluoride release was confirmed by fluorine NMR; under (DHQD)2PHAL, the corresponding ammonium species was not detected and free catalyst was identified as the resting state.
