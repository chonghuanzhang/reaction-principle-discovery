# L212 — Combining Fluoroalkylation and Defluorination to Enable Formal [3 + 2 + 1] Heteroannulation by Using Visible-Light Photoredox Organocatalysis

**Online reference 293; Tier 1.**

Xue-Qiang Chu; Ting Xie; Lin Li; Danhua Ge; Zhi-Liang Shen; Teck-Peng Loh. Combining Fluoroalkylation and Defluorination to Enable Formal [3 + 2 + 1] Heteroannulation by Using Visible-Light Photoredox Organocatalysis. Organic Letters 20(9), 2749-2752 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.8b00963

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.8b00963](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.8b00963)

## Chemical contribution

Enolsilanes derived from aliphatic ketones gave products in moderate yields with reported remarkable regioselectivity, while conversion of 3-phenylpropanal gave a benzylated product in 30% yield. The authors proposed a sequence involving photoredox generation and addition of a perfluoroalkyl radical, oxidation to a carbocation, iodide attack and decomposition to an alpha-perfluoroalkylated ketone, base-mediated HF elimination to a beta-fluoroenone, and final condensation with an amidine.

### L212-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Eosin derivatives gave the best screened yield of 64% NMR, and switching to a TIPS enol ether increased performance to 99% NMR yield and 84% isolated yield; the authors associated this improvement with silyl-group stability and steric hindrance.

Location: 2; Photocatalyst and enol-silane optimization
