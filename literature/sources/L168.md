# L168 — Copper-catalyzed highly switchable defluoroborylation and hydrodefluorination of 1-(trifluoromethyl)alkynes

**SI reference 82; Tier 2.**

Jun Xu; Zhao-Cheng Yan; Li Liu; Long Qin; Xuan Fan; Yu Zou; Qi Zhang; Hua-Jian Xu. Copper-catalyzed highly switchable defluoroborylation and hydrodefluorination of 1-(trifluoromethyl)alkynes. Nature Communications 15(1), 7079 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51519-y

[doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51519-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51519-y)

## Chemical contribution

Time-course monitoring under Condition B showed that compound 102 and diborylated product 3 rose initially and then declined while monoborylated product 4 increased, supporting sequential conversion of 102 to 3 and then chemoselective hydrodeborylation to 4. The authors propose that a cuprous boryl species adds regioselectively to the alkyne, protonation forms intermediate 102, and secondary borylation followed by beta-fluoride elimination gives the Condition A product; subsequent ligand-, base-, and temperature-dependent deborylation steps account for Conditions B-D.
