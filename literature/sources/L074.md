# L074 — Palladium-Catalyzed Stereoselective Defluorination Arylation/Alkenylation/Alkylation of gem -Difluorinated Cyclopropanes

**SI reference 182; Tier 2.**

Ebrahim-Alkhalil M. A. Ahmed; Ayman M. Y. Suliman; Tian-Jun Gong; Yao Fu. Palladium-Catalyzed Stereoselective Defluorination Arylation/Alkenylation/Alkylation of gem -Difluorinated Cyclopropanes. Organic Letters 21(14), 5645-5649 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.9b01979

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.9b01979](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.9b01979)

## Chemical contribution

The gem-difluorinated-cyclopropane scope included aryl substrates bearing neutral, donating, and withdrawing substituents, a pyridine-containing substrate, an alkenyl-substituted substrate that formed a conjugated fluorodiene, and an estrone-derived substrate that gave product in 73% isolated yield. The arylboronic-acid scope included electron-rich, electron-poor, ortho-, meta-, and para-substituted partners and tolerated CF3, CN, aryl chloride, ether, ester, ketone, amine, ketal, and thianthrenyl functionality; the amine-containing substrate furnished only the C-C-coupled product, with no amination product observed.

### L074-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

In the reported optimization, KF did not promote the reaction, the tested Pd(II) catalysts were described as more efficient than the tested Pd(0) catalyst, lowering the temperature to 60 °C gave 45% of 3a, and no reaction occurred when both ligand and palladium catalyst were omitted.

Location: 2; Condition sensitivity and negative optimization results.

### L074-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Alkenylboronic acid gave only trace product under the original optimized conditions but coupled successfully after changing to Pd-G3 and Q-Phos; methylboronic acid also coupled under modified conditions to give product in 72% isolated yield with high Z/E selectivity.

Location: 3; Modified conditions for alkenyl- and alkylboronic acids.

<a id="statement-l074-s004"></a>

### L074-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

The reported standard conditions use gem-difluorinated cyclopropane, 1.5 equivalents of the boronic acid, 10 mol% Pd(OTFA)2, 15 mol% PtBu3·HBF4, one equivalent of Cs2CO3, and CH3CN at 80 °C under argon; the optimized model reaction gave 90% assay yield, 85% isolated yield, and 38:1 Z/E.

Location: 2; Optimization table and its footnotes.

### L074-S005

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; candidate-generation evidence.

The authors propose a catalytic pathway involving Pd(0)-mediated C-C bond activation of the gem-difluorinated cyclopropane, beta-fluoride elimination to an active complex, transmetalation with the boronic acid, and C-C bond-forming elimination that releases the Z-major product and regenerates Pd(0).

Location: 4; Proposed reaction mechanism.
