# L146 — Polymerization Catalysts with Cyclopentadienyl Ligands Ring-Fused to Pyrrole and Thiophene Heterocycles

**Online reference 244; Tier 1.**

John A. Ewen; Robert L. Jones; Michael J. Elder; Arnold L. Rheingold; Louise M. Liable-Sands. Polymerization Catalysts with Cyclopentadienyl Ligands Ring-Fused to Pyrrole and Thiophene Heterocycles. Journal of the American Chemical Society 120(41), 10786-10787 (1998). https://doi.org/10.1021/ja9823215

[doi:10.1021/ja9823215](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja9823215)

## Chemical contribution

The group-4 heterocenes were stable and highly active and stereospecific for propylene polymerization when combined with methylalumoxane, despite the known possibility that free Lewis bases inhibit catalysts. The three rac-Me2Si(4,6-L1)2Zr catalyst isomers produced isotactic polypropylene with Mw/Mn = 2.1, which the authors describe as characteristic of a single-site catalyst. The authors selected the heterocene ligands on the premise that their fused-ring structures and group 15 or 16 elements could help control polymer stereochemistry and molecular weight.
