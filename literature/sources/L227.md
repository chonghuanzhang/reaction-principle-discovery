# L227 — Correlating the Surface Basicity of Metal Oxides with Photocatalytic Hydroxylation of Boronic Acids to Alcohols

**Online reference 305; Tier 3.**

Wan Ru Leow; Jiancan Yu; Bin Li; Benhui Hu; Wei Li; Xiaodong Chen. Correlating the Surface Basicity of Metal Oxides with Photocatalytic Hydroxylation of Boronic Acids to Alcohols. Angewandte Chemie International Edition 57(31), 9780-9784 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201805395

[doi:10.1002/anie.201805395](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201805395)

## Chemical contribution

The reaction proceeded to high yield in anhydrous solvent, increasing water content reduced the initial yield, and the boronic acid pinacol ester gave negligible product yield. Basic and amphoteric oxides including MgO, TiO2, ZnO, and Al2O3 gave high alcohol yields, whereas acidic SiO2 and B2O3 gave low yields. Control experiments gave low product yields when either component of the Al2O3-Ru(bpy)3(2+) system was absent; negligible yield under nitrogen and low yield in the dark establish practical requirements for oxygen and light.
