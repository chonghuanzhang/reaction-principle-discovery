# L268 — Cryogenic Organometallic Carbon–Fluoride Bond Functionalization with Broad Functional Group Tolerance

**Online reference 341; Tier 2.**

D. Lucas Kane; Bryan C. Figula; Kaluvu Balaraman; Jeffery A. Bertke; Christian Wolf. Cryogenic Organometallic Carbon–Fluoride Bond Functionalization with Broad Functional Group Tolerance. Journal of the American Chemical Society 147(7), 5764-5774 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c13956

[doi:10.1021/jacs.4c13956](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c13956)

## Chemical contribution

Secondary alkyl fluorides showed an explicit condition boundary: at −40 °C approximately 10% alkene and 40% isomeric byproducts formed, whereas at −78 °C these side reactions were largely reduced and selected products were isolated in 80–85% yield. Transfer of an isobutyl group to 1-fluoro-2-iodoethane gave only 44% yield, with competing iodine/aluminum exchange identified as at least a partial cause of the compromised result.
