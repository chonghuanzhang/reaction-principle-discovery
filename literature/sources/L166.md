# L166 — Enabling Access to 3‐gem‐Difluorovinyl Lactams via Zn‐Mediated Sequential Single Electron Reductive Hydrodehalogenation

**Online reference 257; Tier 2.**

Jia Zheng; Xuran Liu; Jiawen Yin; Shuaikang Li; Juanjuan Zhang; Weigao Hu. Enabling Access to 3‐gem‐Difluorovinyl Lactams via Zn‐Mediated Sequential Single Electron Reductive Hydrodehalogenation. Chinese Journal of Chemistry 43(3), 255-260 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.202400718

[doi:10.1002/cjoc.202400718](https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.202400718)

## Chemical contribution

Zinc loading controlled reaction progression: 4 equivalents was optimal for 2a, while 1 equivalent gave intermediate product 3a exclusively in 90% yield. A pentafluoroethyl-substituted substrate failed to furnish the corresponding target product and instead formed a compound resulting from loss of one chlorine atom and two fluorine atoms. The authors propose sequential electron transfer and dehalogenation through alkenyl radical A, intermediate 3a, a Lewis-acid-activated carbonyl complex B, alkyl radical C, alpha-CF3 anion D, and final beta-fluoride elimination.
