# L330 — Carbodefluorination via a carbene-initiated rearrangement strategy

**Online reference 394; Tier 2.**

Linxuan Li; Xinyu Zhang; Yongquan Ning; Xiaolong Zhang; Binbin Liu; Zhansong Zhang; Paramasivam Sivaguru; Giuseppe Zanoni; Shuang Li; Edward A. Anderson; Xihe Bi. Carbodefluorination via a carbene-initiated rearrangement strategy. ChemRxiv (2022). https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-h5v60

[doi:10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-h5v60](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2022-h5v60)

## Chemical contribution

Isolated intermediate 209 gave product 3 in 91% yield under the reaction conditions and in 64% yield without silver catalyst. Pre-prepared ether 210 failed to give the defluorinative rearrangement product under standard conditions, which the authors used to exclude an O-H carbene-insertion ether pathway. Reaction monitoring showed initial formation of intermediate 209, which reached maximum intensity within one hour and was converted so that product 3 was nearly the sole reaction component by 16 hours.
