# L361 — Bayesian optimization- and high-throughput-guided synthesis of monofluoroalkenes via defluorinative C–C bond formation

**Online reference 60; Tier 1.**

Ge Gao; Qianghua Lin; Zhuoya Ma; Kuntai He; Yaxian Kong; Chenxi Yang; Chonghuan Zhang; Kuangbiao Liao. Bayesian optimization- and high-throughput-guided synthesis of monofluoroalkenes via defluorinative C–C bond formation. Chemical Science (2026). https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc05561c

[doi:10.1039/d6sc05561c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc05561c)

## Chemical contribution

The reported Pd-catalysed coupling of trifluoroethyl carbonyl compounds with organoboron reagents formed carbonyl-retaining monofluoroalkenes. The recorded baseline records 376 conditions, a 53-experiment selection sequence and 970 expansion records. It supplies the project’s established carbon–carbon starting transformation and an HTE/Bayesian-search context; its organoboron performance and proposed catalytic sequence do not automatically establish the reactive forms or compatibility of nitrogen, oxygen, sulfur, phosphorus or selenium partners.
