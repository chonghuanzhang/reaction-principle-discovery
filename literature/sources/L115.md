# L115 — The not so identical twins: (dis)similarities between reactive electrophile and oxidant sensing and signaling

**SI reference 218; Tier 3.**

Marcus J. C. Long; Kuan-Ting Huang; Yimon Aye. The not so identical twins: (dis)similarities between reactive electrophile and oxidant sensing and signaling. Chemical Society Reviews 50(22), 12269-12291 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1039/d1cs00467k

[doi:10.1039/d1cs00467k](https://doi.org/10.1039/d1cs00467k)

## Chemical contribution

For alpha,beta-unsaturated carbonyl electrophiles, increased electron deficiency and reduced steric hindrance at the beta carbon are associated with faster nucleophilic addition; HNE and ONE are described as reacting with cysteine thiols through 1,4-Michael addition. HNE reacts much faster with cysteine than with histidine or lysine under the summarized conditions: approximately 1 M-1 s-1 for cysteine at neutral pH versus approximately 0.002 and 0.001 M-1 s-1 for histidine and lysine, respectively.
