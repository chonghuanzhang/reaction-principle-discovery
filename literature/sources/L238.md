# L238 — Solvent Effects in the Nucleophilic Substitutions of Tetrahydropyran Acetals Promoted by Trimethylsilyl Trifluoromethanesulfonate: Trichloroethylene as Solvent for Stereoselective C - and O -Glycosylations

**SI reference 316; Tier 3.**

Joanna C. Kendale; Elizabeth M. Valentín; K. A. Woerpel. Solvent Effects in the Nucleophilic Substitutions of Tetrahydropyran Acetals Promoted by Trimethylsilyl Trifluoromethanesulfonate: Trichloroethylene as Solvent for Stereoselective C - and O -Glycosylations. Organic Letters 16(14), 3684-3687 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1021/ol501471c

[doi:10.1021/ol501471c](https://doi.org/10.1021/ol501471c)

## Chemical contribution

Nujol, pentane, hexane, heptane, cyclopentane, cyclohexane, 2,2,4-trimethylpentane, acetone, 2-butanone, and 1-butyl-3-methylimidazolium triflate either showed poor reactivity or failed the authors' availability or substrate-solubility criteria; the supplied text does not distinguish which limitation applied to each solvent. For acetal 1, higher reaction temperature decreased overall selectivity, and added exogenous triflate increased formation of the trans product rather than promoting the desired cis product. The authors attributed the effect of added ions to increased solvent polarity and favored ion-pair dissociation.
