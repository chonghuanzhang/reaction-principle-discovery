# L126 — 4-Oxo-2,3,5,6-tetrafluorocyclohexa-2,5-dienylidene—A Highly Electrophilic Triplet Carbene

**SI reference 229; Tier 1.**

Wolfram Sander; Rolf Hübert; Elfi Kraka; Jürgen Gräfenstein; Dieter Cremer. 4-Oxo-2,3,5,6-tetrafluorocyclohexa-2,5-dienylidene—A Highly Electrophilic Triplet Carbene. Chemistry 6(24), 4567-4579 (2000). https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3765(20001215)6:24<4567::aid-chem4567>3.0.co;2-a

[doi:10.1002/1521-3765(20001215)6:24](https://doi.org/10.1002/1521-3765(20001215)6:24)

## Chemical contribution

Carbene 1b was more reactive toward methane than tetrachlorinated carbene 1c; after irradiation in CH4-doped matrices, carbene and low concentrations of insertion product 8c were observed for 1c, and annealing gave only 34% 8c while 1b reacted almost completely under the same conditions. The relative methane reactivity decreased in the order 1b > 1c > 1a, while 1a did not react under the same conditions.
