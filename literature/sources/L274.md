# L274 — gem -Difluoroallene (DFA): an emerging versatile synthetic precursor to enable diverse fluorinated molecules

**SI reference 57; Tier 2.**

Kota Sathish; Swati Jain; Naveen Sihag; M. Ramu Yadav. gem -Difluoroallene (DFA): an emerging versatile synthetic precursor to enable diverse fluorinated molecules. Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry 22(40), 8078-8096 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob01253d

[doi:10.1039/d4ob01253d](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ob01253d)

## Chemical contribution

Copper/bipyridine-catalyzed defluoroarylation of DFA with aryl boronic esters and KOtBu was reported to form arylated monofluoroallenes by selective beta-fluorine elimination; pyridine-based boronic esters failed, and some highly substituted DFAs were inactive because the expected products were unstable. The authors present a proposed hydrophosphination pathway involving Pd(II)-phosphido formation, DFA reaction, gamma-selective migratory insertion, and protonolysis or sigma-bond metathesis; this mechanism is not established as an experimental fact by the supplied review text.

### L274-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

Rhodium/Josiphos-catalyzed asymmetric hydroamination of DFA with primary and secondary anilines produced gem-difluoroallylic amines; electron-neutral, electron-donating, and halo-substituted anilines gave 61-87% yield and 90-93% ee, while electron-withdrawing anilines gave 43-63% yield and 86-92% ee.

Location: 6; Gamma-selective asymmetric hydroamination of DFA.

### L274-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; candidate-generation evidence.

A rhodium/ligand system enabled beta- or gamma-selective thiol addition to monosubstituted DFA; aromatic and aliphatic thiols gave gamma-addition products in 56-96% yield and up to 99% ee, while a different ligand promoted beta addition in 58-77% yield.

Location: 4; Ligand-controlled regioselective and enantioselective thiol addition.
