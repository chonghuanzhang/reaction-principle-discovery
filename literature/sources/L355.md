## Chemical contribution

The 2023 author-abstract record establishes defluorinative thiolation of gem-difluoroalkenes by convergent paired electrolysis. It contributes an electrochemical activation alternative for constructing a sulfur-substituted monofluoroalkene from an already unsaturated fluorinated precursor. This is relevant to route and product-class comparisons for sulfur partners; abstract-only access does not establish individual yields, full operating parameters or compatibility with the project’s thermal catalyst/base conditions and trifluoroethyl carbonyl starting material.
