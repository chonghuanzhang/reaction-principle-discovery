# L034 — Selective N7 Alkylation of 7-Azaindazoles

**SI reference 150; Tier 1.**

Zhiyu Lei; Jianming Bao; Chen Cheng; Wensheng Yu; Bin Hu; Weichen Jin; Salman Jabri; Song Yang; Lianyun Zhao; Christopher J. Sinz; Joseph L. Duffy. Selective N7 Alkylation of 7-Azaindazoles. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 85(11), 7558-7564 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c00722

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.0c00722](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.0c00722)

## Chemical contribution

The isolated 2,2,2-trifluoroethyl product 14n was reported in 75% yield. The general alkylation procedure uses azaindazole and two equivalents of halogenoalkane in butanone at 80 °C for 16 hours. The authors hypothesize that base-dependent regioselectivity reflects a change from an anionic nucleophile with charge localized mainly at N1 to a neutral nucleophile whose most nucleophilic atom is N7.
