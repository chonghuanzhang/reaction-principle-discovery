# L130 — Defluorinative functionalization approach led by difluoromethyl anion chemistry

**Online reference 232; Tier 2.**

Kensuke Muta; Kazuhiro Okamoto; Hiroki Nakayama; Shuto Wada; Aiichiro Nagaki. Defluorinative functionalization approach led by difluoromethyl anion chemistry. Nature Communications 16(1), 416 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52842-0

[doi:10.1038/s41467-024-52842-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52842-0)

## Chemical contribution

Countercation identity strongly affected the reaction: lithium gave only trace desired product, while sodium and potassium improved difluoromethylbenzene yield, with the organopotassium reductant giving the best selective hydrodefluorination. Longer residence times or higher temperatures substantially decreased yield, establishing strict residence-time and temperature control as an explicit condition boundary. The authors propose that DME coordination draws potassium away from naphthalene, facilitating faster electron transfer and formation of difluoromethyl anion 2a.
