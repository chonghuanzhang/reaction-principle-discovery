# L180 — Stereoselective Rhodium(I)-Catalyzed C–F Bond Arylation of Tri- and Tetrasubstituted gem-Difluoroalkenes with Boronic Acids

**Online reference 31; Tier 2.**

Hao Tan; Yuwei Zong; Yihan Tang; Gavin Chit Tsui. Stereoselective Rhodium(I)-Catalyzed C–F Bond Arylation of Tri- and Tetrasubstituted gem-Difluoroalkenes with Boronic Acids. Organic Letters 25(5), 877-882 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00108

[doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00108](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.3c00108)

## Chemical contribution

For a benzyl-substituted substrate, the standard conditions gave only 27% yield and a 4:1 E:Z ratio, but changing to [Rh(COD)(OH)]2 and K2CO3 gave 88% yield and greater than 99:1 dr. Within the trisubstituted difluorostyrene scope, an alkyl-substituted gem-difluoroalkene was unreactive; alkenyl and alkyl boronic acids were also unreactive. The authors propose that a BINAP–Rh(I)–aryl species undergoes regioselective migratory insertion into the gem-difluoroalkene to form an alpha-Rh(I) alkyl intermediate, followed by beta-fluoride elimination to produce the monofluoroalkene and regenerate Rh(I).
