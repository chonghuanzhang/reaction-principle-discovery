# L343 — Difluoroenol phosphinates as difluoroenolate surrogates: synthesis and applications in defluorination and deoxygenative coupling

**SI reference 2; Tier 2.**

Kurosawa, M. B.; Shimoyama, S.; Tanaka, H.; Yamaguchi, J. Difluoroenol phosphinates as difluoroenolate surrogates: synthesis and applications in defluorination and deoxygenative coupling. Chemical Science 2025, 16, 13390–13400. https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc03915k.

[doi:10.1039/d5sc03915k](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc03915k)

## Chemical contribution

The final article studies P(O)H reagents with RCOCF3, where CF3 is directly adjacent to the carbonyl. Ph2P(O)H/K2CO3 gives a difluoromethyl ketone, while molecular sieves favor isolation of a difluoroenol phosphinate. Higher reagent loading and temperature permit additional defluorination. Strongly electron-withdrawing ketones fail in the reported substrate scope, and a protonated phospha-Brook side product is not a competent intermediate. The authors propose P-centred addition followed by migration from carbon to oxygen and fluoride elimination. The isolated enol products contain C–O–P, not the target vinyl C–P linkage. These observations identify competing pathways and endpoint dependence; they do not demonstrate Ph2PH failure with RCOCH2CF3. Final-version yields and locators are given in [P/Se applicability](../../evidence/applicability/README.md).

### L343-S002

**LITERATURE FACT**; supporting literature evidence.

A quinoline substrate with HP(O)Ph2 gave 20% difluoroketone and 35% further-defluorinated product; dibutyl phosphite at 90 °C and 1.0 equivalent gave 53% difluoroketone.

<a id="statement-l343-s003"></a>

### L343-S003

**LITERATURE FACT**; supporting literature evidence.

The monofluoro enol O–P intermediate formed with HP(O)Ph2 could not be isolated and converted completely to a monofluoromethyl ketone; the corresponding O–P product could be isolated using diethyl phosphite.

<a id="statement-l343-s004"></a>

### L343-S004

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

Ph2P(O)H (1.2 equiv) and K2CO3 (2 equiv), 1,4-dioxane, 80 °C, 6 h convert model RCOCF3 to RCOCHF2 in 80% yield. With 4 Å molecular sieves, DFEP 2Aa is obtained in 80% yield. The change concerns endpoint selection, not disappearance of P reactivity.

Location: Fig. 2A; Primary article paragraph 7

<a id="statement-l343-s005"></a>

### L343-S005

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

2.5 equiv Ph2P(O)H at 130 °C gives monofluoromethyl ketone 4A in 67%; 4 equiv at 160 °C gives methyl ketone 6A in 47%. A monofluoromethyl ketone is not a monofluorovinyl C–P product.

Location: Fig. 2C; Primary article paragraph 9

<a id="statement-l343-s006"></a>

### L343-S006

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

The final primary text reports failure and recovery for strongly electron-withdrawing substituted trifluoromethyl ketones. Individual identities and yields are not assigned in that paragraph. Hydration-related interference is the authors’ mechanistic explanation; the source calls the hydrate a hemiacetal; the exact structural terminology remains unresolved. This result does not establish failure of the project electrophile.

Location: Fig. 3; Primary article paragraph 12

<a id="statement-l343-s007"></a>

### L343-S007

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

Separately prepared protonated phospha-Brook side product 5A gives no 3A under K2CO3/80 °C conditions. This distinguishes an unproductive trapped intermediate from the productive anionic elimination sequence; it is not a failed addition of Ph2PH to project substrate A.

Location: Fig. 4A; Primary article paragraph 16

<a id="statement-l343-s008"></a>

### L343-S008

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

With Ph2P(O)H the monofluoroenol phosphinate could not be isolated and monofluoromethyl ketone formed instead. Diethyl phosphite and p-methoxy diarylphosphine oxide provide isolable enol phosphinates. The relevant retained phosphorus linkage is C–O–P; inability to isolate it cannot establish inability to form direct vinyl C–P.

Location: Fig. 4B; Primary article paragraph 17

<a id="statement-l343-s009"></a>

### L343-S009

**LITERATURE FACT**; P/Se applicability.

Under condition A, competing RCOCF3 reacts while difluoromethyl ketone 3A is recovered in 84% and 4A is not formed. Under stronger conditions the same class can proceed further. Failure is therefore condition/endpoint specific.

Location: Fig. 4C; Primary article paragraph 18

### L343-S010

**AUTHOR HYPOTHESIS**; P/Se applicability.

The authors propose P-centred Pudovik addition, phospha-Brook migration of phosphorus from carbon to oxygen, beta-fluoride elimination and HF-dependent endpoint selection.

Location: Fig. 4E; Primary article paragraph 20
