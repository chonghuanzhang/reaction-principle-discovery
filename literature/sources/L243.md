# L243 — Solvent effects in catalysis: rational improvements of catalysts via manipulation of solvent interactions

**SI reference 319; Tier 3.**

Paul J. Dyson; Philip G. Jessop. Solvent effects in catalysis: rational improvements of catalysts via manipulation of solvent interactions. Catalysis Science & Technology 6(10), 3302-3316 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1039/c5cy02197a

[doi:10.1039/c5cy02197a](https://doi.org/10.1039/c5cy02197a)

## Chemical contribution

In an ionic-liquid Suzuki system, neat [bbim][BF4] gave no reaction for iodobenzene, whereas adding methanol gave greater than 90% yield under equivalent conditions; other neat ionic liquids also failed. Solvents interact directly with catalysts, substrates, and products, and these interactions can increase or decrease catalytic reaction rate or selectivity. DMA is proposed to have dual roles in Suzuki coupling by coordinating to the metal catalyst and acting as a base.
