# L141 — Palladium and copper co-catalyzed chloro-arylation of gem -difluorostyrenes – use of a nitrite additive to suppress β-F elimination

**Online reference 85; Tier 2.**

Andrew J. Intelli; Coriantumr Z. Wayment; Ryan T. Lee; Kedong Yuan; Ryan A. Altman. Palladium and copper co-catalyzed chloro-arylation of gem -difluorostyrenes – use of a nitrite additive to suppress β-F elimination. Chemical Science 15(42), 17571-17578 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1039/d4sc04939j

[doi:10.1039/d4sc04939j](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4sc04939j)

## Chemical contribution

Early reaction development produced mixtures of desired chloro-arylation product, β-fluoride-elimination products, and a chloro-sulfonylation product; substrate electronics affected the side-product distribution. Pd(OAc)2 and copper powder were the best-performing catalyst combination in the reported catalyst-system studies, and routine optimization identified 130 °C in 1,4-dioxane for 3 hours as optimal. The authors propose that nitrite binding to a putative Pd(Cl)(alkyl) intermediate may either block the coordination geometry needed for β-fluoride elimination or accelerate C–Cl reductive elimination through a nitrite π-back-bonding effect.
