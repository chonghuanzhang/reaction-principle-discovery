# L104 — General Co-catalytic Hydrothiolation of gem -Difluoroalkenes

**Online reference 26; Tier 2.**

Jacob P. Sorrentino; Ryan M. Herrick; Mohammed K. Abd El-Gaber; Ahmed Z. Abdelazem; Ankit Kumar; Ryan A. Altman. General Co-catalytic Hydrothiolation of gem -Difluoroalkenes. The Journal of Organic Chemistry 87(24), 16676-16690 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343

[doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343)

## Chemical contribution

The method tolerates both primary and secondary alkyl thiols in high reported yields, and tertiary thiols react with variable moderate-to-excellent outcomes. Aqueous conditions support coupling of protected and unprotected cysteine derivatives and glutathione, with reported yields of 75-92% for cysteine products and 90% for the glutathione product. The authors speculate that the aniline additive may mediate redox events or act as a PCET co-catalyst and hydrogen-atom donor, but explicitly state that robust mechanistic support is absent.
