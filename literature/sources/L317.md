# L317 — Enantio‐ and Diastereoselective Rh(I)‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Arylation of Pentafluoroethyl Alkenes to Access Fluoroalkenes Featuring a Unique F‐ and CF 3 ‐Substituted sp 2 ‐Carbon †

**Online reference 385; Tier 2.**

Hao Tan; Yihan Tang; Gavin Chit Tsui. Enantio‐ and Diastereoselective Rh(I)‐Catalyzed Defluorinative Arylation of Pentafluoroethyl Alkenes to Access Fluoroalkenes Featuring a Unique F‐ and CF3 ‐Substituted sp2‐Carbon. Chinese Journal of Chemistry 43(24), 3535-3542 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.70284

[doi:10.1002/cjoc.70284](https://doi.org/10.1002/cjoc.70284)

## Chemical contribution

Quinoline substitution changed or suppressed stereochemical outcome: the C-3 substrate gave Z product at 96:4 d.r., the C-2 substrate gave E products at 92:8 d.r., and the C-4 substrate underwent essentially no reaction. For the 1,1-disubstituted model reaction, PhBpin and phenylboroxine were comparable to phenylboronic acid, whereas PhBF3K was ineffective; less K3PO4, toluene/water, and temperatures of 50 or 100 °C lowered yield.
