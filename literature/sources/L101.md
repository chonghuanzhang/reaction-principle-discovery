# L101 — CF Activation in Perfluorinated Arenes with Isonitriles under UV‐Light Irradiation

**SI reference 205; Tier 1.**

Abhishek Dewanji; Christian Mück‐Lichtenfeld; Klaus Bergander; Constantin G. Daniliuc; Armido Studer. C–F Activation in Perfluorinated Arenes with Isonitriles under UV‐Light Irradiation. Chemistry – A European Journal 21(35), 12295-12298 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201502298

[doi:10.1002/chem.201502298](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201502298)

## Chemical contribution

Short-wavelength irradiation was essential: irradiation at 365 nm or with visible light gave no reaction, and a 254 nm photoreactor used with Pyrex glass that blocked light below 300 nm gave no conversion. For isonitrile 1a in hexafluorobenzene, irradiation at 254 nm at about 35 °C for 18 h afforded imidoyl fluoride 3a in 98% yield, whereas heating without irradiation produced no reaction.
