# Tier 2 — Defluorination, fluorinated intermediates and related C–F cleavage

This chemical-relevance category contains 101 source records. A tier is not a source-quality ranking and does not imply a uniform access level.

- [L055 — Difluoroenol Phosphinates as Versatile Difluoroenolate Surrogates: Synthesis and Applications in Defluorination and Deoxygenative Coupling](../sources/L055.md). Separately prepared addition product 5A did not form 3A when treated with potassium carbonate at 80 °C, excluding 5A as a productive intermediate under that test. The authors propose Pudovik addition, phospha-Brook rearrangement, and beta-fluoride elimination to form an enol phosphinate and HF; they further propose that HF trapping favors the phosphinate while protonation without a trapping agent gives the difluoromethyl ketone.

- [L343 — Difluoroenol phosphinates as difluoroenolate surrogates: synthesis and applications in defluorination and deoxygenative coupling](../sources/L343.md). The final article studies P(O)H reagents with RCOCF3, where CF3 is directly adjacent to the carbonyl. Ph2P(O)H/K2CO3 gives a difluoromethyl ketone, while molecular sieves favor isolation of a difluoroenol phosphinate. Higher reagent loading and temperature permit additional defluorination. Strongly electron-withdrawing ketones fail in the reported substrate scope, and a protonated phospha-Brook side product is not a competent intermediate. The authors propose P-centred addition followed by migration from carbon to oxygen and fluoride elimination. The isolated enol products contain C–O–P, not the target vinyl C–P linkage. These observations identify competing pathways and endpoint dependence; they do not demonstrate Ph2PH failure with RCOCH2CF3. Final-version yields and locators are given in [P/Se applicability](../../evidence/applicability/README.md).

- [L347 — Diselenide-enabled photocatalytic hydroazolation of gem -difluoroalkenes](../sources/L347.md). Diphenyl diselenide improves photocatalytic hydroazolation of gem-difluoroalkenes and is not consumed by a hydroselenolation side reaction under the reported conditions. Benzimidazole affords a CF2-retaining product in 87%, whereas several azaindoles instead form monofluorovinyl products. The authors propose that selenium-derived species mediate photocatalyst regeneration, deprotonation and hydrogen-atom transfer. This is Tier 2 evidence for intermediate fate and fluorination-endpoint selection, with a secondary role in considering competing endpoints. Selenium is a co-catalyst rather than the transferred nucleophile; the study is not a negative test of project PhSeH coupling.

- [L370 — Palladium-Catalyzed Regio- and Enantioselective Hydrophosphination of gem-Difluoroallenes](../sources/L370.md). Pd-catalysed hydrophosphination of gem-difluoroallenes gives a CF2-retaining allylic phosphorus product from Ph2PH in 83% isolated yield after oxidation. Cy2PH and tBu2PH are unreactive under the examined conditions, and Ph2PH gives no desired coupling with the nonfluorinated terminal-allene control. The authors suggest inhibition for the dialkylphosphine failures. These controls support activation- and donor-specific transfer constraints; they do not demonstrate failure of Ph2PH with RCOCH2CF3.

- [L093 — Recent progress in the racemic and enantioselective synthesis of monofluoroalkene-based dipeptide isosteres](../sources/L093.md). A reported defluorinative allylic alkylation of a terminal 3,3-difluoropropene with triethylaluminium selectively produced a (Z)-monofluoroalkene. The authors describe a proposed heteroatom-nucleophile variant of the defluorinative reaction using Me2AlCl or (iPrO)2AlN3 and report observed (Z)-selectivity, but the extracted text does not independently establish the detailed reaction scope or whether the heteroatom is installed in the initial defluorination step.

- [L321 — Regio-, stereo-, and enantioselective ipso- and migratory defluorinative olefin cross-coupling to access highly functionalized monofluoroalkenes](../sources/L321.md). The authors interpret styrene-isomer accumulation as evidence that alkene isomerization is faster than asymmetric hydroalkenylation, and interpret isotope-labeling results as indicating that the achiral ligand participates in isomerization but minimally, if at all, in styrene hydroalkenylation.

- [L042 — Recent Advances in Transition Metal‐Catalyzed Functionalization of gem‐Difluoroalkenes](../sources/L042.md). - [L318 — Ring-Restructuring of gem-Difluorocyclopropanes: Diverse Access to Four- to Nine-Membered Ring Skeletons](../sources/L318.md). A Pd-IPent-catalyzed reaction of gem-difluorocyclopropanes with enamides constructs multisubstituted N-H pyrroles through cleavage of a cyclopropane C-C bond, two C-F bonds, and a C-N bond; the products were reported in good-to-excellent yields. The review identifies a scope limitation: current gem-difluorocyclopropane methods predominantly use monosubstituted substrates, while sterically demanding disubstituted and multisubstituted derivatives remain a challenge. The review states that gem-difluorinated metallocyclobutane intermediates are proposed for transition-metal-catalyzed cyclizations, but the precise mechanisms remain unresolved and require further experimental and computational study.

- [L322 — Fluoroolefins-Mediated Deoxygenative Substitution of Alcohols: Chemo- and Enantiospecific Construction of C–S, C–N, C–O, and C–Se Bonds](../sources/L322.md). Isolated O-tethered monofluoroalkenes 7 and 9 reacted with thiol 3a to produce thioether 4a in 89% and 96% yield, respectively, with 99% ee, directly supporting their competence as substitution intermediates. Both base and fluoroolefin activator were required: no reaction occurred when either component was omitted. The authors propose that defluorinative reaction of the alcohol with the fluoroolefin forms O-tethered monofluoroalkene A, followed by SN2 substitution with inversion of configuration.

- [L325 — Development of Copper(I)-Catalyzed Synthesis of Functionalized Organofluorine and Organoboron Compounds](../sources/L325.md). Under the reported Chapter 2 conditions, the sterically congested branched substrate (E)-1d gave 88% yield but 50% ee, while the tetra-substituted product 2e was detected only in trace amount; the author attributed the latter result probably to steric hindrance. For the Chapter 1 copper(I)/diboron system, the authors propose coordination and insertion of a gem-difluoroalkene into a borylcopper(I) species, followed by elimination and copper(I)-catalyzed protodeborylation; DFT calculations favor the transition state leading to the observed Z isomer, but the quotation does not directly establish every proposed catalytic intermediate.

- [L326 — Photocatalytic Dual-Defluorination Thiolation of Trifluoromethyl Hydrazones](../sources/L326.md). No product formed without light; omission of the photocatalyst gave dramatically lower yield and poor E/Z selectivity; TEMPO almost completely inhibited the reaction. The authors propose a radical-carbanion relay in which base-generated hydrazone carbanion is oxidized by photoexcited Ru complex, proceeds through radical and diazo species, loses nitrogen, is reduced to an anion, and undergoes beta-fluoride elimination to form a gem-difluoroolefin.

- [L330 — Carbodefluorination via a carbene-initiated rearrangement strategy](../sources/L330.md). Isolated intermediate 209 gave product 3 in 91% yield under the reaction conditions and in 64% yield without silver catalyst. Pre-prepared ether 210 failed to give the defluorinative rearrangement product under standard conditions, which the authors used to exclude an O-H carbene-insertion ether pathway. Reaction monitoring showed initial formation of intermediate 209, which reached maximum intensity within one hour and was converted so that product 3 was nearly the sole reaction component by 16 hours.

- [L333 — Exploring the mechanisms of nickel/rhodium-catalyzed cross-coupling and rhodium-catalyzed olefin hydroboration reactions, and rhenium/tantalum metalacyclic complex formation : a theoretical study](../sources/L333.md). The calculated oxidative-addition barrier is about 27.2 kcal/mol, and oxidative addition was identified as the rate-determining step in the modeled reaction. The reported reaction couples 2,2-difluorovinyl benzoates with arylboronic acids under nickel catalysis to produce gem-difluoroenol ethers. The DFT-supported proposed catalytic sequence is oxidative addition of the vinyl C-O bond, base exchange, transmetalation with the arylboronic acid, and reductive elimination.

- [L336 — Competition of Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution, σ‐Bond Metathesis, and syn Hydrometalation in Titanium(III)‐Catalyzed Hydrodefluorination of Arenes](../sources/L336.md). A brominated substrate undergoes competing catalytic hydrodebromination with low turnover, and the absence of the second hydrodefluorination products seen for hexafluorobenzene was interpreted as evidence that the active species is poisoned. Pentafluoropyridine and perfluorotoluene undergo para hydrodefluorination smoothly at room temperature with 1.2 mol% catalyst loading. For hexafluorobenzene, the computed syn-hydrometallation pathway contains a hydrometallation intermediate followed by facile fluoride elimination; the authors further suggest that fluoride is trapped by excess silane and transferred to titanium.

- [L337 — Defluorinative Alkylboration of Alkenes Enabled by Dual Photoredox and Copper Catalysis](../sources/L337.md). Reaction efficiency was sensitive to the copper source and solvent: other Cu(I) or Cu(II) salts and less-polar solvents gave dramatically lower yields. Under the optimized conditions, the model product was obtained in 86% yield with excellent regioselectivity; only trace side products were reported. The authors proposed SET reduction of an in situ PhCF3/[HO-B2pin2]- species followed by fluoride elimination to form a difluoroalkyl radical, radical addition to the alkene, copper capture, a Cu(III) intermediate, and reductive elimination.
