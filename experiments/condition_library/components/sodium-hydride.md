# NaH — sodium hydride

Type: base/additive. Chemical role: strong deprotonation and precursor-generation dimension.

Evidence level: exact component in related literature or seed. Exact component in alkoxide-mediated vinylic fluoride substitution; NaH/DME enabled tert-butoxy product where Na/tBuOH did not.

Source support: [L294](../../../literature/sources/L294.md) [25]; [10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8); PDF p3. The sodium/tert-butanol condition gave no detected conversion of butenolide 1, whereas the NaH/DME condition afforded product 9 in 53% yield.
