# K3PO4 — tripotassium phosphate

Type: base/additive. Chemical role: inorganic phosphate; basicity/counterion dimension.

Evidence level: exact component in related literature or seed. Exact literature condition on a related CF3-ketone defluorination/arylation; source precursor/metal/temperature remain specific.

The α-trifluoromethylated ketone 3a underwent a Pd-catalyzed defluorination/arylation with phenylboronic acid to give fluorinated α,β-unsaturated ketone 5 in 76% yield with high Z-selectivity.
