Type: base/additive. Chemical role: phosphate plus counterion/hydration dimension.

Evidence level: family only exact dodecahydrate not established. Na3PO4 and hydrated phosphate family support exists; exact dodecahydrate is not established by these citations. Na2HPO4·12H2O and Na3PO4·12H2O are distinct compounds.
