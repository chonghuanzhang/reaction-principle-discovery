Type: base/additive. Chemical role: phosphate plus counterion/hydration dimension.

Evidence level: family only exact dodecahydrate not established. Na₃PO₄ and hydrated phosphate family support exists; exact dodecahydrate is not established by these citations. Na₂HPO₄·12H₂O and Na₃PO₄·12H₂O are distinct compounds.
