# Representative recommendation-to-experiment relationships

The correspondence analysis uses molecular identity and intended donor role rather than response values. Three representative examples illustrate its levels:

- **Exact identity:** recommended indoline and AP044 indoline are the same molecular input.
- **Same scaffold or positional variation:** recommended 2-naphthol and AP047/AP048 1-naphthol retain the naphthol scaffold and O-H donor but differ in substitution position.
- **Same donor family, different scaffold:** recommended 2-naphthalenethiol and AP108 benzene-1,3-dithiol share an aryl-thiol donor class but are not the same scaffold or equivalent reagents.

The mappings make the translation from RP-derived recommendations to laboratory inputs explicit. They do not imply that structurally related molecules share a response, and unmatched recommendations remain visible.
