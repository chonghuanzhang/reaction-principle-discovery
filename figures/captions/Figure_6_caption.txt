These fractions summarize the tested collections under their respective electrophile and reaction-condition combinations. b, Exact structure intersections among the complete experimental ledgers: all 102 unique donors in the RP experimental ledger, 20 direct-LLM structures and 20 unique literature-selection structures. The RP-only, LLM-only, literature-only, RP+LLM and RP+literature counts are 99, 18, 19, 2 and 1, respectively. c,d, Structures and recorded qualitative outcomes for the direct-LLM and literature-selection collections. Direct-LLM `不反应` is shown as No product; literature `trace` is shown as Trace. Solid blue outlines mark exact matches to donors in the complete 102-donor RP experimental ledger. Dashed orange outlines mark exact matches to the 100 RP-guided recommendations: two in the direct-LLM collection and five in the literature-selection collection. Literature trace was counted as a negative outcome.
