Fig. 3 | Evidence-to-principle-to-candidate provenance network. Seventeen evidence nodes support four reaction principles, which connect to 43 precursor families and 100 recommended nucleophiles (N, 40; O, 30; S, 25; P, 5). The network contains 19 evidence-to-principle edges, 172 principle-to-family edges and 100 family-membership edges. Filled and open candidate nodes respectively denote recommendations with and without a direct candidate-level citation link; the links record provenance rather than evidence of reaction success.
