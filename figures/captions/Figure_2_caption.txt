Fig. 2 | From literature evidence to reaction design through four conditional reaction principles. a, Target transformation: selective defluorinative coupling of trifluoroethyl carbonyl substrates with alternative nucleophilic donors to generate monofluoroalkenes while retaining the carbonyl group. b, Organization of 370 literature sources into four evidence tiers according to their chemical roles: reaction precedents (Tier 1, 139 sources), elementary reaction steps (Tier 2, 101 sources), reactive species and medium effects (Tier 3, 88 sources), and competing pathways (Tier 4, 42 sources). Source-linked evidence records distinguish experimental observations, mechanistic interpretations and applicability information. c, Conceptual organization of literature-guided reaction discovery, linking reviewed evidence, the four-principle synthesis and the 100-candidate collection to human evaluation of 112 substrate–partner pairs and Bayesian optimization (BO) in the shared 13,920-condition decision space, with twelve initial conditions and four recommendations per round. d, Four conditional reaction principles governing selective defluorinative functionalization.
