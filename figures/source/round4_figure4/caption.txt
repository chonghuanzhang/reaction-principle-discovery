Fig. 4 | Candidate diversity, experimental associations and novelty classifications. a, Mutually exclusive reacting-centre label sets for 100 recommended molecules; mixed C/O and C/N categories each contain one molecule. Radial bar lengths represent molecule counts. b, Representative neutral, ionic and precursor forms in the recommendation set. c, The collection contains 100 molecules, 115 site or pathway hypotheses and 124 proposed product options. d, Expert assessment considered structural relationships, commercial accessibility and separate product-connectivity (NP) and reaction-precedent (NR) classifications before the final experimental donor list was chosen. The examples associate C048 aniline with indoline (AP039; also AP044), C051 phenol with 1-naphthol (AP048), and C082 benzenethiol with 4-tert-butylbenzenethiol (AP090). All three are structural analogues. Arrows denote structural associations, not chemical transformations or independently documented replacement decisions; NP2/NR2 labels refer to the proposed endpoint of each actual electrophile–donor pair. e, NP and NR category distributions for 100 candidate molecules and 108 recorded experimental pairs. Bar lengths show category fractions within each population; numerals label counts for categories containing at least five records. Mixed, unresolved and not-applicable records remain explicit. NP and NR are separate ordinal classifications from bounded searches; the different record units and endpoints preclude interpreting these distributions as novelty retention, improvement or reaction-success probabilities.
