Fig. 1 | An LLM-assisted, reaction-principle-guided framework for selective defluorinative reaction discovery. a, Literature evidence and LLM-assisted reasoning based on 370 source records. b, Four transferable principles governing acceptor activation, reactive donor speciation, intermediate pathway divergence and product stability. c, Generation of 100 candidate reaction partners and human evaluation of experimental inputs. d, Independent Bayesian optimization for N–H, O–H and S–H in a shared decision space comprising 145 catalyst–ligand and loading formulations, eight solvents and twelve bases, using twelve random initial conditions and four recommendations per round. e, High-throughput experimental validation across 112 substrate–partner combinations. The condition-recommendation workflow and substrate-scope HTE are separate study components.
