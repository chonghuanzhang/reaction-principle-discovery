# Product and experiment counting units

The product, donor and experiment totals in the article answer different
questions and are not expected to be equal.

| Record | Count | Counting unit | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| [Main Table 1](../experiments/product_scope/) | 44 | displayed product assignments | Products 3a-3ar comprise 11 N-, 13 S- and 20 O-functionalized structures. The displayed yields are quantitative 1H NMR yields obtained with an internal standard. |
| Figure 6 RP-guided collection | 35/94 | unique donor structures | A donor is positive when at least one assigned product peak has a numerical UV response greater than zero. This is a donor-level screening summary, not a count of product structures. |
| Experimental-reagent collection | 102 | unique donor structures | Structures represented across the complete ordered-pair ledger. |
| Experimental-pair ledger | 112 | ordered electrophile-donor pairs | One donor may occur with more than one electrophile, so pair and donor totals differ. |
| SI Section S7.5 | 12 | selected NMR characterization entries | It contains 11 monofluoroalkene products from Table 1 and one nonfluorinated phosphorus-containing product outside the 44-product set. |

The 44 Table 1 entries and the 35 positive donors therefore use different
denominators. A single donor can contribute products with different
electrophiles, and a donor-level positive call requires only one assigned
nonzero product response. Selected products from the high-throughput screen
were isolated and characterized by NMR spectroscopy (SI Sections S7.5 and
S7.6). The 12 entries in SI S7.5 document a selected NMR characterization set
and should not be used as the Table 1 product total.

The [Table 1 source and product records](../experiments/product_scope/)
identify 3n as the para-methyl arylthio product (13%, AP097) and 3q as the
para-trifluoromethyl arylthio product (11%, AP100). Both correspond to
existing entries in the 112-pair ledger.

The repository retains proposed product options separately from measured
products. The 103 records in [Table R2](../candidates/product_options/) are
site- or pathway-level proposed connectivities generated during candidate
design; they are not 103 experimentally observed products.
