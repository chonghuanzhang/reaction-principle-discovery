# Product and experiment counting units

The product, donor and experiment totals in the article answer different
questions and are not expected to be equal.

| Record | Count | Counting unit | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Main Table 1 | 40 | displayed product assignments | Products 3a-3an comprise 11 N-, 13 S- and 16 O-functionalized structures. The displayed yields are quantitative 1H NMR yields obtained with an internal standard. |
| Figure 6 RP-guided collection | 35/94 | unique donor structures | A donor is positive when at least one assigned product peak has a numerical UV response greater than zero. This is a donor-level screening summary, not a count of product structures. |
| Experimental-reagent collection | 102 | unique donor structures | Structures represented across the complete ordered-pair ledger. |
| Experimental-pair ledger | 108 | ordered electrophile-donor pairs | One donor may occur with more than one electrophile, so pair and donor totals differ. |
| SI Section S7.5 | 15 | selected characterization entries | This selected set is not a second enumeration of Table 1. It contains 12 fluorinated compounds and three non-fluorinated compounds. |

The 40 Table 1 entries and the 35 positive donors therefore use different
denominators. A single donor can contribute products with different
electrophiles, and a donor-level positive call requires only one assigned
nonzero product response. Conversely, the 15 entries in SI S7.5 document a
selected characterization set and should not be used as the Table 1 product
total.

The repository retains proposed product options separately from measured
products. The 103 records in [Table R2](../candidates/product_options/) are
site- or pathway-level proposed connectivities generated during candidate
design; they are not 103 experimentally observed products.
