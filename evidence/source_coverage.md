## Scope of the principle interpretation

Source-specific observations establish precursor, steric and reactive-site dependence. Connecting those observations to catalyst compatibility provides a conditional design rationale; it does not establish a universal kinetic relationship. The [P/Se applicability records](applicability/README.md), [source-specific cases](source_cases/README.md) and [data interpretation guide](../docs/interpretation.md) provide the individual comparisons and their limits.

The four principle cards specify a relationship, directional implications, source support, exceptions and candidate-design rules. RP1 concerns access to a fluorinated acceptor; RP2 couples donor availability to capture competence; RP3 relates intermediate state to fluorine loss or retention; RP4 relates selective formation to subsequent consumption. Twenty-eight distinct evidence records connect the four cards to eight sources. The candidate crosswalk applies activation, capture, endpoint and persistence questions to 100 structures. It is an explanatory mapping, not a trained prediction model.
