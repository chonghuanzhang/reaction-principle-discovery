# P07 — di-n-pentylphosphine oxide

Di-n-pentylphosphine oxide gives the saturated CF2 product in 50% isolated yield, contrasting with the trace response of diethyl phosphite. This donor-specific positive result concerns a fluorination endpoint different from the target monofluoroalkene.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

Same P(O)H broad label gives a different donor outcome from diethyl phosphite.

Di-n-pentylphosphine oxide forms the saturated CF2-retaining product 4f in 50% isolated yield, whereas diethyl phosphite gives only trace product 4g under the examined radical regime. This donor-specific positive result concerns a different fluorination endpoint; it does not establish formation of the project monofluoro target.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
