# SE06 — diphenyl diselenide as co-catalyst, not transferred Se partner

Diphenyl diselenide acts as a co-catalyst in photochemical hydroazolation and helps select a CF2-retaining C–N product. Selenium is not the transferred partner in this example; it does not establish a C–Se monofluoroalkene reaction or a failed selenium-donor test.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

Selenium functions as co-catalyst in a C-N forming route; this is not a C-Se monofluoroalkene precedent or a failed selenium donor test.

The authors propose photocatalyst regeneration, deprotonation and hydrogen-atom transfer by selenium-derived species, selecting a CF2-retaining C–N endpoint. The source does not establish failed nucleophilic Se incorporation in the project system.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
