# P05 — Ph2P(O)H, 3 equiv

Peroxide-initiated hydrophosphinylation retains two fluorines and gives a saturated phosphorus product. Optimization reports more than 95% 19F NMR yield; the separate scope experiment gives 90% isolated yield under a longer 24–26 h reaction time. Neither result is a monofluorovinyl endpoint.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

Alternative to monofluoro target. Source scope3a gives90% isolated, average of two independent24–26h reactions.

Peroxide-mediated pathway avoids fluoride loss for this substrate/donor combination; mechanism is proposed radical chain.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
