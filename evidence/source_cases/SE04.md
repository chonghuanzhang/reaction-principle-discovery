# SE04 — PhSeSePh

Omitting BnBr lowers the diphenyl-diselenide reaction yield from 91% to 11% by 1H NMR. Product formation remains positive. The authors’ selenium-interception explanation is a mechanistic interpretation rather than an isolated proof of the whole yield difference.
