# SE03 — PhSeSePh

Diphenyl diselenide gives the beta-selenide in 91% 1H NMR yield with 18:1 regioselectivity and 97:3 enantiomeric ratio under the compared NiH conditions. It is positive selenium chemistry on a nonfluorinated substrate and contrasts with phenylselenol in SE02.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

rr18:1, er97:3; nonfluorinated local compatibility comparison.

Diphenyl diselenide gives 3a in 91% 1H NMR yield under the stated amide-directed NiH conditions. This positive diselenide result and the phenylselenol failure compare donor forms on a nonfluorinated substrate; neither establishes the project C(F)–Se outcome.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
