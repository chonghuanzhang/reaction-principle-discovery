# SE05 — PhSeH

Phenylselenol gives PhSe–CH=CF2 in 66% reported yield in the TFHZ-Tfs-derived transformation. Both fluorines remain on the vinyl carbon that is not bonded to selenium; this connectivity differs from the desired monofluorovinyl C(F)–Se product.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

Alternative topology/fluorine count. Cannot be relabelled desired C(F)–Se monoF product.

Two fluorines remain and Se is attached to the other vinyl carbon. It is an alternative endpoint, not the target C(F)–Se connectivity.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
