# P02 — P-bound fluorovinyl intermediate from P01

Hydrofluorination of the fluorovinyl phosphine from P01 gives a complex mixture without the desired phosphorus-substituted gem-difluoride. This later-step result leaves the positive first-stage monofluorovinyl product intact; no detection limit is supplied.

## Interpretation and transfer limits

Complex product mixture. No detection limit supplied. This is NOT a negative label for P01 monoF formation.

This second-step negative cannot erase the first-step positive monofluoro product.

The source record does not establish an exact match to the carbonyl-retaining RCOCH₂CF₃ → RCOCH=C(F)–Nu target.
