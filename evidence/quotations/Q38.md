# Q38 — Phenylselenol vinylation gives a different CF2 topology

## Chemical claim

Benzeneselenol gave PhSe–CH=CF2 in 66% yield under the TFHZ-Tfs-derived coupling conditions. This product retains two fluorines, with selenium attached to the other vinyl carbon.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L346](../../literature/sources/L346.md). [doi:10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z)

**Location:** 10.1038/s41467-018-08253-z; Main-text substrate scope, paragraph beginning “Having established”; Figure 3, product 25.

## Source passage

> benzeneselenol performed equally well, affording the selenodifluoroalkene 25 in 66% yield

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
