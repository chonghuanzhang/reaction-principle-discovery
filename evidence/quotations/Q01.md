# Q01 — Stepwise formation of a difluoroacrylamide intermediate

## Chemical claim

After addition of one equivalent of isopropylmagnesium chloride, 19F NMR monitoring detected a β,β-difluoroacrylamide intermediate at −76.0 and −71.2 ppm. Addition of the second equivalent produced the (E)-fluoroalkene 8.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L088](../../literature/sources/L088.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599; Physical PDF p. 2; Stepwise 19F NMR mechanistic experiment.
