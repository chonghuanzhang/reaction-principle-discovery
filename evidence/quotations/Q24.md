# Q24 — Abstract-level evidence for monofluorovinyl phosphorus products

## Chemical claim

The author abstract reports that gem-difluorostyrenes react with diphenylphosphine oxide or a reagent described as “dialkyl phosphate” in the presence of DBU at room temperature to give monofluorovinyl phosphorus products.

**Attribution:** Author-abstract report.

**Source:** [L357](../../literature/sources/L357.md). [doi:10.1002/ejoc.201801602](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201801602)

**Location:** 10.1002/ejoc.201801602; Publisher-deposited author abstract; no PDF page assigned.

## Source passage

> from gem‐difluorostyrenes with diphenylphosphine oxide/dialkyl phosphate and DBU at room temperature

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
