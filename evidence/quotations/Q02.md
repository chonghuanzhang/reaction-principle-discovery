# Q02 — Proposed rapid fluoride elimination from an unobserved enolate

## Chemical claim

Because the proposed enolate was not detected by 19F NMR, the authors suggested that fluoride elimination from this intermediate is instantaneous.

**Attribution:** Author-proposed hypothesis.

**Source:** [L088](../../literature/sources/L088.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599; Physical PDF p. 2; Mechanistic interpretation based on the absence of an observable enolate signal.

## Source passage

> The enolate intermediate is not observed by 19F NMR, suggesting that the elimination of fluoride is instantaneous.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
