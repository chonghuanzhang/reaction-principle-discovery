# Q21 — Stoichiometry controls diazaphospholene-catalysed hydrodefluorination

## Chemical claim

Diazaphospholene-catalysed hydrodefluorination of trifluoromethylalkenes gives gem-difluoroalkenes or terminal monofluoroalkenes, with chemoselectivity controlled by reactant stoichiometry.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L189](../../literature/sources/L189.md). [doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3)

**Location:** 10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3; Physical PDF p. 1; Abstract: reported reaction and chemoselective product control.

## Source passage

> Here we show a diazaphospholene-catalyzed hydrodefluorination of tri fluoromethylalkenes to chemoselectively construct gem-difluoroalkenes and terminal mono fluoroalkenes by simple adjustment of the reactant stoichiometry.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
