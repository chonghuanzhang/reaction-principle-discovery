# Q31 — Fluorovinyl phosphine hydrofluorination gives no desired product

## Chemical claim

Subsequent hydrofluorination of the monofluorovinyl phosphine gave a complex product mixture, with no desired phosphorus-substituted gem-difluoride detected.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, section III, “Phosphine pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> subjection of this fluorovinyl phosphine to standard hydrofluorination conditions results in a complex mixture of products with no desired phosphorus substituted gem-difluoride detected

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
