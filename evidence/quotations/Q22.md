# Q22 — An observed hydrophosphination intermediate supports a stepwise pathway

## Chemical claim

Stoichiometric and NMR experiments detected a hydrophosphination intermediate. Heating to 70 °C converted this intermediate to the mono-hydrodefluorination product and a fluorinated phosphorus species, supporting a stepwise addition/β-fluoride-elimination pathway.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation and mechanistic inference.

**Source:** [L189](../../literature/sources/L189.md). [doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3)

**Location:** 10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3; Physical PDF p. 2; Stoichiometric and spectroscopic mechanistic evidence.

## Source passage

> A preliminary attempt indicated that the reaction of α-trifluoromethyl-styrene 2a with diazaphospholene 1 primarily gave the hydrophosphination intermediate A. Only handful HDF products 3a and 1-F64 were obtained (Fig. However, the intermediate A was completely converted to 3a and 1-F at an elevated temperature (70 °C). NMR spectroscopic studies showed a hydrophosphination intermediate, which subsequently underwent β-F elimination at elevated temperatures.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
