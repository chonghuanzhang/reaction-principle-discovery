# Q28 — Coacid choice controls fluorovinyl ether hydrofluorination

## Chemical claim

Hydrofluorination of fluorovinyl ether 3 was ineffective with NEt3·3HF alone. Adding MsOH gave 83% yield of the gem-difluorinated ether with substantial side-product formation; using TFA as the coacid in toluene gave 99% yield.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Main text, Results, paragraph beginning “The potential for regioselective hydrofluorination”; Figure 3b.

## Source passage

> with NEt3·3HF increases the hydrofluorination yield to 83%, but with substantial side-product formation

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
