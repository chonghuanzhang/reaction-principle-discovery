# Q08 — Copper-modified Grignard donors favor beta substitution

## Chemical claim

Methylmagnesium bromide and phenylmagnesium bromide, modified with a copper(I) bromide–dimethyl sulfide complex, reacted with butenolide 1 exclusively at the β-position to give C-substituted products 25 and 26. Carbonyl-addition products were not observed.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L294](../../literature/sources/L294.md). [doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8)

**Location:** 10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8; Physical PDF p. 3; Results and discussion: reactions of soft carbanions.
