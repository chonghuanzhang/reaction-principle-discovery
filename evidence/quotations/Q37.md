# Q37 — Selenosulfonates give monofluoroalkenes by nickel-catalysed coupling

## Chemical claim

The author abstract reports nickel-catalysed reductive cross-coupling of gem-difluoroalkenes with selenosulfonates to give selenylated monofluoroalkenes through C–F cleavage and C–Se bond formation.

**Attribution:** Author-abstract report.

**Source:** [L359](../../literature/sources/L359.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.9b01387; PubMed/EuropePMC author abstract; no PDF page assigned.

## Source passage

> formation of thiolated or selenylated monofluoroolefins via regioselective C-F bond cleavage and C-S or C-Se bond formation
