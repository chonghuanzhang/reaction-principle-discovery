# Q04 — Stereoselective Grignard conversion of trifluoropropanamide

## Chemical claim

Addition of commonly used Grignard reagents to morpholine 3,3,3-trifluoropropanamide affords (E)-β-fluoro-α,β-unsaturated amides with high stereocontrol.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L088](../../literature/sources/L088.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599; Physical PDF p. 1; Abstract describing the reported transformation and stereochemical outcome.

## Source passage

> To address this need, we have designed a reagent, morpholine 3,3,3-tri fluoropropanamide, that creates ( E)-β-fluoro-α,β-unsaturated amides upon the addition of many commonly used Grignard reagents. Reactions with this reagent enable a high level of stereocontrol in the fluoroalkene product.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
