# Q36 — BnSePh formation supports interception of a selenium species

## Chemical claim

A preparative reaction using 1.0 mmol of alkene gave BnSePh, consistent with interception of a nucleophilic selenium species generated during diselenide activation.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation and mechanistic inference.

**Source:** [L344](../../literature/sources/L344.md). [doi:10.1002/advs.77070](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77070)

**Location:** 10.1002/advs.77070; Mechanistic investigations, body paragraph 12; Figure 2D.

## Source passage

> BnSePh was formed in 1.77 mmol (mass recovery relative to 1a ), supporting the generation of a nucleophilic selenium species during diselenide activation

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
