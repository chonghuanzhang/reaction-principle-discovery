# Q03 — TMEDA and the stereochemistry of phenyllithium addition

## Chemical claim

Phenyllithium in the presence of TMEDA gave the double-addition product in 75% yield; the ketone was reported exclusively as the (Z)-fluoroalkene. The authors state that TMEDA improved selectivity and yield.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L088](../../literature/sources/L088.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.orglett.0c00599; Physical PDF p. 3; Alternative organolithium reaction and contrasting stereochemical outcome; the compound-number inconsistency is recorded under contradictions.
