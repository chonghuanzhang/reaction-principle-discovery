# Q25 — Peroxide conditions favor a saturated CF2 phosphorus product

## Chemical claim

For p-MeO-C6H4–CH=CF2 and Ph2P(O)H (3 equivalents), 5 mol% di-tert-butyl peroxide in tBuOH at 120 °C for 16 h under N2 gave >95% 19F NMR yield in optimization. The scope experiment for product 3a gave 90% isolated yield, averaged over two reactions conducted for 24–26 h.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L133](../../literature/sources/L133.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562; Physical PDF p. 2 (printed p. 14013), Table 1, entry 8; physical PDF p. 3, Figure 3, product 3a.

## Source passage

> The optimized reaction conditions enabled coupling of a wide array of gem-difluoroalkenes with diphenylphosphine oxide to deliver difluorinated products exclusively

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
