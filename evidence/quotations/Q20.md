# Q20 — Extra silane can remove the third fluorine

## Chemical claim

The authors report that further increasing the amount of PhSiH3 can remove the third fluorine from some substrates bearing electron-withdrawing groups.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L189](../../literature/sources/L189.md). [doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3)

**Location:** 10.1038/s41467-021-23101-3; Physical PDF p. 4; Over-defluorination boundary.

## Source passage

> Note that further increase of the amount of PhSiH 3 could remove the third fluoride from some substrates with electron-withdrawing groups.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
