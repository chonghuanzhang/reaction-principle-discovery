# Q17 — Boronic-acid loading controls overcoupling

## Chemical claim

The reaction generally favoured monocoupled products. For 3m, lowering the boronic-acid loading to near-stoichiometric quantities suppressed overcoupling and allowed isolation of 3m in moderate yield.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L223](../../literature/sources/L223.md). [doi:10.1002/anie.201605651](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201605651)
