# Q26 — Nitro substitution permits fluoride elimination with or without peroxide

## Chemical claim

Nitro-substituted gem-difluoroalkenes underwent fluorine-elimination processes both with and without peroxide under the examined conditions.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L133](../../literature/sources/L133.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562; Physical PDF p. 3 (printed p. 14014), first paragraph.

## Source passage

> gem-Difluoroalkenes containing nitro groups were not tolerated under these conditions, as the reactions proceeded through F-elimination processes both with and without peroxide

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
