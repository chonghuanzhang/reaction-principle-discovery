# Q35 — BnBr improves the diselenide reaction yield

## Chemical claim

With PhSeSePh as the selenium source, omitting the BnBr scavenger lowered the product yield from 91% to 11% by 1H NMR.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L344](../../literature/sources/L344.md). [doi:10.1002/advs.77070](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77070)

**Location:** 10.1002/advs.77070; Results and Discussion, body paragraph 5; Table 1, entry 7 compared with entry 4.

## Source passage

> Omitting the benzyl bromide scavenger resulted in a dramatic collapse in efficiency, yielding only 11% of the product
