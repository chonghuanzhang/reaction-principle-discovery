# Q29 — Hydrofluorination conditions depend on the donor-derived intermediate

## Chemical claim

Phenol-derived fluorovinyl ethers required the stronger coacid MsOH for subsequent hydrofluorination, whereas fluorovinyl alkyl thioethers required pyridine·HF with a CuCl additive.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Main text, paragraph beginning “We next sought to engage phenols”; Figure 4.

## Source passage

> the use of the stronger acid MsOH over TFA is needed to promote subsequent hydrofluorination

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
