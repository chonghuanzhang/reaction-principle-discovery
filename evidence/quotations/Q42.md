# Q42 — A diol gives low yield and a proposed hydrogen-bonding effect

## Chemical claim

The tested diol gave fluorovinyl ether signals corresponding to 28% yield by 19F NMR and poor mass balance. The authors propose that additional hydrogen bonding lowers nucleophilicity.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation and proposed explanation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, “Diol pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> Diol pronucleophiles: during the alcohol pronucleophile studies, we investigated diols and hypothesized that steric effects could influence site-selectivity. We found, however, that these substrates result in low yield and mass balance of the corresponding vinyl ether (e.g., 3-methylbutane-1,3-diol and 1 gave a mixture of characteristic fluorovinyl ether signals that integrate to 28% yield in the 19F NMR spectrum under standard conditions), likely due to a lower nucleophilicity caused by additional H-bonding.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
