# Q33 — A productive fluoroenamine resists subsequent hydrofluorination

## Chemical claim

The N-derived monofluorinated enamine was accessible, but its subsequent hydrofluorination was unsuccessful under several conditions optimized for fluorovinyl ether hydrofluorination.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, section III, “Amine pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> Hydrofluorination of this enamine intermediate was unsuccessful under a variety of our optimized conditions for fluorovinyl ether hydrofluorination

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
