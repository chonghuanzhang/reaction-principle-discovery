# Q10 — Photocatalytic hydrothiolation retains both fluorines

## Chemical claim

The authors report photocatalytic hydrothiolation of gem-difluoroalkenes to afford α,α-difluoroalkyl thioethers.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L104](../../literature/sources/L104.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343; Physical PDF p. 1; Abstract; reaction definition and product class.

## Source passage

> Herein, we report a photocatalytic hydrothiolation of gem-difluoroalkenes that enables access to a broad spectrum of α,α−difluoroalkylthioethers.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
