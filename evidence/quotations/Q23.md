# Q23 — Polar addition and elimination in gem-difluoroalkene chemistry

## Chemical claim

The authors summarize a polar addition/elimination pathway in which nucleophilic addition to gem-difluoroalkenes under basic conditions gives anionic intermediates that undergo β-fluoride elimination. They cite prior primary literature, including Peng et al. (2019).

**Attribution:** Author mechanistic summary of prior literature.

**Source:** [L133](../../literature/sources/L133.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562; Physical PDF p. 1 (printed p. 14012), Introduction.

## Source passage

> nucleophilic addition reactions of gem-difluoroalkenes with a wide variety of nucleophiles that proceed under basic conditions typically generate anionic intermediates that decompose through β−F elimination to deliver monofluorinated products

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
