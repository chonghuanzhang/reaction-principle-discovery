# Q34 — Phenylselenol and diselenide differ in NiH hydroselenylation

## Chemical claim

In the NiH-catalysed hydroselenylation of nonfluorinated N-phenylbut-3-enamide, PhSeH did not deliver the desired β-selenide. Under the compared conditions, PhSeSePh gave 91% 1H NMR yield, an 18:1 regioisomeric ratio and a 97:3 enantiomeric ratio.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L344](../../literature/sources/L344.md). [doi:10.1002/advs.77070](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77070)

**Location:** 10.1002/advs.77070; Results and Discussion, body paragraph 5; Table 1, entries 1 and 4.

## Source passage

> Nucleophilic phenyl selenol ( [Se]‐1 ) and traditional electrophilic selenyl halides ( [Se]‐2 and [Se]‐3 ) failed to deliver any of the desired product
