# Q07 — Regioselective alkoxide substitution on difluorobutenolides

## Chemical claim

Sodium and lithium alkoxides displaced vinylic fluorine in difluorobutenolides 1 and 2 with complete regioselectivity at the β-carbon, affording products 4–10.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L294](../../literature/sources/L294.md). [doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8)

**Location:** 10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8; Physical PDF p. 2; Results and discussion: reactions of oxyanions.

## Source passage

> Results and discussion Reactions of oxyanions All reactions with sodium and lithium alkoxides carried out took place as in Scheme 1.
