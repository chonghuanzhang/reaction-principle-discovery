# Q09 — Organolithium donors cause a different rearrangement pathway

## Chemical claim

Methyllithium and phenyllithium gave rearranged products 27 and 28. These products lacked the characteristic carbonyl absorption of the starting butenolide ring and contained a ketone group.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L294](../../literature/sources/L294.md). [doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8)

**Location:** 10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8; Physical PDF p. 3; Results and discussion: hard carbanions and rearrangement.

## Source passage

> 1 Reactions of hard carbanions and a novel rearrangement Methyllithium and phenyllithium surprisingly 1 afforded compounds 27 and 28 as the end products in reactions with 1 (Scheme 2). Compounds 27 and 28 were isomeric with the products 25 and 26, but did not contain the 2-buten-4-olide ring as shown by the absence of bands at ca. 1775–1780 cm 21 in their IR spectra. Instead, each contained a ketone group ( dC ca. 195–198 ppm; nmax 1706–1723 cm 21).

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
