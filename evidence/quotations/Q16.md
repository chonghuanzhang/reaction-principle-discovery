# Q16 — Organoboron precursor form controls transfer competence

## Chemical claim

The reaction conditions accommodated boronic acids and the reported potassium organoboron salts. Boronic esters reacted sluggishly, and arylstannanes and arylsilanes were inert both with and without added fluoride.
