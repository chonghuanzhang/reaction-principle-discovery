# Q14 — A stable thioether excludes one intermediate pathway

## Chemical claim

The difluorinated thioether remained stable under the tested K2CO3 conditions. The authors therefore exclude it as an intermediate leading to the monofluorovinyl product under those conditions.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation and mechanistic inference.

**Source:** [L104](../../literature/sources/L104.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343; Physical PDF p. 4; Results and discussion; product-stability control.

## Source passage

> Of note, the difluorinated products are stable to the previously reported conditions (K2CO3, 30 °C, MeCN; Figure 4B; S11).22 Thus, difluorinated thioether 16 is not an intermediate en route to the monofluorovinylether product 3.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
