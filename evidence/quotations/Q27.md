# Q27 — Phosphorus donor identity changes the saturated-product outcome

## Chemical claim

Diethyl phosphite gave only trace hydrophosphinylation product 4g, and further optimization was unsuccessful. Di-n-pentylphosphine oxide gave 4f in 50% yield, whereas ethyl phenylphosphinate gave 4e in 29% yield with 20 mol% peroxide.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L133](../../literature/sources/L133.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.3c01562; Physical PDF p. 3 (printed p. 14014), Figure 4 and accompanying text.

## Source passage

> diethyl phosphite [HP(O)(OEt)2] did not react under these reaction conditions and further attempts to optimize the conditions (time, solvent, temperature, other peroxides) were unsuccessful

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
