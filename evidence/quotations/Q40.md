# Q40 — The proposed acidity explanation for piperidine failure

## Chemical claim

Piperidine did not form the fluorinated enamine intermediate, and poor mass balance was observed alongside the gem-difluoroalkene. The authors tentatively attribute this result to the low acidity of alkylamines.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation and proposed explanation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, “Amine pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> Amine pronucleophiles: we found that alkylamines (e.g., piperidine) do not add to give the fluorinated enamine intermediate and poor mass balance is observed alongside gem-difluoroalkene intermediate. This is likely due to the low acidity of alkylamines.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
