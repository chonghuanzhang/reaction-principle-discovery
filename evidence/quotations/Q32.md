# Q32 — N-Methylaniline and piperidine behave differently

## Chemical claim

N-Methylaniline gave the fluoroenamine in 82% yield using NaHMDS in N,N-dimethylacetamide. Piperidine did not give the fluorinated enamine under the examined conditions, and poor mass balance was observed alongside the gem-difluoroalkene intermediate.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, section III, “Amine pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> 1 and N-methylaniline give 82% yield under standard conditions using NaHMDS in N,N-dimethylacetamide

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
