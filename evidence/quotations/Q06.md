# Q06 — Slower reaction of branched alkoxides

## Chemical claim

Under the reported conditions, the branched alkoxides examined reacted more slowly than the primary alkoxides examined.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L294](../../literature/sources/L294.md). [doi:10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8)

**Location:** 10.1016/s0040-4020(00)00167-8; Physical PDF p. 2; Results and discussion: alkoxide scope and condition effects.

## Source passage

> The reactions of branched alkoxides were slower than that of primary alkoxides.

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
