# Q15 — Primary, secondary and tertiary thiol scope

## Chemical claim

The tested primary and secondary alkyl thiols gave products in 82–95% yield. Two tertiary thiols gave the corresponding products in 87% and 39% yield.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L104](../../literature/sources/L104.md). [doi:10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343)

**Location:** 10.1021/acs.joc.2c02343; Physical PDF p. 4; Results and discussion; alkyl-thiol scope.

## Source passage

> The steric hindrance of the thiol did not greatly influence reactivity, with primary and secondary thiols reacting to give products in excellent yields (30d – 30h; 82 – 95%). Further, tertiary thiols, which have not been effectively coupled in other systems, successfully generated the product in moderate to excellent yields (30i, 87%; 30j, 39%).

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
