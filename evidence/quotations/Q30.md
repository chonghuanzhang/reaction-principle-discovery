# Q30 — Positive first-stage formation of a fluorovinyl phosphine

## Chemical claim

Diphenylphosphine reacted with (4,4,4-trifluorobutoxy)methylbenzene 1 to form a monofluorovinyl phosphine in a reported yield of 37%.

**Attribution:** Author-reported observation.

**Source:** [L350](../../literature/sources/L350.md). [doi:10.1039/d6sc01042c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6sc01042c)

**Location:** 10.1039/d6sc01042c; Supporting Information, p. S15, section III, “Phosphine pronucleophiles”.

## Source passage

> diphenylphosphine undergoes the addition/elimination sequence with 1 to afford the corresponding fluorovinyl phosphine interemediate in moderate yield (37% yield) under standard conditions

The passage supports the attributed source claim. Applying it to another donor, substrate, activation regime or product endpoint requires a separate chemical argument.
