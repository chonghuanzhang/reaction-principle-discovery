# L343-06 — Condition-dependent competition between fluorinated ketones

## Source claim

Under condition A, competing RCOCF3 reacts while difluoromethyl ketone 3A is recovered in 84% and 4A is not formed. Under stronger conditions the same class can proceed further. Failure is therefore condition/endpoint specific.

**Source:** [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md). [doi:10.1039/d5sc03915k](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5sc03915k)

**Evidence type:** measured no target. **Access:** primary full text.

**Location:** Fig. 4C; Primary article paragraph 18.

## Source passage

> We also performed a competition experiment (Fig. 4C). Structurally similar trifluoromethyl ketone 1W and difluoromethyl ketone 3A were reacted under Condition A, which is optimized for the synthesis of difluoromethyl ketones. The results revealed that only 1W was converted into difluoromethyl ketone 3W, while 3A was recovered in 84% yield, and no monofluoromethyl ketone 4A was formed. This indicates that under these reaction conditions, difluoromethyl ketones do not undergo further transformations.
