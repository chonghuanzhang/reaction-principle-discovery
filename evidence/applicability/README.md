# Phosphorus and selenium applicability

The target transformation retains the carbonyl of RCOCH₂CF₃ and forms a direct vinyl C–Nu bond in RCOCH=C(F)–Nu. A relevant transfer claim must therefore specify the donor, acceptor, activation regime and desired product connectivity. A different product establishes endpoint competition; it does not establish absence of reaction.

The 25 records below cover nine source identities: 23 substantive observations or mechanistic interpretations and two metadata-only records. None establishes a directly matching negative Ph₂PH/RCOCH₂CF₃ or PhSeH/RCOCH₂CF₃ coupling. These are literature-applicability records, not an experimental success rate or a model benchmark.

- [L343-01](L343-01.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · Ph2P(O)H (1.2 equiv) and K2CO3 (2 equiv), 1,4-dioxane, 80 °C, 6 h convert model RCOCF3 to RCOCHF2 in 80% yield. With 4 Å molecular sieves, DFEP 2Aa is obtained in 80% yield. The change concerns endpoint selection, not disappearance of P reactivity.

- [L343-02](L343-02.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · 2.5 equiv Ph2P(O)H at 130 °C gives monofluoromethyl ketone 4A in 67%; 4 equiv at 160 °C gives methyl ketone 6A in 47%. A monofluoromethyl ketone is not a monofluorovinyl C–P product.

- [L343-03](L343-03.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · The final primary text reports failure and recovery for strongly electron-withdrawing substituted trifluoromethyl ketones. Individual identities and yields are not assigned in that paragraph. Hydration-related interference is the authors’ mechanistic explanation; the text calls the hydrate a hemiacetal, which should not be silently endorsed as exact structural terminology. Do not assign this failure to the project electrophile.

- [L343-04](L343-04.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · Separately prepared protonated phospha-Brook side product 5A gives no 3A under K2CO3/80 °C conditions. This distinguishes an unproductive trapped intermediate from the productive anionic elimination sequence; it is not a failed addition of Ph2PH to project substrate A.

- [L343-05](L343-05.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · With Ph2P(O)H the monofluoroenol phosphinate could not be isolated and monofluoromethyl ketone formed instead. Diethyl phosphite and p-methoxy diarylphosphine oxide provide isolable enol phosphinates. The relevant retained phosphorus linkage is C–O–P; inability to isolate it cannot establish inability to form direct vinyl C–P.

- [L343-06](L343-06.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · Under condition A, competing RCOCF3 reacts while difluoromethyl ketone 3A is recovered in 84% and 4A is not formed. Under stronger conditions the same class can proceed further. Failure is therefore condition/endpoint specific.

- [L343-07](L343-07.md) · [L343](../../literature/sources/L343.md) · The authors propose P-centred Pudovik addition, phospha-Brook migration of phosphorus from carbon to oxygen, beta-fluoride elimination and HF-dependent endpoint selection.

- [L367-01](L367-01.md) · [L367](../../literature/sources/L367.md) · The accessible author abstract reports preparation of the reagent and its use to obtain dimethylphosphinoyl-derived nitriles, amines, acetone, acetic-acid and acetoacetic-acid derivatives. Exact substrates, conditions, atom-of-attack, fluorination state and individual yields require the full text; no project monofluorovinyl product can be assigned from the abstract.
