UV response depends on the absorption characteristics of the detected compounds and can therefore differ across the substrate/product combinations used in the N–H, O–H and S–H systems. We evaluate optimization gains within each fixed substrate pair under consistent analytical conditions. The three trajectories in Fig. 5a thus describe within-system improvements, while their absolute signal levels are not used to compare reaction yields across systems. Representative 12 × 8 grids of existing experimental measurements visualize the response distributions within selected regions of the decision space (Fig. 5b–d).
